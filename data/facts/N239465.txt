The merchandise in question is the Yamaha Rio Series, Audio Network Input/Output Rack Mountable Units (model #s Rio3224-D and Rio1608-D made in Japan) and (model #s Ri8-D and Ro8-D made in China). These units convert standard analog audio signals to digital signals which allow the signals to be transmitted over distances of up to 100 meters, between networked devices, via standard Ethernet cables using the Dante network protocol. The Yamaha Rio3224-D is a rack-mountable input/output network device. It utilizes the Dante network protocol to receive standard analog input signals, convert those signals to digital Dante signal protocol and transmit the signals, using Cat5e communication cabling, to any other Dante compatible device, such as mixers, amplifiers, processors, etc. It can also receive Dante digital audio and data network signals and convert them to analog or AES-3 digital signals. This unit has 32 analog inputs, 16 analog outputs and 8 AES/EBU digital outputs and a pair of RJ45/EtherCON connectors for Dante network connection. The Yamaha Rio 1608-D is a rack-mountable input/output network device that uses the Dante network protocol to transmit and receive digital audio and data signals using CAT5e communication cabling on the digital side and XLR3 on the analog side. This unit has 16 analog inputs and 8 analog outputs and a pair of RJ45/EtherCON connectors for Dante Network connection. The Yamaha Ri8-D is a rack-mountable input network device which utilizes the Dante network protocol to transmit and receive digital audio and data signals using Cat5ecommunication cabling on the digital side and XLR3 on the analog side. This unit has 8 analog inputs and a pair of RJ45 connectors for Dante network connection. The Yamaha Ro8-D is a rack-mountable output network device which utilizes Dante network protocol to transmit and receive digital audio and data signals using Cat5ecommunication cabling on the digital side and XLR3 on the analog side. This unit has 8 analog inputs and a pair of RJ45 connectors for Dante network connection.
