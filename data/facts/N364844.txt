In lieu of samples, illustrative literature, descriptions, and an artist profile were provided. Item 1, “On the Edge of a Cloud (1792),” and item 2, “Lit from Within (1793),” are landscape and sunset oil paintings created by the artist Christine Code that are mounted onto wooden frames. Information provided states, (1) the artist exercised her own aesthetic imagination and conception in creating each piece, (2) they bear her signature, year of creation, are completely executed by hand, and (3) at no time during the creation process was an electromechanical process used. The paintings are derived from the artist’s own references, plein-air studies, sketches, imagination, and they convey tints and shades of yellow, green, red, blue, orange, black, white, and other colors. The dimensions of the paintings are 17 ¾ inches wide by 25 ¾ inches long.
