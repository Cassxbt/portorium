The merchandise under consideration is described as Nutcracker Tealight Holders, item number 9017130. A sample of the holders was submitted with your ruling request and will be returned to you. The Nutcracker Tealight Holders are hollow and are shaped to resemble two styles of nutcracker soldiers. The first features a blue hat and measures approximately 6.25 inches high by 3.5 inches wide by 3 inches long. The second sports a red hat and measures approximately 6 inches high by 3.5 inches wide by 3 inches long. Both holders provide an opening in the back for placement of a tea light candle, and feature 4 star-shaped cutouts in the front. When lit, the light from the candle shines through the cutouts, enhancing the overall decorative effect of the article. In email correspondence dated April 11, 2012, you stated that the holders are made of dolomite ceramic.
