The items under consideration are referred to as Faceted Cup, Left (Part number 303-00035-000.A1) and  Faceted Cup, Right, (Part number 303-00036-000.A1). Both components are faceted ear cups for  Lightspeed’s Zulu 3 aviation headset. The left and right faceted cup assemblies are both composed of a dome  shaped magnesium housing. This design allows for active and passive noise reduction by creating a physical  barrier and seal to isolate and block external noise and facilitate the active noise reduction function of the  headset.
