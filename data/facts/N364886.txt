The product under consideration is described as a modular freestanding high ropes course system designed for recreational climbing and balancing activities. The system consists of structural steel poles and related structural components, platforms, timber components, steel cables, ropes, climbing and balancing elements, connection hardware, and related safety-system components. After being fitted with personal protective equipment and receiving a safety briefing, participants ascend to the elevated course where they choose a route and cross successive suspended challenge elements by climbing, balancing, stepping, crawling, gripping, or otherwise moving to reach the next platform between elements. They continue to choose successive routes and challenges until finally exiting the course. Per your submission, seven product families of the high ropes course system are available, including the KT-6, KT-7, KT-10, KT-12, KT-13, KT-14, and KT-18 configurations. The product families vary in the number and arrangement of poles, platforms, levels, and challenge elements. Compact versions of each product family, with a 20% smaller footprint, are also available. The system may be used as a standalone attraction or utilized at a resort, zoo, municipal recreation site, or amusement park. It will be imported unassembled and may be shipped in multiple containers due to the size of the merchandise.
