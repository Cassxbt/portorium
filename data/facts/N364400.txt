Items 1 through 5 comprise the Kaspi Shelf line, specifically the Large (SHW1906), Medium (SHW1606), and Small (SHW1306) standard models, alongside the Medium (SBW1606) and Small (SBW1306) Open Base variations. All are imported as flat-packed, ready-to-assemble (RTA) wooden furniture. The floor-standing furniture pieces are constructed of medium density fiberboard (MDF) panels with polyvinylchloride (PVC) edge banding and melamine-finished surfaces. The wooden panels of each item are assembled using the included knock-down camlock hardware and dowel pins. After assembly, items 1, 2, and 3 will have 3-tiers of shelving and items 4 and 5 will have 2-tiers of shelving with open bases. Literature provided states, “[T]he merchandise consists of freestanding shelving and storage furniture intended primarily for residential and educational environments, including homes, classrooms, playrooms, reading areas, and other learning spaces. The principal use is the storage, organization, and display of books, toys, educational materials, classroom supplies, and similar household or educational articles.” All five items share a uniform width of approximately 12.2 inches and a height of 24.8 inches, varying only in their length. Item 1 is the longest, measuring approximately 36.8 inches in length. Items 2 and 4 are identical in size, each with a length of approximately 25 inches. Similarly, items 3 and 5 share the same dimensions, both measuring approximately 13.2 inches long.
