The instant sample, identified as style Chenille Twist, is a 100 percent cotton hand loomed rug. The rug measures 21 x 34 inches. The ends of the rug are hemmed while the sides are selvage. The rug is woven with chenille yarns in the weft. The chenille yarns are raised forming a loop pile on the face of the rug. The rug will also be imported in a 30 x 48 and 42 x 66 inch size.
