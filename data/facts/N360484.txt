Item 646470 is a decorative pillow with an outer shell of 100 percent polyester woven pile fabric that is  stuffed with polyester fiber. The pillow comes in the shape of either a dog or a cat; both shapes include a tail. The front of the pillow is printed in a Day of the Dead or calavera motif with a multi-colored skeleton  including a skull with face, leg and torso bones on black fabric. The pillows feature plastic buttons for the  cat eyes, plastic imitation pearls for the dog eyes and a plastic jewel in the shape of a heart between the dog’s  eyes. The dog pillow measures 13 inches wide × 4.5 inches deep × 15 inches tall, and the cat pillow  measures 13 inches wide × 4.5 inches deep × 16 inches tall. The two pillows are sold separately surrounding  the Day of the Dead holiday.
