Images and product information were submitted in lieu of samples. The article under consideration is described as a portable, modular miniature golf game. The game is designed to allow consumers to construct and play a configurable miniature golf course indoors or outdoors. Each set consists of six 18” x 18” putting-surface grid tiles, including one tile containing a golf hole, attachable perimeter walls, three obstacles, one ramp accessory, four foam golf balls, one adjustable miniature-golf putter, anti-slip grip pads, and a carrying/storage bag. All components are packaged and sold together as a single retail set and are designed for use together in playing the miniature golf game.
