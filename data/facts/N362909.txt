Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is a multilayer wood panel. The panel measures approximately 2440 mm x  1220 mm x 18 mm. It consists of three layers: a core of edge-glued lumber that is surfaced on either side  with medium density fiberboard (MDF). The edge-glued lumber consists either of spruce/pine/fir (SPF) (a  coniferous wood), or Falcataria moluccana (a tropical wood), and measures 13 mm in thickness. The MDF  measures approximately 3 mm in thickness on the face and the back of the edge-glued lumber. From the  pictures provided, it appears that the MDF is also covered with a paper layer printed with wood grain. The panel is not considered plywood, veneered panels, or similar laminated wood, as it does not consist of  multiple layers of whole wood. The MDF is considered a surface covering, as is the printed paper layer.
