Photographs and descriptions  of three styles of decorative urns were submitted with your inquiry. The three articles under consideration are described as Decorative Memorial Cremation Urns Made of Casted  Aluminum. These aluminum handcrafted urns are manufactured through a casting process and are  individually hand-finished. The exterior surfaces are decorated with engraved artwork, themed memorial  designs, painted finishes, powder coating, and other decorative treatments. The item described as a Classic  Urn measures 10.5 x 7 inches. The item described as a Teardrop Urn is produced in multiple sizes: 10 x 8  inches or 12 x 8.5 inches. The item described as a Keepsake Urn measures 3 x 1.75 inches. The Classic Urn  and Teardrop Urn are intended for the containment and display of cremated human remains, while the  Keepsake urn is intended to hold a symbolic portion of cremated remains.
