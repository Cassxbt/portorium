The main components of the system consists of a wet feed hooper, wet feed augers and trough, the skid, electric motors (360V and 460V), a dry product auger, a rotary mixer with hardened steel flailing chains, a single belt drive consisting of an industrial grade double sided cogged transmission belt, and a control panel. The overall dimensions of the system are 323 inches (L) x 163 inches (H) x 76 inches (W). The systems weights approximately 11,135 lbs., and its is designed for ease of set-up and teardown. In operation, the FL-243 system uses a rotating chain-flail mechanism (a shaft lifted with free-swinging chains) to mechanically blend a liquid or semi fluid feed material (slurry) with a super absorbent polymer (SAP) to create a by-product that has a low slump discharge. The flail action breaks up and continuously turns the incoming material as SAP is metered in so the polymer distributes evenly and rapidly absorbs free liquid, converting the feed from a flowing liquid slurry into a solid, non-flowing mass suitable for transport and landfill disposal.
