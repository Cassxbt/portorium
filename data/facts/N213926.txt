The sample submitted will be retained by our office. The sample submitted is labeled “Silueta Secrets”. This woman’s support undergarment is constructed of 75% polyamide and 25% elastane knit fabric. The undergarment provides support to the abdominal, hip and waist area and measures approximately 14 inches from the top to the bottom. The undergarment features side seams, a back seam, a lined crotch, elasticized leg openings, an elasticized top which is lined with a soft adhesive silicone strip that prevents it from pulling down on the wearer and a mesh fabric in the lower backside. In your ruling request, you recommend classification 6108.22.90,  however, this garment exhibits the structural characteristics of a girdle. Customs views girdles to be undergarments which provide support and hold in the body along the lower torso, specifically including the waist and hips.
