The Large (BSW4906), Medium (BSW4606), and Small (BSW4306) Kaspi Bookshelves are flat-packed, ready-to-assemble (RTA) wooden furniture. The floor-standing furniture pieces are constructed of medium density fiberboard (MDF) panels with polyvinylchloride (PVC) edge banding and melamine-finished surfaces. The wooden panels of each item are assembled using the included knock-down camlock hardware and dowel pins. After assembly, the bookshelves will have 4-tiers of shelving and 4-front-facing display levels so books may be positioned with their covers facing outward for visibility and ease of access. The bookshelves are intended primarily for use in homes, classrooms, reading areas, playrooms, early-learning environments and similar residential or educational spaces. All three items measure approximately 18.5 inches wide and 24.8 inches high. Their lengths vary by model: Item 1 is 36.8 inches, Item 2 is 25 inches, and Item 3 is 13.2 inches.
