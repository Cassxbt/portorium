Item VX 8806.000, the “VX Bayed Enclosure System,” is a series of floor-standing and unequipped steel  storage cabinets that measure approximately 31" in width, 79" in height, and 24” in depth. The cabinets have  a single front door, rear panel, roof plate, and a mounting plate. The cabinets are constructed of 1.5mm steel  sheet metal and are designed to secure computer server equipment, powder distribution busbars, power  cables, power supplies, air-water heat exchangers, chillers, and additional hardware components (sold  separately).
