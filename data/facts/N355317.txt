The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Blue Bahia stone. Samples were submitted with your  ruling request and were forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Blue Bahia is a surface-worked white stone with blue specks and and blue and black streaks. From the  information you provided, its dimensions upon importation are 125 inches long by 78 inches wide by 3  centimeters thick. It is designed for use as countertops. Laboratory analysis has determined that the Blue Bahia is an other igneous stone.
