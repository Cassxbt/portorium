The ruling was requested on the “Scratch Magic Princess, Scratch & Sparkle Magic Drawing Kit” #5987. The activity kit contains the following items:Twelve Scratch & Sparkle Sparkling Drawing Boards (three each of Glittering Gold, Majestic Multicolor, Princess Pink and Shimmering Silver) One “Scratch & Sparkle” (one size fits all) paperboard crown Three acrylic gemstones with glue squares Two Princess picture framesOne Pretty Princess stencil (20+ shapes)One fine point wood stylusOne medium point wood stylusOne paper instruction sheet The activity kit enables the user to use the paperboard stencil, the black coated paperboard sheets, the paperboard crown and gemstones, and the pointed wood stylus tools in an activity to draw designs with a scratch off technique. The instruction sheet provides the user with step-by-step details to create pictures and designs when the black coated sheets are scratched off with the pointed drawing stylus to reveal the sparkle colors beneath the coating. The finished drawings are inserted into the scored, printed paperboard frames for display. The wood drawing stylus and gemstones are used to decorate the “one size fits all” black coated paperboard headpiece, similar to a crown, which is cut to size and shape, has one jagged side and measures approximately 22 inches long. The kit is designed for ages 5 and up.
