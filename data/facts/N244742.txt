Style 69085 is a knee pad made of polyethylene (PE) foam material. The knee pad will be sold and packaged in plastic bags as a pair. The knee pad is used as an accessory to work coveralls or other work garments. The pads can be inserted into pockets located in the knee area of coveralls or work pants to provide protection and comfort to the wearer. Each pad measures approximately 9” long x 5.5” wide x 0.5” high.
