A sample of the Yogitoes Skidless Premium Mat was received with your inquiry. The mat measures 24” in width x 68” in length and is constructed of 80% polyester and 20% nylon. Covering the entire back of the mat are silicone “eco nubs” spaced approximately ¼” apart. These nubs prevent the mat, when placed on another yoga mat, pad or low-pile carpet, from moving to help avoid slipping when performing yoga exercises. It also acts as an absorbent and hygienic layer between the user and yoga mat.
