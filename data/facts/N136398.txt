The sample submitted, style number UM9575, is a men’s hip-length jacket with a shell composed of two fabrics: a knit 100% polyester fabric on most of the body and sleeves, and a woven 100% nylon fabric on the upper chest, the shoulders and the upper sleeve areas. The entire jacket is visibly coated on the inner surface with polyurethane. The jacket has a stand-up collar, a full front opening secured by a zipper closure that extends to the top of the collar and an overlapping flap with hook and loop closures that covers the zipper area. The garment has long sleeves with a banded cuff secured at the wrist by a tab with a hook and loop fastener, two front pockets with zipper closures at the waist and a drawcord tightening through the bottom hem. The “Under Armour” tradename and logo are featured at various areas of the jacket.
