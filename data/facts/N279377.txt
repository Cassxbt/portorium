Temozolomide, imported in 5, 20, 100, 140, 180 and 250 mg capsules, is a medicinal preparation containing Temozolomide, an alkylating agent, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with newly diagnosed glioblastoma multiforme concomitantly with radiotherapy. Metronidazole, imported in 250 and 500 mg tablets, is a medicinal preparation containing Metronidazole, an antibacterial agent, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the prevention and treatment of certain bacterial and protozoal infections.
