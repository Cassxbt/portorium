A sample of a nonwoven t-shirt style bag was provided with your request and will be retained. You provided photographs and a sample of a t-shirt style bag called “PP T-shirt Bag.”  The bag is composed of a thin, semi-transparent, paper-like, 100 percent nonwoven polypropylene (PP) textile material. To form the bag, the nonwoven fabric is die-cut to shape as one panel, folded over and is sonic welded closed along one side of the gusset, bottom, and top of both carrying handles. These handles give the bag the appearance of a scoop neck t-shirt. The bag features an open top without a closure, an unlined interior, and is printed with the words “Groceries and More, The Best of Asia in America. HMart® since 1982.”  The bottom is not reinforced. The bag will be loosely attached to other t-shirt style bags as a result of the die cutting process and are scored to fit onto a metal rack at the point of purchase in the retail store. The t-shirt bag is available in two sizes: 21 inches in height by 12 inches in width with 6 inch side gussets and 20 inches in height by 11 inches in width with 7 inch side gussets, and is available in two colors: gray and white.
