This is in response to your submission of September 13, 2026, requesting a ruling on the classification of Raw Frozen Coconut Palm Sap. The subject merchandise consists of raw, unfermented coconut palm sap, commonly known as Neera. The product is harvested from the cut inflorescence (spadix) of the coconut palm (Cocos nucifera). The sap is a naturally occurring fluid that flows from the flower stalk when it is cut and tapped. It is not derived from, extracted from, or otherwise obtained from the fruit of the coconut palm. Immediately after collection, the raw sap is placed into food-grade polythene pouches, sealed, and frozen to prevent the natural fermentation that would otherwise occur due to its naturally high sugar content. The frozen pouches are subsequently packed into four-ply carton boxes, shrink-wrapped, and maintained at approximately -20°C during storage and transportation in refrigerated containers. At the time of exportation, the product contains no added ingredients, preservatives, flavorings, or processing aids and has not undergone fermentation or other processing beyond collection, packaging, and freezing. It is imported in its raw, frozen, unfermented condition, in bulk industrial packaging, for use solely as an ingredient in further processing in the United States.
