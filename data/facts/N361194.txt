The belt consists of multiple layers, including arubber top cover, reinforcement layers of 1-8 plies of polyester and nylon textile materials and a rubberbottom cover. The rubber layers are composed of a mixture of natural and synthetic rubber. The belts come inwidth ranges of 300 mm to 3000 mm and thicknesses of 5 mm to 25 mm. The product will be imported inrolls with lengths determined by customer requirements.
