The submitted sample is a plastic, illuminated photo frame,with metal border. The metal border is constructed to fit overthe plastic frame, and may be attached or removed at theconsumer's option. The illumination is accomplished by means ofa 7.5 watt bulb. The housing for the bulb and power cord islocated on the back top area of the frame, over a clear plasticlens, which separates the picture from the housing.
