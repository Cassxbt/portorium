The product under consideration is described as the “PCB CLAW” which is a specialized tool designed to  securely hold printed circuit boards (“PCBs”) during electronic component assembly and soldering  operations. It is made primarily of polyethylene terephthalate glycol-modified (“PETG”) thermoplastic. This  tool features a clamping mechanism with adjustable arms that have grooved grip pads designed to hold  electronic components firmly in place. It will measure six inches by four inches by four inches. The PCB CLAW is produced through fused deposition modeling (“FDM”) 3D printing technology. The  PETG filament is heated and deposited layer-by-layer to create the finished tool. It will be manufactured  entirely in Canada. The PETG filament is sourced from Canada, the PETG raw materials are sourced from  the United States, and the included six metal fasteners (nuts and bolts) are produced outside of North  America.
