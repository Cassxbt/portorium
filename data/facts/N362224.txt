Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as the Ellepot 2.0 Pot Filler (also referred to as the Paper Pot 2.0),  which is an industrial-scale tray-filling line designed for inside or outside greenhouse environments. The  system consists of a main frame (housing a control unit), an integrated 3,000 mm tray conveyor line, a paper  roll frame, a paper pulling/grabbing assembly, a pneumatic knife/sawblade cutting unit, and a soil-substrate  measuring unit incorporating a telescopic tube system and mechanical stirring arm. In operation, empty cell  trays up to 600x400 mm are loaded onto the conveyor belt. Plant substrate (soil mix) is fed from a companion  bulk hopper/soil mixer into an entry terminal of the machine (processing up to 4 cubic meters per hour). Simultaneously, the paper feeding unit advances, cuts, and folds individual paper liners down into the tray  cells utilizing an integrated funnel toolset. The telescopic tubes mechanically meter a precise volume of soil  substrate into the paper-lined cells. The filled “paper pots” are then systematically fed out via the tray  conveyor.
