The item at issue is referred to as a coolant check valve, part number 1923876-00-D. The valve consists of an  upper and lower core that incorporate a spring, a piston, and an O-ring. It operates by responding to pressure  differentials in the cooling circuit, causing the piston to move up and down to maintain a one-way flow of the  coolant and to prevent a reverse flow of this fluid. The check valve, which is primarily constructed of a  polypropylene composite reinforced with glass fibers, is used in hybrid or electric motor vehicles.
