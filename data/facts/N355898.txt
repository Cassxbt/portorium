The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Istanbul stone. A sample was submitted with your  ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Istanbul is an ivory, cream, and gray stone with amber streaks. From the information you provided, it will  measure approximately 2 meters long by 3 meters high and either 2 or 3 centimeters in thickness. After  importation, it will be cut to size and shape for both indoor and outdoor use in such applications as  countertops, wall cladding, surfaces, and flooring. Laboratory analysis has determined that Istanbul is comprised of granite. The top and sides are polished and  the edges are beveled.
