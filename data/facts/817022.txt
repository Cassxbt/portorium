The item concerned is a two-wheeled, collapsible, aluminum and stainless steel cart with an attached rectangular bag. The cart has an L-shaped grip handle and built-in bottle opener and towel hook. The bag has separate lengthwise tubes in its interior, and attached to its exterior are a peg-and penholder, a wrist bag, an umbrella holder and four front pockets. The stated purpose of the cart and bag are to, respectively, transport and carry golf clubs and golfing accessories.
