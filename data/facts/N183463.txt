A sample was provided. The merchandise at issue is referred to as the Strassburg sock, an article that is sold individually and not in pairs. The article consists of a tubular knit material that fits over a user’s calf, much in the manner of a sock. Adjustable textile straps at each end of the sock are fastened to one another (securing by means of hook and loop closures) to maintain the foot in a neutral to slightly stretched position (a position known as dorsiflexion). The article is designed to be worn at night or when at rest by sufferers of plantar fasciitis heel pain and Achilles tendonitis. By keeping the foot in a position of dorsiflexion, the appliance prevents the contraction or shortening of the plantar fascia. The Strassburg sock serves a function similar to that of a night splint and operates along the same principle as the Plantar Brace described in New York Ruling Letter N031122, dated June 25, 2008.
