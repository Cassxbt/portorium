The above-the-ankle footwear has closed-toes and closed-heels, rubber or plastics traction outer soles, and  foxing-like bands. Each is secured to the foot with a functional slide fastener originating on the lateral side by  the smallest toe, going around the toes and up to the topline on the medial side of the ankle. There is also a  functional elastic lace threaded through seven pairs of metal eyelets at the center, with a plastic closure. The  footwear has a pull-on loop and the word “DURAN” embroidered at the back of the heel. The constituent  material of one of the versions of this footwear has a rubber or plastics upper. The constituent material of the  other version has a textile canvas upper.
