Zolmitriptan Orally Disintegrating Tablets and Zolmitriptan Tablets are medicinal preparations containing Zolmitriptan, a serotonin receptor agonist, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the acute treatment of migraine attacks with or without aura and cluster headaches. Both products will be imported in dosage form in 2.5 mg and 5 mg tablets. Indomethacin Extended Release Capsules, imported in 75 mg dosage form is a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). It is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe rheumatoid arthritis including acute flares of chronic disease.
