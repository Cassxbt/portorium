The merchandise under consideration is identified as the ZTR AxleGen, which is further described as a  locomotive axle generator. The subject device is comprised of a magnetic or optical encoder attached to a  shaft withing a metal housing. The generator is attached to the wheelset of a locomotive where it creates a  magnetic or optical field. As the axle rotates, the field is broken and an electrical impulse is sent to the  interface module in the cab of the vehicle. The electrical impulse is translated by the module to interpret the  rotational speed of the axles and wheels.
