Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The items under consideration are replacement water filters, listed under model numbers EFF-6007A, EFF-6013A, EFF-6027A, EFF-6032A, and EFF-6046A. Specifically, water filter EFF-6007A is a replacement for the Whirlpool EDR4RXD1 water filter, water filter EFF-6013A is a replacement for the GE MWF water filter, water filter EFF-6027A is a replacement for the Samsung DA29-00020B water filter, water filter EFF-6032A is the replacement for the LG LT700P water filter, and water filter EFF-6046A is the replacement for LG LT1000P water filter. The lead-free replacement refrigerator filters are designed to reduce chlorine, contaminates, and odor for better tasting water. The filters are pressed into a designated slot of the water filtration system of a refrigerator, and purified water is then dispensed through the door of the refrigerator. The filters are to be replaced by a consumer approximately every six months and discarded.
