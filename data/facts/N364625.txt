The seat dimensions are 33.3 inches high, 20.1 inches wide, 18.9 inches deep, and a weight capacity of 300 lbs. Item 3, the “Mid-Century Dining Chair with Solid Wood Legs & Built-in Handle (C284)” is a general use upholstered seat with a wooden frame.
