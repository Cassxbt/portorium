The subject merchandise consists of natural Christmas wreaths made from fresh balsam fir branches. The wreaths range in size from approximately 12 to 48 inches in diameter. The balsam fir branches used to produce the wreaths are harvested from local farms in Nova Scotia, Canada. The wreaths are assembled entirely by hand and will be imported in an undecorated condition, with no ornaments or other decorative articles added. The finished wreaths are intended for sale to customers in New York and New Jersey for use as Christmas decorations.
