The lockers shelving components will provide storage and organization of huntinggear, sports equipment, and athletic gear. Each storage locker kit will incorporate metal rails, brackets,adjustable wire mesh shelving components, polyester textile fabric zipper enclosures, and a battery-operatedozone generator unit. The ozone generator component, referred to as the common generator, consists ofPrinted Circuit Board Assembly (PCBA), ceramic corona plate, and a rechargeable battery within a plasticenclosure. The common generator functions by drawing in air and passing it through the corona plate. Theplate introduces an electrical charge to the air, which creates charged oxygen, or ozone. This ozone is used tosterilize and remove odors. The Scent Crusher Locker dimensions are approximately 45" in width, 69" inheight, and 27" in depth. The Scent Crusher Locker Lite dimensions are approximately 28" in width, 69" inheight, and 20" in depth.
