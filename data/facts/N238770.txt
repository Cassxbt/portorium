The U-D Doser is designed for accurate dose sampling of specific volumes of liquids in the pharmaceutical industry. It works as a mechanical extension to the rod of a disposable syringe which is used to collect samples of liquid medicine contained in medicine tanks located on drug production lines. This device performs only a sampling function. Testing is performed on a separate machine. The U-D Doser features a handle, shaft and outer cover (chamber) of stainless steel. To operate, a syringe is connected to the U-D Doser’s push rod. The connected unit, i.e., U-D Doser and syringe, are placed into the outer cover of the U-D Doser. The outer cover protects the syringe from being contaminated by the sampled material. The small opening at the bottom of the outer cover allows only the syringe’s tip to be exposed during sampling of the material. The handle acts as a push rod as it slides up and down inside of the protection container. The user pulls up the handle in order to take the sample. Graduations on the handle allow accurate samples to be taken. At time of importation, the U-D Doser is packaged for retail sale with a pack of 5ml disposable syringes.
