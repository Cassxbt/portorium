This is in response to your submission of September 22, 2026, requesting a ruling on the classification of Canola Straw Bedding. The subject merchandise consists of ECONO-PAK® Chopped Canola Straw Bedding, a natural agricultural product composed entirely of Canadian-grown canola straw. The straw is collected following agricultural production and subsequently chopped and processed for use as animal bedding. The product contains no additional ingredients and is intended for use in livestock stalls, pens, barns, poultry housing, equine facilities, and other animal housing environments. It is neither manufactured nor marketed for use as animal feed. The finished product will be imported into the United States in compressed commercial bales weighing approximately 22 pounds each and will require no further processing prior to sale or use.
