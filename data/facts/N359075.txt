The item concerned is a microphone windscreen, model # 000001524.A1. This article is made from polyether  foam and measures approximately 25.4 mm × 12.7 mm × 12.7 mm. It is used on all Lightspeed headsets. In  use, it would be installed over the microphone which is situated on the boom assembly, loosely adhered at the  end of the boom. The windscreen serves several critical acoustic, mechanical, and environmental functions,  such as:  Wind/noise reduction. Enhances active noise cancellation microphone performance. Protection for the microphone element. Provides mechanical damping. Maintains clear speech intelligibility.
