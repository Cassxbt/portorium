An ingredients breakdown,  description of the manufacturing process, manufacturing flowchart, and a pictures of the product  accompanied your letter. The product, “Dehydrated Black Garlic,” is described as fresh, whole garlic bulbs, containing no additional  additives, sugar, water or preservatives, that undergo a multi-step manufacturing process in which the raw  material undergoes an enzymatic hydrolysis and Maillard browning reaction. The black garlic bulbs are  intended for human consumption for use as a snack and will be imported in a refrigerated condition in  food-grade nylon vacuum pouches, sealed airtight within an industrial vacuum packaging machine to block  oxidation and extend shelf life up to 12 months. The black garlic bulbs are said to be manufactured by first harvesting fresh, raw garlic bulbs and transporting  them to the food processing plant. The garlic bulbs are evenly spread onto hot air circulation drying chambers  in a recirculating air drying room at temperatures of approximately 25 degrees Celsius for 12 hours. This step  reduces surface moisture to a target 12–15% epidermal water content. The next step involves taking what is  described as the fully-dried raw garlic bulbs for a re-inspection process to remove any bulbs that developed  surface mold during drying. Approved bulbs are restacked onto food-grade stainless steel wire mesh  fermentation trays, which are transported into an insulated stainless steel industrial fermentation chamber  room to undergo a fermentation process for a duration of 30-60 days. The shorter timespan provides a firm,  lightly sweet black garlic bulb while the latter duration provides an ultra-soft, molasses-like bulb. The air  temperature within the wall-mounted electric resistance heating elements is approximately 60 degrees Celsius  with the chamber containing a relative humidity of approximately 90 percent. During this process, the  fully-dried raw garlic bulbs undergoe enzymatic hydrolysis and a Maillard browning reaction inside the  temperature/humidity-controlled fermentation room which is said to alter its appearance, flavor, moisture  contents and other characteristics such as carbohydrates, protein and fiber. Allicin (sharp garlic pungency) 

breaks down into sweet, antioxidant S-allyl cysteine while sugars and amino acids react to form dark black  melanoidins, eliminating raw garlic’s harsh bite. Once the 30–60 day fermentation cycle completeas, fully  blackened garlic bulbs are still described as being extremely soft and sticky with high surface free moisture,  which would cause rapid spoilage in sealed packaging. Trays are transferred back to the same recirculating  drying chamber that occurred prior to the fermentation step for a secondary mild drying cycle at temperatures  of approximately 45 degrees Celsius to stabilize surface moisture without reversing the fermented flavor  profile. Qualified fermented black garlic bulbs are loaded into food-grade nylon vacuum pouches, sealed  airtight on an industrial vacuum packaging machine to block oxidation and extend shelf life up to 12 months. Packaged finished goods are palletized and transferred to a refrigerated finished goods warehouse.
