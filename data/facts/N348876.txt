The five products at issue are installed in the Tablo Hemodialysis System Console, which provides  hemodialysis treatment for individuals with End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD). The console, which features  four wheels, is comprised of a housing equipped with various components that together remove toxins, waste  and fluid from the body. The Ultrafilter Pump, part number PN-0005384-F, is a component of the console’s water purification  subsystem, which produces dialysis-grade water from tap water. The gear pump transports water from the  reverse osmosis filtration system to a downstream ultrafilter. The pump features tubing connections that  interface with the console, and the pump’s electrical connectors and signal pin configurations are specifically  compatible with the console’s electronics and software. The Dialysate Piston Pump, part number PN-0007291-F, regulates the flow of dialysate fluid to the dialyzer  filter, which is used to extract toxins from the blood. The stroke volume of the pump is calibrated to achieve  the range of flow rates necessary for dialysis treatments. The motorized pump features tubing connections  that interface with the console, and the pump’s electrical connectors and signal pin configurations are  specifically compatible with the console’s electronics and software. The Bicarbonate Piston Pump, part number PN-0007294-F, meters the flow of bicarbonate solution used in  the production of dialysate, which is used to remove toxins from the user’s bloodstream. The stroke volume  of the pump is calibrated to deliver the flow rates required for dialysis. The motorized pump features tubing  connections that interface with the console, and the pump’s electrical connectors and signal pin  configurations are specifically compatible with the console’s electronics and software. The level detector sensor, part number PN-003091-F, is an electronic sensor designed to detect the presence  of air or water within the air separation chamber of the console. The console is used to remove dissolved air  from the purified water prior to the production of dialysate solution. The sensor is designed to interface  mechanically with the air removal chamber and detect when air has accumulated in the chamber and needs to  be pumped out of the chamber. The sensor’s electrical connectors and signal pin configurations are  specifically designed for compatibility with the console’s electronics and software. The sensor is sourced  from China. The Carbon Flush Valve, part number PN-0004691-F, is a three-way solenoid valve. Once actuated, the valve  removes excess carbon from the carbon filters located in the console’s water purification subsystem. The  copper valve is designed to be installed onto a custom designed manifold, and the electrical connectors and  signal pin configurations are specifically compatible with the console’s electronics and software. The valve is  sourced from China.
