A representative sample was included with your submission and will be retained by this office. The product you plan to import is described as a flanged commercial conveyor bearing assembly, model number 1276, used in the manufacture of conveyor rollers. The bearing assembly consists of a machined inner race, 11 carbon steel balls, 2 stamped steel rings which form the outer race and a flanged stamped housing which holds the bearing in place. After importation, one bearing assembly is swaged into each end of a steel tube, and an axle is inserted through the bores of each assembly which creates a roller. The rollers are then installed side by side in the conveyor frame.
