The delay in our response is due to a detailed analysis of your product by our laboratory division. You submitted a sample of an appliqué used to decorate walls. You state that the appliqué is made by die-cutting a design into a flat sheet of compressed felt-like material. Our lab has determined that this material is a nonwoven textile fabric composed of polyester fibers with an acrylic binder. It has a backing of release paper. The appliqué is attached to a wall with the same adhesive that held the release paper in place. It is painted and the material around the design is removed, leaving a painted design in relief.
