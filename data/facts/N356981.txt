The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Cygnus Brown stone. A sample was submitted with  your ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Cygnus Brown is a taupe-colored stone with streaks of white, dark gray, and black. From the information  you provided, it will measure between 115 to 122 inches in length, between 65 to 75 inches in width, and  either 0.75 inches or 1.5 inches in thickness. After importation, it will be cut to size and shape for such  applications as kitchen countertops, bathroom surfaces, and surfaces for office furniture. Laboratory analysis has determined that Cygnus Brown is a non-calcareous metamorphic rock. The top of the  stone has been polished and the sides are simply cut or sawn.
