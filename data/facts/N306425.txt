The product under consideration is described as Arqlite plastic gravel, which is an artificial plastic rock for use as an alternative to expanded clay, volcanic rock, and expanded polystyrene (EPS) beans in construction and landscaping. It is intended to replace, in part or in total, the traditional mineral aggregates in ready mix concrete, drainage layers, and vents. This gravel is made wholly from recycled plastic waste including polyethylene, polyester, and polypropylene.
