The item under consideration is the ANGHVG printed circuit board (PCB), which is a device specifically designed for use with the cobas e 801 analyzer. The PCB contains a potentiostat, which applies the RE-WE voltage while maintaining the cell RE-WE voltage at a constant value. A relay protects the voltage from being applied when the software is not running. The PCB also reports the values of the cell CE-WE voltage and the measuring cell current to the EECL402/403 circuit board. The measuring cells are in the detection unit and are where the actual chemical reaction of patient blood and on-board reagents takes place. The PCB will be imported as a replacement part and will be replaced by a Roche field service engineer in the event the original PCB fails. The PCB cannot be used with any other devices including other Roche analyzers. The cobas e 801 analyzer unit is a high throughput immunochemistry module that performs a broad range of heterogeneous immunoassay tests using ElectroChemiLuminescence technology. The analyzer can perform up to three hundred tests per hour using samples of serum, plasma and urine. The analyzer tests for clot and liquid level detection of samples and foam detection for reagents.
