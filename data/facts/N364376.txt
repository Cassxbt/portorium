The merchandise under consideration is identified as the 10” LCD Sketch Pro Neo Pad bundle. The bundle consists of a 10” electronic writing surface and a stylus. The writing surface is comprised of a cholesteric LCD sandwiched between plastic layers and surrounded by a plastic shell. At the bottom of the device is an erase button. When the user presses the surface with the stylus, the liquid crystals within the device change, making pressed lines visible, allowing the user to write or draw directly on the surface. The erase button, when depressed, activates a small internal circuit which sends an electrical pulse, forcing the liquid crystals back into their original configuration, thus erasing any writing.
