The submitted samples are a curtain, pillowsham, bed canopy, duvet cover,  pintray and lampshade. All of the items are made from 100 percent cotton woven fabric. The curtain is sewn along three sides. The top end of the curtain has 7 tabs. One side of the curtain has an applique of a giraffe and various squares. The pillowsham measures approximately 23 inches square. All of the edges are sewn. The back has an overlapping flap style opening to accommodate the insertion of a pillow. The face of the sham is decorated with embroidery and applique work. The bed canopy is intended to be suspended from a ceiling and drape over a crib. Sewn to the top portion of the canopy is a metal ring that measures approximately 13 inches in diameter. The canopy also features a small plastic ring that is sewn to textile straps. This ring is used to attach the canopy to a ceiling support. The top portion of the canopy features embroidery. The duvet cover is comprised of two panels. Three of the edges are sewn and the fourth has a partial opening with a flap closure. The front panel features embroidery and applique work. The pintray measures approximately 21 x 23 inches. One end has colored tie strings. The pintray has several pockets of various sizes. Some pockets contain an embroidered design. The collapsible lampshade features a metal frame that is not of brass material. The sides of the shade are covered with textile fabric. Sewn onto each corner of the shade is a textile strap onto which a small metal ring is attached. The shade measures 10 inches in width and 11.5 inches in height.
