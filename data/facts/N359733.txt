The product in question is the Simplicity Essence Paper Towel Dispenser, model number T7400TBK. This  wall-mounted, hands-free dispenser is constructed largely of plastic and measures approximately 15 (l) x 30  (w) x 10 (h) inches. The dispenser does not have a motor or sensors, and does not use electricity or batteries. It accepts an 8 inch wide paper roll. To operate the device, the user pulls on the hanging portion of the paper towel, which activates an internal   mechanism that automatically advances and cuts a 10 inch sheet of paper. The dispenser has a hinged front  for loading and a lock to prevent tampering. It also features a compartment for a stub roll (a nearly empty  roll); the dispenser automatically switches to the new roll once the stub roll is finished. Finally, dispenser has  an external side wheel to advance the first piece of paper through the dispenser opening.
