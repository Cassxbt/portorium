The FuseLOX Cervical Cage is an intervertebral body fusion device (IBFD) intended to facilitate anterior  cervical interbody fusion at levels C3–C7. The device is manufactured from PEEK OPTIMA-LT1,  conforming to ASTM F2026, and includes titanium or tantalum radiopaque markers. It is available in  Parallel (0°) and Lordotic (7°) configurations, with a width of 14 mm and heights ranging from 5–12 mm in 1  mm increments. The implant footprint measures 14 mm anteriorly, 12 mm posteriorly, and has a depth of 12  mm. The device is indicated exclusively for skeletally mature patients with degenerative disc disease  confirmed by radiographic studies who have failed at least six weeks of conservative treatment. The device  is single-use and must be used with supplemental fixation and autograft or allograft bone. Autogenous bone  graft material is packed into the hollow core of the device prior to implantation. The SmartLOX Cervical Plate System is an anterior cervical screw fixation system designed to provide  stabilization as an adjunct to cervical fusion. The system includes titanium alloy plates, screws, and a  screw-retaining rail lock. Plates are available in lengths ranging from 20–110 mm. Screws are offered in  two diameters (primary and rescue), in both fixed and variable versions, with lengths from 12–18 mm in 2  mm increments. The device is indicated for anterior screw fixation of the cervical spine in patients with  degenerative disc disease, spondylolisthesis, trauma, spinal stenosis, deformity, tumor, pseudoarthrosis, or  failed previous fusion. The device is single-use only and is used exclusively as supplemental fixation in  cervical fusion procedures for patients with chronic spinal impairment. You claim that the FuseLOX Cervical Cage and SmartLOX Cervical Plate System are indicated for skeletally  mature patients who have undergone at least six weeks of failed conservative treatment and suffer from 

chronic cervical spinal impairment confirmed by patient history and radiographic studies. Both devices are  permanently implanted and specifically designed for use in spinal fusion procedures to treat chronic  degenerative conditions of the cervical spine. They are not general-use surgical instruments or devices.
