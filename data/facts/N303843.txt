Imatinib Mesylate, imported in 100 mg and 400 mg tablets, is a kinase inhibitor with antineoplastic activity. It is indicated for the treatment of newly diagnosed adult and pediatric patients with Philadelphia chromosome positive chronic myeloid leukemia (Ph + CML) in chronic phase. Isosorbide Dinitrate, imported in 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg and 40 mg tablets, is a vasodilator. It is indicated for the prevention of angina pectoris due to coronary artery disease.
