MY01 Continuous Compartmental Pressure Monitor is a single use disposable medical device comprised of two major elements, the Introducer and the Pressure Monitor. The introducer is composed of a needle connected to an open box-like plastic handle, which holds the pressure monitor. The pressure monitor contains an electronic assembly, which consists of the batteries, rigid printed circuit board (PCB), and flexible printed circuit board (FPCB), housed in a plastic casing. The FPCB is extended from the monitor and the end of the FPCB is fitted with what is described as a small stiffener, which will be attached to the needle of the introducer, to aid insertion into the patient. The pressure monitor also has an LCD screen and a button on the front. The Continuous Compartmental Pressure Monitor arrives fully assembled and individually packaged inside a sterilized thermoform tray. Once inserted into the patient, the pressure monitor is able to provide continuous monitoring of the subject’s intramuscular pressure for up to 24 hours, displaying information via the LCD screen. The introducer will be discarded after the insertion. The device will be used by a physician as an aid in the diagnosis of acute compartment syndrome.
