In lieu of samples, technical and illustrative literature, a product description, and a manufacturing synopsis were provided for review. The “LH Cover Wire (88337-DL340)” is a cold-bent steel metal wire rail that is permanently affixed to the metal frames of automobile seats according to Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) specifications. Upon importation into the United States, the article will be welded onto the lower frame of an automobile seat to securely position a plastic shield cover (sold separately). When used in conjunction with the shield cover, the article preserves the designed curvature, contour, and taught finish of upholstery materials and prevents exposure of underlying wiring harnesses, motors, and other seating components. The metal wire rail dimensions are approximately 130.5 mm long by 50.5 mm wide.
