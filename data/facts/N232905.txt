The subject product, Turdorza™ Pressair™, is a dry powder formulation containing aclidinium bromide, an anticholinergic, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the long term maintenance treatment of bronchospasm associated with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), chronic bronchitis and emphysema. The product will be imported in 400 mcg packed in a sealed labeled aluminum pouch.
