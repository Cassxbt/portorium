The product under review is said to be an additive used in the production of chocolate milk that has the  product name of Palsgaard® ChoMilk 150. Palsgaard® ChoMilk 150 is said to consist of mono- and  diglycerides of fatty acids (E471), a dextrose standardized carrageenan (E407), and guar guam (E412). You  state when it is used in the production of chocolate milk, Palsgaard® ChoMilk 150 prevents sedimentation of  the cocoa particles, prevents sedimentation of fats, improves the texture of the chocolate milk giving it a  richier and creamier taste, and reduces the formation of excessive foam during processing and filling.
