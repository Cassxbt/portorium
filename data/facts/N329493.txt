Six samples were submitted to the U.S Customs and Border Protection laboratory (“CBP”) Laboratory for analysis. The samples will be retained for reference purposes. Model numbers 2, 8 and 9 identified as “bike helmets,” are sports protective helmets constructed of a polyvinyl chloride (PVC) outer shell and expanded polystyrene (EPS) inner liner. The helmets feature semi-adjustable strap dividers, several exhaust ports for air circulation and a strap anchor on the back. The models will be imported in sizes S – XL. Model number 15, identified as “toddler helmets,” is a sports protective helmet constructed of a PVC outer shell and EPS inner liner. The helmet features semi-adjustable strap dividers, several exhaust ports for air circulation and a strap anchor on the back. Model number 36 and 38 identified as “multi-sport/skate helmet,” are sports protective helmets constructed of an outer shell of acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (ABS) and an EPS inner liner. The helmets feature semi-adjustable strap dividers, several exhaust ports for air circulation and a strap anchor on the back. The models will be imported in sizes S – XL. The CBP Laboratory determined that helmet models 8, 36 and 38 were not reinforced or laminated. However, models 2, 9 and 15 were found to be laminated, not reinforced.
