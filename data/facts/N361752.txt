The items under consideration include a compact milling machine, F2 XYZ Mech Assembly, F2 Right Cover  Assembly, F2 Back Cover Assembly, and F2 Manifold Assembly. The compact milling machine, Fastmill.io, is designed for dental professionals to create custom dental  prostheses for individual patients. The machine is built from several sub-assemblies mounted on a base frame  and enclosed by protective plastic panels. It features a central precision assembly for dental milling,  supported by various systems that enhance its performance and reliability. The F2 XYZ Mech Assembly is the central subassembly of the compact milling machine, housing all the  linear motion and automation components necessary for precise dental milling. It utilizes a 4-axis cartesian  mechanism to move both the spindle and material holder in coordination, enabling the production of single  unit dental restorations. The assembly includes six high-precision linear rails (two per axis: X, Y, and Z),  three high-precision ball screws (one per axis), three servo motors, a stepper motor, and various structural  components and mounting plates. All necessary hardware and structural parts are included. The F2 Right Cover Assembly is a simple subassembly that attaches to the right side of the milling machine. It houses the door control button, which operates the opening and closing of the cutting area door where  dental material is processed. The cover provides safety by guarding access to moving components during  operation and also contributes to the overall aesthetics of the finished machine. It includes the necessary  printed circuit board (PCB) and cabling for the door control system. The F2 Back Cover Assembly is a simple subassembly attached to the back of the milling machine. It  features two foam cushions that close the gaps between the back cover and the inner structure, helping to  guide internal airflow produced by cooling fans and aiding in the cooling of electrical components. The F2 Manifold Assembly is a basic subassembly that separates and directs the flow of air and coolant  within the milling machine. It channels air and coolant from the lower section, where the pumps are located,  to the upper section, where they are delivered to the spindle. The assembly consists of the manifold, several  fittings, and a proximity sensor that detects whether the coolant trough is properly inserted. As you state the F2 XYZ Mech Assembly, F2 Right Cover Assembly, F2 Back Cover Assembly, and F2  Manifold Assembly are subassemblies that do not have independent functions outside the milling system. Each is solely used in the operation, control, or mechanical functioning of the dental milling equipment.
