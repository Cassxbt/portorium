SKU# 500-00000-016.A1 is a self-adhesive polyvinyl chloride PVC gel mat. It is a square shaped mat with  dimensions of 9 inches by 9 inches. The product is backed with release paper to prevent contamination of the  mat. The mat will be used at the noise-cancelling calibration workstation in the production line for aviation  headsets. It supports operator performance and also dissipates static electricity to prevent its discharge into  sensitive electronic components.
