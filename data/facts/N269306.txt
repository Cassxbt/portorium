The product under consideration is described in your submission as a Cooling Fan Drive Bracket Assembly, item number 25210-2BF01. You indicate that the item is part of the engine cooling fan assembly and its function is to “transfer engine power to the cooling fan”. It consists of four main components constructed of steel: a Cooling Fan Drive Shaft, Roller Bearings, a Cooling Drive Bracket, and one Flange Bolt. The Cooling Fan Drive Shaft is a one piece spindle with a built in mounting back plate that rides on Roller Bearings. The Bracket Assembly is mounted to the side of the engine and serves as a mount for the engine cooling fan and the engine cooling fan clutch. The cooling fan is bolted to the engine pulley, which in turn is mounted to the Cooling Fan Drive Shaft mounting back plate, as a part of the engine cooling fan pulley.
