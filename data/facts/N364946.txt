The Emitter® Tubetrap 2.0, item number 160215-Set (Tubetrap), is a reusable professional rodent-control device designed to detect and mechanically capture rodents. It consists of a protective housing, a spring-loaded metal trapping mechanism with metal strike bars, a movement/proximity sensor, electronic control circuitry, an electromagnetically controlled release mechanism, an integrated cellular/SIM communication function and a rechargeable lithium-ion LiFePO4 battery pack. The principal use of the Tubetrap is the detection and mechanical capture of rodents, principally mice and rats, in professional pest-control applications. The unit is intended to reduce the need for manual inspection by communicating the operating and trigger status of the integrated trap to a remote server. The electronic and cellular features support the mechanical trapping function by detecting movement or the presence of a rodent; controlling the release of the mechanical trapping mechanism; distinguishing operating or trigger states; and transmitting the trap status to the operator or service provider. Without the mechanical trapping mechanism, however, the Tubetrap could not perform its principal rodent-control function. The cellular function does not independently provide a general communication service and does not replace the integrated mechanical trap. The device can be operated in two modes: in capture mode the sensor detects the rodent’s presence and the electronic control system releases the spring-loaded metal strike mechanism to capture the rodent. In monitoring mode the device detects rodent movement or activity without releasing the strike mechanism and communicates the detected status remotely. The Tubetrap transmits information such as armed status, triggered status, movement status and, where applicable, catch-related status through an integrated SIM/cellular connection using the supported cellular IoT network connection to a remote server.
