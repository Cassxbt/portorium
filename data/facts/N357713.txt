In lieu of samples, technical  and illustrative literature, a product description, and a manufacturing synopsis were provided for review. The “OKA Collection (OKA1)” is an upholstered seat affixed to a wooden frame that will provide seating for  a single individual. The seat foundation, backrest, and the armrests will be constructed of solid American  hardwood and Canadian plywood. The seat foundation cushions and the seat backrest cushions will consist  of compression foam encased in custom order fabric. The seat is not a recliner and will not incorporate a  recline mechanism. Steel springs will be incorporated into the structural frame of the seat and polyurethane  legs will be located at the base corners of the seat. Exposed wooden legs will be located at the base corners  of the seat. The OKA1 dimensions will be 28" in width, 21.5" in depth, 40" in height, and will weigh  approximately 65 lbs.
