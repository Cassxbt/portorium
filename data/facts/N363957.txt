The kneepad has a three-layer construction. The three layers are permanently gluedtogether. The outer layer that comes in contact with the roofing surface is composed of a 5 mm thick moldedlayer of vulcanized styrene-butadiene rubber (SBR). The middle layer is composed of a 12 mm moldedethylene vinyl acetate foam. The inner layer is composed of 18 mm polyurethane foam that is covered with apolyester fabric liner.
