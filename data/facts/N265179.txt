The subject product, Zoledronic Acid Injections, is a medicinal preparation containing Zoledronic acid, as the active ingredient. It is in a class of medications called bisphosphonates, which are used to improve bone strength in many diseases associated with bone resorption, such as cancer. Zoledronic acid is indicated for the treatment of Paget's disease in men and women, hypercalcemia of malignancy (HCM; a condition resulting in high calcium blood levels due to cancer), patients with multiple myeloma and documented bone metastases from solid tumors, in conjunction with antineoplastic therapy.
