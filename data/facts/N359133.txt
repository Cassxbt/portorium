The products under consideration are three paperboard bulletin board borders. All three are color printed and  coated with calcium carbonate. The first design is a row of die-cut apples in various colors. The shape is the  outline of the apples with stems and leaves. The apples are printed for coloration, but the printing is  secondary to the shape of the border. The second design is a border with a straight long edge and a wavy  long edge. The paper is printed with a decorative repeating pattern of apples, pencils, paint brushes, rulers,  glue bottles, and stars. The third border is a rectangular piece of paperboard with a repeating, decorative,  colored checkerboard pattern.
