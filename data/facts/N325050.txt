Isotretinoin, imported in 10 mg, 20 mg, 25 mg, 30 mg, 35 mg and 40 mg capsules, is a synthetic Vitamin A acid derivative known as 13-cis-retinoic acid. Isotretinoin capsules are indicated for the treatment of severe recalcitrant nodular acne in non-pregnant patients 12 years of age and older with multiple inflammatory nodules with a diameter of 5 mm or greater. Ivermectin, imported in 3 mg tablets, is a semisynthetic, anthelmintic agent for oral ministration. Ivermectin is derived from the avermectins, a class of highly active broad-spectrum, anti-parasitic agents. It is indicated for the treatment of Strongyloidiasis of the intestinal tract and Onchocerciasis, two conditions caused by parasitic worms. Venlafaxine, imported in 37.5 mg, 75 mg, 150 mg and 225 mg extended release tablets, is an antidepressant. It is indicated for the management of major depressive disorder (MDD), generalized anxiety disorder (GAD), social anxiety disorder (social phobia) and panic disorder.
