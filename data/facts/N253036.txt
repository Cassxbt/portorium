Samples of the top and lower sub-assembly of the auto-injector were provided with this ruling request and are being returned upon your request. The merchandise under consideration are sub-assemblies of a disposable auto-injector identified as the BD Physioject™ which is used for the self-injection of drugs to treat chronic diseases. The top sub-assembly has three plastic components and a spring and the lower sub-assembly has five plastic components and a spring. After importation, the upper and lower sub-assemblies are assembled with a sub-assembly containing the medicine and a needle to complete the syringe.
