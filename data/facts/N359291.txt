The merchandise under consideration is identified as Assembly, Battery Charger, Part number  801-00002-000.A1, which is a standalone battery charger for the lithium-ion battery exclusively used in  Lightspeed Delta Zulu aviation headsets. The battery charger is comprised of a main Printed Circuit Board  Assembly (PCBA) responsible for battery charging control, a sub-PCBA that manages indicator status and  facilitates battery interface, and a USB-C port for charging cables. All internal components are enclosed in a  unique plastic housing designed to fit the lithium-ion battery. It also features indicator lights showing charge  status, battery level, and fully charged status, etc. We note that the lithium-ion battery is not imported with  the subject battery charger.
