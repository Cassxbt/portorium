The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Solid Plenum Cat6 cable with is described as a  gigabit ethernet data cable which is commonly used for structured cabling projects such as vertical,  horizontal, or backbone systems. The cable is internally constructed of 23 AWG copper wire insulated with  FEP. Two color matched conductors are twisted together to form a pair, and four pairs are assembled to form  a core. The core is jacked with a low smoke flame retardant PVC. The cables are imported on reels to be cut  to the desired length by the end user and are not terminated with any connectors. The rated voltage of the  cables is stated to be 48 volts. In use, the cables transmit data and other information within various  installation scenarios.
