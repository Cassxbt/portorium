The “Safer Transfer Board,” part number 1800-2563-CSTB, is a device used by individuals to transition from a wheelchair-to-bed, wheelchair-to-sofa, wheelchair-to-chair, or wheelchair-to-shower bench transfers. The device provides a user with leverage, balance, and stabilization when entering and exiting a bed or chair. The transfer board is constructed of a flat 13" wide high density polyethylene (HDPE) surface, a pair of 1" in diameter hollow stainless steel tube frames that supports up to 500 lbs., and a U-shaped platform baseplate. The device may be used as a standalone support with the U-shaped platform baseplate or may be mounted onto a bedframe rail. The device is height adjustable and accommodates a wide range of bed frames and mattresses; once positioned, the transfer device is held in place alongside a bed frame rail with the use of horizontal and vertical adjustable knobs, locking pins, and an L-shaped bracket that is placed below the mattress. The device dimensions are approximately 17.60 inches long, 16 inches wide, and a cutout handle is located on the HDPE surface for portability.
