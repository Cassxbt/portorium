The merchandise under consideration is a cartridge outer part (1.01 Part 16412 002) which is a filter that sits in a specially drilled hole in the transmission and is the interface between the transmission and the solenoid valve. It is included in the basic components of a 3/2 solenoid valve. The cartridge outer part allows transmission oil to enter the component through its openings. Its function is to filter transmission oil before it enters the solenoid valve. The filtering media consists of type 1.4301 steel (304 stainless steel).
