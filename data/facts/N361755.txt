The items under consideration include the F2 Base Support Assembly and F2 Base Divider Assembly, which  are subassemblies of a compact dental milling machine, Fastmill.io (addressed under a separate cover in NY  N361752). Separate rulings will address F2 Air Pump Assembly, F2 Coolant Pump Assembly and F2  Electrical Enclosure Assembly. As you noted in your submission, all of these parts do not have independent functions outside of the milling  system and are used exclusively for the operation, control or mechanical functioning of the dental milling  machine. The F2 Base Support Assembly is a subassembly of the dental milling machine that forms the base structure  of the mill. It is specifically designed to hold the drawer containing the coolant trough and the coolant flow  meter. A plastic divider behind the coolant trough drawer separates the wet area from the dry area in the  finished machine. The assembly also includes drawer slides with a grab latch for opening, closing, and  locking the drawer. It consists of fasteners, machined parts, sheet metal, and plastic components. The F2 Base Divider Assembly is a subassembly of the dental milling machine that bolts into the base of the  machine and divides the lower half into separate compartments for pumps and electrical components, such as  the power supply. It includes an air filter and an air pressure sensor, as well as provisions for tubing, all of  which support the sealing air system required for the spindle.
