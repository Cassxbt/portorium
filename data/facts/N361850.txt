This submission provides additional information concerning  the application of the product. The Model W68 coolant manifold assembly consists of three rectangular shell components and an additional  fourth component. These components incorporate specially designed pathways for coolant circulation and  storage and provide mounting points for items such as a coolant pump and a valve. Once these components  are incorporated into the layered assembly and it is installed onto an engine of a motor vehicle, the assembly  regulates and maintains the optimal temperature range for elements of a drivetrain system, including the  battery, power electronics, and motor.
