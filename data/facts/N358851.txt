The product under consideration is described as a walker wheel cover. Your submission indicates it is  designed to fit on standard folding aluminum walker wheels. The cover is constructed out of injection  molded polypropylene plastic and is comprised of five connecting legs and a wheel nut cap. Each connecting  leg comes with several teeth that engage with the spoke of the wheel and lock the product onto the walker  wheel. This cover is intended to prevent damage to walls, wall corners, cabinets, and furniture by covering  the exposed walker wheel nut’s sharp edges, while also protecting the wheel nut from damage.
