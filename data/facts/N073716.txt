The merchandise under consideration is described as the All-in-One Reader with 3 Port Hub, item number 97962. You state that it consists of a plastic housing measuring approximately 3 inches wide by 3 inches high by ½ inch deep. The All-in-One Reader features 4 card slots that can accommodate 19 different card types, including Compact Flash, MicroDrive, Secure Digital, MultiMedia, SmartMedia, xD-Picture, and Memory Stick cards. The All-in-One Reader does not contain its own memory, but simply serves as a drive unit to read and write data to and from flash memory media. An integrated USB (universal serial bus) mini connector allows the reader to be connected to the USB port of an automatic data processing (ADP) machine via an included USB cable. The All-in-One Reader features a built-in 3-port USB hub, which adds 3 USB 2.0 ports to connect peripherals to the computer through the reader/writer.
