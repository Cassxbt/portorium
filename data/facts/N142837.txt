Product specifications and images of four items (“Spice Rack Sets”) accompanied your letter. The products consist of bottles or jars filled with dried vegetables, spices, plant parts or salt, mounted on a steel wire rack, packed for retail sale. The racks, except noted below, are enclosed by removable, clear plastic film. Item number A019RA00206 is an assortment of tube-shape bottles containing white peppercorn, black peppercorn, red chili flakes, garlic flakes, rosemary and basil. Item number A019RA00207 is composed of a jar of black peppercorn, coriander seeds, rosemary, oregano flakes, parsley flakes and basil. The rack has a plastic knob on top and a hang tag tied around the knob. Item number A019RA00209 consists of a jar of coriander seeds, parsley flakes, coarse salt, oregano flakes, black peppercorn, rosemary, basil and garlic flakes. The eight jars are put up in a cylindrical steel rack with an “n”-shaped handle, onto which a ribbon bow and hangtag is tied. Item number A019RA00211 contains a jar of thyme, basil, oregano flakes, ground black pepper, garlic powder, red chili flakes, parsley flakes and rosemary. The eight jars are set slantingly in a steel rack with a wood base. The jars are covered in plastic and the rack is wrapped with a ribbon tied in a bow on the top with an ingredients tag attached.
