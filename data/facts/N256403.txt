The merchandise under consideration is a stainless steel “Meg” spray nozzle (Sku 85-200) that weighs 0.2 lbs. The ¼” threaded nozzle is used for standard pressure washers and attaches to the end of the pressure washer wand by tightening in a normal clockwise motion. These nozzles are suitable for use with a wide range of flow rates and operating conditions. They are also available with a choice of spray patterns, spray angles and connections. This spray nozzle does not incorporate a valve/shut off mechanism that controls/regulates the flow of water.
