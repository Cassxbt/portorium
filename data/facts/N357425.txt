There are two items at issue with this request, which you state are cables specifically designed for use with  the Zulu aviation headset. The first cable is identified as the Cable, Upper, Quick Disconnect, part number  300-00047-000.A3. The subject cable is comprised of a length of insulated conductor terminated at one end  with a 9-POS connector and a cylindrical breakaway connector at the other. The cable is meant to connect the  headset to the control box, which houses the necessary electronics for noise cancellation, audio processing,  and power. The cable transmits power and audio signals from the control box to the headset, allowing the  pilot to hear air traffic control as well as utilize the in-aircraft intercom system, with active noise cancellation. The second cable is identified as the Cable, Harness, Quick Disconnect, SR Removed, part number  300-00048-001.A1. This cable is comprised of a length of insulated conductor, split into two at one end and  terminated with multi-pin connectors. The other end of the cable is terminated with a cylindrical quick  disconnect connector. The subject cable is meant to connect each earcup to the control box. The cable  transmits power and audio signals from the control box to the headset, allowing the pilot to hear air traffic  control as well as utilize the in-aircraft intercom system, with active noise cancellation.
