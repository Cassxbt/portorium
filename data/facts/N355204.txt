The items concerned are the left and right faceplates for Light Speed’s Zulu 3 aviation headsets, part #’s   303-00059-100.A4 Faceplate, Left and 303-00060-100.A5 Faceplate, Right. The faceplates will be affixed to the ear cups of the headset. They are made of ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene  Styrene) which is a strong, durable, and impact-resistant engineering thermoplastic. The faceplates provide the following functionality:  Structural Support for Driver/Speaker, they hold the driver unit (the part that produces sound) securely  in place. They ensure precise alignment within the ear cup, which is essential for consistent sound quality and  effective ANC (Active Noise Canceling). Acoustic Optimization, the design and material of the plate can influence sound resonance, vibration  damping, and frequency response. They help reduce unwanted vibrations and enhances clarity, especially in the mid and high frequencies. Integration with Noise-Canceling Microphones. They help support microphone to optimally detect  ambient noise. They contribute to acoustic sealing, which is vital for passive noise isolation  Durability and Longevity, the speaker plate protects the delicate components inside of the ear cup from  mechanical stress, dust and moisture.
