Descriptive information was provided with the submission. The Pump Subassembly consists of a Divert Water Pump and a Slop Water Pump. The Divert Water Pump  and Slop Water Pumps receive separate streams of processed water from separate tanks in a tank farm  installed at the Nanushuk Processing Facility in Alaska. The two pumps are configured as centrifugal pumps  and once mounted onto a frame and connected to separate piping systems, the divert pump circulates water  from the divert and slop tanks within the 331B module onwards. The pumps are single stage, single suction  pumps that each feature a discharge outlet that measures 2.38 inches in diameter.
