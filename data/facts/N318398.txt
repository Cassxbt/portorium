An image was submitted in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described as “Autumn Decorative Pumpkins,” CVS item number 570398. It is a set of decorative pumpkins and gourds, which each measure about two inches in diameter. There are two assortments, the first is only pumpkins and gourds and the second also features four natural pinecones. The bodies of the pumpkins and gourds are made from a molded foam plastic material that is painted to generally resemble the natural products. The stems are made from molded plastic, inserted into the bodies, and then glued in place. Each assortment will be sold in a net bag with a retail tag attached.
