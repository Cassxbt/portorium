The merchandise under consideration is two styles of abrasive discs referred to as Gold Paper Disc, model number Y399, and Green Film Disc, model number G369, and two styles of backing pads. Samples were submitted with your ruling request and will be returned to you. The Gold Paper Disc and Green Film Disc both measure approximately 5 inches in diameter. The Gold Paper Disc is comprised of a paper backing coated on one side with aluminum oxide abrasive grain, while the reverse features a hook-and-loop surface. The Green Film Disc is comprised of a polyester film backing coated on one side with either aluminum oxide abrasive grain or a mix of aluminum oxide and zirconium corundum abrasive grains. The back of the disc features a hook-and-loop surface. The 5” diameter backing pads are designed to be attached to hand held power tools called orbital sanders. When in use, a pad is attached to the power tool. A sanding disc is then attached to the pad. The pads are available in two models in order to accommodate the various types of sanding discs. Each pad weighs 3.5 oz. The first model features a vinyl face to which a sanding disc that has a pressure sensitive backing (“PSA”) can be attached. The second model is composed of a molded polymer construction with a backing that allows the user to attach a disc with a hook and loop system.
