A sample of the product was provided to this office and will be retained for training purposes. Item #JF10461, described as a “Bluey storage bin,” is a textile storage bin designed and intended to store children’s items in the home. The open top, round-shaped bin is constructed of 100 percent polyester woven, dyed light blue, plush fabric on the exterior surface and rim. The bin is held to shape by a 100 percent ethylene vinyl acetate copolymer (EVA) plastic foam core and lined with a 100 percent polyester woven, dyed blue, fabric. The storage bin measures 12 inches in width by 10 inches in height and features two fabric handles sewn to each side for carrying. The front of the bin features an embroidered face of the character Bluey ™ composed of 85 percent polyester and 15 percent polypropylene (PP) nonwoven material.
