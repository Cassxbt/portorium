A sample identified as Rite Aid Home for the Holidays “120 Peel and Stick Gift Tags”, Item# 9026098, was submitted for our examination. The item consists of four sheets of thirty gift tags that are used during the Christmas and winter season. The die cut, self-adhesive tags measure approximately 2 5/8” x 3/4” or 1 2/4” x 1 ¾”. Each tag is lithographically printed with Christmas pictures and/or holiday greetings accompanied with the words "To" and "From" each followed by a space in which a name is to be entered by the user. The gift tags are "packaged for retail sale" in a clear cellophane wrapper with a paperboard hangtag.
