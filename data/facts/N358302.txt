The product at issue is referred to as the OFP Boyles 15 Console Assembly. The console assembly is a  framed unit that has wheels and a console that features gauges, levers and a manifold block that is referred to  as a valve bank. The manifold, which is engineered for hydraulic fluid management, integrates 14 steel  valves that control the flow and direction of hydraulic fluid to perform drilling functions. The console  assembly is purpose-built for mineral exploration and above-ground mining operations.
