The article under consideration is described as a blow pad (Part Name: BLOW PAD FRT LH NO.1_ICE),  Part Number: WE76CA06001A, which is an original equipment manufacturer (OEM) good used in  passenger vehicles. The blow pad is installed beneath the driver-side floor mat to absorb impact when  stepping on the pedals. It is made of a high-density polyethylene (HDPE) injection molded part, with  polyurethane (PU) foam molded onto it, and a fiber pad, which serves to block and protect against the inflow  of foaming liquid into the inside of the injection-molded part during the foaming process. The blow pad is  made into a specific shape and measures 275 x 333 x 60 mm or 10.83 × 13.11 × 2.36 inches.
