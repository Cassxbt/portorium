Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Injection is a medicinal preparation containing Doxorubicin Hydrochloride, a cytotoxic anthracycline antibiotic, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of various cancers, such as breast cancer, bladder cancer, ovarian cancer and acute lymphocytic leukemia. Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Injection will be imported in dosages of 2 mg/ml vials.
