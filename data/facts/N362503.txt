Style 274224 is a women’s upper body garment constructed from 100 percent polyester knit fabric. It is  constructed from four panels sewn together lengthwise and features a right-over-left, full front opening with  four button closures, which are reinforced with knotted thread and smaller buttons on the garment’s inner  surface; a self-fabric spread collar with curved edges; long, hemmed sleeves; two patch pockets on the front  panels below the waist; a center back seam flanked by two two-inch-long vertical tack stitches that extend  down from the back neckline; and a straight, hemmed bottom. The body and sleeves are lined with 100  percent polyester woven fabric.
