The subject merchandise is Banchandanji Jeotgal (Seasoned Squid). The ready-to-eat product is composed of  salted squid, starch syrup, red pepper powder, frozen minced garlic, paprika extract, sugar, frozen red chili  pepper, fresh garlic, sesame seeds, mipung (umami seasoning blend), matsurae (flavor enhancer), ground  ginger, anchovy fish sauce, glycine, xanthan gum, polymix-m (preservative blend) and compound phosphate. The manufacturing process involves cutting the squid into stripes, salting them and then blending them with  the aforementioned seasoning ingredients. You state that the squid is procured and salted in China, then  exported to Korea. In Korea, it is mixed with a proprietary seasoning mix. Jeotgal Seasoned Squid is  commonly consumed as a side dish or as a condiment.
