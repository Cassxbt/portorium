The product is described as a white to beige, free-flowing powder with a neutral taste and odor, marketed as a  synergistic hydrocolloid system that provides high viscosity, thickening, and stabilizing properties. HydroSystem 102P is composed of 80 percent guar gum and 20 percent xanthan gum. The guar gum is  produced in Mexico, while the xanthan gum is sourced from China. The product will be imported into  50-pound kraft paper bags and is intended for use as a versatile thickener and stabilizer in soups, sauces,  dressings, baked goods, and dairy products.
