The item concerned is a bare printed circuit board (PCB) (#1126898). This PCB is designed, manufactured and used exclusively with the Respironics DreamStation BiPAP AutoSV, BiPAP S/T, and BiPAP AVAPS therapy machines. These therapy machines assist people with chronic breathing issues. They provide bi-level non-invasive ventilatory support to treat patients with obstructive sleep apnea and respiratory insufficiency. This particular PCB is designed to fit precisely into the DreamStation devices. Once populated with various components, the location designated on the PCB for each component ensures the proper alignment with other components of the finished good (i.e. pressure and flow sensors will be placed precisely on the PCB so that when the PCB is installed these sensors mate to the gaskets designed into the flow path of the device, an SD card connector, accessory connector, and humidifier connector are all placed to exactly match the openings in the device for external connections, etc.).
