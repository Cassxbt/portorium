You have submitted  photographs and product descriptions for our review. The article at issue is a fitted carry case specifically designed for the Neuro 20 PRO system. The Neuro 20  PRO system consists of a Smart Suit, control box, tablet, and software. The Smart Suit delivers electrical  neuromuscular stimulation to recreate functional movement patterns for treating neurological injuries or  diseases. By delivering biphasic electrical currents that closely replicate voluntary muscle firing patterns, the  system helps establish muscle memory, coordination, and supports neuroplasticity. Stimulation of both upper  and lower extremities, as well as core muscles, enhances sensory feedback, position awareness, and muscle  activation timing. The case is imported empty and separately from the device. The case is constructed with an outer surface of  man-made textile materials. It features one handle and a three-sided zipper.
