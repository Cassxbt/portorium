The Frigidaire beverage cooler, SKU number 188467, is a household chiller that measures 10 inches in width by 25.2 inches in height. The cooler refrigerates items at a temperature range of 46 to 65 degrees Fahrenheit and has the capacity to store beverage cans and wine bottles in three arrangements. The absorption type cooler features removable shelves, a curved glass door, an LED digital display and a blue interior light.
