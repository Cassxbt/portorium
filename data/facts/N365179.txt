The merchandise under consideration is a heat exchange unit, identified as Twa Aluminum Plate-Fin Heat Exchange Unit 2H, used to heat the air within an indoor space. The unit is made of metal (aluminum, copper, and steel), is rectilinear in shape, and measures approximately 8 feet in length, 10 inches in height, and 7.5 inches in depth. It can be mounted on a wall or pedestal and integrated with other units powered through a building’s central boiler or heat pump system. The components include a natural convection coil (Part # COLE-84-0C1H) and metal panels that enclose the coil to distribute heat. The natural convection coil is the main component that generates the heat for this system. It consists of a copper tube enclosed by a louvered grate made of closely spaced aluminum sheet fins. It is roughly the same size and shape as the overall unit but slightly smaller because it is designed to be enclosed by the panels completing the heat exchange unit assembly. The copper tube within runs continuously along the length of the coil multiple times with its two ends extended out on one side. When heated water circulates through the copper tube, the tube heats up and transfers the heat to the aluminum fins. As this happens air passes through the fins and heats the air which is then distributed to the indoor space.
