In lieu of samples, a productdescription and illustrative literature were provided.The “Bonaterra II Sofa” is a 100% polyester woven fabric upholstered seat that is affixed to a wooden frame. The sofa does not incorporate a removeable mattress, convert into a bed, or include recline mechanisms. Thewooden frame consists of engineered wood materials that include (1) ½” oriented strand board (OSB) (41%),(2) bamboo (31%), (3) ¾” laminated veneer lumber (LVL) (27%), and (4) medium density fiberboard (MDF)(1%). The sofa will provide seating for one or more individuals. The sofa’s foam seat foundation cushionsand the foam seat backrest cushions are removeable. The sofa incorporates a heavy-gauge steel springsuspension within the seat foundation frame. In the condition at the time of importation into the UnitedStates (U.S.), a pair of 20” accent pillows will be included with the seat. The sofa dimensions are 27" indepth, 27" in height, and 89" in length.
