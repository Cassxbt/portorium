The products you plan to import are described as grapple cylinders. These double-acting, weld fused hydraulic cylinders (motors) are used on grapple attachments for excavators and consist of a piston rod, a piston head, a piston head locknut, a tube, a flange cap and a seal kit. Hydraulic fluid enters the cylinders and applies pressure to the piston head creating a linear acting force which opens and closes the grapple claw.
