The merchandise subject to this request is the IRP2, an infrared (IR) emitter assembly. This device consists of an IR light emitting diode (LED) housed within a miniature, mouse shaped, black injection molded plastic shell. It incorporates a 10 foot electrical lead, terminated by a two-pin 3.5 mm connector. It is packaged with an IR mask and 2-sided transparent tape. This device converts electrical signals into infrared light signals; it’s a form of transducer. The IRP2 emits IR control signals sent to it by a “Crestron” control system. It allows for the IR control of a variety of audio/video equipment. In use, the IR emitter shell would be adhered directly over the IR sensor window (receiver) of a television, DVD player, or any other IR controllable device. The connector would be plugged into any “Crestron” control system which incorporates a 2-pin terminal block IR output port. Once connected this emitter acts as the 1-way IR control interface between the “Crestron” control system and the A/V device being controlled.
