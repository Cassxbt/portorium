The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Multi-Axis Controller, Part No. 5300027591, which  you state is specifically designed for use with various fork trucks. The controller is comprised of an injection  molded joystick handle with various electrical switches at the top. Within the joystick base is a control  printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), a Hall Effect position sensor, and an electrical connector. In use, the assembly is installed within the lift truck via wired connection. When the user moves the joystick,  the sensor and control board send electrical signals that control various functions on the lift truck. These  include directional steering, movement of the truck’s lifting mechanism, audio/visual signaling, among  others.
