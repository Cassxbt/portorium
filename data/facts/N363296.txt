The merchandise under consideration is a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), known as the Pulse 2.0  Logic Board. This PCBA is further described as the network communication subsystem of the Pulse 2.0  Industrial Energy Monitor. The Logic Board PCBA’s primary function is to receive digitized energy  measurement data from a co-mounted Energy Board via an internal serial peripheral interface (SPI) bus,  convert that data into MQTT-formatted JSON payloads over a TLS 1.3-secured TCP/IP stack, and transmit  those payloads to nClarity's cloud infrastructure via WiFi or a USB-connected Ethernet module.
