This is in reply to your letter dated March 7, 2024, requesting a classification, country of origin determination, and eligibility under the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) ruling. A sample of the polyester textured filament yarn was provided with your request. The sample, described as “1-15036PIM Black,” are tangled textured 100 percent polyester multifilament single yarns. The yarns will be imported on tubes into Canada from Indonesia and will be used to produce woven and knit mattress ticking fabric.
