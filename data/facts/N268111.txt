Descriptive literature and illustrations were submitted with your letter. The item in question is an Outer Valve Body, part number 46215-3B0123. The Outer Valve Body consists of a precisely machined aluminum casting that is bolted to the underside of an automotive transmission. It is one of the most important components of an automatic transmission. It contains a maze of channels and passages that direct pressurized hydraulic fluid to the numerous valves which engage the appropriate clutch pack or band servo for smooth gear shifting. These valves use pressure in order to determine which gear set, also called band servos, to use. Each valve in the Valve Body has a specific purpose and is named for that function. It is said in your submission that the Valve Body, as imported, is just the housing with no other components.
