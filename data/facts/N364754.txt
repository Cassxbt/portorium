Sample pieces of the netting were submitted with your request and will be retained for training purposes. The netting, identified as “PermaNet Screen® Insecticidal Agricultural Netting,” is designed to be used as a long-lasting insecticide-incorporated netting (LLIN) for agricultural applications, including structural screens, crop protection, plant wraps and livestock pest management. The netting is an open-work, warp knit fabric composed wholly of polyethylene; it is dyed and weighs 55 grams per square meter (g/m2). Additionally, the netting features double-knit side borders and unhemmed, straight blade-cut edges at the top and bottom. The fabric is unknotted and treated with the active insecticide deltamethrin. Based on the information provided, the netting will be imported in rolls and will be available in the following eight sizes and weights: 30 x 3 feet (1 lbs./0.46kgs), 30 x 4 feet (1.34 lbs./0.61 kg), 100 x 3 feet (3.37 lbs./1.53 kg), 100 x 4 feet (4.5 lbs./2.04 kg), 100 x 5 feet (5.64 lbs./2.56 kg), 300 x 5 feet (16.91 lbs./7.67 kg), 100 x 8 feet (9.02 lbs./4.09 kg) and 300 x 8 feet (27.05 lbs./12.27 kg).
