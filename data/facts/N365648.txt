Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is a robotic shot peening machine designed for the surface treatment and mechanical preparation of metal components. The machine has a mild steel acoustic-lined cabinet, a dual-satellite table work door, an ABB IRB 4600 robot equipped with an automatic tool changer and venturi nozzles/rotary head, an automatic abrasive adder, a urethane reclaim pipe and cyclone separator, a vibratory classifier, a 6.5 CFT double-chamber media tank with Thompson and Magna valves, an Allen-Bradley PLC with touchscreen HMI, and a cartridge-style dust collector. In operation, a metal component is loaded onto a synchronized turntable or work cart, after which the system loads a digital recipe defining the precise tool path, nozzle distance, and impact angle. Within the sealed blast chamber, the articulated robotic arm guides the peening nozzle along contoured surfaces while closed-loop sensors continuously monitor and regulate blast pressure, media flow rate, and peening intensity. Simultaneously, an integrated reclamation system collects, cleans, and recycles spent media by screening out dust and fractured particles to ensure consistent surface treatment, ultimately inducing beneficial compressive stresses that enhance the fatigue life, durability, and stress-cracking resistance of aerospace, automotive, energy, and industrial components.
