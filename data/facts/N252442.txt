The ruling was requested on “Dog-eared” wooden fence pickets. Photographs and a description of the product were submitted. The fence pickets are sawn boards made of solid pine. They are shaped on one end with a distinctive dog-eared cut. The pickets will be imported in two sizes: 1” thick x 6’ (L) x 4” (W) and ½” thick x 4” (W) x 6’(L). You state that the pickets are imported as separate pieces and not as an assembled fence panel.
