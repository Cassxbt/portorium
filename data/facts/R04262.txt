At issue are various components of a continuous laminated veneer lumber (“LVL”) press line. A LVL press line converts individual sheets of veneer into solid wood stock. Sheets of veneer are stacked one on top of another and mated together into a continuous billet which is heated, compressed and then cut to specifications. The principal components of the LVL press line are the lay-up station, the pre-press machine, the hot press machine and a central electrical control system.
