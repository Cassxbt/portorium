The merchandise under consideration is referred to as the Medicoat AG vacuum plasma spray (VPS) coating system and is described as a system for spraying powdered feedstocks onto substrates such as orthopedic and dental implant components. The system is designed to spray metal or cermet feedstocks composed of titanium alloys, commercially pure titanium, and hydroxyapatite. Its main components include a vacuum chamber housing both a fixture equipped with planetary drives and a plasmatron (a specialized plasma spray torch) held by a robotic arm that manipulates it around the workpiece. Using an electric arc, the plasmatron produces a high-temperature, high-velocity plasma jet that simultaneously melts and sprays the powdered feedstock onto the substrate to apply a uniform coating. Additional system components include a vacuum system for creating the atmospheric environment, a gas regulation system, a powder feeder system, a cooling system, electrical supply cabinets, control cabinets, and filtering systems. All components will be interconnected by piping and imported in a single shipment.
