The merchandise under consideration is referred to as the “Gamechanger” Streaming Kit 2.0, model GC24STREAMKIT. The Gamechanger consists of a tripod, a phone/camera mount, and a mountable carry case, all imported packaged together for retail sale as a set. The carry case can be mounted to a chain link fence or the provided tripod to protect the camera or phone from the sun and provide shelter from the rain. The aluminum tripod is designed to work with the mounting case, a phone, or a camera mount. Moreover, the 51-inch tripod, which contains three legs, is used to reduce random movements.
