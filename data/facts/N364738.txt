The product under consideration is a referred to as a Steel Cord Conveyor Belt. It is steel cord vulcanized rubber conveyor belting reinforced only with longitudinal steel cords. The belting consists of a vulcanized rubber top cover, steel cord reinforcement layer, skim rubber between the cords and a vulcanized rubber bottom cover. The belting is designed for use in heavy-duty conveyor systems to transport bulk materials such as coal, iron ore, minerals, aggregates, etc. It is supplied in roll form in widths from 500 mm-2800 with thicknesses ranging from 10 mm-50 mm.
