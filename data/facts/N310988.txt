The subject product is a food grade “Instantized Blend of Leucine, Valine, Isoleucine and Sunflower lecithin” (also known as Leucine, Valine and Isoleucine Blend). It is imported in a powder form. It is used by mixing 2-10 grams of the product with water or fruit juice (or other liquids) to make a consumable product. It is used in sports nutrition and dietary supplements. For purposes of the ruling we assume that the product is imported in bulk form.
