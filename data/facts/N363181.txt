The items under consideration are the Barb Couple Silicone (Part TU288-1) and the Tubing Compressor  Outlet (Part TU285-2). These component parts are exclusively designed for the FSC Oxygen Concentrator. They connect the feed/waste valves to move ambient air through the sieve beds, delivering oxygen to the  user, and cannot be used in any other product. The Barb Coupler Silicone (Part TU288-1) is a custom injection-molded connector made from medical grade  silicone rubber. It features raised and angled barbs on its exterior, which are designed to slide easily into  tubing while resisting pull-out. The component is very small, measuring 0.9 inches in length with an outside  diameter of 0.27 inches. The Tubing Compressor Outlet (Part TU285-2) is a molded, stiff, transparent tube made of polycarbonate and  includes a stainless-steel bearing. The item is manufactured by injection molding the plastic piece, followed  by the insertion of the stainless-steel ball into the open channels. It has two mounting points and fits into a  horseshoe-shaped fitting. The main body has a total length of 3.4 inches and a width of 1.7 inches (measured  from screw hole to screw hole). The secondary tubing extends slightly over 1.9 inches from the main body. The FSC Oxygen Concentrator is specifically designed for individuals experiencing breathing difficulties due  to chronic diseases, disabilities or other medical conditions. The device is intended to supply supplemental  oxygen that improves the patient’s quality of life and mobility by increasing their blood oxygen saturation  above 90% SpO2. The FSC Oxygen Concentrator is extremely lightweight and features an ergonomic design  that rests naturally against the curves of your body, making it comfortable to wear while out and about. The  device features a simple, one touch operation with a color user interface, a long-lasting battery and utilizes  advanced breath detection technology, such as Ultra Sense, to ensure oxygen is delivered precisely when the 

user requires. FSC Oxygen Concentrators are generally prescribed only by a licensed healthcare provider to  ensure proper dosage and patient safety.
