The products under consideration are hemispherical heads, which are steel articles designed to construct the shell of a containment vessel used for a Dual Axis Radiographic Hydrodynamic Test (DARHT). These heads are made of high strength steel alloys and forged in the shape of hollow hemispheres. In various configurations a circular opening can be cut at the top of the hemisphere and, additionally, there can be semicircular openings cut along the base depending on the vessel’s required design. Upon importation the heads are joined with other heads of similar size and configuration forming the shell of the vessel.
