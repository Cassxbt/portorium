The items under consideration are stainless steel seamless tubes and pipes from China and South Korea. The tubes will be used in a wide range of applications, including some that are suitable for use in boilers, superheaters, heat exchangers, condensers, refining furnaces and feedwater heaters. They will be manufactured in accordance with various specifications including ASTM A213 and ASME SA213, as well as ASTM A268, A269, A270, A312, and A789 specifications. Pipes manufactured in conformity with specification EN10216-5 are also included in the materials in your inquiry. It is stated in your description that the tubes and pipes use cold drawn or cold rolled production methods. You have requested that this ruling should include outside diameters of less than and greater than 19mm. You have described the material as stainless steel and have provided several grades for each specification.
