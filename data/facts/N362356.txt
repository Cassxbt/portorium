Product information and a sample were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is The GivingEgg, item number AW0326.5000. The GivingEgg is a  two-piece, wooden, egg-shaped container measuring approximately 3.5” in height. The species of wood is  black walnut (Juglans nigra), a non-coniferous wood. Inside the container is a metal liner and a tube. The  GivingEgg is designed to have a nice message inserted, and then to be hidden for another person to find.
