The merchandise subject to this ruling is a single green light emitting diode (LED), a plastic housing, a plastic diffuser cover, and an electric end-cap socket. A sample of each was submitted and is being returned to you as per your request. The single green light emitting diode (LED) is identified within your submission as Part # L200. The single light emitting diode contains two lead wire prongs and is used in a fluorescent LED tube lamp assembly. The plastic housing is identified within your submission as Part # B320. It is a small plastic block measuring approximately 5/16 inch in length by 1/4 inch in width by 3/16 inch in height. The block is molded to incorporate two holes into which the leads of light emitting diodes (LEDs) may be inserted. The block does not contain any electrical contacts or connectors. The plastic diffuser cover is identified within your submission as Part # FPCF-LXX. You stated that it will be used to protect light emitting diodes (LEDs) in a fluorescent lamp assembly and to mute the light of a fluorescent LED tube lamp assembly. This component is in the form of a profile shape that is 22 inches in length, 1 inch at its greatest width and 11/16 inch at its greatest depth.
