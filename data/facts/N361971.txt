The item under consideration is the Bottom Outer Housing (Part No.CC0002). Separate rulings will address  Power Supply Board BH-02 (Part No. CC0036), Heater Plate 110V (Part No. CC0047-01), Heater Plate  220V (Part No. CC0047-02), and Heater Plate Mesh (Part No. 0062). The Bottom Outer Housing (Part No. CC0002) is the bottom component of the Bonhawa Respiratory  Humidifier case. Based on the images provided in your email dated 06/08/2026, the Bottom Outer Housing is  imported without any attachments, cables, or electrical components. It is injection-molded from a  high-performance, halogen-free PC/ABS alloy and is specifically designed for exclusive use with the  Bonhawa Respiratory Humidifier. As noted in Headquarters Ruling Letter (“HQ”) H349574, August 25, 2025, the Bonhawa Respiratory  Oxygen Platform is a high-flow oxygen respiratory humidifier for the treatment of respiratory insufficiency  for spontaneously breathing patients with warmed and humidified respiratory gases. It is intended for use by  healthcare professionals in hospitals and long-term care facilities and is only sold to hospitals, long-term care  facilities, and professional medical distributors.
