Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The products under consideration are multiple styles of personalized name or gift tags. The tags are made from 3-layer 1/8-inch Baltic birch plywood. They are cut-to-shape, drilled with a hole, and stained and painted with an individual’s name in chalk paint. The tags may be cut in multiple shapes, like a classic gift tag shape, a stylized gift tag shape, or an animal shape, like a bunny. The tag is threaded with twine to attach it to another item, such as a gift or a basket, and some tag twine is threaded with wood beads for additional decoration.
