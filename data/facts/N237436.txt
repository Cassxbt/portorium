The product under consideration is described as a Sliding Roof Panel Bracket Subassembly, part number 471613-10090. It is composed primarily of aluminum, measures approximately 502 mm long by 2.6 mm wide by 7.5 mm high, and features holes drilled along one edge for fastening. The Sliding Roof Panel Bracket Subassembly serves to connect several vehicle roof subassemblies together. It is designed to be welded to a base panel, called the Sliding Roof Housing Subassembly. It functions to attach the Sliding Roof Rail Subassembly (sunroof rail), the Sliding Roof Housing Frame Subassembly (center frame) and custom mounting brackets to the base panel.
