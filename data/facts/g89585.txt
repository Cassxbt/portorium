You submitted a picture and description from the United States Patent (USA Patent # 5,653,278) of an automobile Automatic Rear Sunshade. You state that the unit will be imported assembled and will be made of metal, while the screen, itself, will be made of nylon mesh type material. The Abstract of the United States Patent states that “…the device includes a base, a housing for containing a sunshade unit, two support arm units able to be folded in the housing and to be swung up to lift a shade curtain of the sunshade unit by rotation of a motor. When the shade curtain is lifted to shade the front or rear windshield of an automobile, the motor is cut off by an acting extension of the lower arm of one of the support arm unit pressing a contact block of a first contact switch. The first contact switch can be adjusted in its position to change the height the shade curtain is lifted. The housing can be adjusted in its angle relative to the base to adapt to various angles of different front or rear windshields of automobiles.”
