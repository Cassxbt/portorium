Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as an automatic pool cleaner, which is designed to clean the floor and walls of inground pools. The pool cleaner is equipped with a dual-motor system that provides up to 90W of power and filters 71 gallons of water per minute. It is capable of collecting and filtering a wide range of debris with the use of the enclosed reusable filter bag. In operation, the user will press the button on the device to start the cleaning process. The cleaner will roll its way around the pool collecting all of the debris. The cleaner will automatically dock when the battery is low, making it convenient to retrieve. The battery is said to last 100 minutes of cleaning time or up to 1,076 square feet of surface area with a three-hour full recharge. The quick drainage feature allows for easy cleaning and efficient access to the filter bag. The automatic pool cleaner weighs 10 pounds, and measures 16 inches long by 14 inches wide by 9.5 inches high.
