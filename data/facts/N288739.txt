The item under consideration is described as a pressure reducing valve and pipe assembly, part number H28728-C1100-EX. The assembly functions as a pressure reducing valve that opens and closes as the flow of exhaust increases or decreases inside the muffler of an automobile. The assembly is constructed of stainless steel and the main components of the assembly consist of a housing, a gate, a short length pipe, a shaft and a spring.
