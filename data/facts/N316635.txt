The product under consideration, identified as item 122220413, is a raw cylinder body for use in hydraulic cylinders for agricultural tractors. According to your submission, this cold-drawn nonalloy steel welded tube is made to European Standard 10305-2 E355 C. The tube, which has an outside diameter of 53 mm and a wall thickness of 4 mm, is cut-to-length, drilled to make the first oil hole and then deburred. After importation, the tube undergoes the following processes: machining, welding of oil ports, washing and preparation for assembly as a finished hydraulic cylinder.
