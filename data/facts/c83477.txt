The merchandise under consideration is a device which isknown as "Universal Serial Bus (USB) Smart Cable". By utilizinghardware and software, the "USB Smart Cable" provides intelligentand transparent protocol conversion between the USB on thepersonal computer (PC) side and IEEE 1284 parallel on the printerside. It will allow any "Windows" PC with a USB port to printnearly any parallel printer. The "USB Smart Cable" is constructed of a USB series Aconnector, a USB compliant two meter 5 conductor insulated andshielded cable, and an IEEE 1284-B connector attached to aprinted circuit board installed inside the IEEE 1284 connectorhousing. The heart of the device is the USS-720 Instant USBintegrated circuit. This device is compatible with USB hubdevices and virtually all parallel printer devices.
