Photographs and a description of the “Curse of the Unholy Grail” game were submitted with your inquiry. The “Curse of the Unholy Grail” is an adult drinking game comprised of 10 uniquely colored dice, 126  “Chaos” cards, 25 “Trial” cards, 4 “Unholy Grail” cards and game instructions. Gameplay is driven by  drawing cards to initiate actions or challenges and to ultimately locate the “Unholy Grail.”  Certain cards  initiate 'trials' that are competitive, dice-driven challenges between players. Each player uses a die as their  game piece. Once all 4 “Unholy Grail” cards have been discovered, the game culminates in a final ‘trial’ that  determines the winner.
