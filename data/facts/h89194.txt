The product under consideration is an electronic user-worn interface device identified as a P5™ computer input. In its imported condition, the product will consist of a “glove,” a “docking station,” cables, and software on a compact disc. The glove is worn on the user’s hand and with its sensors and electronic circuitry allows the user to control and manipulate computer and gaming system applications. The glove is connected by wire to the enclosed “docking station” which converts and transmits useable information received from the glove to a data processing machine via a USB cable. You have indicated that the product will support all platforms that are USB based including PC’s, Mac’s, and the newer gaming systems such as the Sony PS2 and the Microsoft Xbox.
