The product under consideration is described as the “HH26 BLK/ORG TREE 3FT.”  It is a decorative  three-foot-tall Halloween tree. This artificial tree will come in two versions, one that is black and the other  which is orange in color. Each tree has a plastic base, a central metal tube, and polyvinyl chloride (PVC)  plastic branches constructed from plastic “pine needle” strips that are bound to a twisted wire core.
