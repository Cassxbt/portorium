The merchandise under consideration is White Ornamental stone. A sample was submitted with your ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This analysis has been completed. The White Ornamental is a polished white, gray, brown, and dark red mottled stone designed for such applications as countertops, wall coverings, flooring, and backsplashes. From the information you provided, upon importation it is polished and already cut to its final measurements of approximately 130 centimeters long by 76 centimeters wide by 3 centimeters thick; it will not be further cut or worked after importation. In your ruling request you state that the stone which comprises the White Ornamental is granite. However, laboratory analysis has determined that it is not granite; it is a polished other stone.
