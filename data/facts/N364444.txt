The product in question is a Land-Based Autonomous Environmental DNA Sampler (eDNA Sampler). The eDNA Sampler will be installed at a fixed, shore-based location and used to collect and preserve DNA samples from a body of water without performing any measurement or analysis. The eDNA Sampler as imported will include the sampler enclosure and internal frame, a pump and internal fluid path, a water intake or sampling tube, a filter carousel and its integral sample holder cases, an automated preservation subsystem, an integral ultraviolet (UV) antifouling emitter, electronic controls and a cellular/satellite telemetry module. All components are permanently assembled into a single housing and imported together as a single apparatus. In operation, the eDNA Sampler’s pump draws a metered volume of ambient water through the intake tube from an adjacent body of water. The water passes through a disposable membrane filter which mechanically retains suspended particulate matter, including cellular material and DNA fragments shed by aquatic organisms. The filtered water is discharged back into the environment. The filtrate will not be retained, treated, or put to any use. The loaded filter is indexed into a sealed holder within the carousel and subjected to an automated preservation sequence (drying and chemical fixation) to stabilize the captured material. The sequence repeats, advancing through a carousel of up to 96 filter positions. Upon retrieval, the sealed sample holder cases are removed by the operator and transported to a laboratory, where the preserved filters are subjected to DNA extraction and analysis by separate laboratory instrumentation.
