The merchandise under consideration is an audio media receiver with an AM and FM tuner, model  MVH-S230BT, which is used in motor vehicles. This radio broadcast receiver has Bluetooth connectivity  and USB input for easy connection to portable devices, including smartphones, MP3 players, or USB drives,  and can read and playback sound directly from a USB memory device. Moreover, this radio receiver is an  after-market product designed to replace a standard car stereo in a motor vehicle.
