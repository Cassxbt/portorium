The ruling was requested on steel sheds and cabinets for home use. Photos and product information were submitted for our review and will be retained for reference. The ruling addresses two categories of products in various designs and sizes. The first category is metal garden sheds, imported complete but requiring assembly. These are made from corrugated steel, have walls, roofs, and sliding or hinged doors, and use the ground as a base/floor. The sheds range in size from 1.86m (W) x 0.85m (D) x 1.90m (H – walls) to 3.63m (W) x 3.63m (D) x 1.90m (H – walls). Each shed is designed for a human to enter and utilize the interior space. The second category is cabinets which also range in style and size, with most measuring approximately 1.85m high. The cabinets have single or double hinged doors, interior shelves or compartments, and some are open shelves without doors. Each is designed for placement on the floor. The cabinets and shelves do not accommodate human entry.
