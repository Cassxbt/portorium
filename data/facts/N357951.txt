In lieu of samples, technical and illustrative literature, a product description, and a manufacturing  synopsis were provided for review. The “Jean-Marie Mid-Century Record Player Stand” is an unassembled wooden storage cabinet. The cabinet  incorporates no electrical components, mechanical components, or integrated speakers. The cabinet is  constructed of solid hardwood (black walnut) and incorporates a tambour sliding door panel (white oak slats)  with textile backing. The cabinet is affixed to detachable solid hardwood legs. The cabinet will allow for the  placement and storage of objects. The cabinet dimensions will approximate 24" in width, 20" in depth, 30" in  height, and will weigh 45 lbs. The ruling request outlines an operation wherein black walnut hardwood, textile backing, and glue will be  sourced from the United States, metal threaded inserts and screws will be sourced from Germany, and  magnets will be sourced from China. The material components will be shipped and exported to Canada. In  Canada, white oak hardwood will be locally sourced. The manufacturing process undertaken in Canada  involves (1) the black walnut and the white oak hardwood are prepared, joined, and planed using measuring  tools, table saws and miter saws, (2) the black walnut and white oak hardwood are cut to shape using clamps,  jigs, saws, drills, orbital sanders, and routers to fabricate the cabinet case, shelf, back panel, and the legs (3)  wooden door grooves are cut, drilled, and machined in preparation for joining of the cabinet legs with the  cabinet frame, (4) the cabinet door is fabricated using wooden slats and textile backing materials, (5) the  cabinet body and door are assembled using glue, (6) magnets are assembled onto the sliding door, (7) the 

cabinet legs undergo sanding and surface preparation using jigs, and (8) the final unassembled cabinet is  coated with an Osmo finish and cured. In Canada, the unassembled wooden cabinet will be inspected,  packaged, and then exported to the United States. Photographs of these processes were provided.
