Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is Item 865858, a decorative owl figure. The form of the figure is  constructed of Styrofoam, and the form is covered with multiple materials. The eyes (the most prominent  feature) of the owl are surrounded by natural, unspun sisal fibers. The belly of the owl is also covered with  sisal fibers. The whole of the head (approximately 50 percent of the surface are of the figure) is covered with  fine strands of cattails. The wings and tail feathers are made of a combination of birch wood twig slices,  straw, and banana leaf. The owl is perched on a wooden twig with a pine cone and plastic pine needles, and  it has plastic eyes.
