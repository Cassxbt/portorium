The 900-00450 Rack Direct Contact Liquid Cooling (DCLC) Cadence Air Heat Exchange (AHx) Silvertip Module is a liquid cooling device. It is designed for use with a commercial data center and can be used in a “top of the rack” or a “in-rack” configuration. The module consists of a fan, centralized pumps, a control/monitoring system, a leak detection/migration system and a 4.3” LCD touch screen. While in operation, coolant is pumped through the tubes of the unit. This absorbs heat from the data center and coupled with the fan, results in a cooling process.
