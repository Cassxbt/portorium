The merchandise under consideration, identified by part number 1067424, is described as an automotive power cable for use with the Respironics Trilogy ventilator, a medical device intended to assist those individuals with chronic breathing issues. The subject cable consists of a length of insulated conductor cable affixed at one end with a specially designed electrical connector and a 12-volt automotive power plug at the other. You state that the specially designed connector can only attach to the aforementioned ventilator. Further, the cable contains a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) that allows for the matching of voltage and other electrical requirements of the ventilator and the cable. The subject cable is used to facilitate the flow of electrical power to the ventilator while in a vehicle.
