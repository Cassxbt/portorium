Additional information was submitted by email dated July 10, 2024. The items under consideration are described as DMC Medical Polycarbonate Syringes and DMC Medical NuGen™ Eastman Tritan ™ Copolyester Syringes. Both types of syringes consist of a calibrated barrel, a color ABS plunger, a silicone stopper, and a male luer lock and are available in 1ml, 3ml, 6ml, 10ml, 20ml and 30ml sizes. The syringes will be imported in bulk in non-sterile form. They will undergo sterilization and further packaging into surgical kits in the United States before entering the market. The syringes are intended to be used without a needle for administering various drugs and contrast medium during medical procedures.
