The articles in question are described as a “Desktop Air Conditioner – Personal Cooling System” and a “20-function  folding multi-tool”. Descriptive information and samples were submitted. The “air conditioner” is a desktop fan that incorporates a plastic container of frozen water. The fan is a “personal cooling system” designed to move volumes of air over the chilled container and blow cooled air directly on the user. The fan features a self-contained battery operated motor. The “folding multi-function tool” is a composite tool of stainless steel with a nylon carrying case. The tool incorporates 20 different components including pliers, screwdrivers, knife blades, wrench slots, a saw blade, a wire cutter, hook remover, a bottle  opener, a file and a punch.
