The products under consideration are two types of Steel Liquid Containment Barriers: The FM Fire  Spillbarrier and The TÜV Chemical Spillbarrier. The Steel Liquid Containment Barriers are devices designed to prevent the uncontrolled release and spread of  liquids through openings such as doorways, gates, and access areas within industrial facilities. These barriers  are constructed for installation flush with the floor, positioned either in front of or within an opening where  liquid containment is a critical requirement. Each barrier system comprises several key components: a  movable barrier flap, robust side posts, effective sealing elements, an integrated floor housing for  containment, mechanical closing mechanisms, and other necessary parts to ensure its automatic containment  function. These barriers operate independently, requiring no external power sources, electrical sensors,  hydraulic systems, pneumatic systems, or complex control systems. The closing function is intrinsically  activated by the presence of liquid itself, ensuring a responsive and autonomous operation.
