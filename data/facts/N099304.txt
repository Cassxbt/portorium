The merchandise under consideration is described as a Door Hinge Pin, part number 982461-08039. It is made of carbon steel and designed exclusively for use in an automobile door hinge. A door hinge connects a movable car door to the fixed chassis of a vehicle. It includes a fixed member mounted to an inner portion of the door, a fixed member mounted to the door pillar of the chassis, and a hinge pin. The door hinge pin connects the fixed components of the hinge and enables the door-mounted portion of the hinge to pivot, thereby allowing the door to swing open and close.
