The merchandise under consideration is identified as the AY02 electric patient lift and is described as a  foldable sling lift used to transfer elderly individuals, people with disabilities, or other individuals with  limited mobility. The patient lift has a steel frame composed of a base with casters, a vertical mast, and a  boom with a sling hook. The base is adjustable so that the lift can fit through narrow doorways. The frame is  equipped with an electrically powered motor actuator, a control pendant, a control unit, and a push handle for  maneuvering the unit. This particular model has a 400-pound lifting capacity.
