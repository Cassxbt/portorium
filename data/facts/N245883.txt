The C018 cold roll steel stop bar is a fabricated part designed for use only in the manufacture of Gillette razor blades. It is incorporated in the Upstack Carousel which puts empty dispensers in place so Quantity 5 razor cartridges can be loaded into the dispenser. The stop bar does not contain any mechanical or electronic components. If the proximity switch fails, the stop bar acts as a secondary physical barrier to protect the Upstack Carousel from over traveling/rotating so that the cartridge dispensers will unload/unload smoothly.
