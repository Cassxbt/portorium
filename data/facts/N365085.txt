Photographs and descriptive literature were provided for our review. The Shockwave Motion System Simulator is a stationary audiovisual and physical motion experience providing an amusement park-like ride. The unit consists of a motion platform, 2 passenger seats, 2 motion actuators, a control cabinet with electronic components, a computer, a screen and speakers, all of which are designed to function together as a single integrated system. The screen and speakers provide the audiovisual component of the experience while the motion platform and actuators provide the corresponding physical movement. These elements operate together to create a unified motion-simulation attraction. Passengers watch the audiovisual content displayed on the screen while the motion platform moves the seats in synchronization with the pre-programmed audiovisual content. The passengers do not have any operational control of the unit, but rather experience an entertaining ride simulation. The subject unit is intended for installation and operation in entertainment venues, amusement parks, attractions and similar environments.
