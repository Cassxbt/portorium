The subject merchandise is described as “Grapefruit Flavored Rum”. The product has an alcoholic strength by volume of 35 percent (70 proof). The “Grapefruit Flavored Rum” will be blended and bottled in Italy in bottles ranging in size from 50ml to 1.76 L to United States consumers. In all cases the imports will be valued at more than $3.00 per proof liter.
