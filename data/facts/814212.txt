You are requesting the tariff classification of a children's game, product number 540, referred to in the ruling request as "Lil' Learner Amazing Coloracers". The item is a children's game (no board required) for ages four and older. The game is intended for two players, and may be played on any flat surface. The equipment includes: 2 coloracers(cars), 6 traffic cones, start and finish gates, and a die. The players, in turn, roll the die and move the car until the color in the sunroof matches the color on the die. When the colors match, the turn is completed, and the other player rolls the die. The first player to pass through the finish gate wins the game.
