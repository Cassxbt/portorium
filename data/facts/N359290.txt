The items concerned are microphones used on the boom assembly of aviation headsets, (model #’s 2  60-00009-000.A1 Mic, Boom, 5 ohm and 260-00010-000.A1 Mic, Boom, 150 ohm). Each of the  microphones is mounted/encased within a GE Cycoloy PC/ABS, grade C1200 HF housing. The function of  each is to transmit sound waves from the users voice through the headset. Both the “Mic, Boom, 5 ohm” and  the “Mic, Boom, 150 ohm” are used with Lightspeed’s Zulu 3 aviation headset.
