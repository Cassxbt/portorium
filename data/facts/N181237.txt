Descriptive literature was submitted. The article in question is described as a bulk cement powder offloading system for a transport barge. The system is comprised of forty-eight “airslides”, which employ blowers to fluidize the dry cement powder, and two heavy-duty screw-type pneumatic pumps. The fluidized cement powder is transported on top of the airslides to the cement pump. Within the cement pump the cement powder is packed into a chamber by a mechanical screw and then conveyed off the barge pneumatically by compressed air supplied by two compressors.
