Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as an automated retail capsule (ARC), which is a dome-shaped, walk-in aluminum structure designed for use in retail environments. The ARC functions as a controlled environment for measuring a customer’s body dimensions to facilitate the production of made-to-measure clothing. The interior features two changing rooms and a central body-scanning room equipped with a TG3D Scanatic360 body scanner. This scanner utilizes infrared, structured-light sensor technology to capture a full-body 3D scan in approximately three seconds, generating over 250 individual body measurements. Following the scanning process, the customer’s measurements are automatically linked to their profile. The system then selects the optimal pattern based on the product type and transmits the data to the company’s manufacturing portal for production. The exterior of the ARC features two “Proto Luma” multi-touch holographic displays, which provide a high-fidelity interface for 3D product visualization, virtual garment try-on, and checkout. The unit also includes a ventilation system, an intercom system, soft interior lighting, and interior finishing. The ARC is imported unassembled and measures approximately 18 feet in length by 11.5 feet in height and weighs 3.5 tons.
