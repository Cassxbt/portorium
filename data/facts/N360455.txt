In lieu of samples, descriptive  literature and pictures were provided with your request. The items under consideration are the Khyber V1 Multi Pivot and V2 Fixed Pivot Racks. The subject  merchandise are hitch-mounted racks designed to transport bicycles on the rear of a motor vehicle. The  products attach directly to a standard 2-inch trailer hitch receiver installed on passenger vehicles and provide  support and transport of bicycles during vehicle operation. The products are constructed of 6061-T6 aluminum and 304 stainless steel with a small amount of plastic and  rubber. The aluminum structural components form the main frame, and the stainless-steel components are  incorporated into the vertical mast, lower wheel support bar, and wheel baskets. The products are designed  for specific use with motor vehicles and provide no independent function outside of the vehicle’s attachment. The subject merchandise has multiple variations that allow for additional configurations yet perform the same  function of support and transport of bicycles.
