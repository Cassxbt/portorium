The merchandise under consideration is referred to as the EARISE M60, which is a set consisting of a battery-operated loudspeaker with a built-in radio receiver and sound reproducing apparatus, two wireless microphones, and a remote control that can be used as PA system. The system’s main unit consists of a Bluetooth loudspeaker with a built-in FM tuner, a USB port, an SD card slot, and a ten-inch subwoofer. Moreover, this portable radio receiver can reproduce sound from a USB memory device or an SD card.
