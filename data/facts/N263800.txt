Control Panel, part number 87404, is a device that connects to an Emergency Water Tank Assembly. Together, the Control Panel and Water Tank Assembly provide potable water during power outages. The Control Panel is housed in a plastic casing and includes an air compressor, a check valve, two electric relays, a 12 volt lead acid battery, a fuse block, a solenoid, a trickle charger, a pressure switch and a manifold. This device plugs into a 12 volt household receptacle, enabling the battery to charge and the electric relays to monitor the electrical service. Upon the occurrence of a power outage, the compressor inflates air into the internal rubber bladders of the water tanks via an air pressure line. The Control Panel continuously maintains a range of 30 to 50psi of air pressure within the water tanks. Once electricity is restored, the pressurized air is expelled from the storage tanks, allowing the tanks to fill to capacity with water.
