The first product, Brivaracetam, imported in dosages of 10 mg, 25 mg, 50 mg, 75 mg and 100 mg tablets, is  an anticonvulsant drug. It is indicated for the treatment of partial-onset seizures in patients 1 month of age  and older. The second product, Eltrombopag, imported in 12.5 mg, 25 mg, 50 mg, and 75 mg tablets, is a  thrombopoietin receptor agonist. It is indicated for treatment of thrombocytopenia (low blood platelet  counts) in patients with persistent or chronic immune thrombocytopenic, thrombocytopenia in patients with  chronic hepatitis C infection, and patients with severe aplastic anemia.
