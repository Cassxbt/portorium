Images were provided in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described as a stand-alone modular jewelry storage system. This item is  made primarily of acrylic plastic and molded plastic components. It is designed to store, organize, and  protect jewelry such as necklaces, bracelets, rings, earrings, and other personal jewelry items. The storage  system measures approximately 12 inches wide by 8 inches deep by 24 inches tall and is intended to be  placed on a shelf, dresser, or similar household surface. It can also be hung on a wall using standard  household mounting hardware. The interior consists of interchangeable and removable plastic modules that  allow users to customize the storage layout. The product includes flocked plastic panels, plastic slot walls,  jewelry bars, ring holders, a low-rise drawer, and an acrylic earring panel door.
