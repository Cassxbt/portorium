The item under consideration is titled in your submission as a “Water Temperature Control Assembly”, part number 25500-82C00. This item is known in the automotive industry as a Thermostat Housing Assembly. The components of the assembly consist of an aluminum housing that incorporates two thermostats, an engine coolant bypass nipple, and two pipe connectors that serve as pathways for the engine coolant to flow into the radiator and heater. The assembly also incorporates a Coolant Temperature Sensor. It is said the sensor is designed to measure the temperature of the engine coolant and send such information to the ECU. The assembly, with all of its components, is designed to regulate the engine temperature by controlling the flow of engine coolant to the radiator. This is accomplished through self-regulating valves (thermostats) that are installed inside the assembly. The thermostats use a temperature sensitive pellet to open and close the valve depending upon the temperature of the engine coolant. As the temperature of the engine coolant heats up, the thermostats open, allowing for more coolant to flow to the radiator.
