The submitted sample, UL 20L Christmas Plastic Mesh “Feliz Navidad”, identified as item number A073EA03692, is a decoration comprised of the words “Feliz Navidad” in polyvinyl chloride (PVC) red mesh lettering that measure approximately 10-3/4” in height x 16-1/2” in length x ¾” in thickness. The lettering is highlighted with attached PVC holly leaves and holly berries. The article is attached to a glass pane via suction cups and is intended purely as a window decoration. An attached power cord affords the article the capacity to light up. The words “Feliz Navidad” are Spanish for “Merry Christmas” and limit the article’s use to the Christmas holiday.
