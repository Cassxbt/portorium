The products under consideration are described as swaged steel tubes presented in four different configurations. Each tube has an outside diameter of approximately 1.25 inches, a wall thickness of 0.07 inches, and an overall length of 243 inches. One end of each tube is swaged 3 inches to an outside diameter of approximately 1.06 inches. Additionally, each tube features four drilled holes, each measuring 0.91 inches in diameter. The swaged end is designed to allow one tube to nest or fit inside another. These swaged steel tubes are intended to be used as fence posts in the construction of a livestock fence.
