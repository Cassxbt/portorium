The ruling was requested on several articles; namely, asmall wooden box, a lap desk, a guitar stand and a wooden boardgame. Photographs were submitted. The small box measures 5-1/2 inches wide by 9-1/2 incheslong by 2-1/2 inches high. It is made of walnut or maple wood. It appears to have a hinged lid and it is not lined or covered. The lap desk is a portable storage box with a writingsurface. It has the appearance of a small desk without legs. Itmeasures approximately 12 inches wide by 18 inches long by 4inches high and is made of solid cherry, butternut or walnutwood. It has a hinged lid which opens up to a divided storagespace and which functions as a writing surface. The guitar stand is a portable stand on which guitars can berested. It measures 3 inches wide by 16 inches long by 30 incheshigh and can be folded. It is made of cherry, ash or walnut woodand it has leather straps. The "Tides" game is a game of skill originally from Africa. It consists of a folding wooden board with a leather hinge andtie strap and polished beach stones. Playing instructions areincluded.
