The merchandise in question is an interactive digital audio MP3 music player, which is referred to as the “BF-1 BodiBeat.”  The BodiBeat is an MP3 music player that can automatically play music to match your running /jogging/walking pace or aerobic level. This composite machine measures approximately 3 inches by 1.5 inches by .8 inch and has an internal 512MB Flash ROM memory as well as an internal lithium-ion battery. Furthermore, the BodiBeat tracks the metrics of your workout, including distance, time, pace and heart rate (using the small heart-rate monitor that clips to your ear). The principle function of this composite machine is performed by the music player. In its condition as imported, the BodiBeat player will be packaged for retail sale with the following: armband, earphones, USB cable, cable clip, earphone pads, CD-ROM software for the PC, and literature.
