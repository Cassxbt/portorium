You are requesting the tariff classification on an item that is described as a “Gel Target”. The product will be available in 2 different sizes, however, in all other aspects each target board will be identical to the other. The outer ring and the frame are constructed of molded polypropylene with a thermoplastic rubber material making up the adhesive gel surface. Underneath the gel surface there is layer of soft foam bonded to the adhesive gel layer. The foam portion is printed with the rings of the target and the appropriate numbers. The target board is used with soft air guns for both indoor play and target practice.
