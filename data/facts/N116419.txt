The merchandise under consideration is A/V (audio/video) receivers, models Onkyo TX-NR1008 and Integra DTR-40.2. The Onkyo TX-NR1008 9.2-Channel Network A/V Receiver features the following: a USB port,  HDMI ports, network capability for streaming audio files from the Internet or PC, Internet radio connectivity, HD radio ready and Sirius satellite radio ready, a music optimizer, an Ethernet port, a universal port for single-cable connection of optional Onkyo dock for iPhone/iPod or HD radio tuner, a 135 watts per channel amplifier, an AM/FM tuner, component video inputs and outputs, and S-video inputs and outputs. The Integra DTR-40.2 7.2-Channel Network A/V Receiver features the following: a USB port, HDMI ports, network capability for streaming audio files from the Internet or PC, Internet radio connectivity, HD radio ready and Sirius satellite radio ready, a music optimizer, an Ethernet port, a universal port for single-cable connection of optional Onkyo dock for iPhone/iPod or HD radio tuner, a 110 watts per channel amplifier, an AM/FM tuner, component video inputs and outputs, and S-video inputs and outputs. Both receivers, which cannot operate without an external source of power, contain radio broadcasting reception apparatus, amplification and various connectivity and networking features. In addition, the submitted literature indicates that the USB input, in both receivers, can be used to play music files stored on USB mass storage devices (e.g., USB flash drives and MP3 players), which can be plugged into the A/V receiver's USB port. Therefore, both receivers are capable of reproducing sound.
