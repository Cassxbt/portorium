The request was returned to you for additional information, which was received by this office on July 26, 2018. Product information was submitted for our review. The product under consideration is Type 1 steel pressure vessels for compressed natural gas. Each vessel is forged from cylindrical seamless steel tubing, formed with spherical ends, and terminated in nozzles. Each vessel may measure 18.7 feet or 38.7 feet in length and 36 inches at its outer diameter, and is designed to sustain a working pressure of 5500psi. The nozzles are machined and threaded on their inner and outer diameters to accommodate bull plugs and mounting flanges that are added prior to importation.
