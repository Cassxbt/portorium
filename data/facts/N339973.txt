The item under consideration is a lateral drain assembly for water filtration equipment, specifically Newterra’s Ion Exchange Vessel. The laterals are made from 3-inch stainless steel pipe and have a series of holes drilled along the length of the pipes to allow contaminated water to pass through them. The same laterals are partially encased in a well-screen overlay to further facilitate the filtration process. As mentioned above, the design and manufacture of these stainless laterals are specific for use in the Newterra Ion Exchange Vessel, which has the required 10-foot diameter vessel, and include specific angles, branches, lengths, and connections. The laterals are not compatible for any other use. Newterra’s Ion Exchange Vessel is designed for high-efficiency treatment of water and wastewater in industrial applications. The vessel utilizes ion exchange resins to selectively remove contaminants such as heavy metals, hardness ions, and other dissolved solids, resulting in high-purity water suitable for various industrial processes. The ion exchange process is an exchange of one ion for another, using a synthetic resin to perform the transfer of ions. These technologies are widely used for the removal of inorganic dissolved solid impurities from the feed water to steam-generating boilers or other process equipment to prevent scaling and oxidation damage.
