The merchandise is described as water coolers. Models DSPOUWCF1and DSPOUWCF5G are imported with connections that directly connect to a water supply. Water bottles are the water source for models DSBWCT1, DSBWCT5G, DSBWCB1 and DSBWCB5G. Hot and cold water is dispensed from each cooler by depressing the appropriate push button water control. Cold water is produced by a reciporicating compressor that chills water. The condenser coils mounted on the back of the unit receive refrigerant circulated by the compressor.
