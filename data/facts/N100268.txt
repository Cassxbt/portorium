Descriptions and photos of three different frozen seafood products were submitted for our review. You also provided additional information by telephone. All of the products will be imported packed in non-airtight plastic bags placed within printed cardboard retail boxes. After importation, all of the products will be sold in supermarkets to be taken home and either cooked in the oven or deep-fried. The first item, identified as “Honey Shrimp,” is a frozen prepared shrimp product (Panaeus vannemei) comprised of the following ingredients: peeled/tailless/headless shrimp (60%), wheat flour, tapioca starch, rice flour, corn flour, water, baking powder, honey, sugar, salt and vegetable oil. Based on a submitted photo, the goods have the typical appearance of whole, individual breaded shrimp. Sachets of honey sauce (containing wheat flour, honey, sugar and water) will also be included in the retail box.
