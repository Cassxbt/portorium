Background information on John Greer indicates that he has studied in the area of fine arts from 1962 – 1967 in Halifax, Montreal and Vancouver, Canada. He taught sculpture at NSCAD University in Halifax for 26 years. He is based in LaHave, Nova Scotia; Dallas, Texas; and Pietrasanta, Italy. His resume of exhibited sculpture is extensive in Canada, while also exhibiting in the United States, Iceland, Korea and Italy. Some of his more current recognizable works include: Humble Ending 2009, Italy; Senseful 2003, USA; Gathering 2001, Korea; and Reflections 2001, Canada. He has been the recipient over four decades of numerous awards in the free fine arts, including as of late the Governor General’s Award, 2009. Based on Greer’s works, exhibitions, recognition and numerous awards, we find that he is recognized as a professional artist (sculptor) of the free fine arts.
