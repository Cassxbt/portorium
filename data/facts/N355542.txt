The item concerned is a headband assembly (Part # 285-00023-000.A2) for Lightspeed’s Sierra aviation  headset. The housing/structure of the headband assembly is made from ABS-PC - a thermoplastic blend of  polycarbonate (PC) and acrylonitrile-butadiene-styrene (AB). The headband incorporates insulated electrical  wiring, connectors and a spring. The functions of this item include:  Maintaining a secure fit - A sturdy headband keeps the ear cups pressed gently but firmly against your  ears. This helps the ear seals form a tight seal, which is essential for blocking out external noise. Supporting consistent positioning - Noise canceling works best when the headset is properly aligned. A  rigid headband ensures the ear cups stay in the right place, so the microphones and speakers used in  active noise canceling can do their job effectively. Durability and Longevity - Hard plastic is lightweight but strong, so it can handle daily wear and tear  without losing its shape. This allows the headset to continue performing effectively over time. Comfort with Structure - When paired with soft head pads, the hard plastic provides structure without  sacrificing comfort.
