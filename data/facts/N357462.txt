The merchandise under consideration is identified as the AireMed Sit-to-Stand Electric Patient Lift, model  number AIR-10ST, and is described as equipment for raising patients from a seated position to a standing  position, and vice versa. It consists of a steel frame with adjustable legs, front and rear casters, a removable  foot plate, a padded leg support, and grip handles. The handles have oversized hooks for slings that can  support patients weighing up to 450 pounds. Attached to the frame is an actuator assembly, a control unit  with an emergency stop button, a 24 V rechargeable battery, and a hand pendant.
