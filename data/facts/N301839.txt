The four items at issue are tire valves, item numbers TRI412/L, VHI724MS/BG, VHI726MS/BG, VHI728MS/BG and VHI729MS/BG. Each hand-operated valve has a metal cap that seals and protects the valve mechanism when the tire valve is not in use, a threaded stem and a rubber mounting. The tire valves allow air to flow in or out of a tire once the stem of the valve has been depressed. The brass valves have a pressure rating of 448.15 kPa.
