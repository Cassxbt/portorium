You submitted samples and detailed descriptions of three styles of an item identified as the MonthlyCup. The MonthlyCup is a reusable menstrual cup composed of silicone, worn inside the vagina during menstruation to collect menstrual fluid. The cup shall be taken out approximately 2-4 times/day. The cup shall not be inserted for more than 12 hours without being taken out. The MonthlyCup will be made in three model sizes to allow for the variability of the female vagina due to normal differences between women. In addition to the three model sizes, the MonthlyCup will be made in several different colors. The cup is designed in three sizes to facilitate different needs, based on menstrual flow. The MonthlyCup will not be supplied sterile. The mini size is especially made for the first years of menstruation and this is actually one of the smallest cups on the market. A little softer and a little smaller, it can hold 15ml. The normal size can hold 22ml and is suitable for most. The plus size can hold 34ml.
