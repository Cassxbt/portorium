The products in question, model numbers S19-002A and S-19-003A, are hand powered push sweepers for lawns, patios, and driveways. Each unit has two 10-in. diameter wheels that connect to a main housing which holds two horizontal rotating roller brushes. When pushed forward, the ground drive wheels cause the brushes to rotate, sweeping up fallen leaves, grass clippings, dead branches, gravel and debris on the ground into a removeable hopper collection bag. The brushes are height adjustable. Model S19-002 has a hopper capacity of 7 cu. ft. and a cleaning width of 26 in. Model S-19-003A has a hopper capacity of 12 cu. ft. and a cleaning width of 30 in.
