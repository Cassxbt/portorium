This ruling is being issued in reply to your letter dated May 26, 2023, requesting a tariff classification determination. In lieu of a sample, a picture and product descriptions were provided. The subject article is identified as a Black Marble C-Table with SKU # 2330-BELLABLK. It is a 22" high, C-shaped table made of natural stone. The edge-beveled tabletop and the horizontal foot measure 14" (W) x 10" (D). The table is intended to hold snacks, a TV remote, or other small items for someone seated at home on a chair, lounger, or sofa. It will be imported assembled and is made in India.
