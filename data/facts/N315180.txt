The merchandise under consideration, identified by part number 1118463, is described as a DC power cable designed to be used exclusively with the Respironics DreamStation bilevel positive airway pressure (BiPAP) or continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) machine. The cable consists of a length of insulated cable conductor affixed at one end with a DC power connector and at the other with a multi-pin printed circuit connector. You state that the printed circuit connector is specially designed to connect directly to the main PCB of the DreamStation device. The cable is used to facilitate the flow of electrical power from a power supply to the DreamStation device.
