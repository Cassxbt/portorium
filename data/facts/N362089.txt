Product information and a sample were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is SKU #44712401, Pokémon 3D Puzzle Model Kit. The item is a  9.2”-high, three-dimensional model of a Pokémon character that the user assembles. The model is  constructed from die-cut pieces with tab and slot assembly. The pieces are constructed of a sheet of  polystyrene foam sandwiched between two sheets of printed, coated paperboard. The foam gives the pieces  dimension and stability.
