The items concerned are referred to as Headband Slider A, Painted (303-00120-000.A5) and Headband Slider  B, Painted (303-00121-000.A4). The headband sliders (A & B) are used on the headband assembly of  Lightspeed ’s Zulu 3 aviation headset. They connect the headband top to the ear cups of the Zulu 3 headset   and allow adjustability for use among all head shapes and sizes. The slider allows pilots to adjust the headband length for a secure and personalized fit. Proper adjustment  ensures the ear cups maintain a tight seal, which is critical for both passive and active noise cancellation  performance. The slider is part of the headband assembly that provides mechanical strength and stability. The sliders are made from a magnesium alloy.
