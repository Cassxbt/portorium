The merchandise under consideration is referred to as CVA RR Carpet EPP Pad, Part Number  DSGCVAEPP01, which is a foam pad that goes under the carpet assembly of a passenger vehicle and it is  composed of 100% Ethylene Propylene Copolymer. The component is precisely molded for installation  beneath the carpet assembly, which will ultimately go on top of the metal floor pan within a passenger  vehicle. The molded piece is used to reduce the road noise and vibration transmitted from the underside of  the vehicle.
