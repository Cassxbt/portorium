The casters under consideration are described as a steel, twin wheel swivel caster with a solid stem, item  number 2940; and a steel, twin wheel locking caster with a central lock stem, item number 2944. Both items  are made of a steel housing and mounting stem, and a polyamide wheel. They each have a wheel diameter of  4.92 inches (125 mm), and a wheel width of .47 inches (12 mm). These casters will be used on an OEC Elite  imaging cart.
