The PICOMASTER XF Series consists of five models of focused UV laser beam systems for semiconductor  lithography and microelectronic/nanofabrication applications: PICOMASTERXF200, PICOMASTERXF500,  PICOMASTER800, PICOMASTER1000, and PICOMASTER1400. The XF Series models employ a  multi-beam laser to directly (i.e., without a photomask) draw or “write” patterns on the nanometer scale onto  semiconductor devices and other photosensitive substrate materials. The XF Series models are designed for  and principally used in semiconductor manufacturing (mask creation, direct writing on semiconductor  materials), photonics (fabrication of optical components), LED manufacturing, flat panel display patterning,  MEMS/NEMS (fabrication of micro/nano-electromechanical systems), microfluidics, and RF device  component creation. The key components of the PICOMASTER XF Series machines include: (1) main housing; (2) writing  module consisting of GaN laser diode source and high-precision optical components including beam shaping  optics; (3) Grating Light Valve (GLV) for parallel beam processing; (4) positioning system (air bearing  stages, linear motors, optical encoders with nanometer resolution); (5) control electronics; (6) and  environmental controls (HEPA filter, temperature and humidity control systems). GLV technology permits  the XF Series to achieve higher throughput than the PICOMASTER Standard Series single focused beam  writers which are the subject of N348150.
