The merchandise under consideration is identified as a rechargeable lithium polymer battery, part number  ICP241019, and is described as an ultra-thin, rectangular pouch cell with lead wires. It measures  approximately 18.5 millimeters in length, 11.2 millimeters in width, and 2.4 millimeters in thickness. In the  United States, the battery will be incorporated into a glucose sensor transmitter, which is a component of a  glucose monitoring system. The function of the glucose sensor transmitter is to gather data, such as voltages,  electrochemical impedance, and other readings, which are then transmitted to a display device. The battery  powers the transmitter and has a nominal voltage of 3.7 VDC and a nominal capacity of 25 mAh.
