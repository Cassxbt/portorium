The products under consideration are vulcanized rubber livestock feeding nipples identified as Components 1002710 and 1002711. Component 1002710 is a vulcanized rubber lamb nipple. The flange diameter on the bottom is 2.35 inches, and the overall height of the nipple is 2.65 inches. The tip is cut in a cross pattern to allow liquid to flow under suction. Component 1002711 is a vulcanized rubber calf nipple. The flange diameter on the bottom is 2.35 inches, and the overall height of the nipple is 3.00 inches. The tip is cut in a cross pattern to allow liquid to flow under suction. Both components are imported in bulk in a clear plastic bag inside a carton box containing 1,500 pieces each. After importation, each nipple will be repackaged in a retail bag as an individual replacement nipple for a Farm Babies™ nursing bottle or repackaged in a retail bag with a Farm Babies™ nursing bottle and a screw cap as a complete nursing bottle. For the purpose of tariff classification, the feeding nursing nipples are considered hygienic articles.
