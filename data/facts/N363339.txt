The merchandise under consideration in this request are three versions of Littelfuse ESD suppressors,  identified individually as model numbers AXGD1, PGB1, and XGD. All three models are described as  one-channel, bidirectional polymer ESD protection devices designed for sensitive electronic equipment and  high-speed dataline applications. In use, the devices are surface mounted within electronic assemblies or  circuit board assemblies to suppress fast-rising electrostatic discharge transients while preserving signal  integrity and minimalizing data loss. All models are rated at a maximum DC voltage of 24V.
