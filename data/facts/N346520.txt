In lieu of samples, photos and  product descriptions were provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as “valentine foam heart and bead garland,” intended to be used as  decoration in the home. The garland is constructed of foam hearts and wooden beads (Maple wood) strung on  hemp rope with a ribbon and hemp rope tassel at each end. The garland features two pink, two red and two  purple foam hearts and twelve white, eight red and eight pink wooden beads. The item is available in  different colors. The garland measures approximately 30 inches in length.
