The subject merchandise, based on the submitted information, is a set top box, which is referred to as the “Explorer® Model 3250HD Settop Box.”  This non-screened device incorporates the following: a tuner that allows for both analog and MPEG-2 digital channels to be tuned for television display; a 32-bit/166 MIPS RISC microprocessor that allows for improved processing performance; and a DAVIC cable modem and reverse-path transmitter that enables this device to communicate instantly with the cable headend, thereby allowing the cable service provider to offer two-way services such as Internet browsing, video-on-demand (VOD), subscription-based VOD (SVOD), e-mail, and real-time chat. It is stated that this set top box will be packaged with a power cord, a coaxial cable, and a user’s guide, all of which, at the time of importation, will be put up in a manner suitable for sale directly to the user without repacking.
