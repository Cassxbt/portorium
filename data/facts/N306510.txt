Illustrative literature, a product description, and a sample were provided for review. Item 1, SKU # FB_UCSB, described as the “Canvas Basket with Eight Folding Flaps,” is a flat unfolded basket constructed of textile, velcro strips, and a 5mm fluted polypropylene plastic insert. Prior to the folding of the flaps, the basket measures 57” in length and 30.75” in width prior to the install of a metal wire frame surround (not included at the time of import). After a metal wire frame surround is inserted, the eight flaps fold onto the velco strips and securely hold the metal wire frame surround in place. Once folded and ready for use the canvas basket will measures 41” in length and 15” in width.
