The subject merchandise, “Taro Lime Flavor Chips,” is said to contain taro, palm oil, sugar, corn  maltodextrin, natural flavors, salt, citric acid, and malic acid. The product is presented in the form of thin  curled discs, similar in appearance to common potato chips. The “Taro Lime Flavor Chips” will be imported  in 16.93 ounce bags for retail sale. The “Taro Lime Flavor Chips” are said to be prepared by peeling taro root vegetables, then washing, cutting,  and slicing in preparation for frying. After frying into a crisp, the product undergoes sorting, seasoning, and  packaging for import into the United States.
