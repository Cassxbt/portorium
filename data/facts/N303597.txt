Descriptive information was included with your submission. Your inquiry covers the components of a split ductless air conditioner system. The products at issue are evaporator units and condensing units. The condensing units are installed outside of a home and are designed to be mounted on the ground or an outside wall. The pimary components of the condensing unit are a compressor, a fan, a motor, a capacitor and a coil. The evaporator unit, which primarily consists of a coil, a fan and a motor, is designed to be mounted to the wall of a home. Electrical lines and tubing through which refrigerant passes between the indoor and outdoor units connect the evaporator and condensing unit to each other.
