Descriptive literature was  provided for our review. The items under consideration are described as dolomite ceramic soap dispensers, which are devices designed  for dispensing liquid soap via a hand-activated piston pump mechanism. This mechanism includes a plastic  tube extending to the bottom of the dispenser to draw up the liquid soap. The dispensers are offered in  various designs, such as butterfly, mermaid, astronaut, and pirate ship, and have overall dimensions of  approximately 4.5 inches by 2 inches by 6.75 inches. Each dispenser will be secured in shape-fitting  polyfoam and subsequently packed in a cardboard box prior to shipping to the United States.
