The subject polymerization reactor, item number 1R231, is described as a continuously stirred reactor  designed for polymerizing ethylene. It is made of high-strength carbon steel and has a 33,099-gallon  capacity. The reactor features a jacketed design and three sets of internal cooling coils, which are used to  control the heat generated by the exothermic reaction. It is also equipped with thermal elements, which work  together with steam circulating in the reactor jacket to provide heat to initiate the reaction process. Lastly, the  reactor has an agitator assembly that mixes the reactants and catalysts. Headquarters (HQ) Ruling Letter 961365, dated June 24, 1998, reviewed a reactor vessel with a similar  configuration that was used to produce rubber latex emulsion. Based on the documents in your submission,  we find that the subject polymerization reactor is similar in both configuration and function to the reactor  reviewed in HQ 961365.
