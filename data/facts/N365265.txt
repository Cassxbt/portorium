The merchandise under consideration consists of two models of audio players, identified as the Verkada NP21 Two-Channel Network Audio Player (“NP21”) and the Verkada NP81 16-Channel Network Audio Player (“NP81”). The NP21 provides audio inputs and outputs to digitize audio for network routing and provides audio outputs from digital signals connected to an external amplifier. The NP21 includes an Ethernet port and two HDMI ports (with up to eight channels per port), analog audio ports for digitizing audio for further processing, and general purpose inputs and outputs to allow connectivity to other devices. The NP81 is a rack-mounted audio processor that routes audio data into, out of, and through a network. While functionally similar to the NP21, the NP81 has two HDMI output ports that can accommodate up to 16 channels, 8 analog audio input channels, and 16 analog audio output channels. Both audio players do not amplify audio themselves or record.
