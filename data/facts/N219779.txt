The merchandise under consideration is castors, mounting brackets and closet rods which are imported separately and designed for use in flexible Modular Closet Furniture Systems. Model number 2572-SC-US is described as Flex Shelf Brackets. These folded steel mountings are imported in pairs (left and right) and are designed to hold 1 inch Flex shelves. Each bracket incorporates two hooks designed to fit into corresponding slots in the Flex Telescopic Post. Model number 2698-SC-US is described as Flex Drawer Block Brackets. These folded steel mountings are imported in pairs (left and right) and are designed to hold Flex drawer boxes. Each bracket incorporates two hooks designed to fit into corresponding slots in the Flex Telescopic Post. Model number 5590-SC-US is described as a “Flex Set of 4 Casters.”  Each castor measures 60 mm in diameter and includes a plastic locking wheel with a steel stem and mounting plate. They and are used on the base of drawer boxes as an alternative to fixing the drawer boxes to the Flex Telescopic Post. Model number 2629-SC-US is described as a Flex Telescopic Closet Rod. It is an adjustable closet rod consisting of two telescoping steel tubes and two mounting brackets. Each bracket incorporates two hooks designed to fit into corresponding slots in the Flex Telescopic Post. Model number 2969-550-SC-US is described as a Flex Fixed Length Closet Rod. It consists of a steel tube and two mounting brackets. Each bracket incorporates two hooks designed to fit into corresponding slots in the Flex Telescopic Post.
