The merchandise under consideration is an industrial steel water cooling tower, model number OTTX-250L, with an induced-draft, cross-flow, and open-circuit design. It features a cold-water basin, a hot-water basin, and dual vertical fill banks mounted in a structural frame enclosed by panels. During its operation, hot process water is piped to the top of the unit, where it is distributed by gravity over PVC packing in the fill banks, which are located on opposite sides of the unit. At the top of the unit is an induced-draft fan assembly equipped with an axial air-foil fan. The fan draws ambient air into the unit, where it flows perpendicular to the falling water and then passes through drift eliminators before exiting the tower. As the water and air make direct contact, heat is transferred through evaporation. The cooled water collects in the cold-water basin at the base and returns to the process equipment. This unit has a cooling capacity of 975,000 kcal per hour and measures approximately 2.4 meters in length, 4.4 meters in width, and 3.19 meters in height.
