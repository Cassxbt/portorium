The first two samples consist of the Scoop Wireless Pointer (Model 1) and the Scoop Wireless Presenter (Model 4), which operate as radio-frequency remote controls powered by two AAA alkaline batteries. They are shaped and designed as point-and-click devices to enable users to navigate content and application choices on personal computers (PC’s). Model 1 is a PC/TV remote control for an HTPC (Home Theater PC), a PC, or a Mac connected to a television. It includes a scroll wheel and nine buttons. Model 4, which is a slight variation of Model 1, can operate as a presenter remote control. Both models have the capability of accessing slides of a PowerPoint presentation, with Model 4 having the subsidiary function of a laser pointer. Each remote control uses an accelerometer and a gyroscope to monitor its position.
