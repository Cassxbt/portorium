The subject merchandise consists of plain, unadorned wreaths constructed on circular metal rings and composed primarily of fresh evergreen foliage. Fresh evergreen branches are wrapped around the metal ring and secured with floral wire. The wreaths range in size from approximately 12 to 60 inches in diameter. By weight, the metal ring comprises approximately 5 percent of the finished wreath, while the remaining 95 percent consists of fresh evergreen foliage. At the time of importation, the wreaths contain no ornaments, ribbons, bows, lights, or other decorative embellishments. They are imported in their finished wreath form and require no further assembly. The wreaths are packaged in bales for transportation to the United States. Following importation, retailers open the bales and sell the wreaths individually in their plain condition. The wreaths are marketed, sold, and displayed by retailers as exterior Christmas decorations for use during the Christmas holiday season.
