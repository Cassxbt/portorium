Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The items under consideration are described as the FloPath series pressure and flow measuring instruments (model numbers PFM1, PFM1-A, and PFM1-T), which are pressure measuring devices engineered for specific monitoring requirements through a shared multi-channel, multi-file logging architecture. The PFM1 model serves as a decentralized data logger that utilizes 2MB of non-volatile flash memory to record up to eight independent data streams simultaneously. Since it lacks cellular connectivity, it operates silently in submersed environments, requiring field technicians to manually retrieve pressure data and update configurations via a non-contact USB-to-IrDA infrared link. The PFM1-A model retains this core pressure logging capability but enhances operational efficiency by integrating a long-range cellular telemetry module, which automatically transmits data packets over cloud-hosted platforms, thereby eliminating the need for manual intervention. Designed for advanced fluid dynamics analysis, the specialized PFM1-T model employs a “two-loggers-in-one” dual-speed architecture, which maintains standard slow-speed trend logging while simultaneously monitoring signals for threshold violations at 10Hz. Upon detecting a pressure anomaly, the device instantly activates a high-speed engine to capture transient events, such as water hammers, at burst rates of up to 1,000 readings per second, subsequently isolating these high-velocity profiles into separate files and triggering an immediate cellular alarm to notify operators of critical surges.
