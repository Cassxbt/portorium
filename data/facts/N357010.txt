Venlafaxine HCL, imported in 37.5 mg, 75 mg, and 150 mg extended-release capsules, is in a class of  medications called selective serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors (SNRIs). It is indicated for the  management of major depressive disorder (MDD), generalized anxiety disorder (GAD), social anxiety  disorder (SAD) and panic disorder (PD).
