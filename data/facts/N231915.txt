We are retaining your samples. Sample 1 is a back cover for an iPhone device. It is constructed of rigid molded plastics and is designed to snap onto the back of an iPhone. The cover is essentially flat with curved sides to enclose the two long edges of the device. The cover incorporates a small oval cut-out for the camera lens and one of the curved sides incorporates a cut-out to provide access to the volume controls. The cover does not have transverse sides to protect the short edges of the phone, and it does not extend over any portion of the front of the device, leaving the entire face and two sides of the device completely exposed. The cover measures approximately 4.5” (L) x 2.25” (W) x 0.50” (D). Samples 2 and 3 are iPhone cases constructed of non-rigid molded plastic. They are specifically shaped and designed to contain an iPhone device. The four sides of each case curve around the front of the device enclosing it within the case. They have several cut-out openings to provide access to the side ports and camera lens. The cases provide storage, portability, and protection to an iPhone device and measures approximately 2.5” (W) x 4.5” (L) x 0.50” (D).
