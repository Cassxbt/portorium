The submitted sample of a  woman’s shoe was examined and will be returned as requested. Style 786707 is a woman’s casual flat shoe that does not cover the ankle. The submitted laboratory report  shows the external surface of the upper (esau) consists of more than ninety percent rubber or plastics. A  long, thin, elastic lace is threaded through eight pairs of metal eyelets located on the sides of the vamp  opening. The lace lies on the skin and is meant to be wrapped and tied around the lower half of the leg. It  makes up less than ten percent of the esau. The outer sole is one hundred percent rubber/plastics and the shoe  does not have a foxing or foxing-like band.
