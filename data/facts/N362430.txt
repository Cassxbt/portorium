Three products are under review, called Whole Moon Organic Cultured Pistachio® yogurt alternatives, and  described as organic, plant-based, dairy-free, vegan products. The items are imported ready for consumption,  packaged in five-ounce cups and twelve to a shipping case. It will be marketed in the United States only for  retail sale. The Strawberry Cream contains pistachio ‘milk’ made of water and pistachios, soybeans, coconut meat,  strawberries, tapioca starch, cane sugar, pectin, natural flavors, and probiotic cultures. The Golden Mango contains pistachio ‘milk’ made of water and pistachios, soybeans, coconut meat, mango,  tapioca starch, cane sugar, pectin, natural flavors, lemon juice concentrate, and probiotic cultures. The Maple Cinnamon contains pistachio ‘milk’ made of water and pistachios, soybeans, coconut meat,  tapioca starch, maple syrup, cane sugar, pectin, natural flavors, lemon juice concentrate, cinnamon, and  probiotic cultures.
