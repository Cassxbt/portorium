The item under consideration has been identified as a Tensioner Assembly-Timing Chain (Item Code 244102E000). In your request, you state that the Tensioner Assembly-Timing Chain is used to manually open and close the air intake and exhaust valves of an automobile engine. The cam shaft is operated by the timing chain. With the continued use of the timing chain, it is gradually stretched, and the opening and closing time of the valve will be changed accordingly. Therefore, to prevent this from happening, the tensioner is needed to control the tension automatically. By controlling the tension of the timing chain, it performs a dampening function, maintaining the stretched chain stable during the operation by controlling the tension of the timing chain. The Tensioner Assembly-Timing Chain contains an aluminum housing, a locking pin and a tension unit. The tension unit consists of a steel housing, a spring, a rachet ring and a plunger.
