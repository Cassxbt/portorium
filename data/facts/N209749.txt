Per the description on the retail package, this Model 828 “combines the features of the traditional Sliding T-bevel and a Digital Protractor,” and it is “ideal for measuring, transferring and storing angle measurements when doing fine carpentry…”The main body is a rectangular handle with a metal straight edge that the can be swiveled and slid along a slot. It will be placed against one piece of work and then the straight edge will be secured in position so that its outside edge can be used later to mark out the desired, matching line on a piece to be cut. The angle made by the blade to the handle is displayed in degrees on a digital readout so it is not necessary to place the T-bevel over a protractor to determine the size of the angle, if desired.
