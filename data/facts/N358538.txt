The sample, SKU#JDN45, described as an “American Girl Hangtag Fastener,” is a textile string fastener  designed to attach a paper hang tag onto a doll carrier. During the injection molding process, the braided yarn and the 100  percent polystyrene plastic components are joined together. The completed fastener measures 5 ½ inches in  length by ¼ inches in width and features a plastic pin on one side which is inserted into a square plastic lock  ing mechanism on the other end to secure the fastener.
