On September 13, 2010, you electronically requested a tariff classification ruling. The ruling was requested for three gift bows, Style A, Style B (identified by product number SH-62/3C), and Style C (product number No: 4(9)). Samples of each bow have been reviewed. Style A, “3 Curly Bow”, is a retail unit of three gift bows, each consisting of ten bundled strips of curled polypropylene (PP) and polyethylene terephthalate (PET) ribbons. The strips measure 6mm wide and approximately 30” long in their uncurled state. Each curled gift bow measures approximately 6” in diameter. The ribbons are taped together at their centers and stapled to an adhesive-backed paperboard card, used to attach the bow to a gift. Style B (SH-62/3C), “3 Curly Bow”, is also a retail unit of three gift bows, each consisting of nine bundled strips of PP and PET ribbons. The ribbons are metallic and have a holographic, confetti-like surface effect. The ribbons are curled and crimped at 4mm intervals to give a geometric, 9-sided effect to the curls. The strips measure 6mm wide and approximately 27” long in their uncurled state. Each curled gift bow measures approximately 6” in diameter. The ribbons are taped together at their centers and stapled to an adhesive-backed paperboard card, used to attach the bow to a gift. Style C (No: 4(9)), is a single gift bow consisting of 19mm wide PP ribbon looped several times and stapled in the center to form a rosette. The bow is approximately 6” in diameter. Stapled to a ribbon loop at the center of the rosette is a die-cut, polyvinyl chloride (PVC) plastic, metallic decoration that states “Happy Birthday”. The decoration measures approximately 3” square. The rosette is stapled to an adhesive-backed paper rectangle, used to attach the bow to a gift.
