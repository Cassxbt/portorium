The products under consideration are identified as PEX plumbing fittings: 1) coupling (model # SSPEX-C,  SSCEP-C); 2) plug (model # SSPEX-P, SSCEP-P); 3) female adapter (model # SSPEX-FA, SSCEP-FA); and  4) male adapter (model # SSPEX-MA, SSCEP-MA). These fittings are made from type 304 stainless steel  and manufactured to ASTM F1807 and F1960 standards. Representative photos were included with your  submission. The coupling (model # SSPEX-C, SSCEP-C) has two barbed ends and is attached to a plastic PEX pipe on  each end. You indicate that the connection to the fitting is made with either a crimp ring, pinch clamp, or  cold expansion sleeve. The plug (model # SSPEX-P, SSCEP-P) is made from stainless steel raw bar. This barbed fitting closes the  plastic PEX pipe/tube aperture. The female adapter (model # SSPEX-FA, SSCEP-FA) has one barbed end that attaches to a plastic PEX pipe  and one internally threaded end that attaches to a metal pipe. The male adapter (model # SSPEX-MA, SSCEP-MA) has one barbed end that attaches to a plastic PEX pipe  and one externally threaded end that attaches to a metal pipe. The coupling, female adapter, and male adapter attach pipe made of a single extruded compound to pipe and  the plug closes the pipe/tube aperture. For tariff purposes, these products are considered pipe fittings.
