In lieu of samples, illustrative literature, a description, and an artist profile were provided for review. Sabastian Acker’s creation, the “Soft Power,” is a contemporary sculpture derived from a single length of 100% refined copper tubing. The sculpture’s initial creation process entails studying, bending, curving, positioning, repositioning, and further experimenting with rubber tube materials until a specific shape is achieved. The time sculpting resulted in a finished and polished copper tube that is no longer rigid or linear, but reflects asymmetrical sloping, and concave and convex curves. Documentation provided states, “I conceived the object and carried out the principal physical intervention myself: bending the copper tube. The resulting form was subsequently hand-finished.” The sculpture dimensions are approximately 26 cm x 65 cm x 11 cm and the artist estimates approximately 40 hours of work was employed from conception, experimentation, and creation of the piece.
