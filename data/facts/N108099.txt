The submitted sample is identified as an S/2 Sprinkler Hide-A-Key, SKU# 64560. This item consists of a two hollow cylindrical molded cases that are each made of acrylonitrile-butadiene-styrene (ABS) plastic material. Each case has a screw-on cap that is also made of ABS. The cap is molded to resemble the head of a sprinkler and the entire item has the appearance of a lawn sprinkler. The internal compartment is designed for the purpose of hiding keys on the grounds of a home. The item can also be used to hide small documents, notes or money. Each case measures approximately 4” in height with an internal compartment that measures approximately 2¾” in depth and 1½” in diameter.
