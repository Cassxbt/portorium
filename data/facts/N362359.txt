A  sample of the product was provided to this office and will be retained for training purposes. The imported item is an unfinished facial exfoliating cloth composed of two layers of 100 percent cotton  cheesecloth fabric. The two layers of fabric are treated with tannin pre-mordant, cut into 6 x 6-inch squares,  and sewn together with an overlock stitch. After importation, a charcoal dye is applied, and a decorative  zigzag border stitch is sewn around the edges. The finished product is sold as a facial exfoliating cloth under  the Romjay™ brand. The “So Polished Facial Cloth Refill,” SKU R-LE002, is sold at retail with two cloths. The “So Polished Facial Cloth Kit,” SKU K-LE001, is sold at retail with two cloths and a magnetic hanger.
