Drawings and descriptive literature were provided with your letter. The HearFones are non-electronic auditory feedback devices composed of plastics that are intended to enable a person to hear himself as others hear him. The device resembles a set of headphones with ellipsoidal plastic extensions projecting out from each earpiece. These extensions act as reflectors to focus and direct the voice into the ear canal by enhancing the feedback. The HearFones are said to help singers to practice and speakers to improve speech.
