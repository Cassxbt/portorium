A sample, ingredient information, and a description of the manufacturing process were received by this office on June 15, 2010. The sample was examined and disposed of. F.E. Chlorophyll is a green, aqueous solution said to contain 1.15 grams chlorophyll extract, 0.035 grams potassium sorbate, 0.011 grams sucralose, and 0.01 grams mint flavor. The label instructs the user to take two tablespoons of the solution twice daily with water or as directed by a health professional. The product is intended for use as a dietary supplement and is packaged for retail sale in 500 milliliter bottles.
