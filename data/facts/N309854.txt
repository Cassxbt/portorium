The first product, Nystatin Topical Powder, is an antifungal medication indicated for the treatment of cutaneous or mucocutaneous mycotic infections caused by Candida albicans and other susceptible Candida species. The second product, Fluocinonide Cream USP, 0.1% is a synthetic corticosteroid that is indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid responsive dermatoses in patients 12 years of age or older. The third product, Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Liposome Injection, imported in 20 mg and 50 mg, is a cytotoxic anthracycline antibiotic. It is indicated for the treatment of patients with ovarian cancer, AIDS-related Kaposi’s sarcoma, and in combination with bortezomib for multiple myeloma.
