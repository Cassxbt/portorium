The merchandise under consideration is described as industrial controllers that are used in the control and operation of automated guided vehicles (AGVs). The first item under consideration is identified as the mBox, which is a metal enclosure containing a power supply and a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) with various outputs and interconnection with a controller area network. The PCBA consists of a 1.6 GHz Intel Atom processor, 1 GB memory, two RJ-45 sockets, four RS-232 sockets, four USB sockets, and a DVI connector. The mBox has an embedded Linux operating system and is said to electrically control AGV vehicle functions such as driving, steering, handling, communication, and navigation. The second item under consideration is identified as the mBox-IO, which is described as a PCBA fitted with numerous terminal blocks and contacts, 21 Sinking inputs, 24 Sourcing (PNP) outputs, 3 incremental encoder channels, 6 analog differential inputs, and 3 Analog outputs 0-10V. Based on the information provided, the mBox-IO is an industrial controller for AGVs and electrically controls a number of different types of automated vehicles, such as forklifts and coil handling trucks. The third item under consideration is identified as the mBox-GO, which is described as a PCBA fitted with numerous terminal blocks, contacts, fuses, status LEDs, and power handling circuitry. Based on the information provided, the mBox-GO is an industrial power controller for AGVs that electrically controls and/or electrically protects a number of different types of automated vehicles, such as forklifts and coil handling trucks.
