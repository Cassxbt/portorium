The PICOMASTER Standard Series consists of five models of focused UV laser beam systems for  semiconductor lithography and microelectronic/nanofabrication applications: PICOMASTER Start100,  PICOMASTER100, PICOMASTER150, PICOMASTER200, and PICOMASTER350. The Standard Series  models employ a single focused laser beam to directly (i.e., without a photomask) draw or “write” patterns on  the nanometer scale onto semiconductor devices and other photosensitive substrate materials. The Standard  Series models are designed for and principally used in semiconductor manufacturing (mask creation, direct  writing on semiconductor materials), photonics (fabrication of optical components), LED manufacturing, flat  panel display patterning, MEMS/NEMS (fabrication of micro/nano-electromechanical systems),  microfluidics, and RF device component creation. The key components of the PICOMASTER Standard Series machines include: (1) main housing; (2) writing  module consisting of GaN laser diode source and high-precision optical components including beam shaping  optics; (3) positioning system (air bearing stages, linear motors, optical encoders with nanometer resolution);  (4) control electronics; (5) and environmental controls (HEPA filter, temperature and humidity control  systems).
