The first product, Acyclovir Tablets, is a medicinal preparation in 400 mg and 800 mg, containing Acyclovir, an antiviral, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of herpes zoster. The second product, Potassium Citrate Extended-Release Tablets, is a medicinal preparation, containing Potassium Citrate, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the management of renal tubular acidosis.
