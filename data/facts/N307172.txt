Illustrative literature and product descriptions were provided for review. ZT Systems, items RK-00185-006, RK-00175-010, and RK-00269-002, the “Server Equipment Racks,” are a series of cold-rolled, floor-standing, unequipped, steel storage cabinet racks on four (4) casters, measuring approximately 24” in width, 78” in height, 43” in depth, and weigh approximately 11 lbs. The cabinet racks are imported assembled, have a static capacity of 2,200 lbs., can sustain up to 3,130 lbs. of internal assembly, and have adjustable leveling feet for stability. The cabinet racks feature front and rear vented doors for airflow that are removable and lockable, however, at the time of importation the cabinet rack includes only the following:  a roof cable port, cover, roof cable trough, vertical position marks, and four (4) universal interior mounting brackets. The cabinet racks are designed to secure computer server equipment, transfer switches, power cables, power supplies, uninterruptable power supplies, shelves, rails, network cards, adapters, additional hardware components, and are used in large data centers.
