The items under consideration are pole climbing shoes, identified as “Gekko Pole-Climbing Shoes.” These items function as a pair (one left and one right shoe) and are designed to be attached to a user’s footwear enabling the user to climb wood or composite poles. The components of these shoes are made of various base metals and plastics with aluminum being the predominant material (84 percent). Each shoe has a foot mount on one end and two sets of spikes used to grip the pole being climbed. The foot mount consists of a rail with a heel plate, and a toe plate with a plastic strap that can be adjusted for securing various footwear sizes. One set of spikes is attached to the front inner toe so the user can grip the pole at the front from the feet, and the other set is attached to the end of a bent extruded metal bar extending from the foot mount. This bar is meant to bend out around the pole that is being climbed, so the spikes can grip the pole from opposite side of the user.
