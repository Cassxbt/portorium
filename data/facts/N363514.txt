Photographs and descriptive literature were provided for our review. The item under consideration is the “MOZA R3 Direct Drive Racing Simulator Kit (PC Version)” consisting  of a steering wheel, steering wheel base, pedals, desk clamp, power adapter and USB data cable. The kit is  imported into the United States in retail packaging and contains a complete set of racing game control  peripherals. It is designed to connect to a personal computer (PC) via a USB interface and operate racing  simulation games in conjunction with an external display (such as an HDTV or computer monitor). The kit is  not a standalone game console; it does not contain game content or a computing unit. Rather, it functions as  a dedicated input/output control device for the PC platform, designed to enhance the realistic driving  experience in racing games.
