Style “SUEDE HJNN283868-BLACK” is a textile fabric base featuring leather flocking for a suede-like  appearance designed for footwear uppers. The fabric consists of a polyethylene terephthalate (“PET”) knit  textile base coated with polyurethane (“PU”) and is then flocked with dyed black leather fibers on one side. Various fillers in low quantities, such as calcium carbonate, chromate, and pigment are also present. This  imitation suede leather fabric is marketed under the Earth® brand. Style “SUEDE HJNN282576-TAN17” is a textile base featuring leather flocking for a suede-like appearance  designed for footwear uppers. The fabric consists of polyethylene terephthalate (PET) knit textile base coated  with polyurethane (PU) and is then flocked with dyed tan leather fibers on both the front and back sides. Various fillers in low quantities, such as calcium carbonate, chromate, and pigment, are also present. This  imitation suede leather fabric is marketed under the Earth® brand.
