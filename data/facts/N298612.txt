You submitted a sample, identified as “Basket Filler & Decorative Garland,” Rite Aid item # 9044480, which consists of a length of extremely thin metal wire wrapped with metalized, polyvinyl chloride film and decorated with chick, bunny and flower shapes cut from thin plastic film that easily detach. Mere casual handling of the article in this office caused the insubstantial cut-out shapes to fall off from the wire, making long-term use of the article impractical. Although referred to as “basket filler,” the article is actually used as a disposable decorative garland thrown away after one-time use at a party. This is evidenced by examination of both the weak article itself with its feeble hanging shapes and websites selling comparable garlands, and your own citation of New York Ruling N030402 which classified a similar article with attached frail chick shapes as a throwaway garland and not as basket filler.
