The Ladle Blocks (LB1, LB2,LB3) are used to display a singleladle. The Spoon Racks (SSR6, SSR12, SSR24) are used to displaybetween six to twenty four spoons. The Cutlery Rack (CR12) isused to display a set of twelve spoons, knives or forks in anupright position. The Knife Racks (KR2, KR6) are used to displaycarving sets of knives and forks. The Hunter Watch Stands (WSH) are used to display pocket watches. These display items arecomposed of acrylic and will be used in museums and retailestablishments.
