An ingredients breakdown for the Quorn brand “Hot Dog Sausages 12 oz. 4’s” (A03059), and packaging samples for similar products were submitted with your letter. Additional information was provided on the quorn paste via email. The Hot Dog Sausages are an imitation meat product based on “mycoprotein,” a meat free protein. The Hot Dog Sausages are composed of quorn paste (mycoprotein), water, rapeseed (canola) oil, hot dog seasoning, albumen, pro fit QM1, flavoring, pectin and methyl cellulose. The Hot Dog Sausages will be imported in frozen condition, and packed for retail sale in containers.
