The product is said to be prepared by the selection and preparation of freshavocados, proceeded by the addition of ascorbic acid in small amounts during processing to minimizeoxidation and to preserve its natural color. The avocado undergoes freeze-drying to remove the water contentsubsequent to the dried material being mechanically milled into a powder. The resulting product is a finegreen powder with avocado organoleptic characteristics. The product is said to be intended for use as a readyto eat powder and a single-ingredient fruit product typically within snacks, baked foods, beverages, andprepared foods. The product, “Freeze Dried Avocado Powder,” is said to be free of carriers, fillers andpreservatives.
