An ingredients breakdown for the Quorn Turk’y Burger (A02851) and a packaging sample for similar products were submitted with your letter. The Turk’y Burger is an imitation meat product based on “mycoprotein,” a meat free protein. The Turk’y Burger is composed of mycoprotein, rehydrated egg white, wheat flour, fresh/frozen onions, canola oil, yeast extract, natural flavoring from non-meat sources, salt, tapioca starch, pectin, and potato maltodextrin. The burgers will be imported in frozen condition, and packed for retail sale in containers.
