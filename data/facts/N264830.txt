Photographs of the “Inflatable Stand-Up Paddle Board,” styles Zephyr and Vapor, were received with your inquiry. The inflatable paddle boards, which can be inflated to 20 psi, are constructed of a sturdy double-wall PVC with a top surface made of EVA to prevent slippage. When inflated, the Zephyr measures approximately 10’6” (L) x 30” (W) x 5” (D) and has a wider, more stable shape for less experienced riders whereas the Vapor has a more performance driven shape. They are designed to be used in the sport of paddle boarding in ocean surf as well as in lakes or rivers. The inflatable paddle boards are imported packaged together for retail sale with a plastic hand pump with hose and aperture and nylon bag.
