The item concerned is referred to as a strap cover, which is used in the production of Lightspeed’s Sierra  Aviation Headset. The applicable SKU number is 250-00023-000.B2 - Assy, Strap Cover. This strap cover assembly is used in the production of the headband of the Sierra aviation headset. This part is made from ABS-PC - A  thermoplastic blend of polycarbonate (PC) and acrylonitrile-butadiene- styrene (ABS).
