The merchandise under consideration is Andino White stone. A sample was submitted with your ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This analysis has been completed. The Andino White is a polished natural stone which is mottled with white and gray, and speckled with flecks of brown. It is designed for both indoor and outdoor applications, including countertops, backsplashes, surfaces, floors, and landscaping features. From the information you provided, upon importation it is polished and already cut to its final measurements of 126 centimeters long by 76 centimeters wide by 3 centimeters thick; it will not be further cut or worked after importation. In your ruling request you state that the stone which comprises the Andino White is granite. However, laboratory analysis has determined that it is not granite; it is a polished other stone.
