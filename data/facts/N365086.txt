The products in question are Hydrapro thermal expansion tanks, models HPTET2 (2 gallon) and HPTET45 (4.5 gallon). The expansion tanks are constructed of a welded steel shell and installed near a water heater to absorb thermally expanded heated water. This allows a place for the heated water to expand out of the water heater and not exert undue pressure within the potable water plumbing system. The expansion tanks consists of two chambers separated by a rubber diaphragm. Water enters on one side of the diaphragm while air is on the other. As water is heated in the water heater tank, the water expands, creating pressure. The hot water then travels to the thermal expansion tank to be temporarily held. This helps alleviate pressure build up in the potable water plumbing system. The water presses on the diaphragm, compressing the air. When water cools and the water pressure drops, the air pressure pushes on the diaphragm, mechanically forcing the water back into the plumbing system to be used.
