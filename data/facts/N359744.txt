Images were provided in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described as the “SWASH” (Squeegee With Applicator Sponge and  Handle). It is intended to be used to clean the user’s vehicle. It consists of two primary components, a  plastic bucket with a carrying handle, and a squeegee/sponge with an aluminum handle. The lid for the  bucket is attached to the squeegee/sponge handle as these two items are designed to be fitted together.
