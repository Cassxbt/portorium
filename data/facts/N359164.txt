Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is a decorative plaque constructed of medium density fiberboard (MDF) and  plywood. The various styles of plaque measure from 12” x 9” to 30” x 24”. The back panel is constructed of  MDF, and laser-cut poplar plywood pieces are glued onto the front of the panel. The plaque is hand-painted  and may be laser-engraved. Each plaque is personalized; the sample photo shows the silhouette of a couple  next to a tree with several cut leaf shapes. There are also laser-cut names, “Matthew and Loretta April 19,  2025”. The plaque is imported with two permanent markers with which individuals can write messages on  the plaque, and a plywood stand to hold the plaque upright on a shelf.
