The busbar is not externally insulated in a manner that would prevent leakage ofelectric current from the conductor or prevent it from damage. For enhanced cooling functionality, the busbarincludes two internal copper tubes which connect to the cooling system of a rack-based power system via an EPDM hose fitted with Push-Lok type fluid fittings and UQD-style stainless steel liquid quick disconnects.The busbar is enclosed in a galvanized steel cage with plastic spacers to prevent an electrical short at eachmounting location.
