A sample identified as a “Fuzzy 3D Puzzle” (Item # 3074, Unicorn) was submitted for our examination and is being returned to you as requested. It consists of an 11” x 15” sheet of partially flocked paperboard incorporating a number of die-cut, punch-out shapes representing unicorn body parts. The paperboard has been shrink-wrapped together with five markers (felt-tipped pens) in assorted colors. The user is encouraged to color the unflocked areas of the shapes, punch them out and assemble them into a three-dimensional unicorn figure. The paperboard pieces incorporate slots to facilitate interlocking them with one another in the proper configuration. The packaging includes illustrations providing a simple, step-by-step guide to assembly.
