PA6-imide is a polyamide based thermoplastic monofilament. It is an extruded monofilament having a solid, circular cross-section with a diameter of 1.75 millimeters. Documentation notes that the monofilament contains no reinforcements or filler. The monofilament will be imported on spools. The spools serve solely to hold and dispense the monofilament and contain no coding or identification circuitry. The principal use of the monofilament is feedstock for material extrusion additive manufacturing.
