The Pioneer is a compact electron beam lithography (“EBL”) system designed for high resolution patterning of semiconductor wafer materials. It is a direct electron-beam write-on apparatus for the drawing of circuit patterns onto wafers coated with sensitized semiconductor materials. This EBL/SEM (“scanning electron microscope”) hybrid system for these semiconductor nanostructures is capable of fabrication, i.e., lithography, as well as imaging (electron microscopy), metrology (measurements using laser interferometer stage), X-ray spectrometer and energy dispersive microanalysis. No specialized wafer lifting, handling, loading or unloading equipment is included. Customers supply their own wafer handling equipment based upon their own methods/devices. The turnkey EBL system is complete at time of importation.
