Images were provided in lieu of samples. The products under consideration are described as THALZY-brand artwork which are three-dimensional, wall-mounted, resin reliefs. The principal component is an epoxy plastic resin relief with sculptural wave-like forms. Each piece may have different coloring, three-dimensional geometries, proportions, contours, and relief profiles. The final surface includes a manually applied glossy epoxy resin layer. The finished reliefs are manually mounted to an aluminum composite panel and installed into a wooden frame. They may range in size from approximately 10 centimeters by 10 centimeters up to 30 centimeters by 30 centimeters, excluding the wooden frame.
