The items under consideration are described as battery chargers for Freestyle Comfort Oxygen Concentrators,  which are identified by model numbers BT036-1 and BT036-2. Both battery chargers are designed to charge  the 8-cell or 16-cell lithium-ion battery pack (13,400mAh) proprietary to the Freestyle Comfort Oxygen  Concentrator, which is a portable device for individuals with impaired respiratory functions due to chronic  diseases, disabilities or other medical conditions. They are both comprised of a 60W AC/DC adapter, battery  charging unit and a power cord. We note that the battery packs are not imported with the charger. The only  difference between the two models is the type of power cord connector. The BT036-2 has a power cord  connector compatible with European standard receptacles.
