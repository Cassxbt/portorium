The item under consideration is the Main Board, BH09, identified as part number CC0082, which is a  component of the Telesair Bonhawa respiratory therapy humidifier and is described as the humidifier’s  central processing control unit. In use, the main board is programmed to manage and coordinate core  therapeutic operations such as airflow regulation, temperature and humidity control, oxygen monitoring,  alarm processing, user interface communication, and system safety functions. The board receives signals  from integrated sensors and electrically controls a number of connected components such as the blower,  heater, and monitor subsystems.
