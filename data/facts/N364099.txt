Product information was submitted with your request. The items under consideration are three models of fully automated post-etch punch (PEP) machines (ATP-1000/3000E, ATP-7800, ECP-4000/8000). Using numerous cameras, the machines position etched printed circuit board (PCB) panels of varying size and thickness and punch holes at specified locations. The punched holes are used to align PCB layers during subsequent lay-up and lamination processes. In addition to punching, the machines provide automatic panel loading and unloading functions to eliminate operator intervention. You state that the machines will be used to punch PCB panels consisting of a thin conductive copper layer on one or both sides of a predominant core of non-conductive substrate such as woven fiberglass cloth impregnated with flame-retardant epoxy resin (FR-4), composite epoxy material (CEM), polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE), and polyimide. As you described, the machines may or may not punch through copper depending on the customer’s design, but they will always punch a core substrate. Notwithstanding, in instances the machines punch through copper and substrate, it will predominantly punch substrate since it is the dominant material. You clarify that the machines are capable of processing flexible PCB panels, but the most commonly processed panel will have a core of rigid FR-4 material. Accordingly, the machines will principally punch hard material during operation. The machines will feature a Windows 11 operating system user interface and will be numerically controlled.
