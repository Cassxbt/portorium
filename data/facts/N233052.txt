The first product, Escitalopram Oxalate, imported in 5 mg, 10 mg and 20 mg tablet form is a selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor (SSRI). It is indicated for the treatment of major depressive disorder (MDD). The second product, Telmisartan, imported in 20 mg, 40 mg and 80 mg tablet form is a non-peptide angiotensin II receptor (type AT1) antagonist. It is indicated for the treatment of hypertension. The third product, Pioglitazone Hydrochloride, imported in 15 mg, 30 mg and 45 mg is an oral anti-diabetic agent. It is indicated for the treatment of type 2 diabetes mellitus by decreasing insulin resistance.
