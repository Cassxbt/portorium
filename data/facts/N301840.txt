The four items at issue are tire valves, item numbers VHIE323, VHIE324-27, VHIE325-27, VHIE326-27 and VHIE327-27. Each hand-operated valve has a metal cap that seals and protects the valve mechanism when the tire valve is not in use, a threaded stem and a rubber mounting. The tire valves allow air to flow in or out of a tire once the stem of the valve has been depressed. The brass valves have a pressure rating of 448.15 kPa.
