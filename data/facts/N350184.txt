The products, “Brand’s Essence of Chicken,” and “Brand’s Essence of Chicken with Cordyceps Extract,” are  manufactured from chicken that is purchased from the USA. In Taiwan, the raw chicken is minced and  loaded into a pressure cooker. The coarse mincing and cooking and extraction process includes the addition  of water and the grounded poultry being cooked between temperatures of approximately 239-257 degrees  Fahrenheit for 4.5 to 8 hours. At the conclusion of the heating cycle, the interim product is sieved, which  removes all the meat and bones. The liquid fraction is further filtered mechanically to produce a clear liquid  essence of chicken drink by removing the fat fraction. The clear liquid is then concentrated through an  evaporator to remove excess water.
