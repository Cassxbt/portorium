Additional information was  provided via email dated January 14 and January 22, 2026. The products under consideration are unassembled I.V. flush syringes imported as complete kits, typically  packaged in bulk master cartons. Each kit contains all the necessary components for one syringe: a barrel, a  plunger, a grommet (stopper), and a tip cap. After importation, these kits are assembled into complete 12ml  syringes, which are then filled with 0.9 percent saline solution.
