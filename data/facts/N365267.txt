The merchandise under consideration is three models of network speakers, identified as Verkada Network Speakers, model numbers NS141/NS151, NS261, and NS631. These speakers are further described as follows: The Verkada NS141/NS151 Network Speaker is a network bookshelf speaker that can also be wall mounted with an accessory bracket. These Network Speakers include a microphone array with integrated signal processing. They do not include an integrated Ethernet switch or general-purpose port. The Verkada NS261 Network Speaker is a ceiling speaker that includes an integrated Ethernet switch and an additional Ethernet port into which other networked devices can be plugged. It also includes a general-purpose port, and a microphone array with integrated signal processing. The Verkada NS631 Network Speaker is a wall mounted indoor/outdoor network speaker with an integrated Ethernet switch and additional Ethernet port, a general-purpose port, and microphone array.
