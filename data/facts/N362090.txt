The Zenith jacquard fabric swatch has a fiber content of 60 percent  polyester and 40 percent PE coated with a TPU waterproof backing. Each fabric swatch measures 11 inches  in length by 7 ¾ inches in width and is hemmed on all four sides. Each fabric swatch is permanently glued to  the spine of the swatch book and beneath each swatch is printed information identifying the fabric’s functions  and performance details. The completed swatch book measures 12 ½ inches in length by 9 ½ inches in width  by ¾ inches in thickness and features a front and back cover and three interior pages made of color art paper.
