The products under consideration are five articles used in store fixtures and displays. All articles are made of steel. Item “STT50 with Addon” is a floor standing unit comprised of vertical and horizontal components, that when assembled, form a “T” shaped structure onto which various configurations of shelves, hooks, and other fixtures (not included) can be mounted for the display of merchandise. When assembled, the unit measures 50 inches in height. “KHD185 Wall Standard” is a wall standard that measures ¼-inch wide and is available in 8 foot and 10 foot lengths. It is designed to mount to a wall with screws or other fasteners. Along its length are evenly spaced slots that allow for the attachment of shelf brackets, hooks and similar fixtures. “KHD702-U Bar” is a “U” shaped component that is available in 24 inch, 30 inch and 40 inch widths. Notched tabs at each end of the U Bar enable it to mount without fasteners to either the STT50 or the KHD702. It serves as a support for a shelf, hooks or other fixtures. “HDS Shelf” is a rectangular shelf with notched tabs at each end that enable it to mount without fasteners to either the KHD185 Wall Standard or the STT50. The HDS Shelf measures 14 inches deep and is available in 24 inch, 30 inch and 48 inch lengths. “K106 Hook” is a hook with an attachment at the end that enables it to mount to the KHD702-U Bar. It is available in 4 inch, 6 inch, 9 inch and 12 inch lengths.
