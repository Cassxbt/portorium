The merchandise under consideration is a used Yanmar self-propelled mini hydraulic excavator, model  number Vio45-6A. The excavator has a mechanical arm engineered for a backhoe bucket attachment, a mini  dozer blade, and an operator cab with an open canopy. The upper structure, which includes the operator cab  and mechanical arm, can rotate 360 degrees and is mounted on a rubber track undercarriage. The excavator  has a maximum digging force of 3,220 kilograms, a maximum digging depth of 3,740 millimeters, and an  operating weight of 4,800 kilograms.
