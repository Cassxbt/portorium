In your submission, the subject product is identified as Fabric Refresher Spray. It is described as a surface-  active auxiliary washing preparation for textile substrates. It contains an organic nonionic surfactant (Laureth  -3) and a chemical neutralizer to penetrate fibers, emulsify soils, and eliminate odors within the fabric matrix. This product will be imported in 22 FL OZ retail-ready plastic spray bottles. You state that there is specific chemical synergy between two primary components of the product’s chemical   mixture: Laureth-3 and Sodium Bicarbonate. Laureth-3 is a non-ionic surface-active agent, which serves as  the primary vehicle for this preparation. This surfactant actively reduces the surface tension of the aqueous  solution. This mechanical reduction allows the liquid preparation to deeply wet and penetrate the tight,  porous weave of textile or carpet fibers. Once inside the fiber matrix, the Laureth-3 physically emulsifies and  suspends hydrophobic soils and organic particulates trapped within the substrate. By lowering the surface  tension, the laureth-3 enables the sodium bicarbonate to physically reach deep-seated, trapped malodor  molecules. Most stubborn fabric odors are composed of volatile organic acids. The sodium bicarbonate acts  as a chemical neutralizer, undergoing an acid-base reaction that converts these volatile compounds into  non-volatile, odorless salts.
