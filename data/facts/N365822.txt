The “Upholstered Queen Bed (item 1862201),” is an upholstered bed frame that will support a mattress without the use of a box spring. The bed frame is constructed of wood (solid rubberwood, plywood panels, medium-density fiberboard (MDF), poplar), textile fabric, and foam materials. The headboard and the footboard of the bed frame are upholstered and have visible wooden rail surrounds; the left and the right side rail panels and the four deck board foundation panels are not upholstered. The bed frame dimensions are 64.1" wide, 86.7" deep, and 45" high.
