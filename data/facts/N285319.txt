Additional information was submitted, which included color photos and a limited material breakdown table. Part number 416-00-881-02-01 is described as an “Arm Cap” for an aircraft seat. The “Arm Cap” is not the full armrest, and as its name indicates, it is the top surface component of an armrest onto which one places their arm. The “Arm Cap” consists of polyurethane, formed on a metal insert made of aluminum, covered in leather. On the underside of the “Arm Cap” there are four bored-out holes that are used to secure the component to the armrest.
