You state that the instant items are four inorganic mixtures that are manufactured by blending different types  of pozzolan with pulverized limestone. These raw materials are mixed at the Graymont (QC) ‘s site and are  then shipped to the consignee. You state the four instant items do not have cementitious value but will  chemically react with calcium hydroxide in the presence of water to form compounds that possess  cementitious properties. You state the four items have the following product designations:  1) GRAYBOND 4BF  2) GRAYBOND 80E  3) GRAYBOND 242A  4) GRAYBOND 242C  GRAYBOND 4BF is said to consist of Fillcoat #10 Boiler Ash Pozzolan, 3M™ Natural Pozzolan NP100W,  and pulverized limestone. GRAYBOND 80E is said to consist of quicklime, ground glass pozzolan, and pulverized limestone. GRAYBOND 242A is said to consist of quicklime, volcanic ash pozzolan, and pulverized limestone. GRAYBOND 242C is said to consist of quicklime, metakaolin-kaolin and other kaolinic clays pozzolan, and  pulverized limestone.
