The item under consideration has been identified as a 1996 Toyota HiLux 4 full door pickup, frame number LN107-0024711. This vehicle is equipped with a 3-liter (2,779 cc) diesel engine, and interior seating for five (5) passengers with two (2) foldable rear facing jump seats in the open cargo area. In your request, you suggest classification in 8703.32.0150, which provides for, “Motor cars and other motor vehicles principally designed for the transport of persons…: Other vehicles, with only compression-ignition internal combustion piston engine (diesel or semi-diesel): Of a cylinder capacity exceeding 1,500 cc but not exceeding 2,500 cc: Used”. This office disagrees.
