In lieu of samples, technical and illustrative literature, a product description, and a manufacturing synopsis were provided for review. “EVENaBAG” is a combination travel bag with storage compartments and a portable seat that is constructed of Cordura textile, leather, a pair of fiberglass rods, and sewn-in foam padding. The bag has two large main compartments for storage and organization of various items, accommodates a laptop with a screen size of up to 16 inches, and is equipped with shoulder straps and a shoulder pad. The leather elements are located on the bag’s flap, base, and the storage compartment. The pair of fiberglass rods and the foam padding support the shape and stability of the backrest. The shoulder pad and straps allow the user to carry the bag over the shoulder, across the body, or as a backpack. A zipper pocket is located at the base which stows a mat that unfolds and extends for seating. The mat provides portable seating for outdoor recreation such as picnics, camping, hunting, fishing, beach, lakes, concerts, hiking, and sporting events. The dimensions approximate 14.96” in length, 14.7” in width, 5.11” in depth, weights 3.75 lbs.
