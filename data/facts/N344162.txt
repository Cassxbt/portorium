Descriptive and pictorial information were submitted. The Women’s Kelmarsh Slip On – style number P31257 is a vulcanized rubber slip-on shoe. It is a below the ankle shoe, which has an open heel, and a closed toe. It is not considered protective against water, oil, grease or chemicals or cold or inclement weather. The outer sole unit is said to have a low heel and an 8mm sidewall that overlaps the upper, constituting a foxing-like band.
