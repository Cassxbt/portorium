The merchandise under consideration is referred to as a battery module subassembly, part number  260-00061-000.A6, and is described as a battery for the control box of an aviation headset. The assembly  consists of a printed circuit board assembly and a single 18650 lithium-ion battery cell enclosed in a  protective plastic housing. The protective housing is designed to fit the control box body and align with its  electrical contacts. The battery has a nominal voltage of 3.7 VDC and a nominal capacity of 2,600 mAh.
