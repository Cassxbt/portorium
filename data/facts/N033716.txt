The submitted sample is identified as a soap dispenser with Magic Loc suction system, VPN# 18221100. This item is designed to contain and dispense liquid soap or shampoo, neither of which will be included with the importation. The submitted sample is labeled with the word “shampoo.”  The container is made of an acrylic plastic material. It is cylindrical in shape, has a frosted appearance and is designed to be attached to a wall by means of a removable suction cup that is made of natural rubber. The bottom of the container is attached to a base made of a molded plastic material that has a chrome-look finish. The suction cup is attached to the back of the base and a tip made of polypropylene (PP) plastic material is attached to the bottom of the base. This item is not spring loaded and does not operate by any mechanical process. It does not contain a valve. Soap is released when the tip is either squeezed or pushed back against a small piece of molded plastic. This action forces the soap out through a slit at the bottom of the tip. The submitted sample is contained in a retail box that has an illustration that depicts this item with a top lid that is also made out of a molded plastic with a chrome-look finish. However, the lid was not included with the submitted sample. The item measures approximately 8 inches high without the lid. The tip measures approximately 1-3/4 inches in height.
