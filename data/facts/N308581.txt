The merchandise under consideration is identified as the SimplyGo Rotor Part # 1101863, which is described as an electric rotor that is said to be used exclusively with the SimplyGo Portable Oxygen Concentrator (POC). The subject rotor is comprised of a dual output shaft, which allows the SimplyGo portable oxygen concentrator to run a dual cylinder compressor, and is of a specific configuration for use with the specialized POC motor stator. We would note that this ruling pertains only to the rotor and the stator is not subject to this review.
