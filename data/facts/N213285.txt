An ingredients breakdown for the Quorn Meat free & Soy free Meatballs (A02907) and a packaging sample for similar products were submitted with your letter. The Meatballs are an imitation meat product based on “mycoprotein,” a meat free protein. The Meatballs are composed of mycoprotein paste, water, meatless mince, albumen, textured wheat protein, onions, rice starch, natural flavoring from non-meat sources, corn maltodextrin, salt, sugar, glucose syrup, gum arabic and silicon dioxide. The meatballs will be imported in frozen condition, and packed for retail sale in containers.
