The item concerned is a bare printed circuit board (PCB) referred to as the “Helix System PCB” (#1127968+R5). This specialized PCB is designed, manufactured and used exclusively with the Respironics’ Trilogy Evo Ventilator devices. The Trilogy Evo ventilator provides continuous or intermittent positive pressure ventilation for the care of individuals who require mechanical ventilation. The Trilogy Evo ventilator is intended for pediatric through adult patients. The “Helix System PCB” is a custom-made/designed, six layer bare printed circuit board specifically used for controlling the Trilogy Evo ventilator. The printed wiring pattern of this PCB was made specifically to accommodate the electrical elements needed/used to create the finished printed circuit board assembly. Given the highly custom nature of the board, it has no use other than in the assembly of a Trilogy ventilator. The “Helix System PCB” is imported unpopulated and then populated in the U.S.
