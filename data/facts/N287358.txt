The product at issue is described as a plastic sprayer pump, used to disperse cleaning solution from a separately imported eight ounce bottle. Per the photo included with your submission, the plastic sprayer dispenses liquids and serves as the closure for the bottle with which it is used. The product is a simple piston-pump spraying appliance that operates through the repeated pumping action of the operator’s finger. Based on the information provided, the plastic sprayer is similar in form and function to the fine mist sprayer covered by New York Ruling Letter N007732, dated March 22, 2007.
