The request concerns two models of motorized dumpers designed for off-highway applications such as  construction, mining, and grounds maintenance. They are low-speed vehicles intended for hauling debris,  rocks, dirt, and similar materials short distances at low speeds in rough terrain. The first item under consideration has been identified as an AUSA articulated dumper, model # D601AHG,  manufactured by JLG. You state that the dumper body is made of ASTM 572 Grade 50 and AISI 4140 steel  in thicknesses ranging from 3 – 12 mm. The dumper body is designed to offer protection to the cab/driver, in  that the highest wall of the dumper body faces the driver. This model also has an additional panel extension  for further protection. The dumper body is designed so that the floor slopes upward toward the rear. Model  D601AHG may be ordered with, or without, a cab. You claim that when a cab is installed, it is half the width  of the dumper body. Model D601AHG possesses the following features:  Axles bolted directly to the frame  No suspension components  Hydrostatic brakes  Heavy-duty hydraulic service brake  Parking brake designed to ensure reliable holding on slopes  Maximum speed 15.5 mph. Earth-moving construction tires  Tare weight/payload ranges from 1.4 to 1:1.26 (depending on whether the product has a cab or an  unenclosed driver's seat) (4,360-4,755 kgs.)  Maximum payload 6,000 kgs. (13,227 lbs.) 

The second item under consideration has been identified as an AUSA articulated dumper, model #  D350AHG, manufactured by JLG. The dumper body is designed to offer protection to the driver,  in that the highest wall of the dumper body faces the driver. The dumper body is designed so that the floor  slopes upward toward the rear.
