The articles under consideration are semi-finished airbag inflator tubes, Part Numbers CTL_T921A,  CTL_V910A, CTL_V677A, CTL_V678A, CTL_R671A, CTL_V690D, and CTL_C255B. They each consist  of a seamless steel tube that is cut to a specified length and incorporates an inner chamfer at each end. The  tube has an outside diameter ranging from 20 mm to 40 mm and is manufactured for further processing into  an automotive airbag inflator. Each end is chamfered specifically to match its corresponding mating  component on the inflator.
