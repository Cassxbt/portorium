The product at issue is identified as a simulator frame (alternatively described as a motion platform). The simulator frame, composed of a steel base combined with molded plastic components and other elements, measures 80" long by 29" wide by 53" high. The product is said to serve as a frame for a variety of heavy construction equipment training simulators (such as excavators, backhoes, loaders, bulldozers, etc.). The simulator frame features inbuilt electronics and a series of vertically installed actuators that can deliver three axes of motion to the frame when it is combined with the other components of the complete simulator (computer, video screens, controls, etc., which are not imported with the frame). When the complete simulator is in use, the electronics in the frame interface with the simulator’s computer to control the motion of the actuators in response to the actions taken by the user during a given training scenario.
