Illustrative literature and description were provided. Costco item number 1650063 is described as the “Aluminum Framed Zero Gravity Outdoor Lounger.”  The merchandise concerned is composed of a tubular aluminum frame having a powder coating, onto which 20mm foam padding encased in 100% Polyester fabric is attached. The filled fabric is constructed in the 1-piece and runs down nearly the entire length of the lounger. The arm rests, cup holder and phone holder are comprised of plastic and the side table is made of a composite aluminum panel. This item has full lounge capabilities, as well the ability to fold into a carrying position. Consistent with New York ruling N280111 dated November 7, 2016 and New York ruling N281624 dated December 16, 2016, this office is of the opinion that the “Aluminum Framed Zero Gravity Outdoor Lounger” is not principally or predominately used in one’s household, but rather for outdoor recreational activities. Although purchased by one’s household, it is not principally used within the confines of one’s home.
