The subject merchandise identified as series AR2-28, BIT25, BD1-28, and BR1-28, are multi-purpose electronic real time defrost controllers for use in cooler/freezer applications. Series AR2-28 and BIT25 each have a power supply rating of 230Vac and feature an LCD display unit. Series BD1-28 and BR1-28 each have a power supply rating of 100-240Vac and feature an LED display unit. The documentation provided show these devices to be programmable using panel buttons or via an RS485 connection in conjunction with “ZOT”. The internal printed circuit board assembly contains the microprocessor, and memory to store the operating instructions, necessary to independently manage the cooler/freezer compressors, fans, alarms, or other devices connected to the controller’s outputs. The subject controllers are intended to regulate/control the internal temperature and defrost cycles in a cooler/freezer by receiving temperature inputs and data from a thermostat or other attached sensor apparatus. They also control the operation of the compressor and evaporator fan, as well as several alarm sources such as: temperature, door, and condenser high temperature.
