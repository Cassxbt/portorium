Four samples were submitted with your request. Style Buster is a woven fabric composed of yarns of different colors. Your letter indicates that the fabric has a very thin coating of latex on the reverse side to facilitate cutting; however, that coating is not visible to the naked eye. The fabric is woven with a complex weave, giving the surface the appearance of a basket weave. U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) laboratory analysis indicates that this fabric is of a woven construction other than plain, twill or satin weave, with a composition by weight of 100% textured polyester filament, and weighs 334.9 g/m2. Your correspondence indicates that this fabric will be imported in widths of 150-152 centimeters, and will be used for upholstery. Style Song (Light Version) is a bonded fabric consisting of a woven face fabric laminated to a plain weave backing fabric. The adhesive that bonds these fabrics together is not visible in the cross section. The face fabric is characterized by its rough textured yarns of different colors of various widths. CBP laboratory analysis indicates that the face fabric is of a woven construction other than plain, twill or satin weave, composed wholly of textured filament polyester yarns and weighs 270.4 g/m2. The plain weave backing fabric is composed wholly of textured polyester filament yarns and weighs 37.7 g/m2. The bonded fabric weighs a total of 311.7 g/m2. Your correspondence indicates that this fabric will be imported in 148 centimeter widths, and will be used for upholstery.
