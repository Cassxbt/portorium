The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Cable, Lower U-174 PFX, part number  300-00019-000.A1, and Cable, Lower, U-174 Plug, part number 300-00004-000.C3. Both cables consist of a  single cable with a 5POS connector on one end and the U-174 audio plug on the other end. The cables are  both used in the assembly of Lightspeed’s aviation headsets. The 5POS connector will be permanently  attached to the control box of the headset and the U-174 connector will plug into the audio jack of helicopter  intercoms. The U-174 plugs are used specifically for helicopters to transmit only voice signals.
