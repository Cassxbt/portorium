The sample submitted, Crazy Foam, is a foam surfactant in a dispenser. It consists of a cylindrical container which is 9 inches long (including push button) and 2 1/8 inches in diameter with foam surfactant under pressure and plastic head of a submarine diver mounted atop of the container. A push on the plastic head at the top of the container releases the foam surfactant through a small orifice in the mouth of the plastic head.
