The merchandise under consideration is identified as the RIC2Discover System, which is designed to provide  feed and water to cattle. It also records the feed and water intake per animal using RFID identification and  integrated weighing technology. You have stated that the system will be used on research farms, universities,  and dairy and beef farms that work with feed companies, genetic companies or pharmaceuticals companies. The complete system will be imported with the following components:  18 stainless steel feed-weigh troughs with RFID gates. 2 water-weigh troughs. Control system that includes management software, process controllers, interface cables, and zone  units. Mounting poles, brackets, and an installation kit.
