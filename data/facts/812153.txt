A sample of "Word Seek 41" was submitted with your     inquiry. The article is a printed puzzle book consisting of     320 word search puzzles and solutions. The object of word     search is to find and circle specific words that are located     within a mass of jumbled letters. The words may be positioned     to read vertically, horizontally or diagonally. In addition     to the conventional form of reading from left to right the     words may be read from right to left, top to bottom and vice     versa.
