The items under consideration include the F2 Door Assembly, F2 Coolant Trough Assembly, F2 Coolant  Intake Assembly and F2 Basin Assembly, which are subassemblies of a compact dental milling machine,   Fastmill.io (addressed under a separate cover in NY N361752). A separate ruling will address the F2 LED  Assembly. As you noted in your submission, all of these parts do not have independent functions outside of the milling  system and are used exclusively for the operation, control or mechanical functioning of the dental milling  machine. The F2 Door Assembly is a subassembly of the dental milling machine. It consists of a plastic door that  encloses the cutting area and features a window for viewing the restoration process. The door mounts to a  multi-piece sheet metal frame, which provides proper alignment and includes features for attaching to guide  rails during final assembly. The F2 Coolant Trough Assembly is a subassembly of the dental milling machine that serves as the main  reservoir for the coolant. The coolant recirculates through the machine to cool and lubricate the grinding  process during dental restoration production. The assembly includes two baskets with mesh screens to filter  larger particulates at the coolant entry and exit points. A small metal “flag” interacts with another assembly  to detect when the trough is properly inserted. The assembly consists of four injection-molded plastic  components and one sheet metal component. The F2 Coolant Intake Assembly is a subassembly of the dental milling machine used to monitor the coolant  level and draw coolant from the trough, serving as the intake for the coolant recirculation system. It features a 

linkage system that mechanically connects to the coolant trough drawer, allowing the level sensor and intake  tube to move in or out of the trough as the drawer is opened or closed. This subassembly consists of multiple  sheet metal, plastic injection, and buyout components. The F2 Basin Assembly is a subassembly of the dental milling machine that contains the basin, where the  spindle and dental material interact and dental restorations are created. It includes a sheet metal housing that  secures the basin and mounts it to the main structure of the machine, as well as seals to prevent the coolant  from escaping during grinding. The assembly also incorporates belts and pulleys for opening and closing the  door that covers the large front opening of the basin.
