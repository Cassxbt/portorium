Photographs of the item were submitted with your request. This ruling is part of a split ruling request. The other portion of the request was assigned Ruling Number N365065. This ruling covers only the Bandaby Hamster Home. The Bandaby Hamster Home is an A-frame enclosure for small hamsters. The item is available in three model numbers: BND-HH-TC (GTIN 4270005471730) with a light terra cotta exterior color, BND-HH-CG (GTIN 4270005471754), with a mint green exterior color, and BND-HH-PG (GTIN 4270005471778), with an off white and pale grey exterior color. When fully assembled, the hamster home measures approximately 47.24 inches long by 27.56 inches high by 23.62 inches wide. The hamster housing consists of an base panel, right and left side panels, middle and bottom back beams, top and bottom beams, bottom beam welding brackets, window bar, and door handles. All of which are made from extruded aluminum with an Aluminum Association Series Designation (AASD) of 6063. The front and back panels and the door are made of Polymethylmethacrylate (PMMA). The hardware includes steel hinges, steel screws, steel cap nuts, rubber foot pads, and two magnets.
