The merchandise under consideration are the 29-109-01 4 Pin Target Assembly and the 29-110-02 4 Pin Plunger Assembly. They are a mated pair of connectors designed to connect the wiring harness in the sliding door of mini-vans or similarly equipped motor vehicles with the vehicle’s main wiring harness. The 4-Pin Target Assembly is a female connector designed for installation in the door post of a vehicle body. It is comprised of a plastic housing encasing four brass electrical contact pads, each with its own terminal pin which extends out the back of the housing. Each contact pad with terminal pin is electrically isolated from the others, and each will allow for the completion of an individual circuit. The terminal pins are designed to accept a wiring harness fitted with a specific electrical connector; the contact pads are designed to accept the 4 Pin Plunger Assembly. The 4 Pin Plunger Assembly is a male connector designed for installation in the sliding door of a mini-van or similar vehicle. One end of its plastic housing encases four terminal pins which are designed to accept a wiring harness fitted with a specific electrical connector; the other provides four spring-loaded plunger-type electrical terminal pins extending from the face of the device. When the vehicle door is closed, the plungers of the 4 Pin Plunger Assembly come into contact with the contact pads of the 4 Pin Target Assembly, completing four individual electrical circuits.
