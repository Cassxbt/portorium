The subject product, Lebon Toothpaste, is indicated for use as a dentifrice. It is packaged for retail sale in a 75 ml aluminum tube and is available in nine flavors: Back to Pampelonne, Cap Ferrat Mood, Fearless Freedom, Le White, Noacarlina, Rhythm is Love, Sweet Extravagance, Tropical Crush, and Une Piscine à Antibes. The toothpaste contains sodium fluoride, hydrated silica, hydroxyapatite, organic green tea extract, xylitol, aloe vera, hyaluronic acid, codium fragile extract, and other ingredients.
