A sample of the “Foldable Doll Bed with Storage,” was submitted with your inquiry. The item consists of a foldable canvas-covered paperboard bed and mattress measuring approximately 20”(L) x 11”(W) when expanded to its full size. It comes with an appropriately sized canvas pillow and blanket measuring approximately 7”(L) x 4.5”(W) and 19”(L) x 15.5”(W), respectively. All of these items are packed into a polyester doll case with a zipper closure. It has a small plastic window so that the doll (not included) can “look” out. There is also a handle on the top and side for transporting purposes.
