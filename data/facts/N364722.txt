There are two items under consideration in this request, a current-carrying electrical support rail identified as material number 22169087, TECTON T 5 WH (Article 1) and an electrical feed-in module identified as material number 22169063, TECTON SF WH (Article 2). These items are tracks (referred to as “trunks”) of the TECTON lighting system which is endlessly customizable to the end user’s lighting design requirements and application. Article 1 is further described as the electrical and mechanical backbone or “trunk” of the TECTON lighting system and consists of an outer U-shaped steel housing, an inner U-shaped steel connection rail, polypropylene insulating conductor carriers, and copper conductors. The copper conductors extend along the entire length of the article providing a passive electrical connection function from one section of the “trunk” to another section of the “trunk”. Article 1 is mounted directly to the surface of a ceiling or wall or suspended pendant-style from a ceiling. We note that article 1 does not include any lamps, LED boards, reflectors, lenses, drivers, ballasts, or similar components that generate or direct light. It incorporates electrical wires, terminal blocks and circuit terminals. Article 2 is further described as a dedicated electrical feed-in module that accepts a power feed from electrical wiring and distributes that electricity through internal terminal blocks and multi-pin electrical connectors. Article 2 consists of an outer U-shaped steel housing, an inner U-shaped steel connection rail, multi-pole terminal blocks, polyamide electrical contact holders, multi-pin electrical connectors, a metal cover plate, a cable clip, fasteners, and other associated components. Article 2 connects to one end of the beginning section of article 1 distributing electrical power to that trunk section and on to subsequent sections of article 1 as per the design requirements of the end user.
