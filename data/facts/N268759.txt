The item concerned is referred to as a “Unit-Lane Departure Warning System”, item # 95890-C1000. This unit consists primarily of an image sensor/camera and a printed circuit board (PCB) assembly within a common housing. This unit is used within an automobile to provide lane departure detection, automatic control of the high beams and forward vehicle collision warning. In use the unit would be installed within an automobile, behind the rearview mirror, with the sensor/camera facing out through the windshield. Images detected by the sensor/camera are analyzed by the imbedded software installed on the PCB assembly. The unit detects whether the vehicle is drifting too close to the lane markers or if it’s getting too close to the vehicle in front of it. It can be used to detect an oncoming vehicle and then turn down the high beams if necessary. Once the captured images are analyzed/processed by the PCB assembly, the data is transferred to the automobile’s Controller Area Network (CAN) so that the appropriate warnings or actions can be initiated.
