Item 5303.114, the “VX IT STD RAL 7035 Rack,” is a series of floor-standing, empty, and unequipped steel and aluminum frame storage cabinets that measure approximately 31.5" in width, 47.24" in height, and 31.5" in depth. The roof plate panel is comprised of multiple steel pieces and a concealed cut-out; it will allow for the entry of cables and an optional fan unit. The roof plate is symmetrically welded to forward and rear vertical steel posts and a base plate panel that can be bolted to the floor. On the reverse side of the cabinet is a two-hinged access door panel with a perforated surface to allow for ventilation and a two-point locking rod. Steel sheet panels are not present on the left or the right sides of the cabinet, this hollow configuration will permit multiple cabinets to be joined (bayed) to create larger modular configurations. The cabinet face is a two-hinged aluminum frame front door with a steel sheet panel and a two-point locking rod. The cabinet is designed to secure, organize, and protect computer server equipment, networking devices, route associated cabling, and permit airflow to specific information-technology (IT) hardware, fan units, cables, and additional hardware components (sold separately).
