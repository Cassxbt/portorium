The subject merchandise is whole, raw, uncleaned, frozen octopus (Octopus vulgaris) harvested from  Moroccan waters within Food and Agriculture Organization Fishing Area 34. The octopus is shipped to  Spain where it is defrosted and cleaned, then disinfected using ozone treatment. It is subsequently weighed  with the addition of salt and permitted processing aids. The octopus is then packaged, and frozen. After  freezing, the product is blown/glazed, followed by final packaging and labeling, including metal detection. The finished product is prepared for transport and shipment to the United States. You seek a determination as to the tariff classification and country of origin of the above-described product.
