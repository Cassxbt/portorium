A sample of the article was provided. Key Safety Systems Part Number 2003369-3 is a pool filter bag designed to filter material between 100 and 120 microns and collect debris of various sizes from the bottom of a pool. The sample is to be paired with a vacuum unit which is powered by a booster pump, both of which are required for the filtration operation. According to the information obtained and physical examination of the sample, the filter, sewn from woven polyester fabric, is coupled to the cylindrical output of a pool vacuum via a vinyl sleeve located on the bottom of the filter bag. The filter bag has two individual chambers separated by a seam; the rear of each chamber is fitted with (1) a zipper and (2) a narrow piece of hook and loop fabric, sewn parallel to each other, which facilitate the operation of the filter and the subsequent removal of debris.
