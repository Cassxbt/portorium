The two items under consideration are described as 3-1/2” Swing Clear Hinges. Both styles are made of steel and consist of two hinge leaves bent at 90 degrees that are connected by a removable hinge pin. Item number S3558SW has a 5/8" radius corner and item number S3514SW has a 1/4" radius corner. These are a type of offset hinge that is designed to allow the door to open completely outside the door jamb so that the full width of the opening is available for passage for easy access for persons using wheelchairs or walkers. You stated that these hinges can convert doorways otherwise not compliant with the 32-inch minimum clearance of Section 404.2.3 of the ADA Standards for Accessible Design, which measures clearance width between the face of the door and the stop, when the door is open at 90 degrees. Each style is sold as a single hinge.
