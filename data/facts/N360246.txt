Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Liposome Injection, imported in dosages of 20 mg/10 mL and 50 mg/25 mL  vials, is a cytotoxic anthracycline antibiotic. It is indicated for the treatment of patients with ovarian cancer,  AIDS-related Kaposi’s sarcoma, and in combination with bortezomib in patients with multiple myeloma.
