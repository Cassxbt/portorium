Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is a decorative woven rush cross known as St. Brigid’s Cross. Rushes are  harvested, dried, and woven by hand. The rushes are flattened and woven into a square that forms the center  of the cross, and the arms of the cross are each offset to one side of each side of the square. We estimate the  cross to be approximately 5 inches square. The cross is designed to be hung on the wall. The cross is  attached to a paperboard card that is printed with St. Brigid’s blessing and the story of St. Brigid.
