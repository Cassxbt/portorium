The submitted samples are Item n. 1, ref. “Angel” (mattress cover) and Item n. 2, ref. “Susi”(quilted mattress pad). The mattress cover is made from 50 percent cotton and 50 percent polyester woven fabric. The fabric is printed and not napped. It is designed to fully encase an approximately 9-inch thick mattress. The cover has a zippered opening along one end partially extending down the sides. The top and back layers of the quilted mattress pad are made from 50 percent cotton and 50 percent polyester woven fabric. It is stuffed with a polyester fiber filling. All three layers are quilted together. A polyester knit fabric elasticized skirt is attached around the edges and is used to secure the pad to the mattress.
