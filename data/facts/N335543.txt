The merchandise under consideration is described in your request as the GRIDLOK 10D Channel. It is made of galvanized steel and is designed to reinforce a drywall suspended ceiling framing system in seismic zones. The GRIDLOK 10D Channel is comprised of two primary components: The Saddle Short and the 4-way fly plate. The Saddle Short is an inverted L shaped flange. The 4-way fly plate consists of a bracket made of a single piece of metal in the shape of a cross. Three ends of the 4-way fly plate are bent upwards at a 45 degree angle while the fourth end is bent upward at a 90 degree angle. The fly plate is attached to the inverted L shaped flange with a single bolt and nut. Included with the GRIDLOK 10D Channel is one BC-90 and two BC-45 connectors, which are angled brackets. Once installed the GRIDLOK 10D Channel functions to provide reinforcing support to the drywall suspended ceiling framing system when an earthquake occurs.
