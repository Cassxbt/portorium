The merchandise under consideration is a standard full-size keyboard measuring approximately 17.5 inches in length by 5.5 inches in width x .75 inches in depth. The Querty keyboard features extra large keys made of 100% plastic, and does not have any special command keys. The keyboard is imported with a 56” USB cable that connects directly to the central processing unit (CPU) of any personal computer (PC).
