The product under consideration is described as the “Dymmoy Self-Feeding Bowl Clamp.”  It is a reusable  and washable feeding accessory designed for babies and toddlers that are six months of age and older. This  article is used to secure a separate feeding bowl to a dining table or highchair tray during mealtimes to  prevent the bowl from being pushed, lifted, tipped, or flipped while a child practices self-feeding under adult  supervision. It can accommodate a bowl with a diameter of approximately 8 to 12 centimeters and in a  variety of bowl shapes. The item is constructed primarily of acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (ABS) plastic  along with internal metal components which support the internal adjustment and clamping mechanism.
