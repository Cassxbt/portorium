Product information and photographs of the subject clamp were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is described as a mounting clamp used to secure the handle to a follow spotlight. The mounting clamp is comprised of two pieces, a bottom half and an upper half. When connected together the two halves form a hinged circle which clamps around the cylindrical shape of the handle. The upper and lower halves of the mounting clamp are held together by two screws. The entire mounting clamp is then attached to the spotlight with two additional mounting screws. The mounting screws are not included at the time of importation. The clamp is made from 6061 aluminum alloy, is manufactured through an extrusion process, and features a black anodized finish.
