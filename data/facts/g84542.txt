The merchandise to be imported consists of three models of metal storage furniture with textile hanging bins as follows:The Storage Organizer consists of powder-coated steel tubing frame including a steel wire bottom shelf. Three textile bins hanging from the frame are made from woven polyester with nylon mesh inserts. The tops of the bins fold over the steel wire frame and are attached using a hook and loop type fastener. The Storage Organizer is designed to stand on the floor. The Storage Etagere consists of powder-coated steel tubing frame including three steel wire shelves. The hanging bins are made from woven polyester bonded to a PVC lining. They include nylon straps at each corner, with plastic hooks. The hooks will attach to either the wire frame or to another bin. Each bin has a bottom liner made of plywood with a PVC jacket. The bins will not stand alone; they can only be used when hanging from the wire frame or from another bin. The Storage Etagere is designed to stand on the floor. The Workbench consists of powder-coated steel tubing frame with a steel bottom shelf and a work surface tabletop made of medium density fiberboard. It has large and small hanging bins identical to the Storage Etagere. The Workbench also includes a textile “tool rack” made of the same PVC-backed woven polyester material as the bins. The tool rack features several pockets sewn to a panel, which hangs from the steel wire frame by means of plastic hooks. The Workbench is designed to stand on the floor.
