The items under consideration are portable sawmills designed to receive round logs and cut them into boards, slabs, beams, and other dimensional timber. Model M7 is a chainsaw-powered sawmill that consists of a fabricated metal frame and log bed, cutting assembly, guide, support system, sizing mechanisms, controls, and safety guards. According to the product brochure, this model will not be imported with a motor but will rely on a user-supplied chainsaw to power the integrated cutting blade. It is capable of sawing logs 4.2 meters long (extendable to 8 meters) and 1.3 meters in diameter. Furthermore, it is able to make standard cuts of 7 inches by 7 inches and double cuts up to 14 inches wide. Models M10/M12 consist of a fabricated metal frame and log bed, saw head, circular saw blade, blade guard, petrol engine or electric motor, drive shaft, bearings, gearbox or transmission, feed, drag-back mechanism, log-sizing mechanism, controls, and safety guards. In addition, model M12 may include a hydrostatic feed, electronic or push-button sizing controls, and related control equipment. Model M10 is capable of sawing logs 4.2 meters long (extendable to 8 meters) and 1.3 meters in diameter. It can make standard cuts of 10 inches by 10 inches and double cuts up to 20 inches wide. Model M12 is capable of sawing logs 6 meters long (extendable to 8 meters) and 1.8 meters in diameter. It can make standard cuts of 12.25 inches by 12.25 inches and double cuts up to 24 inches wide.
