In lieu of samples, illustrations, drawings, an artist profile, and a synopsis of the creation process were provided for review. Alice Wang’s drawings, the “Bride, Groom, and Wedding Guests” are a group of 130 individual and unframed composition drawing place cards that illustrates the subject’s pose, facial features, expression, name, and may include decorative design elements. The drawings are individually created and personalized for a specific wedding guest; each original piece begins with a blank watercolor paper that bears no pre-printed or pre-stenciled illustrations. The materials used in creating each piece includes blank watercolor paper, brushes, watercolor, ink/calligraphy pens, colored pencil/crayons, acrylic markers, glitter pens, and other materials. Each drawing will take approximately 30 minutes to create, are original artworks, and are not replicas. The place card dimensions are approximately 3.94 inches wide and 5.91 inches long, and each place card will bear the signature of the artist on the reverse side.
