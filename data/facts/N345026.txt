Olmesartan Medoxomil and Hydrochlorothiazide, imported in 20 mg/12.5 mg, 40 mg/12.5 mg and 40 mg/25 mg tablets, is a combination drug containing Olmesartan Medoxomil (angiotensin II receptor blocker) and hydrochlorothiazide (diuretic). It is indicated for the treatment of hypertension, to lower blood pressure. Lowering blood pressure reduces the risk of fatal and nonfatal cardiovascular events, primarily strokes and myocardial infarctions.
