The ruling was requested on two styles of plinth blocks. The subject blocks are made of a solid wood, coniferous (pine) species and a primed medium density fiberboard (PMDF). You submitted representative samples of the plinth blocks in both forms for our examination. This ruling will address the chamfered style, coniferous (pine) species plinth block sample submitted. The face of the pine plinth block is chamfered along three edges and it is continuously shaped on top with a decorative ogee profile in a horizontal direction.
