The samples will be retained for training purposes. The sample submitted, “VICIS ZERO1,” is an unassembled football helmet. The helmet consists of an outer shell, “flexible columns,” and an inner shell. The outer and inner shells are made of thermoplastic and the “flexible columns” are made of polyurethane. The “flexible columns” are located between the outer and inner shells and are designed to mitigate collisions. After importation the helmet will be painted, the components will be screwed together and the facemask, liners and pads will be attached.
