The merchandise under consideration, identified by part number 1127397, is described as a valve cable harness designed to be used with the Respironics Trilogy Evo ventilator, a medical device intended to assist those individuals with chronic breathing issues. The harness consists of a set of insulated cable conductors terminated at each end with specially designed connectors. Additionally, the harness incorporates a printed circuit board (PCB) embedded with electronics and software that allow the cable to only be connected to the aforementioned ventilator. The subject harness is used to electrically connect the ventilator to a custom oxygen blender module.
