Item 1, the “Essence,” item 2, the “Elmwood,” and item 3, the “Cabico,” are a series of floor and wall  mounted display units that include (1) standing door displays, (2) wall mounted door displays, (3) single and  double door displays, (4) pullout displays, and (5) atelier displays that are constructed of wood, plywood, and  particleboard frames. The display units incorporate shelves and drawers for storage and display. The  dimensions of the displays range from 22 ½" - 108" in width, 10 7/8" - 23 3/4" in depth, and 43" - 96" in  height.
