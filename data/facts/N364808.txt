The ruling request was for hardwood trailer floor kits. Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is hardwood trailer floor kits. The flooring consists of maple, oak, or apitong woods that have been edge-glued. You also identify that some boards are made of composites. You do not, however, identify the nature of the composites, so these products will not be addressed in this ruling. The boards are cut to length, and the wood is profiled on each edge, per customer specifications. The boards are then painted, coated with wax and polyurethane, and bundled in a specific order for installation.
