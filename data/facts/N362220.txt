Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is a structural timber frame kit to construct a robust, permanent gazebo. The  structure has posts, trusses, and a roof, but no walls. The kit consists of many structural wood members of  Douglas fir (a coniferous wood) that are each shaped with mortise and tenon joinery. The kit includes oak (a  non-coniferous wood) pegs for assembly, steel hardware and connectors for assembly, and engineered plans  for assembly. The kit does not include any dimensional lumber.
