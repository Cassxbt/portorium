(Upland, CA). The subject merchandise is Nutracor High Palmitic Lipid Compound Prills. The sample submitted is a light yellow dry micro-sized substance stated to be 300 micron. The sample will be retained. The product is composed of 54.5 percent distilled high palmitic fatty acids, 25.6 percent palm fatty acid distillate, 20 percent fractionated palm fatty acid distillate, less than 1 percent butyl hydroxytoluene (BHT) and ethoxyquin (both antioxidants). The product is a feed additive indicated to act as a fatty acids replacer in ruminants. It is indicated to improve butterfat content in milk, higher digestibility and equitable energy partitioning in cattle. The product can be applied as mix into feed, top dressing, pelleting or drench mix. Nutracor High Palmitic Lipid Compound Prills will be imported in 50 pounds bags or 1 ton totes (2000 pounds), respectively.
