The item under consideration is identified as the ECU Assembly, Item Code BE6003S300, which is described as the component of the Electronic Stability Control system that controls each wheel to provide stability during driving. The subject ECU consists of an enclosure containing a printed circuit board assembly, electrical coils, and a gasket, and is mounted to the Hydraulic Control Unit and wheel assembly for the purpose of independently controlling the amount of torque applied to each wheel. In use, the subject ECU receives vehicle sensor signals such as wheel speed, wheel angle, wheel yaw position, G-force, and cylinder pressure, and based on its programming determines if an unstable condition exists. Once an unstable condition is identified, the ECU sends a signal to the vehicle supervisory controller which then adjusts engine torque and applies hydraulic pressure to the wheels.
