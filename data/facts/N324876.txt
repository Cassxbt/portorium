Pemetrexed for Injection, imported in 100 mg, 500 mg, and 1000 mg vials, is in a class of medications called antifolate antineoplastic agents. It is indicated for the treatment of pleural mesothelioma and non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Diclofenac Potassium, imported in 50 mg tablets, is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). It is indicated for the treatment of mild to moderate pain associated with osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, dysmenorrhea, and acute migraine. Prochlorperazine Maleate, imported in 5 mg and 10 mg tablets, is an antipsychotic drug. It is indicated for the treatment of severe nausea and vomiting, as well as short-term management of psychotic disorders such as generalized non-psychotic anxiety and schizophrenia.
