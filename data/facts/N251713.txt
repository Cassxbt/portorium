The merchandise under consideration is referred to as an “automotive tuner assembly,” part number B521-M85 (B811-A99A). This device is designed to only operate on the ISM (Industrial, Scientific, and Medical) band and is used within a specific frequency determined by the customer. This RF (radio frequency) receiver receives data from wireless key fob remotes and wireless tire pressure sensors and converts the data signals to digital pulses that are transmitted through a wired connection.
