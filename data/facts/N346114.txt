The merchandise under consideration is an electrical cable, part number 000000733.B1, which is used in the Zulu 3 and Sierra headsets within the general aviation industry. The cable is comprised of a red wire and a black wire joined together by an electrical connector at one end, while the opposite ends are unterminated and stripped of insulated covering. The cable is used within the headset to convert power transmitted from the AA battery, providing voltage to the control box that powers the headset and provides noise canceling capabilities.
