The item at issue in this request is an automotive overhead console assembly, identified by part number P708. The overhead console is comprised of a molded plastic base onto which numerous electrical switches, two dome/map lights, a sunglasses compartment, and several indicator lights are mounted. The entire assembly is finished with a color-matched molded bezel with cutouts for the various switches and lights. There are dedicated switches used to control the dome/map lights, moonroof, and interior dome/door lamp functions, along with a series of auxiliary switches that can be used to control optional accessory equipment such as auxiliary light bars, snowplows, winches, pumps, and similar equipment.
