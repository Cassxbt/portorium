The product under consideration is identified as “Macrowave Oven”, which is a combination microwave, air  fryer, and toaster oven. Each mode can be operated independently or in combination with the other modes, all  within the same housing. This flatbed countertop unit measures 15 X 23 X 21 inches, is rated at 1,000W at  120 volts, has a 7 inch one-touch control panel, operates at household speeds, and features a capacity of 25.5  liters. The Macrowave Oven is designed primarily for domestic use.
