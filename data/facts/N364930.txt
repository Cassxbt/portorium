Informational material was submitted with your request. The merchandise under consideration is an F-16 simulator cockpit shell meant to simulate the experience of piloting a fighter jet. It is a life-sized stationary metal body resembling the cockpit of an F-16 fighter jet and when assembled covers an approximate area of 47 square feet with a height up to 33 inches. After importation it will be assembled and fitted with additional furnishings and gaming equipment making it more realistic. The cockpit shell features a space for a user to sit acting as the pilot, a metal body extending out from the cockpit opening resembling a portion of the fighter jet’s fuselage, and a metal ladder attached to the side for a user to enter and leave the cockpit. We note that the cockpit shell is composed of two metals: steel and aluminum.
