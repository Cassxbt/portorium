The first group of items under consideration are referred to as front-end loader attachments for tractors, part  numbers MLA1100L, MLA2100L, MLA1626LR, MLA1635LR, MLA1640LR, MLH45504L,  MLMX5155L, and MLTEC60754L, imported from India. The loader attachments are optional and  detachable equipment that give a tractor the ability to lift and move materials. Each loader attachment  consists of a metal boom assembly with parallel arms and pivoting sections, which are operated by actuator  assemblies powered by the hydraulic system of the tractor. Each loader will be imported with a  general-purpose shovel bucket that can be used to load and unload materials.
