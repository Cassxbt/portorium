It also  includes two sets of bilateral self-fabric adjustable straps with snap buttons permanently sewn on both sides  of the covering to secure the covering to a mobility device. You state the neckline is finished with 95 percent  polyester and 5 percent cotton woven binding fabric. The covering is intended to provide full coverage from  the shoulders to the ankles when in a seated position.
