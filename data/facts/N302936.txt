Doxepin Hydrochloride imported in 150 mg capsules is a tricyclic antidepressant indicated to treat symptoms of depression and/or anxiety associated with alcoholism, psychiatric conditions, or manic-depressive conditions. Acetazolamide imported in 500 mg extended-release capsules is a carbonic anhydrase inhibitor. It is indicated for the adjunctive treatment of chronic simple (open-angle) glaucoma, secondary glaucoma, and preoperatively in acute angle-closure glaucoma where delay of surgery is desired in order to lower the intraocular pressure.
