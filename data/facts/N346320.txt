Dorzolamide HCL/Timolol Maleate Ophthalmic drops, imported in 10 mL, is a combination medication  containing, Dorzolamide Hydrochloride (a carbonic anhydrase inhibitor) and Timolol Maleate (a  beta-adrenergic receptor blocking agent), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the reduction of elevated  intraocular pressure (IOP) in patients with open-angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension who are insufficiently  responsive to beta-blockers.
