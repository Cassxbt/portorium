The product at issue is referred to as a pump housing casting, item number LR47761-1Z. The casting is a  component of a rotary positive displacement pump used in the lubrication system of an aircraft. The casting  features mounting holes that allow it to be bolted to an accessory gearbox, six inlets and two outlets, and is  configured to incorporate a splined drive end, a rotor, a blade with vanes and other components that  contribute to the process of pumping oil. The casting is constructed of aluminum material.
