The merchandise under consideration is described as an Isolated Phase Bus (IPB) system. The subject system  is comprised of a hollow aluminum tube held within an outer aluminum enclosure by cast-resin posts. The  inner tube functions as an electrical conductor, able to carry high levels of current. The air gap between the  inner conductor and the enclosure acts as dielectric insulation, while the cast-resin posts are the structural  component. Based upon the information provided, the inner conductor component is bare aluminum and is  not coated or covered with any electrical or thermal insulating material. The IPB system is designed to carry electricity over distances within power plants and other high-voltage  systems where large amounts of power need to be transferred safely. We note that the system can be  configured to the specifications of the end customer and is imported into the United States in sections to be  installed as needed.
