The first product is referred to as a roller and is described as a component of a car wash conveying system. The roller is evenly spaced along the length of a chain that is connected to a sprocket within the car wash conveyor system. As the rollers move with the chain, they contact the front and rear wheels of a vehicle, helping it move along in a car wash tunnel. The second product to be imported is a log chain for use in a car wash conveyor system. The welded log chain is made of grade 80, heat treated alloy steel and has a 5/8" (15.87 mm) diameter. The third product to be imported is a 458 forged conveyor chain for use in a car wash conveyor system, referred to as a 458 chain. The pitch is 4" (101.6 mm) and the components are held together by pins.
