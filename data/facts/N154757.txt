Best Buy SKU 2365344/Model #DX-MP3MT, marketed as the RocketFish MP3 bicycle/car mount, is a mounting kit that can accommodate a variety of mobile devices, such as GPS devices, MP3 players and cell phones. The kit includes three components: a cradle for the mobile device, a suction cup mount and a thumbscrew clamp bracket. The cradle can be attached to either the suction cup mount or the clamp bracket. Although the suction cup mount is described as a car mount, it can be affixed to any smooth surface. Although the clamp bracket is described as a bicycle mount, it can be secured onto motorcycle handles, exercise equipment, or any place with a rod or frame.
