Product information was submitted with your request. The merchandise under consideration is identified as narrow body gravure roll, Inline part no. 201889. The part is an intermediate stage in the production of a gravure cylinder, which is used in a rotogravure press. The three components of the imported narrow body gravure roll consist of the rolled steel body and the two steel side journals. Prior to importation, each component is machined to precise dimensions and parameters to fit into the rotogravure press. The components will be imported, unassembled, in a single shipment. You have stated that no journal or body will be imported separately, and that no spare pieces or other components will be included in the shipment. After importation, the body and two journals will be assembled by means of a heat shrink-fit process. Once assembled, the rolled portion is copper-plated and polished, the gravure cell pattern is engraved into the copper, and the engraved surface is chrome-plated and finish-polished. The finished gravure cylinder is then installed into the rotogravure press. The engraved cells of the gravure cylinder pick up liquid from a pan, a doctor blade wipes off the excess, and the cylinder transfers the liquid to the paper web at the nip with the impression roll. The journals carry the roll in the press bearings, and the keyed journal is driven by the press.
