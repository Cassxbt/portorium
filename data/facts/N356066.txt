The item concerned is referred to as an “Assembly, Ear Cup, Left, H Mod” that is used in the production of  aviation headsets. The applicable SKU number is 285-00060-000.A1. This item is a component of  Lightspeed’s Zulu H Mod Cup Kit. It is essentially the left ear cup of the internally mounted headset used  within the helmet of helicopter pilots. This item contains a speaker, microphones and a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA). The ear cup  provides audio to the pilot while also enabling Active Noise Reduction (ANR). The microphones detect  ambient cockpit noise, and the PCBA processes the signal to generate an anti-noise waveform through the  speakers, canceling unwanted sound. The ear cup interfaces with the helmet’s control box via an upper cable  and forwards signals to the aircraft intercom through a lower cable. There are foam layers in the cup which  provide passive noise attenuation, forming a tight seal around the ear to block external noise.
