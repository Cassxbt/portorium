Photos and product descriptions were provided for our review. The products under consideration are two similar versions of decorative wall signs consisting of framed MDF boards that have been silk screened with autumn-themed phrases and motifs, such as “We harvest love here” and “I love fall most of all” surrounded by leaves and pumpkins. Each one measures 15.75” high and 11” wide. A metal sawtooth hanger and jute rope facilitate hanging.
