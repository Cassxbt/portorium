The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Copenhagen stone. A sample was submitted with your  ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Copenhagen is an ivory-colored stone mottled with dark gray. From the information you provided, it will  measure between 115 to 122 inches in length, between 65 to 75 inches in width, and either 0.75 inches or 1.5  inches in thickness. After importation, it will be cut to size and shape for such applications as kitchen  countertops, bathroom surfaces, and surfaces for office furniture. Laboratory analysis has determined that Copenhagen is made of granite. The top of the stone has been  polished and the sides are simply cut or sawn.
