The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Cable, Emergency Jack, part number  300-00031-000.A2. The subject device is comprised of multiple individually insulated cable conductors  terminated at one end with 3.5mm stereo receptacle and a multi-pin connector at the other. The cable will be  connected at one end to the PCBA within the earcup of an aviation headset. The user can then connect a  power and audio cable to the stereo jack, allowing for power and audio signals to be routed to the headset in  the event of wireless link failure or battery depletion. We note that the cables are designed to carry both audio  and power signals.
