The merchandise under consideration is a Check Valve and Reservoir Assembly, item number 558493M000. It is designed for use in a motor vehicle and based on the facts presented, this item is a composite good that consists of a check valve, an aluminum reservoir, a connection port, two adaptors and two steel brackets. It is stated that the brackets affix the assembly within the suspension system and the reservoir holds air that is supplied by the compressor. The connection port allows for air to exit and enter the reservoir and the check valve controls the movement of air that is released from the reservoir. The adaptor connects the connection port and the check valve to the reservoir.
