The imported products in question are various machines and equipment used in an integrated and largely interconnected assembly line for assembling heterojunction technology (HJT) photovoltaic (PV) solar cells into finished HJT solar panels (aka solar modules). The machinery and equipment will be used together to perform the connecting, assembling, laminating, testing, and finishing of the solar panels. You state that the goods will arrive in multiple ocean shipments and moved in-bond to a manufacturing Foreign-Trade Zone (FTZ) located in South Carolina. Once all equipment is in the FTZ is hooked up and ready to start production, TSL then will file a formal entry with CBP and pay all required duties and taxes. In your submission, you group the various machines and equipment into five (5) production or assembly zones, as follows. Zone 1 involves cell stringing, glass loading, and initial layup operations to create aligned modules stacks for interconnection and pre-lamination operations. The main components in this zone include automatic solar stringer layup machines used to automate the assembly of individual solar cells into strings, a gantry type layup machine to transfer with battery string out of the string welding machine to the layup machine, a glass loader, a module film scraping and melting machine (equipment that applies, repositions, or separates encapsulation films from PV modules), a foil cutter, buffer units (automated storage units that prevent assembly bottlenecks), and various conveyors. Zone 2 involves bussing, interconnection, and pre-lamination preparation to create an electrically connected, lamination-ready module stack. The zone’s main machinery includes a flipper machine, various conveyors, an auto strip (or edge trimming) application machine, a backsheet cutting and laying machine, a bussing
machine, butyl rubber coating equipment, a double glass layup machine, a G-G edge sealing machine, a G-G rework gantry, glass loader, buffer units, lateral lifting transfer unit, and a lead flattening machine. Zone 3 involves the production of laminated PV module bodies for junction-box (J-Box) and framing operations. The main equipment in this zone includes laminators, an auto adhesive tape machine, buffer units, conveyors, and lifters. Zone 4 involves J-Box installation/welding, lead shaping, trimming, edge work, frame handling, glue, mounting and framing to produce a finished physical solar module ready for curing, electrical testing and final inspection. The principal equipment here includes automatic framing machines, an automatic trimming machine, an automatic edge peeling machine, an automatic J-Box installation machine, J-Box soldering machine, busbar lead shaping machine, frame handlers, frame gluing machine, a visual inspection flipper, robotic jig (mounting system) installation machine, buffer units, and various conveyors. Zone 5 involves curing, labeling, testing and sorting. This zone includes an automatic capping machine (to seal J-Boxes to solar panels), corner grinding and protector machines, a curing line and curing line handler, glue potting hander, module upender/tilter (handling machine), robotic jig removal machine, sorting machines, two-component glue machine, buffer units, and conveyors.
