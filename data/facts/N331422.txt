The Circuitry Assembly consists of welded copper segments and resin insulation and electrically connects the vehicle’s battery system/inverter, via the Power Connector, to the motor’s stator at intervals to supply high-voltage 3-phase AC. The Power Connector consists of copper conductors, bushings, and resin insulation. In use, the formed conductors overlap and are encapsulated in electrical insulation to form a single 3-phase AC connector that is bolted onto the motor and the Circuitry Assembly to connect the motor to the vehicle’s electrical system.
