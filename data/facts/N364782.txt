The articles under consideration are four snap rings, part numbers 45956-01980, 49556-02320, 49556-02420, and 49556-02590. The snap rings are designed for incorporation into a motor vehicle drive shaft assembly. Part number 49556-01980 measures 1.2 × 25 × 25 mm; part number 49556-02320 measures 1.2 × 27.7 × 27.7 mm; part number 49556-02420 measures 1.2 × 28.7 × 28.7 mm; and part number 49556-02590 measures 1.2 × 30.4 × 30.4 mm. The four articles share the same configuration and function and differ only in their dimensions. The subject snap rings are manufactured from SK5 high-carbon steel. During manufacture, the steel is blanked to form the shape of the snap ring, heat treated to increase its strength and hardness, barreled to remove residual burrs, and inspected for dimensional accuracy, hardness, and elasticity. Within the drive shaft assembly, each snap ring is inserted into a machined groove on the shaft and functions to secure the trunnion in its designated position. The snap ring prevents axial movement and dislodgement of the trunnion resulting from loads and vibrations generated during vehicle operation. Additionally, during the telescoping or plunging movement of the drive shaft assembly, the snap ring maintains the trunnion in its proper position.
