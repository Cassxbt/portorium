Ltd. Descriptive information was submitted for review. The product at issue is a coolant hub module, item number 25429-CV500, which is referred to as a vehicle  coolant reservoir. The module, which is part of a cooling system used in an electric vehicle, is a composite  good that consists of two single suction water pumps, a reservoir with a 2.2. liter capacity, a three-way valve,  and various hoses and mounting brackets. In use, the closed coupled pumps discharge coolant stored in the  reservoir out of 1.89-centimeter outlets to dissipate heat released by the battery system and various electrical  systems.
