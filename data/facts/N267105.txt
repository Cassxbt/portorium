The item under consideration is referred to as the Control and Drive Unit Assembly and is described as an automotive door switching device consisting of a 140 Watt DC electric motor, a controller and a drive unit. The assembly is intended to be mounted on the trunk hinge of a passenger vehicle. The motor is operated by the controller receiving an operation command from an external source, for example:  a wireless key or the operation button in the vehicle. Once activated, the motor pushes or pulls the trunk lid hinge resulting in the opening and closing.
