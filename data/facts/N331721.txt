A sample was submitted and sent for laboratory analysis. The sample identified as “MyloTM mycelium leather” is described as a nonwoven mycelium/lyocell fabric that is coated on one side with polyurethane. CBP Laboratory analysis indicates that the sample of MyloTM is composed of a nonwoven fabric composed of 84.2 percent mycelium and 15.8 percent rayon. It was found to be coated with a layer of cellular polyvinyl acetate.
