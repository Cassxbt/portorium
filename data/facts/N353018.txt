There are four unique card products. Product information and photos  were submitted for our review. Item 1, CVS #455579, Positive Vibes Valentines, includes 16 paper Valentine’s Day cards printed with a  rainbow and phrases such as “Reach for the stars” and “Follow your dreams”. Affixed to the front of the card  is a printed thermoplastic rubber gel cling in the shape of a star, unicorn, flower, or cloud. The cling is to be  removed from the card and played with separately.
