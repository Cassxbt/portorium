Informational material was submitted with your request. The merchandise under consideration is flat aluminum plates collectively described as the Grid JF2 heatsink components. The components are used in a heatsink assembly installed in a LGES JF2 Energy Storage System (ESS) battery module. There are three specific plate models in this assembly: Top A (Part # ST-JF2-TPA), Top B (Part # ST-JF2-TPB) and Bottom (Part #ST-JF2-BTM). Each plate is made of various aluminum alloys and roughly rectilinear in form. Before final assembly, each plate is imported separately in a semi-finished state. Following importation these plates are attached to each other and undergo a process creating a hermetic seal. From there they can be incorporated into the battery module, where they will circulate high-pressure coolant.
