The items under consideration are “Aqua Doodle Books.” Each of the samples received features a different theme: Farm, Zoo, Favorite Foods or Vehicles. Each book is constructed of five paperboard sheets that are spiral-bound at the top edge, with a “coloring pen” secured to the book by an elastic loop. The upper portion of the inside pages is printed with instructions and guided play activities, while the bottom portion is printed with a full-color image that pertains to the theme of the book. Each book contains four colored images covered with a layer of white coating. When the coloring pen is filled with water, a child can use it to apply water to the white coating to reveal the hidden images. As the pages dry, the white coating becomes opaque again, allowing for repeated use.
