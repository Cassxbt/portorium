The items concerned are referred to as a driver cap (303-00118-000.A2 Driver Cap, Left, PFX) and a  microphone cover (303-00131-000.A1 ANR Mic Cover, Right, PFX). Both items are used in the  manufacturer of Light Speed’s PFX Aviation Headset. The driver cap, (303-00118-000.A2 Driver Cap, Left, PFX), is used within the left ear cup assembly. It is  made from ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene). Its function is to:  Protect the speaker/driver by shielding the delicate driver diaphragm from dust, debris and physical  damage. Prevent wear and tear that could degrade sound quality over time. Reduce unwanted resonances and smooth out frequency response, especially in the mid and high  ranges. Help maintain internal geometry of the earcup, which is vital for consistent ANC (Active Noise  Cancelation) performance. Ensure that the driver remains properly aligned with the ear cup and the microphone 

The microphone cover (303-00131-000.A1 ANR Mic Cover, Right, PFX), is used within the right ear cup  assembly. It is made from ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene). Its function is to:  Protect the microphone by shielding the delicate microphone from dust, debris and physical damage. Prevent wear and tear that could degrade sound quality over time. Reduce unwanted resonances and smooth out frequency response, especially in the mid and high  ranges. Help maintain internal geometry of the earcup, which is vital for consistent ANC (Active Noise  Cancelation) performance. Ensures that the microphone remains properly aligned with the ear cup and the driver/speaker.
