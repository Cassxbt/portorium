The merchandise under consideration is described as the Freesuns Solar Roof Tile identified by model name  Solaris Heritage. The solar roof tile mimics slate roof tiles in appearance but generates electricity using the  solar cells within the tile, while also providing weather protection for the roofs of commercial and residential  buildings. The solar roof tile measures 28 x 20 inches which contains 7 half cut standard monocrystalline  photovoltaic solar cells laminated between two layers of tempered glass. The tile also features a junction box  with two cables affixed with MC4 connectors and a bypass diode. We note that the subject tiles are not  imported with inverters, optimizers, or any similar power managing components.
