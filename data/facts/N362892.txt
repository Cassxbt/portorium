This product in question is the Richpeace Automatic Perforation, Embroidery and Sewing (Rotating Head)  Machine, model number RPCE-L-SP+E+S-1-1200X800-B-SP6+F9+IS2-VR1, VR2-LH50, RH360, AO,  3P220. This programmable, industrial CNC workstation is designed to sew, embroider, and perforate (punch)  automotive interior components, primarily of leather, including steering wheel covers, seat upholstery,  dashboards, door panels and other trim. The machine’s working area (X and Y axis) measures 1200×800 mm. You state that the machine is not intended for general textile production. The machine includes a bridge-style frame, and features automatic programmable sewing using a 360-degree  rotary sewing head, a 9-needle embroidery head for decorative stitching, and a 6-pin rotary perforation  system to punch and create ornamentation and decorative patterns. As imported the unit includes a  servo-controlled CNC positioning system, automatic thread trimming, an automatic vacuum cleaning system,  an automatic lubrication system, and CAD pattern software.
