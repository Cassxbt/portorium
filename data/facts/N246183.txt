The Powerqube, Model Number PQ0812 is a square-shaped electrical power strip in a form of a plastic housing measuring approximately 6 inches square by 2 inches tall. The item has six 3-prong AC electrical outlets/receptacles and three USB ports along its three sides. Inside the housing there is a custom designed printed circuit (PC) board. Three 3-prong AC outlets/receptacles are located on each side of the housing. They are powered directly from the main “plug-in” power source through a 5½ foot power cord that is permanently attached to the housing. There is a “Joemex 77 Series Push to Reset thermal Circuit Breaker” in the back side of the housing. The Powerqube is designed to distribute the electric power and it also has the ability to charge 3 devices via the USB ports.
