The merchandise under consideration is identified as the J1 Pod with Cap, which consists of an empty plastic tank, a heating element, and a plastic cap. The J1 Pod is intended to hold liquids for use with personal vaporizers and functions as the mouthpiece and reservoir when attached to an appropriate battery unit. In use, the J1 Pod is filled with vaporizing fluid and inserted into a battery unit. The two electrical contacts on the J1 Pod connect the internal heating element to the electrical power source thereby producing vapor that the user inhales.
