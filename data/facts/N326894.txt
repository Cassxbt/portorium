Images and product specifications were submitted in lieu of samples. There are three articles under consideration in this request, a housing base, a housing top, and an off-the-shelf picatinny weaver rail clamp. The housing base and top are made of PU8150 plastic and are rectangular in shape. The housing base will also include a molded in seal made of PU8400 plastic. Together they will serve as a housing for a system control unit (SCU) board that allows the end user to remotely control an X-ray device. Per your submission, at the time of importation, there will be no wires, connectors, terminals, or any electronics mounted to or within the SCU housing. The 21-millimeter picatinny weaver rail base is made of aircraft aluminum and has an anodized finish. The rail clamp consists of the following components: the base, an attachment screw, an adjustment screw, an adjustment plate, an adjustment screw housing, a release lever, and a connection screw. It will be imported as an assembled part, and after importation will screw onto the back of the housing top. The clamp will secure the housing to the X-ray device.
