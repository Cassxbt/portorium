The goods in question are frozen, peeled, cooked, farm-raised shrimp. The production process is described as follows:Fresh shrimp are peeled. Shrimp are deveined (a small cut on the back of each shrimp is done so that the small vein can be removed). No flesh is lost in this process. Shrimp are fully cooked in a steam cooker. Shrimp are cooled down in a cooling unit. Shrimp are individually quick frozen (IQF) in an IQF tunnel. Shrimp are weighed and packed in sealed plastic bags (either 5-pound or 2-pound bags). Shrimp are stored in the freezer until ready for shipment.
