A sample of a novelty baseball, described as the “Image Ball Ornament,” was received with your inquiry. The baseball is constructed with an external leatherette/PVC covering embossed with four batters and their names from the Major League Baseball Red Sox team. The ball also contains the players’ “signatures” and a sewn-in “B” for Boston. While the article appears to be a baseball, it does not meet the regulation weight or construction of a traditional baseball. The sample is being retained by this office.
