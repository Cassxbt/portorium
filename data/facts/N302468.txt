Descriptive literature and pictures were provided with your request. The items under consideration have been identified as an UPR Roll Rod (Item Number 21970-S1000), RR RH Brake Hose Assembly (Item Number 58738-S1500), and a FRT Strut (Item Number 54610-G2000), which are designed for use in motor vehicles. The UPR Roll Rod Assembly consists of insulators with an inner core and outer sleeve of steel, and a layer of vulcanized natural rubber between the two metal components. You state that the function of this assembly is to reduce vibration and noise from the engine by means of the rubber element. The RR RH Brake Hose Assembly consists of a rubber hose with steel fittings. The function of the hose is to transfer oil pressure to the brake system to stop the vehicle. The FRT Strut is constructed of mainly steel with a small rubber element. It is a component of the suspension system, which functions as a noise and vibration damper.
