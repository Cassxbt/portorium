The merchandise under consideration, identified by part number 1127215, is described as a power supply cable designed to be used with the Respironics Trilogy Evo ventilator, a medical device intended to assist those individuals with chronic breathing issues. The cable consists of two insulated cable conductors terminated at each end with specially designed connectors that allow connection only to the aforementioned ventilator. The subject cable is incorporated within the ventilator to electrically connect the power supply to the internal electrical components.
