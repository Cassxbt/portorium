The merchandise under consideration is referred to as White Delicatus stone. A sample was submitted with  your ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. White Delicatus is a white, cream, and light gray stone with black streaks and dark reddish-brown flecks. From the information you provided, it will measure approximately 2 meters long by 3 meters high and either  2 or 3 centimeters in thickness. After importation, it will be cut to size and shape for both indoor and outdoor  use in such applications as countertops, wall cladding, surfaces, and flooring. Laboratory analysis has determined that White Delicatus is an igneous rock comprised of quartz and albite. The top and sides are polished and the edges are beveled.
