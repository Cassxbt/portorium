The first product, Acceleron® B-300 SAT (“B-300”), is based on Penicillium bilaiae spores, as the active ingredient. B-300 is a biological seed applied technology. The active ingredient in B-300 is Penicillium bilaiae spores, a naturally occurring soil fungus, which releases nutrients such as phosphate that are bound in the soil. B-300 improves phosphate availability, which can lead to enhanced early vigor, bolstered root and shoot development and increased yield potential. It will be imported packed for retail sale in a liquid formulation. The second product, Acceleron® B-360 ST (“B-360”), is based on Lipo-chitooligosaccharide (“LCO”), as the active ingredient. B-360 is used as a seed treatment that features Lipo-chitooligosaccharide (LCO) technology. The LCO in this product stimulates germination of mycorrhizal spores and promotes mycorrhizal symbioses in the root zone. It will be imported packed for retail sale in a liquid formulation. The third product, TagTeam® LCO (“TagTeam LCO”), contains Bradyrhizobium japonicum, Penicillium bilaiae, and LCO, as the active ingredients. TagTeam LCO combines three active ingredients, a specially selected bacterium Rhizobium inoculant for increased nitrogen fixation with the phosphate solubilizing Pencillium bilaiae fungi and LCO technology. The benefits of using TagTeam LCO include increased nitrogen fixation and uptake, improved phosphate availability, enhanced nutrient availability, which supports root and shoot growth and a higher yield potential. In a follow-up email to this office, you stated that Tag Team LCO will be imported packed for retail sale in both a liquid form and in a granular form. In the granular form, all three active ingredients are included in the granule. The liquid form is sold as a three-component retail package, which includes the Bradyrhizobium japonicum in liquid formulation, the LCO in a separate liquid formulation and the Penicillium bilaiae as a bottle of wettable powder.
