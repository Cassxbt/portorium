The article under consideration is a roof rack system engineered and configured for a specific vehicle. The product named “SpaceRack” is composed of extruded aluminum profiles of alloy EN AW-6063 T66,  laser CNC-cut aluminum plates of alloy AlMg3 (EN AW-5754), and stainless steel components and fasteners  (approximately 70% aluminum and 30% stainless steel by weight). The components are laser-cut to vehicle  unique specifications. The system is imported unassembled as one complete kit, containing all necessary  parts required for installation. Once assembled, the device is permanently mounted on the roof of the specific  motor vehicle and serves to carry loads on that vehicle.
