The item under consideration has been identified as a 1982 Land Rover Series 3, VIN SALLBAAG1AA59980 (UK Plate PKY 220X). Although you state in your request that this is a MY 1983 vehicle, the VC5 registration, which you provided to this office via email on October 3, 2019, shows the date of manufacturer as 11/1982. This Land Rover is equipped with a 2,286 cc spark-ignition internal combustion reciprocating piston engine (gas), a right-hand drive system and a removable soft-top. Although the VC5 describes this Land Rover as having a “light 4x4” body with an open rear area, there are a pair of permanently mounted side facing rear seats, and you affirmed having had rear safety belt installed before importation (photographs provided to this office), making this a vehicle for the transportation of five (5) passengers.
