Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as the SD-D05 (A·TRACS), which is a composite, self-contained, battery-powered electronic sensor tag enclosed in a compact plastic housing. It is designed primarily as a wireless environmental-monitoring and condition-sensing tag for utilization in transportation, logistics, and supply-chain asset management. The device incorporates an array of integrated micro-sensors engineered to measure, log, and monitor ambient environmental criteria (including temperature, relative humidity, light exposure, and tri-axial acceleration (shock/tilt)) to verify the structural and thermal integrity of commercial cargo during transit. Data acquisition is managed by an internal microcontroller that automatically cross-references environmental measurements against user-defined safety thresholds. The device features a short-range wireless Bluetooth communication transmitter that transmits recorded telemetry, real-time status alerts, and historical data logs to external gateway receivers, handheld mobile terminals, or centralized cloud networks.
