Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as the AngleGenie (model AG-01), which is a battery-powered electronic angle-tracking and measurement device designed to assist users in maintaining a precise bevel angle during the manual sharpening of cutlery edges on a whetstone. The device features a compact composite housing containing a rigid-flex printed circuit board assembly (PCBA). The core electronic components include a six-axis Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU), incorporating a 3-axis accelerometer and a 3-axis gyroscope, a micro-controller unit (MCU), LED light indicators, a low-energy Bluetooth transceiver module, and a lithium coin cell battery. In operation, the AngleGenie is temporarily secured to a standard kitchen knife. Upon activation, the device utilizes gravity and its internal IMU to establish a calibrated horizontal baseline relative to the sharpening surface. As the blade is moved across a sharpening medium, the device continuously sends real-time pitch and roll angle data of the blade to the mobile device. The information is processed by the software into angular coordinates which are sent to the user as feedback to indicate whether the blade is within the user’s targeted degree of inclination (e.g., a 13-degree or 15-degree bevel). The integrated Bluetooth module provides the real-time streaming of angular telemetry data to a paired smartphone or computing device hosting an external graphical application. The app sends LED commands back to the device over the same link, so the LED can signal the user while their eyes are on the blade.
