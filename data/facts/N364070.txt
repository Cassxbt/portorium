The merchandise under consideration is described as Caliper-Integrated Electronic Parking Actuator Assembly, part number 51784330, which is an electro-mechanical actuator designed exclusively for use in an automotive Electronic Parking Brake (EPB) system. The assembly is comprised of a 12V brushed DC motor with maximum power output of 200W, multi-stage planetary reduction gears, a lead screw, seals, and electrical terminals housed in a plastic enclosure. The actuator assembly is intended to be mated to a compatible brake caliper assembly to generate linear force that actuates the brake piston to apply or release the brake. We note that the brake calipers and brake pistons are not imported with the actuator assembly.
