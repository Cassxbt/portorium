In the future, please limit your ruling request to a maximum of five items of the same class or kind. In your request, you described these sleeves as cellphone wallets. They are listed as items numbers: SM-2556, SM-2557, SM-2559, SM-2562, SM-2565, and SM-2568. These articles are made wholly of molded plastic. They are intended to be attached to the back of a cellphone to allow the user to store identification and/or credit/debit cards. All adhere to the cellphone back using adhesive, except item number SM2565, which features silicone plastic straps that warp around the corners of the phone. Item number SM2565 also includes a molded silicone plastic lanyard. Item number SM-2568 is built with a RFID signal blocker. Please note, these articles are similar to the plastic sleeves ruled upon in New York Ruling N300913, dated October 24, 2018.
