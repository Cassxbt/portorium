GS-1100, Gloss, Matte Gold Transfer Film is a metallized polyethylene terephthalate (PET) sheet with  acrylic release and scuff-resistant coating. MG-2100, Gloss, Matte Silver Transfer Film is a metallized PET sheet with acrylic release and scuff-resistant  coating. HF-3100, Holographic Transfer Film is a metallized PET sheet with a holographic pattern and abrasion  resistant coating.
