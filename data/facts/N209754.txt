Two samples, labeled Pro-Angle II, in 5 inch and 10 inch length versions, were provided. Per the description on the retail package for these Models 822 and 823, each “measures corners precisely for accurate miter cuts of moldings or frames with no waste.”     Each item is primarily two metal straightedges, about 1.3 inches wide, marked with millimeters on one edge and inches on the other. They meet at a hinge which can be locked in place for transferring the angle for marking on the work to be cut. The angle made between the two blades is displayed in degrees on a digital readout so it is not necessary to place it over a protractor to determine the size of the angle, if desired.
