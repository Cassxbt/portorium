The merchandise under consideration is referred to as White Ice stone. A sample was submitted with your  ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. White Ice is an off-white stone with light gray to charcoal gray mottling. From the information you  provided, it will measure between 115 to 122 inches in length, between 65 to 75 inches in width, and either  0.75 inches or 1.5 inches in thickness. After importation, it will be cut to size and shape for such applications  as kitchen countertops, bathroom surfaces, and surfaces for office furniture. Laboratory analysis has determined that White Ice is an other igneous rock comprised predominantly of  quartz and plagioclase feldspars. The top and sides are polished and the edges are beveled.
