The subject product, Earth Alive Soil Activator, is a soil inoculant consisting of a blend of three strains of bacteria (Bacillus subtilis, Bacillus amyloliquefaciens and Pseudomonas monteilii) on a proprietary carrier. It also contains another ingredient to help maintain the pH level that is necessary for the viability of the bacteria. According to the marketing literature, Earth Alive Soil Activator will regenerate and reactivate depleted soils, increases nutrient uptake, improve soil water holding capacity and help plants develop a stronger and healthier root system. It is available in 4 kg pails, 25 kg pails and 500 kg bulk totes.
