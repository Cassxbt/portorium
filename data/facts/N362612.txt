The product under consideration is described as the “Actisweep Industrial Push Broom with Bristle  Segments.”  It is a heavy-duty, non-motorized mechanical sweeping device designed to be mounted onto a  motorized host vehicle, such as a forklift, agricultural tractor, or telehandler, for intensive industrial-grade  indoor and outdoor cleaning operations. The broom is comprised of a rectangular steel frame, a brush system that features multiple rows of  high-density polypropylene (PP) fiber bristles, and a hitch system, which is a heavy mechanical interface  made of galvanized steel that is bolted or fastened to the top of the steel frame and is interchangeable  depending on the user’s host vehicle. The underside of the steel frame features integrated, factory-welded  tracking rails specifically dimensioned to hold, secure, and allow the sliding exchange of the bristle rows. The sweeping action is generated by the friction and forward mechanical propulsion provided by the host  vehicle to which the brush is attached. The industrial push broom is available in three models which vary in  the number of bristle rows and in bristle length. Per your submission, the steel frame is manufactured in Turkey and the bristles are manufactured in the  European Union. The push broom is imported unassembled but consolidated in a single complete kit ready  for final bolt-on assembly by the end user.
