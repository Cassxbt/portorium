The product under consideration is Item 501-00001-000.A1-Grease, Cable, Headband. The product is a  silicone grease described as a translucent, hydrophobic, silicone dielectric compound. It is composed of over  90 percent poly(dimethyl siloxane) with silicon dioxide filler and other stabilizing additives. The product is  used to seal, waterproof, lubricate, and insulate both indoor and outdoor electrical connections.
