The item under consideration are referred to as a Cup, Contoured, Left (Part number 303-00191-000.A4). This item is an ear cup assembly for Lightspeed’s Zulu H aviation headset. The left ear cup assembly is  composed of a plastic blend of polycarbonate and acrylonitrile butadiene styrene resin, molded to a dome  shape. This design allows for active and passive noise reduction by creating a physical barrier to isolate and  block external noise and facilitate the active and passive noise cancellation function of the headset.
