The ruling was requested on a wooden pet bed with a foam cushion. A picture and a description of the product were submitted. The bed measures 55-1/2” long by 24-5/8” deep by 20” high. It is a high-end style bed with a headboard, a footboard and a platform bottom with drawers. A foam cushion with a synthetic sheepskin cover, measuring 45” long by 24.5” deep by 2” high, fits on top of the platform bottom. The bed is made of a combination of solid wood, veneered particleboard and medium density fiberboard. The wooden pet bed with a cushion is a composite good consisting of different components and materials.
