Five samples were submitted. Series 777 is disposable patient examination glove made from nitrile polymer. The 4 mil, 9.5-inch ambidextrous glove has a rolled cuff and comes both powdered and powder free. Series 610 is a disposable glove for industrial use made from nitrile polymer. The 6 mil, 11-inch ambidextrous glove has a rolled cuff and is lightly powdered. Series 898 is a disposable glove for heavier industrial use made from nitrile polymer. The 8 mil, 11-inch ambidextrous glove has a rolled cuff and is lightly powdered. Series 503 is a reusable glove for household use made from flock lined nitrile polymer. The 16 mil, 13-inch glove has a textured finish on the palm and back portions and is hand specific. Series 802 is a finely knit glove with a heavy dipped coating of nitrile polymer. The 28 mil, 13-inch glove has a textured finish on the palm and back portions and is hand specific.
