Your inquiry describes a line of hose accessories and connectors that are designed to work with standard garden and irrigation hoses that range in diameter from 12mm to 25mm. All of the components are made of ABS plastic. Specifically your request covers the following pieces: adjustable hose nozzles, parts no. 1250010 and 1240045; tenderly shower sprayer, part no. 1200051; adjustable multipurpose spray gun, parts no. 1250053/1250081 and 1250056/1250082; hose attachments, parts no. 1250005, 1250070, 1250024, 1250025 and 1250051; and hose attachments with built-in shut-off valves, parts no. 1250006 and 1250072. The hose nozzles are twist type garden hose flow control nozzles, while the "tenderly shower sprayer" is an add-on attachment that converts a solid stream of water into a fine spray by forcing the water through 540 pin-sized holes. The spray gun is a trigger-type hose nozzle with pistol grip. The hose attachments are connectors that allow various accessories, such as nozzles, to be quickly connected to the hose. The ones with built-in shut-off valves are designed to stop the flow of water from the hose when one of the attachments is disconnected.
