The item concerned is a bare printed circuit board (PCB) referred to as the “Trilogy MMV PCB” (item #1035178+R5). This PCB is designed, manufactured and used solely with the Respironics’ Trilogy Ventilator devices. The Trilogy ventilator provides continuous or intermittent positive pressure ventilation for the care of individuals who require mechanical ventilation. The Trilogy MMV PCB is a custom ten layer printed circuit board, custom designed to fit and function with the Trilogy ventilator device. It is an integral part of, and used exclusively with the Trilogy series of ventilators.
