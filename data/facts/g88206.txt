You have submitted a sample of a pair of children’s size open-heel, open toe “rope sandals” with upper straps and outer soles both comprised of interwoven corded rope materials, which you state are of polypropylene textile fibers. This sandal is considered to have both an upper and a bottom/outer sole of man-made textile materials for footwear classification purposes. Your letter states that the finished rope sandal footwear sample you are submitting is actually made in the USA, but in the future you plan to import completed rope sandal footwear outer soles from China, made of the same materials. Since you also explain that an actual sample of only the rope sandal outer soles that you intend to import into the USA is not yet available, we will presume that for the purposes of this ruling we are being asked to classify only an outer sole identical, or almost identical to the one that is part of this finished submitted sample sandal.
