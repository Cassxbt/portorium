The merchandise under consideration is identified by part number 300-00049-001.A1, Cable, Longer Upper. The subject cable is comprised of a length of insulated cable conductor terminated at each end with a  multi-pin connector. The cable is meant to be used specifically with the Delta Zulu aviation headset in order  to connect the control box to the headset. The cable is meant to carry electrical power signals as well as  microphone and speaker signals, allowing the pilot to hear air traffic control while providing active noise  cancellation.
