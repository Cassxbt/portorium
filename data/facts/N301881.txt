You submitted representative samples of drawstring gift bags. The gift bag is made up using two layers of manmade fabric. The outer layer is a transparent mesh constructed of nylon and polyester fabric and the inner layer is constructed of a nonwoven polypropylene fabric. The bag is cut and stitched along the sides with an open top. The open top is closed by means of a drawstring ribbon. The gift bag will be imported in one style and various sizes from small to large. All the gift bags will be used to hold and decorate a gift item.
