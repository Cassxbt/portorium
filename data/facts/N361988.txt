A sample of the product was  provided to this office and will be retained for training purposes. The sample, identified as a “Heating Pad” is a Berkshire® electric heating pad designed to be worn over the  wearer’s shoulders, neck, and back and is intended to provide heat therapy to relax tight muscles and warmth. The heating pad is composed of four layers consisting of: a top and bottom outer shell of 100 percent  polyester knitted plush, dyed, fabrics; a second layer of 100 percent polyester nonwoven needle-punched  fabric; and a third layer of 100 percent polyester nonwoven needle-punched fabric bonded to heating wire. When laid flat, the cape-like heating pad measures 26 inches in width and 34 inches in length. When worn, it  provides a contoured fit measuring 23 inches across the shoulders and 28 inches in height. It features two  sets of snap buttons in the center front to secure the item around the shoulders and a 1 ½ inch wide by  23-inch-long self-fabric strap sewn to either side designed to be tied across the abdomen. The heating pad is  kept in place by glass beads partially incorporated along the edges of the collar and the bottom edge of the  pad. The heating pad also features a detachable handheld controller with ten temperature settings and three  timer options ranging from one to three hours, with a two-hour auto-off safety default setting.
