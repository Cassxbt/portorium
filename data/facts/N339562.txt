Item 9R254710, the “All-Season Infant Car Seat Cover,” is a cut to shape textile fabric canopy that is contoured to fit the removeable motor vehicle child safety bucket seat component found on combination car seats/infant strollers. The article is comprised of knit and woven polyester panels and is designed to provide shade from the sun, temporary protection from wind, rain, or snow, as well as incorporating an optional-semi-enclosure for privacy when fully extended. The article dimensions approximate 24” in length and 20” in width. The article is not permanent and is designed for temporary easy installation and removal. The final purchaser will choose the manner in which the article is used, whether it be a cover for the infant car seat component only or in conjunction with a combination infant stroller.
