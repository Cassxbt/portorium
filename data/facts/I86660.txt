The assortment submitted, Item #930656, “Easter Egg Pop-Out Surprise”, consists of five stuffed animals (3 bunnies, 1 pig and 1 duck) attached inside five colored plastic eggs. When the plastic egg is squeezed, the egg opens and the animal pops up. The animal can be pushed back into the egg, closed, and the process repeated. The 3 bunnies (pink, blue and white) are decorated with a carrot on their chest. The white pig is decorated with 3 colored eggs and the words “Happy Easter.”  The yellow chick is decorated with 3 colored eggs and the words “Happy Easter.” The animals although not symbols themselves of Easter, are draped in colored eggs, which are acceptable symbols of the holiday.
