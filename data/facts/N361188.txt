Thestake puller consists of a rigid steel frame incorporating a screw-driven linear lifting mechanism using athreaded rod and corresponding nut assembly. Rotation of the threaded rod causes a movable carriage totravel vertically along the frame, applying a controlled pulling force to extract the embedded object.The stake puller is powered by an external portable impact wrench, not included with importation, whichengages the threaded rod assembly. The stake puller incorporates thrust bearings, bushings, interchangeabletool-holding plates for different impact wrench types, and a ground support base for stabilization duringoperation.
