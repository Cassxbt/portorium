The merchandise in question are two transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (TENS) systems, (Model #N602-1C and Model #0434 Comfy TENS). The TENS systems are intended to deliver electrical impulses to nerve fibers for the purpose of pain relief. Model #N602-1C system is packaged ready for retail sale and includes a dual channel electronic nerve stimulator, four adhesive electrode pads and connecting cables, a 9 volt battery, and an instruction manual. The electronic nerve stimulator unit measures 9.5 cm x 6.5 cm x 2.5 cm and incorporates various selection/operation buttons on the front panel, and two electrical sockets and user dials located on the top of the device. The two electrical cables incorporate connectors on each end which connect the stimulator unit to the electrode pads. The electrode pads are placed directly on the user’s skin at the desired region of the body and the TENS system delivers electrical impulses to stimulate the user’s nerves. Model #0434 Comfy TENS system is packaged ready for retail sale and includes a dual channel electronic nerve stimulator with LCD display screen, four adhesive electrode pads and connecting cables, a 9 volt battery, and an instruction manual. The electronic nerve stimulator unit measures 10 cm x 6 cm x 2.5 cm and incorporates various selection/operation buttons on the front panel, and two electrical sockets and user dials located on the top of the device. The two electrical cables incorporate connectors on each end which connect the stimulator unit to the electrode pads. The electrode pads are placed directly on the user’s skin at the desired region of the body and the TENS system delivers electrical impulses to stimulate the user’s nerves.
