Images were provided in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described as polypropylene plastic protective outer sleeves designed for  vinyl record jackets. They are intended to be used to store, protect, and preserve vinyl records and their outer  jackets during storage, handling, display, and transport. Further, these sleeves help protect record jackets  from dust, scratches, moisture, fingerprints, handling marks, and general wear. They will be sold as a pack of  100 sleeves in retail packaging.
