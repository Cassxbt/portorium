The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Echo Smart Activ, which is further described as an acoustic location aid for visually impaired dogs. The subject device is comprised of two piezoelectric ultrasonic emitters along with internal electronic control circuitry and an internal rechargeable battery. These components are enclosed within a plastic housing and attached to a collar strap, which is meant to be worn on the neck of the dog. In use, the device is placed on the neck of the blind or visually impaired animal and emits an acoustic sound that bounces off the objects in the surrounding area. The dog hears the sound reverberations and perceives the objects, allowing the dog to move around the area freely. The device does not receive or process the sound; it only emits and allows the dog to develop a form of echo location.
