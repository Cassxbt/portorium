The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Copenhagen stone. Samples were submitted with your  ruling request and were forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Copenhagen is a cream, light gray, charcoal, and amber mottled stone with a polished surface. From the  information you provided, it measures approximately 125 inches long by 78 inches wide by 2 or 3  centimeters thick, and is designed for use in such applications as countertops, backsplashes, and flooring. Laboratory analysis has determined that Copenhagen is an other igneous stone.
