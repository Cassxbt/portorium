The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Istambul stone. A sample was submitted with your  ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Istambul is a cream-colored stone with streaks and flecks of dark brown, gray, and black. From the  information you provided, it will measure between 115 to 122 inches in length, between 65 to 75 inches in  width, and either 0.75 inches or 1.5 inches in thickness. After importation, it will be cut to size and shape for  such applications as kitchen countertops, bathroom surfaces, and surfaces for office furniture. Laboratory analysis has determined that Istambul is an igneous stone comprised predominantly of quartz and  alkali feldspars, and meets the geological definition of granite. The top and sides of the stone has been  polished and the edges have been beveled.
