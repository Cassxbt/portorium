The items, described as “Handmade Hand-Fans,” are foldable fans intended to be used by waving it back and forth to provide a cool stream of airflow to a person’s face. The folded fabric is glued to the ribs of either the wood or plastic frame on one side. The wood frames are held together by a metal rivet and the plastic frames are held together by a plastic rivet at the bottom of the fan.
