This is a response to your letter dated September 3, 2019 requesting a tariff classification, country of origin determination and the applicability of the United States - Korea Free Trade Agreement (UKFTA) to Frozen Tuna Loins imported from Korea on behalf of your client, Wismettac Asian Foods (Santa Fe Springs, CA). You submitted a flow chart depicting the various stages of operation and a pictorial representation of the product. You have outlined a scenario in which tuna is caught by a Chinese-flag vessel in Chinese ocean waters. Onboard the vessel, the heads, tails, gills and guts will be removed from the fish, which will then be frozen and exported to Korea. Although lacking heads, tails or viscera, the tuna will arrive in Korea having the basic look of a whole fish. In Korea, fish will be processed into loins, frozen, vacuum packed individually and then exported to the United States.
