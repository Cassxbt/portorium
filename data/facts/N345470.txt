The ruling was requested on painted, beaded fiberboard panels. Product information and a sample were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is a panel measuring 4’ wide by 8’ long and which may range in thickness from 4.8mm to 19mm. The fiberboard panels are made from the “dry production process”, wherein dry wood fiber is agglomerated with resin and pressed into panels. The panels are then continuously shaped with regularly spaced beads. The panels are to be used as decorative wall coverings.
