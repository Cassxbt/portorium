Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is identified as a wall-mounted sword and scabbard support, retention and display fixture. The fixture measures approximately 42" tall x 12" wide x 3/4" thick. The fixture is manufactured from a walnut (a non-coniferous wood) edge-glued lumber plaque that is grooved over most of its surface with a decorative lined design, and is fitted with four iron rings attached to leather loops. The two upper leather loops hold the crossguard of the sword and the lower leather loop holds the blade in its scabbard. The leather loops hold the sword and scabbard against the walnut board so as to look like the sword and scabbard are mounted on a wooden plaque. The sword and scabbard are not imported with the display fixture. The display fixture is predominantly wood and the metal rings and leather loops comprise a very small portion of the fixture. Therefore, the fixture will be classified by the wood portion.
