The merchandise of your request is referred to as the “T.V. Sound Box Wireless Speaker System.”  It is marketed as a wireless extension speaker for home television systems. The retail package contains a wireless speaker with a built-in radio frequency receiver and lithium ion battery, a transmitter base which also serves as a charging dock for the receiver, a 100-240VAC power adapter and an audio cable. In use the base unit would be connected to the audio output of a television or other audio/video device using the included audio cable. The speaker system utilizes a 2.4 GHz radio frequency signal to wirelessly transmit an audio signal from the base unit to the wireless speaker.
