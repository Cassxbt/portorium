The articles under consideration are described as the JL Hood Hinge Pivot Pin, part numbers AA1381-P009AC and AA1381-P009AB. Based on your technical drawing, the pins are made of carbon steel and are 93.72mm long and have a body that is 8.83mm in diameter. The pins are plated with tin zinc. Both ends of the pin are knurled. The largest knurled end is 9.34mm in diameter and the smallest is 7.29mm in diameter. Protruding from the end with the smallest diameter is a 3mm tip with a chamfered end. The pivot pins are designed for use in a hood hinge for the Jeep Wrangler. The hood hinge connects a moveable vehicle hood to the fixed vehicle body. The hood hinge is made up of a hood bracket and a body bracket. The pivot pin serves to connect the two brackets and enables the hood bracket to pivot, thereby allowing the vehicle hood to open and close.
