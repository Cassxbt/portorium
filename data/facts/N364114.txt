The first items under consideration are described as remote monitoring units (model numbers RMU1, INT1 measurement expansion peripheral, ERP1 measurement expansion peripheral, RMU1 Lite, RMU1 SUB, RMU1ER, RMU2, RMU3, RDL1, and SUNBIRD SP). Each device is part of the Mobiltex CorTalk product line, which offers a comprehensive suite of Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) solutions designed to automate cathodic protection (CP) data collection, enhance structural integrity, and streamline compliance reporting for pipeline and utility operators. At the core of the ecosystem is the RMU1, which is a compact, battery-powered remote monitoring unit that fits within standard 3-inch test stations to capture precise AC and DC parameters, such as pipe-to-soil potentials and structural bond applications, with cellular or satellite connectivity and a 10-year battery life. Several specialized variations of the RMU1 exist to address specific field requirements: the RMU1-SUB is engineered for subgrade valve boxes to withstand water immersion and heavy traffic; the RMU1-LITE provides a streamlined, cost-effective option for basic pipe-to-soil potential measurements; and the RMU1-ER is specifically designed to measure metal loss and corrosion rates via electrical resistance (ER) probes. Furthermore, the RMU1 can be enhanced with peripherals such as the INT1, which enables GPS-synchronized current interruption for critical bonds and anodes, and the ERP1, which expands the system’s capability to log ER corrosion probe data. The RMU2 offers a two-piece design for continuous tracking of rectifiers and AC mitigation systems, while the RMU3 provides an advanced, two-piece solution that integrates both remote monitoring and GPS-synchronized interruption for critical infrastructure. Other specialized units include the RDL1, a hybrid device combining a stationary data logger with an RMU, and the SUNBIRD-SP, a solar-powered unit designed for remote or difficult-to-access locations that eliminates
the need for manual battery maintenance. Each device has a recording device, measures current/voltage and integrates seamlessly with the cloud-based Mobiltex CorView platform, providing operators with real-time visualization, instant alerts, and simplified data management. The second items under consideration are described as data loggers (model numbers uDL1, uDL2, and BMM1). The uDL1 is a rugged, pocket-sized logger designed for high-precision AC and DC measurements, featuring an integrated LCD and advanced capabilities such as DC Wave Capture and Interruption Tracking. The uDL2 is a two-channel device specifically engineered for monitoring CP coupons and pipeline integrity, utilizing a 1.01-ohm shunt resistor for current sensing, high input impedance, and robust AC noise rejection to ensure data stability. While both the uDL1 and uDL2 are designed to fit under standard test station caps for secure, long-term deployment, the BMM1 functions as a versatile wireless data logger that supports both handheld and stationary use. It distinguishes itself through Bluetooth connectivity for real-time data backhaul and integrated GPS time synchronization, making it particularly effective for large-scale close interval surveys. Although the individual instruments differ in configuration and intended application, each performs the same fundamental function of measuring electrical quantities, processing, recording and storing the resulting measured values, and communicating those measured values to authorized users for review and evaluation.
