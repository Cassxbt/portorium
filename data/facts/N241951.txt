The merchandise under consideration is several large industrial air handling units, identified as the AW-I-3 series, the PMI-IF series and the AW-D series. The units are constructed primarily of galvanized or galvannealed steel with copper and aluminum heating and cooling coils. Each unit contains a blowing device integral to the unit. The AW-I-3 and PMI-IF series are indirect gas fired furnaces and make-up air units. The AW-D series is a direct gas fired make-up air unit. The AW-I-3 series and the PMI-IF series incorporate internal exhaust flues for burnt gases. The AW-I-3 series features an efficient 3-pass stainless steel drum and a tube style heat exchanger. The PMI-IF series is a convoluted tube furnace with a stainless steel heat exchanger. The AW-D series incorporates a high efficiency burner, which is capable of a high turndown ratio, maximizing discharge air temperature control with minimal combustion by-product.
