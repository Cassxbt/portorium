There are three articles under consideration that you identify as the Motor Cover, Item 198-3108, the Nameplate Raw, Item 198-202, and the Rotor Housing, Item 198-3116, which are described as components to an electric motor circulator pump. Based on the information provided, the Motor Cover and the Nameplate Raw, which are constructed of molded plastic, are intended to fit together to house the motor’s printed circuit board assembly (PCBA). You state that the PCBA’s function is to convert incoming AC electricity to the DC electricity usable by the motor. While the Motor Cover is specially molded to encapsulate the PCBA and attaches directly to the motor housing, it is not part of the motor and its function is to attach and enclose the PCBA. Further, we would note that the Nameplate Raw forms the front facing plate for the Motor Cover and has a cutout for a light pipe window that indicates operation and a cutout for mechanical flow speed indication. Item 198-3116 is referred to as the Rotor Housing and is described as a plastic pump component that houses the rotor and functions as an interface for the rotor and the impeller shaft. Once the rotor housing is secured to the motor, the impeller shaft presses on the rotor housing, allowing water to circulate to the impeller and the rotor.
