The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Desert Atlas stone. A sample was submitted with your  ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Desert Atlas is a sage gray stone with large patches of dark green, olive, amber, and cream. From the  information you provided, it will measure between 115 to 122 inches in length, between 65 to 75 inches in  width, and either 0.75 inches or 1.5 inches in thickness. After importation, it will be cut to size and shape for  such applications as kitchen countertops, bathroom surfaces, and surfaces for office furniture. Laboratory analysis has determined that Desert Atlas is an igneous rock which contains almost no quartz. The  top of the stone has been polished and the sides are simply cut or sawn.
