The item under consideration is a magnetic article sorting system (MASS). The system is designed for use in  material recovery and recycling facilities to separate ferrous metal from mixed recyclable waste streams  without human assistance. The MASS consists of an integrated overhead permanent magnet, magnet  housing/support assembly, ferrous discharge chute/deflector arrangement, conveyor, hopper/sidewall  assembly, guarding, controls, and related support components. In operation, mixed recyclable material is loaded into the system and conveyed through a magnetic  separation zone. As the material passes underneath the overhead permanent magnet, ferrous metal is attracted  to the magnetic field and removed from the mixed material stream. The captured item is directed to a  dedicated discharge area designated for ferrous material. The non-ferrous and non-magnetic material  continues through the system for further handling.
