The subject patient lift system is referred to as a Go-Lift with a Patient Lift Pendant and is described as a  complete patient lift solution. The system includes the Amico Go-Lift Patient Lift, previously reviewed in  New York ruling N350412, dated July 9, 2025, and a Patient Lift Pendant. The Patient Lift Pendant is a  ceiling-mounted track system consisting of a structural boom that pivots at the ceiling mount. The boom  contains an electrified track that moves the Go-Lift horizontally along the length of the boom. Suspended  from the far end of the boom is a console with an electrical receptacle. The system is intended for institutions  that prefer not to purchase ceiling tracks separately for the Go-Lift unit.
