In your submission the subject product is described as a roll of pre-moistened, flushable, personal-cleansing wipes, made from plant-derived nonwoven spunlace material, which are impregnated with a surface-active cleaning preparation. The wipes are perforated and wound onto a polypropylene center tube in roll form and are designed to be dispensed from a conventional toilet paper holder. The finished product contains approximately 240 perforated wet sheets on a roll. The roll is approximately 114 mm wide and is supported by a plastic center tube. The finished roll incorporates a protective flexible barrier film and a plastic dispensing/closure component that allows the consumer to access individual wet sheets while helping retain moisture. The product is intended for personal cleansing after toileting.
