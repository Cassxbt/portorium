The merchandise in question is a digital recorder/player device, which is referred to as the “Pocketrak 2G.”   The Pocketrak 2G can record and playback music and data, transfer music and data files to and from a computer, and charge the battery with its built-in USB connector. Moreover, this portable recorder contains 3 gigabytes of built-in semiconductor memory, a built-in speaker, and a microphone in noting that it can record up to 3 hours using the microphone or the built-in line input. The principle function of this 1.7 ounce multifunction machine is to record band rehearsals, music lessons, conferences, and meetings for later review. In its condition as imported, the Pocketrak 2G will be packaged for retail sale with the following: a USB connection cable, earphones, a DVD-ROM, an owner’s manual, a rechargeable battery, and a leather case with holes for a tripod.
