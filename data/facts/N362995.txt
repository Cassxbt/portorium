The item concerned is a light emitting diode (“LED”) status bar assembly referred to as the F2 LED  Assembly, part # 1013272. This status bar assembly is composed of a stainless-steel housing, an LED  diffuser made of polycarbonate, and an LED printed circuit board which is an electronics board made of  flame-retardant fiberglass-reinforced epoxy resin, with the LED chips (RGB LED chips) mounted on it. In use, this status light is installed within the “fastmill.io™ In-Office Mill,” an in-office milling machine,  used in the dental field. The milling machine’s programmable logic controller communicates with the status  light when signals are received from corresponding components/systems within the machine. The status light  provides the user with indications of what is happening within their machine through displays of both solid  lights (multiple color options) and changing light patterns (“breathing”, “blinking”, “wavy”, etc.).
