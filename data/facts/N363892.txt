The merchandise under consideration is referred to as a manual hydraulic mobile container jack carrier,  model number YYLC-20, and is described as a lifting system for ISO shipping containers. It consists of four  corner lifting units, each comprising a steel frame equipped with a twist-lock mechanism (for engaging  container corner castings), a manually operated hydraulic cylinder, and a hand pump. Each frame is  supported by two heavy-duty solid rubber wheels mounted on a single axle. During operation, each corner unit is attached to a lower corner casting of the container. Each corner is lifted  independently using the hand pump, with the four units used together to raise the container. Once the  container is raised, the lifting units are linked via connecting rods, steering rods, and a tow bar to form a  unified system, which is then maneuvered by a towing handle. The lifting units do not have engines, motors,  batteries, transmissions, drive axles, or integrated steering mechanisms. Because they are not self-propelled, a  lifted container must be moved by a towing vehicle, forklift, or tug. The system is rated for a maximum lifting capacity of 20,000 kilograms, a maximum lifting height of 450  millimeters, and a maximum towing speed of 12 kilometers per hour. Each individual corner unit measures  approximately 1.03 meters in length, 0.82 meters in width, and 1.52 meters in height.
