The merchandise under consideration is the Green Cell PowerPlay Ultra Power Bank, model number  PBGC04, a device that features a compact rectangular case with two USB-C ports, two USB-A ports, and  LED indicators. The power bank uses lithium-ion chemistry, has a combined maximum output of 128 W, a  nominal capacity of 26,800 mAh, and is compatible with various fast charging technologies. Its overall  dimensions are approximately 186.1 millimeters in length, 80.9 millimeters in width, and 22.6 millimeters in  height. The power bank can be used to provide additional charging support for the Oskol 2, an augmentative and  alternative communication system previously reviewed in New York Ruling N360335, dated April 21, 2026,  through the USB-C port on the device.
