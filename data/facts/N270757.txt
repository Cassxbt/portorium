You intend to import an assembly that consists of an aluminum distributor with aluminum feeder tubes and a brass and copper Thermostatic Expansion Valve (TXV), part numbers 119F7417 and 119F7289. The distributer with the feeder tubes distributes the refrigerant to the individual sections of the evaporator coil circuit and depending on the part number, may include 2 to 22 feeder tubes that measure from 8 to 44 inches.
