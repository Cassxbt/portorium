The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Desert Atlas stone. A sample was submitted with your  ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Desert Atlas is an ivory-colored stone with patches of olive, dark green, and amber, as well as dark green  veining and streaks. From the information you provided, the top surface area of Desert Atlas measures  approximately 6 square meters, and it is 3 centimeters thick. After importation it will be cut to size and shape  for applications such as countertops and tables. Laboratory analysis has determined that Desert Atlas is an igneous stone which contains almost no quartz. The top is honed and the sides are simply cut or sawn.
