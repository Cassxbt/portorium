Neither a marked sample nor a photograph of the product was submitted with your request. The merchandise at issue are magnetic drive pump units, models SLM AVP, SLM GV and SLM SVO. The drive pump units are used in the petrochemical and refinery industries. Each unit consists of a centrifugal pump from Germany, a motor from Taiwan and a steel base plate with a coupling and coupling guard from Germany. The magnetic coupling connects the electric motor with the pump’s internal magnet and the motor, along with the magnet, drive the pump’s impeller. All of the components are assembled into a complete unit and packaged for export in Germany. Together, the components displace fluid.
