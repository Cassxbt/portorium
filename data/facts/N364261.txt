There are five items at issue with this request, which are described as Wireless Volume Controllers (WVC). The devices are individually identified as follows: WVC Base Station WVC-LITE Base Station WVC-LITE-6 Base Station WVC-PRO Base Station WVC-PRO-6 Base Station We note that the five models listed above are virtually identical in function and only differ in the number of audio channels, control capabilities, and hardware configuration. The subject devices are presented as a metal enclosure, within which are the various electrical components. On the outside of the enclosure are various RCA inputs and outputs as well as radio frequency (RF) antennae. The subject device incorporates an internal digital to analog converter, wireless communication, and a digital control interface. In use, the device is placed within a vehicle and connected to the various components of the stereo system, i.e. speakers, amplifier, etc. An external volume knob, which is not at issue with this request, is then connected wirelessly to the WVC. The knob transmits digital control commands to the WVC, which processes these commands to electronically control the level of the analog audio signals.
