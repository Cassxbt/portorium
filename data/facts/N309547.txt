This is in response to your letter dated February 5, 2020, requesting a ruling on the origin of imported Utility Vehicles. The item under consideration has been identified as the MTD Cub Cadet, a 4-wheeled, utility vehicle powered by a 546cc spark-ignition internal combustion engine. The vehicle is equipped with two (2) bucket seats and an open rear cargo bed with a 500lb payload capacity. The vehicle measures 118 inches by 61.75 inches by 76.75 inches, and weighs approximately 1,456lbs. You state these vehicles will be imported to the United States from Vietnam after assembly in Vietnam as part of a knock down operation.
