The merchandise under consideration is a kit containing components for the Hercules H-DU lift bot, an  automated lifting robot used in Amazon fulfillment centers to lift and transport products. The kit contains  four components: an H-DU Linkage Kit Rotary (part number 400-01256), an H-DU Rear Chassis  Subassembly (part number 400-02390), an H-DU Panhard Left Assembly (part number 400-01281), and an  H-DU Panhard Right Assembly (part number 400-01282). These components can be separated and stored as  inventory, sold individually, or sold together as a kit. The H-DU Linkage Kit Rotary is a subassembly for the inner lifting mechanism of the lifting robot and is  composed of aluminum castings and steel shafts with plastic bushings. This assembly is driven by an electric  motor and used to raise a lifting plate. The motor, gears, and electrical components are not imported with the  subassembly. The H-DU Rear Chassis Subassembly forms the lower structure of the robot and is a die-cast aluminum  frame with plastic bushings. It has a semi-circular profile and features mounting points, cutouts, and notches  throughout its body. The H-DU Panhard Left Assembly and H-DU Panhard Right Assembly are left and right aluminum linkage  components for the inner lifting mechanism. They resemble elongated, rod-like components with mounting  points at each end. In your submission, you asked if the merchandise can be treated as a kit or set.
