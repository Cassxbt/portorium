This ruling is being issued in response to your letter dated April 1, 2025, requesting a tariff classification  determination on behalf of your client, Bassett Furniture Industries Inc., on an etagere. In lieu of samples, a  picture, website URL, and product description were provided. The subject article is identified as the Keswick Bronze and the Keswick Black Etagere, with SKU number  6216-K682. The article measures 38" (W) x 18" (D) x 78" (H) and has a hand-distressed wrought iron frame  with five natural Banswara marble shelves. It is available in either a black iron or textured bronze finish. It is  intended for household use and made in India.
