Product information and a sample were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is a multilayer wood flooring panel. The flooring panel is constructed of 3  wood plies. The face ply, measuring 3.8mm in thickness, consists of a continuous sheet of white oak  (Quercus spp., a non-coniferous wood) wood veneer. The center ply, measuring approximately 9mm in  thickness, consists of blocks of spruce (Picea spp., a coniferous wood) wood. The back ply, measuring  approximately 2mm in thickness, consists of radiata pine (Pinus radiata, a coniferous wood) veneer. The  grain of the center ply runs at a 90-degree angle to that of the face and back plies. The surface of the face ply  is coated with a clear urethane that does not obscure the face ply. The panel is tongued and grooved on its  edges and ends.
