The Turnout Remover and Carrier System (TRACS) is a type of railroad infrastructure maintenance and  construction machine. It is designed to lift and transport prefabricated railway track sections, such as railway  switch and crossing track panels, underneath its frame. The machine has an adjustable frame, allowing it to  straddle railway tracks of different rail gauges. The TRACS is self-propelled, diesel-powered, and remotely operated. It features hydraulically powered  telescopic columns at each corner of its rectangular frame. The columns are mounted on two mobile crawler  track bases, and the frame is equipped with an engine, a startup control box, a remote-control receiver, and a  central lifting assembly. The lifting assembly consists of a high-capacity hydraulic ram with 1.219 meters of  vertical travel, and can be fitted with various beam attachments, such as standard H beams, hydraulic H  beams, and hydraulic panel grabs. These attachments have anchor points for adjustable chains or lift straps,  which are used to secure and lift different track sections. The TRACS will be imported in several models, designated as TRACS Alpha, TRACS Bravo, and TRACS  Charlie. All three are functionally the same but differ in overall dimensions and extendibility, with overall  lengths ranging from 3.292 meters to 6.37 meters.
