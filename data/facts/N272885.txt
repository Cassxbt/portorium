The merchandise under consideration is referred to as the CardAccess™ Heavy-Duty Power Controller (HPC), model number HPC10A-ZP. This device is described as a high voltage power controller specifically designed for use with the Control4® wireless home automation system. The HPC is housed in a metal enclosure that measures 4.6 inches in height, 10.5 inches in width, and 2.2 inches in depth, and has an external antenna attached to it for receiving radio frequency (RF) signals from the Control4® host controller, which you refer to as the “brain” of the home automation system and which is not subject to this request. The HPC controller consists of two 100-240 VAC 30A relays that controls motor loads, resistive loads, and ballast type loads. It also consists of four low-voltage dry contacts to support external dry contact sensors or hard-wired manual override controls that allow it to be used in a stand-alone mode independent of a Control4® host system.
