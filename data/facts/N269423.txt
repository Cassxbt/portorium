The submitted sample is identified as Sponge Coils, Item# 55208. This item is made of grey foam polyethylene (PE) plastic material. It is in the form of a sponge that is tapered in the middle when viewed from side to side and is also tapered in the center between the top and bottom surfaces. The top surface features 33 small, round, shallow apertures and the bottom surface, which is smooth without apertures, is designed to be hand gripped when the item is in use. The item measures approximately 2¼” in height by 5¾” from side to side by 3¼” from front to back. This item is designed to be used to curl hair. After applying holding spray, pomade or styling gel (none of which are included) to the hair, the user rubs the surface of the sponge with the apertures against the scalp in small circular motions to create the curls.
