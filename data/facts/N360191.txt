The merchandise at issue is a plastic bag manufactured in Turkey. The bag is constructed from a  multi-layered laminate sheet of oriented polypropylene (OPP), metallized polyethylene terephthalate (PET),  and low-density polyethylene (LDPE). The polyethylene comprises 80 percent by weight of the bag. The  bag, with dimensions of 6.5 x 5 x 2 inches (height x width x depth), has an integral extruded closure at the  top and is intended for supplementary packaging.
