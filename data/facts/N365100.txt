The “LH Wire INR (88387-DN840)” is a cold-bent steel metal wire component engineered into a specific shape. It is directly welded to the lower frame of the automobile seat as a permanent, non-detachable assembly according to Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) specifications. Upon importation into the United States (U.S.), the article will be positioned onto the lower frame of an automobile seat and functions to support a seat cover so that it remains properly positioned and retains its designed shape. The pocket portion of a seat cover will engage the metal wire, thereby drawing the seat cover taut and causing it to adhere closely to the seat foam. When used in conjunction with the seat’s shield cover, the article preserves the designed curvature, contour, and finish of upholstery materials and prevents exposure of underlying wiring harnesses, motors, and other seat components. The metal wire dimensions are approximately 10cm x 3cm x 3.5cm.
