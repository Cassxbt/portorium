The item concerned is referred to as a “solar station”. This product is a stationary automated RFID tag reading system. It is comprised of a solar panel which is used to charge internal batteries, a cellular modem and an M250 RFID reader. This system allows for the reception and transmission of tracking/location data for equipment that contains an RFID tag. In use an RFID tag with specific data encoded within it would be attached to a piece of equipment. When that tag receives a signal from the M250 RFID reader it transmits the embedded data back to the RFID reader. The M250 RFID reader converts the wireless data transmission into a digital format and transmits that data to remote servers via the cellular modem.
