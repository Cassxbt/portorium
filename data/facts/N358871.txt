Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is a rigid paperboard box with a hinged lid. The box measures 8 inches  wide by 8 inches long by 3 inches high, and has an internal textile ribbon that prevents the box from opening  more than about 90 degrees. The box itself is constructed of rigid paperboard and the paperboard is overlaid  by molded, thermoformed pulp paper. The result is a surface with a textured floral pattern. The box may or  may not be printed.
