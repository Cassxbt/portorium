Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as a tracing projector (item 736816), which is a projecting art set  designed for children aged 3 and older. The set includes a compact plastic projector light and two  interchangeable plastic disks. The user inserts and rotates the disks to project images of Disney characters  onto paper. The set also provides a plastic tracing marker with ink, enabling users to trace the projected  characters, along with 14 blank paper tracing sheets.
