The product in question is the portable oil extractor and collection system, model 372700.300, described as a  mobile used-oil suction and collection system. The system is comprised of a 100-liter steel recovery tank, a  wheeled base with handle, and an external Evacumaster pneumatic suction pump module mounted on the  collector assembly together with an air-operated PM2 1:1 oil suction pump and its external hose/filter/probe  circuit. The unit is designed to extract used engine oil from vehicles via suction probes inserted into the  dipstick tube. The extracted oil is conveyed through the pump module and into the steel tank for temporary  accumulation prior to discharge.
