Item 1987687, the “Leather Storage Console with Hidden Cupholders,” is a modular sofa sectional unit. The  upholstered sofa sectional unit is constructed of leather, foam, polyester fiber, and polyvinyl chloride (PVC)  that is affixed to a wooden frame. The wooden frame consists of engineered wood materials that include (1)  plywood, (2) laminated veneer lumber (LVL) and Southern pine wood. The modular sofa sectional unit will  have USB-A and USB-C charging ports and a storage compartment with a pair of cupholders. The modular  sofa sectional seating unit contains assembly hardware in the form of connection posts and/or connection  clips for integration and assembly onto additional sofa sectional units (sold separately). The modular sofa  sectional unit dimensions are approximately 40.5" in length, 12.5" in width, and 26.3" in height.
