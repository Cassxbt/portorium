The items under consideration are described as five models of urns that are displayed on a table or shelf in a home as decorative vases. Item numbers AD-2801 and AD-2803 are made of 65% Copper and 35% Zinc and measure 6.9" x 6.9" X 10.3". Item numbers AD-2807-BG, AD-2807-BS, and AD-2808-BG are made of 100 % Aluminum and measure 7.1" x 7.1" x 9.5".
