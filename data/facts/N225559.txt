The articles in question are described as a “Generation 8 ECU (Electronic Control Unit) attached to the Hydraulic unit”, a “Generation 8 ECU stand alone (Ceramic Substrate)”, a “Generation 8 ECU housing” and a “Generation 8 ECU attached to the housing”. Based on the information made available, the “Generation 8 ECU (Electronic Control Unit) attached to the Hydraulic unit” is a solenoid actuated valve that controls the direction and volume of hydraulic fluid, i.e. brake fluid, to braking components in response to control signals from the ECU. The “Generation 8 ECU housing” is the housing for the ECU and incorporates the solenoids and other electrical components necessary to actuate the hydraulic unit. The “Generation 8 ECU attached to the housing” comprises the ECU and its housing and is assembled ready for attachment to the hydraulic unit. The “Generation 8 ECU stand alone (Ceramic Substrate)” is the central processor for the antilock braking system. It is a hybrid integrated circuit. The manufacturing process begins with a ceramic insulating substrate upon which a thick film circuit has been formed, allowing for resistors to be produced at the same time. The substrate is then populated with semiconductors by mounting them onto the surface of the substrate.
