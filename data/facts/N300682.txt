The items under consideration have been identified as a Latch Assembly (Part Number 2390723B; Customer Part Number 23392725) and a Cable (Item Number 9576907B), which are components of a door module used in passenger vehicles. The Latch Assembly is described as a LATCH 7 ACTR ASSY-EG MAJOR RH, which is a locking endgate latch in the door module. The assembly includes both a latch that keeps the truck endgate/tailgate in a closed position and an actuator that receives a signal from a touchpad switch to unlock/open the latch. The Cable is described as a P LF CABLE-D/MOD UPPER&LOWER FRT EXP, which is installed in the door module and operates the window of a vehicle up and down. It consists of a cable, two fixed parts, and a buffer.
