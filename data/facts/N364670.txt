The articles under consideration consists of five pipe breather assemblies designed for use in motor vehicles. Part numbers 17137-6FB-A000 and 17137-66V-A000 are identified as Pipe Comp, Breather assemblies. The assemblies are designed for use in sedans and smaller motor vehicles and functions to manage crankcase pressure and vapors by routing blow-by gases into the vehicle’s intake system for combustion. Part number 17137-6FB-A000 consists of a breather pipe, water pipe, and three stays, while part number 17137-66V-A000 consists of a breather pipe, water pipe, and three plates. Part number 1954A-6VJ-A000 is identified as a TC Water Return Pipe Sub Assembly. The assembly is designed for use in motor vehicles and functions to rout engine coolant through the vehicle’s cooling system to assist in maintaining proper operating temperatures. The assembly consists of a TC water return pipe, two TC water outlet hoses, and a clamp. Part numbers 36169-6MA-J000 and 36169-6MA-A00 are identified as Pipe Comp, Purge assemblies. The assemblies are designed for use in small sport utility vehicles and sedans and functions to connect purge-control components of the vehicle’s evaporative emission control system to safely route fuel vapors. Each assembly consists of two pipes, four stays, and a plate.
