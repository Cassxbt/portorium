The item in question is referred to as a Card Access Wireless Motion Sensor, (Model #: ZCA-WMS10-CL-ZP). This device incorporates an integrated PIR (Passive Infrared) motion detector and an integrated ambient light sensor (~0.1 to 10,000 lux). It senses motion and light inside and outside the home. This wireless motion sensors communicate motion and ambient light events to the Control4 system using ZigBee®. This sensor is powered by a 9V alkaline battery.
