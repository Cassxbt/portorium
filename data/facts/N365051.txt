In lieu of a sample, photographs of the garland were provided with your request. Item #914751, described as a “JOYWARD Garland” is a hanging garland intended to be used as a decoration in the home. The garland is constructed of multiple textile hearts made wholly of polyester nonwoven felt fabric, stuffed with foam, and strung on a plied red-and-white yarn composed wholly of polyester staple fiber. The garland features six larger red hearts, measuring 2 by 2 inches, and five smaller pink hearts, measuring 1 by 1 inches, arranged in an alternating pattern on a 5-foot-long string. Each heart features glitter on the front side. The string is looped at each end for hanging.
