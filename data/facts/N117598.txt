The merchandise subject to this ruling is media converter/optical transport modules. You have furnished two samples of these media converter/optical transport modules. The two samples are being returned to you. Optical transport modules (OTMs) convert a local electrical NE signal to a single or multimode T3, E3, or STS-1 optical signal and transmit that to receiving OTMs up to 50km away. Remote OTMs receive the optical signal and reconvert it to an electrical signal, sending that to a remote NE. Conversely, the remote OTMs convert and transit optical signals to local OTMs. These modules provide bi-directional electrical/optical, point-to-point conversion and extension of DS3 signals over fiber optics, and signal extensions up to 50km over single mode fiber.
