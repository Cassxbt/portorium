The item under consideration is a flexible straw bracelet, identified as the “Wristraw Signature.” This item functions as both a bracelet and a flexible straw for consuming beverages, similar to the article described in New York Ruling N364333, dated September 1, 2026. The primary components of this article include a flexible corrugated steel tube that can be 20.6 centimeters or 23.8 centimeters long, and a silicon containment tube. The flexible steel tube is inserted into the silicon tube and held in place by two steel plugs at each end. The entire item can then be wrapped around a person’s wrist to be worn like a bracelet and secured in position by a titanium clasp. When needed, the item can be removed from the wrist, and the flexible steel tube can be removed from the containment tube and can be used as a drinking straw.
