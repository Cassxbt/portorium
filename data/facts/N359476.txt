Tolvaptan, imported in 15 mg and 30 mg tablets, is in a class of medications called vasopressin V2 receptor  antagonists. It works by increasing the amount of water released from the body as urine. It is indicated to  slow kidney function decline in adults at risk of rapidly progressing autosomal dominant polycystic kidney  disease (ADPKD). It is also indicated for the treatment of clinically significant hypervolemic and euvolemic  hyponatremia (including SIADH and heart failure) to raise serum sodium levels.
