Photographs and Descriptive literature were provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as the ARENA video game console, which utilizes a camera-free  sensor array combining LiDAR and radar technology to track basketball shots with over 99% accuracy. The  LiDAR system operates on the time-of-flight principle, emitting rapid, invisible pulses of laser light toward  the court or backboard area. When a basketball or player interrupts the beam, the light reflects back to the  sensor’s detector. By measuring the precise nanosecond travel time of the returning light pulse, the onboard  processor calculates distance. This continuous stream of range and trajectory data maps the 3D coordinates of  the ball’s movement, allowing the device to distinguish makes, misses, and shot distance entirely without  optical video recording. The ARENA mounts to the top center of the basketball backboard, connects to LED strips around the outside  edge of the backboard and plugs into an electric outlet. The device also connects to the huupe application  which streams the game results to a television receiver, a monitor or other external screen or surface. Typical  display configurations include outdoor television sets in the area of the hoop, computer monitors near the  hoop, or portable devices like tablets, laptops, or phones being used to track gameplay in real time. The  device comes pre-loaded with several games that allow players to compete against each other. These games  include the number of three point shots made, number of consecutive shots made, number of “swishes”, etc. The ARENA also has internal parameters that interpret the tracked real-time physical activity in the context  of the game. This means that the video game console not only sees from where a shot is attempted and 

whether it is successful but also interprets the data and creates output on an external screen that shows  whether the attempt was a sufficient distance to be worth three points, or is of an appropriate distance to be a  free throw.
