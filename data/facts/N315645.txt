The items under consideration are described as “Reinforcement Intermediate Cross Side”, part numbers 65918-S9000(LH) and 65928-S9000(RH). According to the information you provided these items are mountings designed and manufactured for use in four-door passenger vehicles. They are mounted on the left and right side of the intermediate cross member of the rear floor panel assembly. These items provide a mounting surface for the vehicle components which are mounted to the rear floor of the vehicle. The Reinforcement Intermediate Cross Sides are made of flat-rolled non-alloy steel and when installed not only provide a mounting surface but also reinforce the rigidity of the vehicle body.
