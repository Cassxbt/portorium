The item concerned is a bare printed circuit board (PCB) referred to as the “Helix Display PCB” (#1127940+R3). This specialized PCB is designed, manufactured and used exclusively with the Respironics’ Trilogy Evo Ventilator. The Trilogy Evo ventilator provides continuous or intermittent positive pressure ventilationfor the care of individuals who require mechanical ventilation. It is intended for pediatric through adult patients weighing at least 2.5 kg. This Printed Circuit Board (PCB) is an integral part of, and is designed to be used exclusively with, The Trilogy Evo series of ventilators. The item concerned is a custom-made/designed, eight layer printed circuit board made specifically for use in controlling the display of the Trilogy Evo ventilator. Given the highly custom nature of the board, it has no use other than in the assembly of a Trilogy Evo Ventilator.
