The three Organic Gypsum Prill products consist of the following: Organic Gypsum Prill, Organic Gypsum Rock Phosphate Prill, and Organic Gypsum Coated Prill. Organic Gypsum Prill is composed of Falkland Gypsum and Cameron Coat Binder. Organic Gypsum Rock Phosphate Prill is composed of Falkland Gypsum, Rock Phosphate, and Cameron Coat Binder. Organic Gypsum Coated Prill is composed of Falkland Gypsum, Cameron Coat Binder, and Micro CalPlus Coating. You state that the products are granulated, low-dust soil amendments used to supply calcium and sulfur, loosen compacted soils, improve drainage, and help leach salts and aluminum.
