Description, illustrative literature, photographs and a sample were received. Part number 81111601 is described as a “Lumber Support Pillow.”  The lumber support pillow is an internal plastic component incorporated into the Fern® Task Chair. The lumber support pillow is made of 100% BASF Elastollan 1190A TPU Polyether plastic. The lumber support pillow is 11.5 inches in length, has the shape of a fluted column, and has one left and one right hole for screws to go into for attachment to the lumber support column and has a notch for insertion to the lumber support column. The lumber support pillow is placed and attached horizontally in relation to the vertical support column. Photos depict that the horizontally placed lumber support pillow is height adjustable, and raises and lowers by means of a lumbar handle located at the back of the chair. This component was exclusively designed by Haworth. The Fern® Task Chair is marketed in part and depicted in a photograph as a workforce swivel chair on casters, providing total back support and having an Infinity Edgetm (Edgeless Comfort), and comes with a message (abridged) “Sit Well, Work Well and Feel Well.”  Illustrative literature indicates that the Fern® Task Chair is available in a plastic model and aluminum model.
