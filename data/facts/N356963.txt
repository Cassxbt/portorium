In lieu of samples,  illustrative literature and product descriptions are provided for review. The subject merchandise is Areca nuts (also known as betel nuts). The product is harvested, sun-dried for two  months, de-husked, graded on size and weight requirements, and packed eight betel nuts per package with a  net weight of 500 grams. The product which will be imported into the United States is intended for retail sale  at grocery stores.
