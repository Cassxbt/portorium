In a letter dated May 5, 2026, a tariff classification ruling was requested on a women’s upper body garment. Our response was delayed due to laboratory analysis. The submitted sample will be returned as requested. Style 253-SW421090 “Crystal Sands Bateau Nk” is a women’s pullover garment with a stated fiber content of 54 percent cotton, 40 percent viscose (rayon), 3 percent polyester, and 3 percent metallic. The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures less than 10 stitches per linear centimeter in both the horizontal and vertical directions. It also measures more than nine stitches per two centimeters, and less than 18 stitches per two centimeters, in the direction the stitches were formed. Style 253-SW421090 features a contoured boat neckline, long sleeves with rib knit cuffs formed by a change in knit pattern, and a rib knit bottom band formed by a change in knit pattern. All components (the front and back panels, the sleeves, and the neckline) are knit to shape and have been assembled by looping and linking.
