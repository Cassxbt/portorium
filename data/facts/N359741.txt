The items under consideration are described as wall-mounted soap dispensers (models SN30TBK and  SN46TBK), which are plastic dispensers featuring a black pearl finish that suits both commercial and public  restrooms. Both models are designed for high-impact durability, utilizing a manual, wide-surface push bar to  operate. They feature a specialized funnel-shaped reservoir that directs all soap toward the pump to minimize  waste, along with a transparent gloss window for monitoring refill levels. The SN30TBK offers a 30 oz. capacity, while the larger SN46TBK provides 46 oz. capacity for higher traffic areas. Both include a locking  mechanism to prevent vandalism. These universal dispensers accommodate a wide range of bulk liquid  soaps, lotions, and sanitizers (excluding pumice).
