The articles under consideration are steel rods that connect the vehicle’s exterior door handle to the internal latch and contribute significantly to the door opening and closing system. The items associated with this request have been identified as P NX4a ROD-OUTSIDE FR, LH (Part Number B9000847); P NX4a ROD-OUTSIDE FR, RH (Part Number B9000848); P NX4a ROD-OUTSIDE RR, LH (Part Number B9000849); and P NX4a ROD-OUTSIDE RR, RH (Part Number B9000850). The products function by transmitting the operating force generated when the user pulls the door handle to the latch, allowing the door to open. According to the submission, these items are designed solely for use within the vehicle door system and do not perform a locking function itself, nor do they operate as constituent parts of the door lock mechanism. You further state that each rod has no independent mechanical function.
