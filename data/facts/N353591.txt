Aspen M2 Advanced Ankle Foot Orthosis (AFO) is used to provide support (stabilization and positioning) to  the foot and ankle. It is available in both anterior and posterior configurations and different sizes, for both the  right and left foot. It's designed to help conditions that affect walking and mobility, such as foot drop and  neurological disorders. The M2 AFO can also help with conditions like spasticity, which causes increased  muscle tone and stiffness, affecting normal gait. It consists of a footplate and lateral-posterior strut made  from carbon fiber and a pad with straps. The weight limit is 220 pounds. It is required to be prescribed and  adjusted by a physician as measurements, physical examination, and device modification will be needed  depending on the patient’s foot size. The M2 AFO is specifically designed to facilitate a normal gait; its  function is so specific that it cannot be used by the general public without having to be altered depending on  the patient’s foot and leg size. The M2 AFO is sold directly from Aspen to physicians and medical device  distributors.
