The first item under consideration is an Output Carrier Assembly, part number H418C-3. The assembly  consists of three gears on a shaft. Once installed in a ring gear, it meshes with a sun gear of a stepper motor  located on the engine of an aircraft. The assembly is part of a planetary gear set used in a fuel control system  and is primarily constructed of stainless-steel material. The second item under consideration is a planet gear, part number 1065-400-039. This gear, which is a  component of the Output Carrier Assembly, is a wheel that features teeth on the outer diameter and a center  bore. The gear is primarily constructed of stainless-steel material.
