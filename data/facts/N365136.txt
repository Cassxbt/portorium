The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Neural DSP Quad Cortex, which is further described as a digital guitar and instrument effects processor. The subject device is comprised of a metal enclosure, within which are the necessary electrical components. On the outside of the device is a 7-inch LCD touchscreen interface along with a rotary encoder and multiple footswitches. On the back of the device are the various input and output connections, including audio inputs (XLR, TRS, mono, etc.), MIDI, and USB. In use, a guitar or other electrical musical instrument is connected through the input ports. The device receives and digitally processes the audio signal from the instrument, allowing the user to modify the sound. This modification includes the addition of various characteristics as well as the reproduction of external amplifiers, cabinets, pedals, and other audio equipment. The processed sound is then sent to amplification, recording, or other audio equipment.
