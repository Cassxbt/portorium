The item in question is part number 6931EA2001E, which is a bi-metal thermostat assembly designed for use in washing machines and other home appliances. The bi-metal thermostat measures and maintains a constant temperature by utilizing a flat metallic strip comprising two metals with different expansion rates. The different expansion rates force the bi-metal metallic strip to bend or curve one way when electrically heated, and in the opposite direction when cooled, opening and closing a circuit to switch the heating on and off.
