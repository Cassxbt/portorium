The items under consideration are four models of hearing loop drivers, identified by model numbers VLD1, ID-7, V15+, and V7+, and a hearing loop listener, identified by model number RX20. A hearing loop driver is a specialized type of audio transmitter module. It is a component of an induction loop (also called a hearing loop) system, which provides assistive listening for people with hearing loss. A hearing loop listener is a compact listening device for use with hearing loop systems. It enables people to listen to a hearing loop system as if they were wearing a hearing device switched to the T-Coil Program. The four loop drivers and the hearing loop listener are for sole use within an assistive hearing loop system. A hearing loop system is an assistive listening solution that delivers sound directly into hearing aids and cochlear implants. Hearing loops require these specialized transmission devices to feed a loop of wire to create an electromagnetic field, which carries the audio signal. The persons with hearing loss who are in the electromagnetic field pick up the audio signal through the telecoil in their hearing aid or cochlear implant. The hearing loop transmission devices are created to meet the international standard IEC 60118-4. Only people using hearing aids, cochlear implants or a loop listener/receiver with a telecoil can utilize the hearing loop system.
