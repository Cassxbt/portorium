The products under consideration are five articles used in store fixtures and displays. All articles are made of steel. STT50+ is a component in the line of products identified as the “Multisystem Gondolas,” exclusively offered by ADCO Displetech. The MultiSytem Gondola is a visual display system set up for purposes of displaying and holding retail merchandising products. The component in question is comprised of two vertical sections connected end-to-end with a third section connected at one end and perpendicular to the other two sections to form a “T” shaped floor standing unit. This product is shipped in unassembled condition. Upon assembly, the STT50+ appears to be a vertical frame of panels joined together for the attachment of shelves, hooks and other fixtures. The vertical frame of panels has the option to be fitted with height adjustable feet and/or casters. Photos indicate a free standing vertical frame incorporating “uniboard melamine panels” available in different colors, thereby forming two framed panels joined together and a third framed panel joined perpendicular to one of the other framed panels. It is our opinion that the unassembled vertical framed panels joined together are “components of a display rack” over that of separately presented elements of unit furniture.
