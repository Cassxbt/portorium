Style SZ9201 is a girls’ one-piece dress constructed from knit and woven fabrics. The upper portion consists of a white woven shirt constructed from 55 percent cotton and 45 percent polyester woven fabric, featuring a pointed collar, partial front opening secured with two buttons, and short hemmed sleeves. Over the shirt portion is a rib-knit sleeveless overlay component constructed from 60 percent cotton and 40 percent polyester knit fabric, permanently sewn to the upper portion. The lower portion consists of a 100 percent polyester woven fabric pleated skirt, permanently sewn to the upper portion. The garment extends from the shoulder to approximately the mid-thigh and is intended for school uniform wear.
