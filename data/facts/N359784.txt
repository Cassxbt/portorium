In lieu of samples, technical and illustrative literature and a product description were provided for review. The “ALRK-20BK Bakery Rack” is a 20 tier unassembled and unequipped aluminum frame storage rack. The rack is used for vertical stacking, preparation, baking, storage, and displaying bakers’ goods. The rack  base is affixed to two locking and two non-locking 5” caster wheels. The rack dimensions approximate 21” in  length, 26.5” in width, 70” in height, and has a weight capacity of 650 lbs. In the condition at the time of U.S. importation, the rack is not equipped with interchangeable wire mesh decking or pan holders (non-wire mesh  decking).
