There are three low-voltage DC electrical distribution and battery equipment enclosures under consideration with this request, individually identified by model numbers AL8, AL12, and AL12+. While these models differ in cabinet size, equipment capacity, and internal layout, their internal electrical components are nearly identical. All three models are composed of a powder-coated aluminum enclosure fitted with positive and negative bus bars, a main DC circuit breaker, a 6-amp double-pole control breaker, an interposing relay and socket apparatus, three or four ventilation fans (depending on the model), an adjustable thermostat, electrical conductors, wiring sets, bonding conductors, mounting hardware, and cable supports. The rated voltage is stated to be less than 60 volts for all three models.
