Photographs and descriptions of  the merchandise were included in your inquiry. The products under consideration are described as iron holders for nautical rope fences. There are two  different bracket configurations. The front mount consists of a flat, square, steel plate with a mounting hole in  each corner and an iron ring extending perpendicular from the plate. The corner mount consists of flat,  square, steel sheet bent to a 90-degree angle with a mounting hole in each corner, and an iron ring extending  perpendicular from the outside of the angle. Both styles are made from a mounting plate that measures 2.5  inches square and a 3.8-inch solid steel ring, and both configurations are imported in sizes to support a 1-inch  or 2-inch diameter nautical rope within the ring. The surface finish can be raw iron or painted black. The steel  brackets are mounted vertically to a fence so that nautical ropes can hang from ring to ring, forming a fence.
