Photographs and descriptions of two athletic groin protectors (protective cups) were submitted with your  inquiry. The “MMA Cup Collection,” consists of two athletic groin protectors sized and shaped to fit an athlete’s age  and gender. The protective cups that are designed for men and boys are: “MMA Cup Adult” (16 years and  older) and the “MMA Cup Kids” (7 to12 years old). The products are worn during sports activities to shield  the genital area from impact injuries. The groin protectors are composed of a rigid plastic shell with an  attached soft lining and soft edge frame, combined with an elastic cord set used to fasten the protection  device to an athletic support garment.
