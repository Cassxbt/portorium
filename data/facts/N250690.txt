The submitted sample is being returned. The merchandise under consideration is referred to as the “Wireless 0-10V Dimmer”, part number C4-TV120277. This device is a component of a home automation system. The C4-TV120277 is a programmable switch/dimmer combination comprised of a printed circuit board assembly, two metal–oxide–semiconductor field-effect transistor (MOSFET) switches, relays, eighteen pin connector and five electrical wires. The device receives its signal from the Control4 Controller via a radio frequency (RF) communication to adjust the intensity level of the attached lighting load and to indicate the feedback on that status of the system such as, changing an LED on the button to a specific color. The buttons on this device may be used to activate any number of devices that are controlled by the automation system to adjust the light levels, to turn on the music, to lock the doors, etc. The dimmable light load is 0-10V signal to a 0-10V. It is stated that the C4-TV120277 does not contain a potentiometer and without the automation controller this device would only be able to control the lighting load wired to it.
