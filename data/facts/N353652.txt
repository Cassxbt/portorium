The three items concerned are all parts of the Zulu 3 Aviation headset (HCC-94). This headset is used within  an aircraft by the pilot or passenger. The headset is wired and must be plugged into an audio jack of the  intercom to communicate with ATC (air traffic control) or communicate within the cockpit while in flight. The headset provides noise cancellation for the pilot and passengers so that they can communicate within the  cockpit and with ATC. The first part is referred to as an ear seal (part # 250-00052-000.A4). The ear seal is used on all Lightspeed  Headsets. One ear seal would be mounted to each ear cup on the headset. Its function is to block outside  noise (the ear seals create a tight seal around the ears, which helps physically block sound from the  environment), improve sound quality (by sealing off outside noise, they help you hear the headset’s audio  more clearly), support active noise canceling (in headsets with electronic noise cancellation, ear seals reduce  the amount of outside noise that reaches the ears, making it easier for the system to cancel out the remaining  sound), comfort and fit (a good seal also means the headset stays comfortably in place, which is important for  long-term use and consistent performance). The ear seals are made of polyurethane (PU) leather, memory  foam, thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) & polyvinyl chloride (PVC) plastic. The second part is referred to as a head pad (part # 250-00054-000.A2). The functions of the head pad are,  comfort (head pads make the headset more comfortable to wear, especially during long periods), stability  (they help keep the headset in place, which is important for maintaining a good seal around the ears), support  for noise cancelation function (while head pads do not directly block noise, they help the headset stay  properly positioned. This ensures the ear seals can do their job effectively, which improves both passive and  active noise cancelation). The head pad is made of PU leather, memory foam, and PVC plastic. The third part is referred to as a tall head pad (part # 305-00001-000.A2). The functions of the tall head pad  are, comfort (head pads make the headset more comfortable to wear, especially during long periods), stability  (they help keep the headset in place, which is important for maintaining a good seal around the ears), support  for the noise canceling function (while head pads do not directly block noise, they help the headset stay  properly positioned. This ensures the ear seals can do their job effectively, which improves both passive and  active noise cancelation). They are made of PU leather, memory foam and PVC plastic.
