Ther merchandise under consideration is described as custom engraved souvenir hockey pucks. They are  solid, round metal discs manufactured to the dimensions of a standard hockey puck (approximately 76 mm  diameter x 25 mm thick). The pucks are machined in Canada from raw aluminum, bronze, brass, or steel  stock that is sourced in the U.S. They are then laser-engraved in Canada with decorative custom text, logos,  or commemorative artwork and shipped directly to customers in the U.S. The metal hockey pucks are  ornamental or commemorative articles for use as display pieces, gifts, or keepsakes.
