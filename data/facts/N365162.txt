The merchandise under consideration is described as an Integrated Performance Computer Unit (IPCU) used within a vehicle in order to facilitate autonomous driving. The subject device is comprised of two printed circuit board assemblies (PCBA), both comprised of a main processing unit, memory, and various communication and connection components. The first PCBA is stated to be dedicated to autonomous driving and parking functions. The second is meant to connect and communicate with various systems and apparatus within a designated vehicle. These include cameras, radar, radio, phones, and others. The subject unit is placed within a vehicle and functions as a centralized platform that consolidates data from multiple vehicle sources. With this information, the device runs artificial intelligence algorithms allowing the vehicle to function autonomously.
