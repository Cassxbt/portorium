There are five items at issue with this request, which are all described as ethernet data transmission cables. The subject cables are individually identified by model numbers CAT5E, CAT6, CAT6A, CAT7, and CAT8. All cables are internally constructed of four twisted pairs of copper conductors within various levels of  shielding and insulation, depending on the application (i.e. indoor/outdoor use). The cables are imported on  reels to be cut to the desired length by the end user and are not terminated with any connectors. Based upon  the information provided, the cables are rated at 300V. In use, the cables are used to transmit data and other  information within various telecommunications installations.
