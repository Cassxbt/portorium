The article concerned is referred to as the “MoodSign”. This electrical device consists of a plastic housing that incorporates two transparent plastic arms. The plastic arms are hinged to the top outside corners of the housing. The hinge allows the arms to fold down into the housing or be extended up and out of the housing. The base of the plastic arms contain light emitting diodes (LEDs) which are used to illuminate the arms when raised from the housing. There are 4 different colors of illumination to choose from. Each color is meant to represent a different level of urgency. The unit operates on 2 AAA batteries. The “MoodSign” is a type of visual signaling device. In use it would be placed on an important paper/document, or a note would be taped to its housing. Then one arm of the device would be raised and illuminated to indicate that the document is important and needs urgent attention. After the intended recipient sees the signal and reads the document/note, they would raise and illuminate the second arm to indicate that the document has been read.
