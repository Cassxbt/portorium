There are three items concerned, each used as a part of Lightspeed Zulu 3 aviation headset. The part names  and model numbers are:  000000350.B Knob, Volume Slider  000000676.A Cover, Microphone Housing Pot  000000814.A1 Button, Bluetooth Control, Painted  The Knob, Volume Slider (volume slider knob) is used on the control box assembly of the Zulu 3 headset. It  is a part of the control box housing. In use this knob is mounted to the housing in a way that allows it to slide  up and down. Within the control box the knob connects to a potentiometer slide controller. This external  knob allows the user to position the internal potentiometer slider as needed. The volume slider knob is made  from a thermoplastic blend of polycarbonate and acrylonitrile-butadiene-styrene (molded ABS-PC). The Cover, Microphone Housing Pot (microphone housing pot cover) is used on the mic boom of the Zulu 3  headset. It is used to cover the microphone gain control on the mic boom. The microphone gain is a control  used to adjust the amplification level of the user’s voice. The microphone housing pot cover is made from m  olded ABS-PC. The Button, Bluetooth Control, Painted (Bluetooth control button) is used on the control box assembly of the  Zulu 3 headset. It is a part of the control box housing. This externally accessible button is used to activate the  internal Bluetooth switch on the PCBA within the control box. The Bluetooth control button is made from  molded ABS-PC.
