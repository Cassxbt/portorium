The item concerned is referred to as the FieldView™ Drive 2.0. This device plugs into farming equipments’ CAN diagnostic port, capturing both machine and field data. It connects farming equipment to a user’s iPad mobile device via Bluetooth, allowing the user to collect data and store it as they pass through a planting field. The item is compatible with many types of farming equipment such as tractors, combines, liquid applicators, sprayers and planters. It facilitates the collection and input of data without the need for manual data entry.
