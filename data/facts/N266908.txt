The merchandise under consideration, which weighs 13 pounds and measures 7 inches x 12 inches x 12 inches, is referred to as the “Single Shot Listening Station.”  This device, which features volume controls, a play button, and a skip button, can reproduce a 10- to 30-second clip of a song that is prerecorded onto the listening station via an SD card; this device is also stated to be capable of playing 3 to 5 song clips preloaded onto the SD card. Moreover, this sound-reproducing listening station is located in stores for customers to test the quality of Skullcandy’s headphones.
