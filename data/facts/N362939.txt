Famotidine Oral Suspension, imported in 40 mg/5 mL, is a histamine H2 receptor antagonist. It is indicated   for the treatment of active duodenal ulcer (DU), active gastric ulcer (GU), symptomatic nonerosive  gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD), erosive esophagitis due to GERD, and Zollinger-Ellison syndrome.
