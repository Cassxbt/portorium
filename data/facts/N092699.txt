A description and literature with pictures were submitted with your request. The submitted article is the Proxi-Motion patient lift. It is a mobile device that is designed to be used by caregivers to assist in moving a patient or disabled person from a bed or chair. The patient would be seated in a sling which is attached to the arm of the machine. The arm, when raised vertically, would lift the patient in the sling. When not in use, a table top can be attached to the arm for the patient’s use.
