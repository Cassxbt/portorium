In lieu of samples, a product description and illustrative literature were provided.The “Bonaterra III Sofa” is a 100% polyester woven fabric upholstered seat that is affixed to a woodenframe. The sofa does not incorporate a removeable mattress, convert into a bed, or include reclinemechanisms. The wooden frame consists of engineered wood materials that include (1) ½” oriented strandboard (OSB) (19%), (2) bamboo (56%), (3) ¾” laminated veneer lumber (LVL) (17%), and (4) mediumdensity fiberboard (MDF) (8%). The sofa will provide seating for one or more individuals. The sofa’s foamseat foundation cushions and the foam seat backrest cushions are removeable. In the condition at the time ofimportation into the United States (U.S.), a pair of accent pillows will be included with the seat. The sofadimensions are 27" in depth, 27" in height, and 89" in length.
