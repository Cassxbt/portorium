The merchandise under consideration is identified as the ELO Touch Screen Controller, Part Number E754351, which consists of a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) having various control circuitry and multiple connectors that are mounted onto a bare circuit board. The touch screen controller is said to be used in conjunction with ELO’s TouchPro brand of touch screen overlay; however, the display and/or overlay are not imported with the subject controller board. In your request, you state that the TouchPro touch screen overlay cannot function without the controller PCBA and that the overlay adds touch functionality to the display by detecting the presence of user interaction and communicating the X and Y coordinates to the processor. In our view, the subject controller PCBA electrically controls the overlay and interfaces with the display (or other device) for the purpose of electrical control.
