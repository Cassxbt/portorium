Photographs and product information were submitted for our review. The product under consideration, identified as item SKU PL2006, is a 24’ round pool liner that is used with an above-ground swimming pool with 48” - 52” side walls. The function of the liner is to hold the water within the swimming pool. This liner is the original liner or a replacement part to be installed when the original liner is damaged and needs to be replaced. It is constructed of full 20 and 25 guage virgin vinyl and contains strong, double-welded seams to withstand harsh winters.
