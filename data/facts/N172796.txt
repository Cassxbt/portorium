Photographs of the tire plug kit were submitted with your letter. The tire plug kit consists of eight items: five string plugs, one rubber cement tube, one T-handle tire awl and one T-handle plug insertion tool. All are packaged together for retail sale in a blister card, designed to be hung on a peg hook. The string plugs, which are black, are composed of 9-ply nylon/rubber (cured butyl rubber and nylon fibers) and measure 100 millimeters in length (+/- 3 millimeter) and 6.5 millimeters in diameter (+/- 1.0 millimeter). The rubber cement weights .41 fluid ounces and comes in a tube container with a cap. The T-handle tire awl and the T-handle plug insertion tool are made with polypropylene handles and steel stems.
