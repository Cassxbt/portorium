You have submitted descriptive literature and pictorial representations. The product to be imported you describe as a window wall system. The manufacturer procures aluminum profile shapes from an extruder. The window frame consists of a head, sill and two jambs. The window frames and sill tracks are painted, cut, machined and assembled. Then the glass is installed in the window frame. Therefore the final product at time of importation is a window wall system consisting of sill tracks, accessories and assembled windows. The system is designed for commercial buildings. The Aluminum Association Series Designation number is 6063.
