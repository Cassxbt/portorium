In your submission, the subject product is identified as Play-Doh Blooms Garden Bouquet. You describe the   Play Doh Blooms Garden Bouquet as a creative playset designed to allow users to create decorative flower  arrangements using Play Doh modeling compound and reusable plastic accessories.
