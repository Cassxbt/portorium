The article under consideration is described as the MagnaLatch Series 3. It is a key-operated locking pool gate latch made of metal and plastic. The device measures approximately 20.5 inches tall and includes a steel door latch mechanism at the bottom and a brass 6-pin keyed lock, a locked indictor, and a pull-knob to unlatch the gate, at the top. It is installed onto a pool gate so that the lock and pull-knob can be positioned at a height that meets pool safety code requirements. Mounting hardware is included.
