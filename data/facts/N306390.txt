Samples were provided for our review and will be returned, per your request. “My Guardian Angel” and “Lives of the Saints” are hardbound books geared to children. “My Guardian Angel” contains 16 pages of text and illustrations describing how the child’s guardian angel walks with the child to guide, protect and provide moral lessons. “Lives of Saints” contains 128 pages of text and illustrations. It tells the stories of 60 Catholic saints and provides a prayer for each. The text is not subordinate to the illustrations in either book.
