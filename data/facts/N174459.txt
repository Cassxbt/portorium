Redispersion Polyvinyl Acetate resin (EP-5016M, EP-5016H or EP-6040V) is described as an unplasticized  poly(vinyl acetate) white powder consisting by weight of 85-95% poly(vinyl acetate) CAS-9003-20-7, 2-10% poly(vinyl acetate - vinyl alcohol) CAS-25213-24-5 and <0.1% vinyl acetate CAS-108-05-4. The product is designed for use in construction adhesives, joint compounds, wood putties, polymer mortars, paper adhesives and crack fillers.
