Amoxicillin Potassium Clavulanate is an oral antibacterial combination consisting of the semisynthetic antibiotic amoxicillin and the ß-lactamase inhibitor, clavulanate potassium (the potassium salt of clavulanic acid). It is indicated for use in the treatment of bacterial infections including streptococcal pharyngitis, pneumonia, urinary tract infections, and skin infections.
