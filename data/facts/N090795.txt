The ruling was requested on a decorative “pumpkin”, identified as Liss item # 9008501. A sample of the product was submitted for our review and will be returned to you as requested. The sample consists of a Styrofoam center. The Styrofoam center is covered by narrow strips of raffia placed vertically side by side and secured together at the top and bottom. Two decorative polyester leaves are placed at the top of the pumpkin along with two metal springs and a wooden stem. The pumpkin measures approximately 3.5” (h) x 7.06” (a). The pumpkin’s intended use will be for decoration.
