The products under consideration are described as unfinished perforated gun carriers which are components of perforating gun assemblies. These hot-rolled, seamless alloy steel tubes are imported in 12, 14, 16, 18 and 23 foot lengths. The tubes have outside diameters ranging from 2.75 inches (69.85 mm) to 7 inches (177.8 mm) and wall thicknesses ranging from 0.312 inches (7.92 mm) to 0.500 inches (12.70 mm).
