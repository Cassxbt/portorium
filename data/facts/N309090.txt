The item concerned is referred to as an NTC thermistor sensor (Part # 6322FR2046G). It consists of a diode-type NTC (Negative Temperature Coefficient) thermistor sensor/probe covered with a cap and terminated with an electrical connector. The electrical resistance provided by the NTC thermistor decreases as the surrounding temperature increases. The electrical resistance output is routed to a PCBA assembly/microcontroller, which then converts the resistance output into temperature data, the PCBA/microcontroller is not part of this request. This thermistor sensor is used within a variety of home appliances such as rice cookers, microwave ovens, refrigerators, etc.
