The articles under consideration are five pipe breather subassemblies designed for use in motor vehicles. Part numbers 1713A-5MR-A000, 1713A-69F-A001, 1713A-61A-A010, 1713A-6MD-A000, and 1713B-69F-A000 are identified as breather pipe subassemblies. The assemblies are designed for use in sedans and smaller motor vehicles and function to relieve internal crankcase pressure and route blow-by gasses back into the vehicle’s intake system for combustion. Part number 1713A-5MR-A000 consists of a breather pipe assembly, two water hose assemblies, and a tube clamp. Part number 1713A-61A-A010 consists of a breather pipe assembly, a water hose assembly, and a tube clamp. Part numbers 1713A-69F-A001, 1713A-6MD-A000, and 1713B-69F-A000 each consist of a breather pipe, hose clamp, and rubber breather joint.
