The Sterile Diluent for Treprostinil Injection will be imported in 50 ml single-dose vials and is available by prescription only. Each vial contains 94 mg glycine, 73.3 mg sodium chloride, sodium hydroxide (added to adjust pH), and water for injection, USP. This product is a drug diluent for use only with intravenous infusion of Treprostinil injection, which is a vasodilator used to treat pulmonary arterial hypertension.
