Samples were provided for the standard saddle and noodle and will be returned separately. The articles at issue are flotation devices described as saddles, noodles, and lounges. They are all under the AIRHEAD® Sun Comfort® brand. The saddles come in a deluxe and standard version, with the deluxe being 1.5 inches thicker. The saddles, noodles, and lounges all have different style numbers dependent on the color of the float. The colors are listed as lime swirl, raspberry swirl, and sapphire swirl. The submitted documentation indicates they are all constructed with EcoCell foam, which is considered to be a plastic. These floats are all solid foam and are not inflatable.
