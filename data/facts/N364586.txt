Images were provided in lieu of samples. There are three products under consideration, and all are described as reusable archival preservation containers. The first, STiL 16mm Archival Film Preservation Containers STILAP16400, STiLAP16800, STiLAP161200, and STiLAP162000, are specifically designed for the long-term storage and preservation of 16-millimeter (mm) motion-picture films. Each container incorporates ventilation and airflow features designed to allow air circulation around, above, and below the stored film and to assist with long-term preservation. They will be imported empty and sold independently from the films they are designed to preserve. Further, while the containers are reusable, they are intended to remain with the film during long-term archival storage. The second, STiL 35mm Archival Film Preservation Containers STiLAP351000, STiLAP351200, STiLAP352000, STiLAP353000, and STILAP652000, are the same as the above products, except they are specifically designed for the long-term storage and preservation of 35mm motion-picture films. The third, STiL 1/4-Inch Magnetic Audiotape Archival Preservation Containers, are specifically designed for the long-term storage and preservation of 1/4-inch reel-to-reel magnetic audiotapes. These containers incorporate ventilation designed to assist with humidity stabilization and long-term preservation, as well as
reel centering and storage stabilization features. They are designed specifically for archival storage rather than ordinary commercial packaging, and though reusable, they are intended to remain with the audiotapes during long-term archival storage. Models STIL-APA200C, STIL-APA270C, and STIL-APA305C differ primarily in dimensions and reel capacity but are otherwise identical.
