The subject merchandise is a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), model number TC250-S, which is the functional component of the Demand Response Receiver. The PCBA is populated with a radio frequency receiver, a microprocessor, control relays, electrical connectors, and other associated electrical components. In use, the PCBA remotely receives encoded radio signals to electrically control residential air conditioning units via hardwired connections for the purpose of reducing electrical grid demand in accordance with specified parameters.
