There are four items under consideration with this request, which are all identified as deadend connectors for  use in high voltage overhead power transmission or distribution systems and are individually identified as  (Item 1) Aluminum Bolted Deadend with Stirrup Clamp (Model No. ADES70N) (“the Stirrup Model”); (Item  2) Straight Line, Spring-Loaded, Side-Opening Aluminum Bolted Deadend (Model No. ADEZ116N) (“the  Spring-Loaded Model”); (Item 3) Straight Line, Strain Clamp Aluminum Bolted Deadend (Model No. ADS88N) (“the Aluminum Model”); and (Item 4) Aluminum Quadrant Strain Clamp Bolted Deadend  (Model No. SD185N) (“the Quadrant Model”). Item 1, the Stirrup Model, is used with distribution conductors made of aluminum, steel reinforced  aluminum, or aluminum alloy. It includes two U-bolts which are used to secure conductors in place to make  an electrical connection. The body and keeper are made of aluminum alloy, and the stirrup is made of copper. Electricity flows from the conductor through the deadend connector to the stirrup, where a hot line clamp can  be attached to a downstream electrical apparatus (e.g., switch, capacitor bank, protection device). Item 2, the Spring-Loaded Model, is used with distribution and light transmission conductors made of  aluminum, steel reinforced aluminum, or aluminum alloy. It includes a side opening with a spring-loaded  keeper and two U-bolts used to secure conductors in place to make an electrical connection. The body and  keeper are made of aluminum alloy, allowing for the safe and efficient flow of electricity. Electricity flows  from the conductor through the deadend connector to allow for a connection to be made to other parts of the  grid (e.g., insulators, fuse disconnect switches, lightning arrestors, or distribution transformers) using a clamp  or similar connector. Item 3, the Aluminum Model, is used with distribution and light transmission conductors made of aluminum. It includes two U-bolts which are used to secure conductors in place to make an electrical connection. This  model is manufactured using nonferrous conductive aluminum alloy. Electricity flows from the conductor  through the deadend connector to allow for a connection to be made to other parts of the grid (e.g., insulators,  fuse disconnect switches, lightning arrestors, or distribution transformers) using a clamp or similar connector. Item 4, the Quadrant Model, is used with transmission conductors made of aluminum. It includes five U-bolts  to secure the conductors in place to complete the electrical connection. The body and keeper are made of  aluminum alloy. Electricity flows from the conductor through the deadend connector to allow for a  connection to be made to other parts of the grid (e.g., insulators, fuse disconnect switches, lightning arrestors,  or distribution transformers) using a clamp or similar connector.
