Your request concerns two items collectively referred to as the igus Mobile Shore Power Outlet (iMSPO) system, which is used to connect externally generated shore power to a berthed vessel. You describe two configurations in which the iMSPO system may be imported, detailed as follows: In Scenario 1, the iMSPO carriage and its onboard equipment are imported. In this configuration, the imported article is the iMSPO carriage—a self-propelled shore-side electrical interface that can be positioned at any point along a marine terminal berth—along with its onboard mechanical, electrical, control, communication, grounding, and safety-interlocking equipment. This equipment comprises two drive motor/gearbox/wheel assemblies (rated 400/480 V, three-phase, up to 36 kW each); socket boxes rated at 6.6 kV or 11 kV; one or more medium-voltage sockets; and a control cabinet featuring a PLC, motor drives, a sensor package, communications equipment, and a 400/480 V-to-24 V transformer serving the control system. In Scenario 2, the iMSPO carriage and onboard equipment are imported along with an energy chain system and associated cable-management package as a single complete system. The iMSPO carriage and onboard equipment in this configuration are identical to Scenario 1. The energy chain system and cable-management package feature a mechanical cable carrier that supports, guides, separates, and protects the shoreside power, grounding, pilot, auxiliary, control, and communication cables. The components of this system include painted steel guide-rails, aluminum guide-troughs, anchoring and mounting assemblies, molded polymer cable carriers, cable separators and strain-relief components, high-voltage shore-power conductors, protective-earth and pilot conductors, 400/480 V auxiliary conductors, fiber-optic and other control/communication cables, and connection interfaces.
