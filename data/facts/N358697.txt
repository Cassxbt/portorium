Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The item under consideration is “butcher block”, i.e., finger-jointed, edge-glued rubberwood (Hevea spp., a  tropical wood) boards. The board is rectangular and measures 30 inches wide by 60 inches long by 1.5  inches thick. It is straight-cut on the edges and ends. The board is sanded, but unfinished. While you note  that the butcher block is “Perfect for kitchen countertops, desktops, tables, workbenches, etc.,” it is not  dedicated by its shape or imported condition for any particular use. As such it is merely edge-glued lumber. The classification is not affected by the finger jointing.
