Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The items under consideration are described as Single-Stage Carbon Block Under Sink Water Filtration  Systems (model numbers WD-15UC and WD-15UCW), which provides clean water directly from a faucet. The products generally follow the same structure and packaging steps, with the only difference being the  WD-15UC is black in color and the WD-15UCW is white in color. The devices connect to the cold-water line  leading to the sink. The filters are designed to reduce chlorine, contaminants, and odor for better tasting  water. The filters are to be discarded and replaced by a consumer approximately every twelve months. In  addition to the main filter, there are also several other components that help the user to install the unit. All of  the items are packaged together into one retail package. The package contains a stainless-steel water supply  pipe, timer, nuts, Teflon tape, a spring, screw, O-rings, ¼ craw, connector switch cover, connector switch,  fixed ring, joint upper cover, joint cup holder, stent, and T-joint.
