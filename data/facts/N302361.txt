Ranolazine Extended-Release Tablets (100 mg and 500 mg) are indicated for the treatment of chronic angina. Oxcarbazepine Tablets (150 mg, 300 mg and 600 mg) are indicated for use as monotherapy or adjunctive therapy in the treatment of partial seizures in adults and as monotherapy in the treatment of partial seizures in pediatric patients aged 4 years and above with epilepsy, and as adjunctive therapy in pediatric patients aged 2 years and above with partial seizures.
