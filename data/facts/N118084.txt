The merchandise under consideration is the 14” Net Media Center Combo, Part No. ICRESDC14K. This media center is an enclosure/cabinet constructed of white powder coated metal measuring approximately 14 inches by 14 inches and is 4 inches deep. This enclosure has a hinged cover with locking latch and features seventeen knockout punches for routing cables, and various other holes for mounting terminal strips and other modules. This cabinet is designed for both surface mounting and recessed mounting between 16 inch wall joists. The media center is packaged together, ready for retail sale with two terminal strips and a 1 x 6 video splitter module, which can be arranged within the box horizontally or vertically by use of the integrated snap-in module system, enabling the user to quickly rearrange the terminal strips and splitter module as desired. The media center is designed to serve as a distribution point for voice, data, video, audio and security systems for residential structured wiring systems.
