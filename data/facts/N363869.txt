The subject merchandise consists of four varieties of Frozen Mochi Ice Treats. The products are described as mochi dough encasing a dairy-free frozen filling and coated with chocolate. They will be imported in retail packages containing six pieces, with a total net weight of eight ounces per package, and are intended for retail sale as frozen confections. The four varieties are described below. The first product, Chocolate Mochi Ice Strawberry Flavor, is composed of mochi dough, chocolate compound, strawberry, sugar, strawberry juice, coconut powder, wheat flour, emulsifier (mono- and diglycerides [soy]), stabilizers (locust bean gum, guar gum, carrageenan, and hydroxypropyl distarch phosphate), and natural colors (anthocyanin). The second product, Chocolate Mochi Ice Green Tea Flavor, is composed of mochi dough, chocolate compound, coconut powder, sugar, glucose syrup, green tea powder, wheat flour, emulsifier (mono- and diglycerides [soy]), and stabilizers (locust bean gum, guar gum, carrageenan, and hydroxypropyl distarch phosphate). The third product, Chocolate Mochi Ice Salted Caramel, is composed of mochi dough, chocolate compound, coconut powder, sugar, glucose syrup, wheat flour, emulsifier (mono- and diglycerides [soy]), salt, stabilizers (locust bean gum, guar gum, carrageenan, and hydroxypropyl distarch phosphate), caramel color, and caramel flavor. The fourth product, Chocolate Coated Dubai Mochi Ice, is composed of mochi dough, chocolate compound, coconut powder, sugar, pistachio paste, kunafa, glucose syrup, wheat flour, emulsifier (mono- and diglycerides [soy]), stabilizers (locust bean gum, guar gum, carrageenan, and hydroxypropyl distarch phosphate), copper chlorophyllin color, and pistachio flavor.
