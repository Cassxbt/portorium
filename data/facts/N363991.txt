In lieu of samples, technical and illustrative literature, a product description, and a manufacturing synopsis were provided for review. The “Outdoor Mounting Kit” is a wood and metal ground support anchor that will function as a pedestal base for the Little Free Library (sold separately). The kit will consist of (1) a pressure-treated pine wood upper mounting plate that will attach to the post, (2) a decorative pressure-treated pine wood post, (3) a black powder-coated galvanized steel helical ground anchor that will be screwed directly into the soil, and (4) assembly hardware. The mounting plate will have a laser engraved phrase that states, “Take a book & Leave a book.” The mounting plate measures approximately 28cm in length, 19cm in width, and 7.6cm in height. The wooden post will have a flat full face and sloped recessed panel inserts on each side. The wooden post measures approximately 800mm in length and 89mm in width. The tapered anchor measures approximately 7cm in diameter and 68.5cm in length. The kit will mount to the Little Free Library and is intended to be installed above ground in a garden or in front of a private residence where members of the community may freely leave and take books. In the condition at the time of importation into the United States, the kit will be packaged together and offered for retail sale as one unit.
