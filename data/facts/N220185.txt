The replaceable steel blades are designed for use in “MSD7 LaBounty Shears”. The shears themselves are incorporated into a hydraulic shearing machine. The blades are over 30.5 cm in length and they fit in the cutting section of the shearing machine. The “MSD7 LaBounty Shears” are used for scrap metal processing, demolition and road/bridge construction, building demolition, wood demolition and recycling. The blades are capable of working on materials such as pipes, concrete, steel plates, brick, blocks, wires, tires, tree stumps/logs and railroad tires.
