The merchandise under consideration consists of auxiliary motorcycle footpeg kits imported from Norway. Each kit consists of two footpegs with stainless steel mounting hardware, and is designed for installation on motorcycles. Each footpeg is CNC-machined from solid aluminum alloy EN AW-6082 T6. According to the information provided, a complete kit weighs approximately 1,700 grams, of which approximately 1,550 grams consists of aluminum alloy and the remaining approximately 150 grams consists of stainless steel. The auxiliary footpegs are model-specific and are installed in addition to the motorcycle’s original equipment footpegs, which remain in place. The subject footpegs mount directly to the motorcycle frame and provide the rider with an additional foot position during operation.
