Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The items under consideration are replacement water filters, listed under model numbers EFF-6001A, EFF-6009A, EFF-6014A, EFF-6015A and EFF-6017A. The lead-free replacement refrigerator filters are designed to reduce chlorine, contaminates, and odor for better tasting water. The filters are pressed into a designated slot of the water filtration system of a refrigerator, and purified water is then dispensed through the door of the refrigerator. The filters are to be replaced and discarded by a consumer approximately every six months. The filters are substantially similar and are only differentiated by a slight change in shape that allows them to fit into a specific model refrigerator.
