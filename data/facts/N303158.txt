The first product, Bicalutamide is a non-steroidal anti-androgen. It is indicated for the treatment of metastatic carcinoma of the prostate. The second product, Linagliptin is a dipeptidyl peptidase-4 inhibitor. It is used in combination with diet and exercise for the treatment of type 2 diabetes mellitus. The third product, Aripiprazole is an atypical antipsychotic. It is indicated for the treatment of schizophrenia, bipolar mania and major depressive disorder.
