You submitted photographs of an item you call the Fishing Lasso. The merchandise is made up of an elastomeric filament sheathed in polyester braided fabric; this is considered a textile cord for tariff purposes. These cords are looped and knotted on the end and include cord locks to create the finished Fishing Lassos, which measure either 12” or 24”. To use, the Fishing Lasso is stretched around the items to be secured then the cord lock is drawn up the cord to bind the items tightly together.
