Samples were submitted with your ruling request and were forwarded to the U.S. Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This analysis has been completed. The merchandise under consideration is referred to as a “Chopped Strand Mat”, item number M723A. From the information provided, the thin sheets (voules) are used in boats, recreational vehicles, and truck panels for plastic reinforcement and acoustical insulation. The thin sheet is less than .19685 inches thick, and is imported in rolls 38 inches, 50 inches, and 60 inches in width. Our Customs Laboratory has determined that the article is composed of glass strands consisting of filament fibers distributed in random order. The strands are coated with epoxy.
