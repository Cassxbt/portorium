An ingredients breakdown for the Quorn brand Meatless & Soy-Free Meatballs (A02976), descriptive literature, and copies of existing and new packaging artworks were submitted with your letter. The meatballs are an imitation meat product based on “mycoprotein,” a meat free protein. The meatballs are composed of mycoprotein (37 percent), textured vegetable protein (wheat flour, sodium alginate, and caramel), onions, partially rehydrated egg white, textured wheat protein (wheat protein and wheat starch), coconut oil, rice starch, yeast extract, salt, natural flavors from non-meat sources, barley malt extract, gum arabic, sugar and silicon dioxide. The meatballs will be imported in frozen condition, and packed for retail sale in containers holding 10.5 ounces (300 grams), net weight.
