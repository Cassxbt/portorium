The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Andino White stone. A sample was submitted with  your ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Andino White is a cream-colored stone mottled with gray and dark red flecks. From the information you  provided, it will measure between 115 to 122 inches in length, between 65 to 75 inches in width, and either  0.75 inches or 1.5 inches in thickness. After importation, it will be cut to size and shape for such applications  as kitchen countertops, bathroom surfaces, and surfaces for office furniture. Laboratory analysis has determined that Andino White is an other igneous rock comprised predominantly of  quartz and plagioclase feldspars. The top and sides are polished and the edges are beveled.
