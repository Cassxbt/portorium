The merchandise under consideration is referred to as the “Talking Pet Starter Set,” SKU #5334273, which is a sound recording and reproducing apparatus. This battery-operated, interactive item is used to teach a pet how to communicate their needs with the use of the included devices. The user records words into the device, which are then played back when the pet presses the paw-shaped button on top. Moreover, the set comes with four different colored buttons, so four different words can be recorded/played back.
