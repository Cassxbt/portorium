The items under consideration have been identified as “WJF Swamper" and "LRF Trekker", which are front and rear bumpers designed to be used for off-road vehicles, such as Jeep and Land Rover. The bumpers are made from standard carbon steel, and the dimensions are 72" x 23" x 18". The bumpers are imported ready for installation. The manufacturing processes done overseas include laser cutting components from sheets of steel, computer numerically controlled (CNC) bending and welding together.
