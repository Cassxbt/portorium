The items under consideration are Cascadia Clips. The clips are exterior wall cladding support/thermal spacers consisting of pultruded fiberglass reinforced resin. The Cascadia Clips are manufactured in depths ranging from 2 to 8 inches. The clips/spacers are used in three different applications: Steel Stud Application, Wood Stud Application & Concrete/CMU Application. The clips/spacers are identical for each application however; the type of steel fasteners will differ based on application. Each clip/spacer will be imported with two steel fasteners for attachment to structural substrates. They will be imported in cartons of either 24 clips/spacers with 48 fasteners or 48 clips/spacers with 96 fasteners. The fiberglass clip/spacer separates the exterior z-girt, hat track or subgirt from the building’s backup wall and exterior insulation to reduce thermal bridging.
