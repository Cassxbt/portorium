The item under consideration is the WR-90 Waveguide Circulator, part number FM-W90CR001. The item is described as a passive, three-port, WR-90 waveguide circulator designed to route microwave/RF energy between its three ports over the 8.2 to 12.4 GHz frequency range. The primary function of this circulator is to allow signals traveling in different directions to share a common RF transmission path while providing isolation between designated ports. In use, this three-port device is intended for the line between an antenna and a transceiver, allowing the receive signal to come from the antenna (Port 1) to the receiver (Port 2), while the transmit signal goes from the transmitter (Port 3) to the antenna (Port 1).
