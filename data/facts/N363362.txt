Photographs and Descriptive literature were provided for our review. “Full Throttle Axolotl” is a children’s game for 2 to 5 players consisting of a toy biker figure, 3 “buddy”  cards, 67 game cards, (40 “training wheels” cards, 27 “look, no hands” cards), game instructions and 1  sticker sheet. The “training wheels” cards are designed to be played by younger players while the “look, no  hands” cards may be incorporated for older children. The object of the game is to find the 3 “buddy” cards. The “buddy” cards are shuffled with 15 random question cards, placed faced down and spread out onto a  table. The game begins by spinning the biker’s head to choose which player goes first. Players flip cards and  either answer a question, follow instructions, or find a “buddy” card. The player who takes the next turn is  determined by what is written on each card. If a “buddy” card is found, the player takes an additional turn. The player who flips over the last “buddy” card is the winner. The game is designed for children 4 years of  age and older. “Treasure Guess” is a children’s game for 2 to 5 players consisting of  9 treasure cards, 6 blank cards (for  customization), 9 treasures pieces (cork, key, wooden eye, sponge, rope knot, button, fluff, dice, ring), 1  treasure chest, a score pad and game instructions. Players take turns acting as the “Captain.”  The captain  hides a treasure in the chest while the other players close their eyes. Each player takes a turn shaking the  chest to determine what is inside. Once everyone has had a turn shaking the chest, players point to a treasure  card that matches their guess. The blank cards are used to add personal treasures to the game. Players make  a drawing of their treasure on a blank card. Each player with a correct answer receives a star on their score  sheet. Should all players fail to guess correctly, the captain is awarded a star. The player with the most stars  at the end of the game is the winner. The game is designed for children 4 years of age and older.
