The merchandise under consideration is described as Electronic Parking Brake (EPB) Actuator Assembly, part number 51788010, which is an electro-mechanical actuator designed exclusively for use in an automotive Electronic Parking Brake system. The assembly is comprised of a 12V brushed DC motor with maximum power output of 170W, multi-stage reduction gear mechanism, housing, output shaft, electronic connectors, seals, and internal actuating components. The assembly is intended to be mated to a brake caliper assembly to apply and release the parking brake by generating brake clamping force. We note that the brake calipers and the brake pistons are not imported with the actuator assembly.
