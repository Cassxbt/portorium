The subject merchandise is Freeze-Dried Worms. The product consists of whole, dead, farm-raised freeze-dried Perinereis aibuhitensis, a species of marine polychaete worm containing no added ingredients. The product is packaged and sold solely for use as recreational fishing bait and is not intended for human consumption or for use as animal feed. The product will be imported into the United States in retail packaging for commercial sale as recreational fishing bait, including anticipated fulfillment by Amazon (FBA).
