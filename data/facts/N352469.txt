Along with your request, you  submitted photographs and an exploded-view diagram depicting the helmet and its various components. The item at issue, identified as “The Ozmo Sidekick,” is a bicycle helmet constructed of multiple  components. The outer shell is composed of Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS), while the inner shell is  composed of Expanded Polystyrene (EPS) foam. Between these layers are several shock absorbing elements  made of Ethylene Vinyl Acetate (EVA) foam and Thermoplastic Elastomer (TPE) soft rubber, leaving a  narrow air gap of approximately 2mm. The bottom edge of the EPS liner is in-molded with polycarbonate  (PC), forming a PC trim that encircles the lower circumference of the helmet.
