The merchandise under consideration is described as a stainless-steel Belleville washer. This washer has a  circular shape with a central hole and uniform thickness. It has an outer diameter of 3 inches, an internal  diameter of 1.755 inches, and a thickness of .133 inches. It is designed to distribute load and reduce surface  damage when used with threaded fasteners. Belleville washers, which are made to conform to industry  standards for spring washers, are intended for use underneath a nut or screw head to maintain tension. They  are a type of lock washer.
