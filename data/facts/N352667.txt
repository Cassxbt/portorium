The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Snow Fall stone. Samples were submitted with your  ruling request and were forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This  analysis has been completed. Snow Fall is a polished white and light gray stone with amber and black streaks. From the information you  provided, its measurements upon importation are 125 inches long by 78 inches wide by 2 or 3 centimeters  thick, and is designed for use in such applications as countertops, backsplashes, and flooring. Laboratory analysis has determined that Snow Fall is an other igneous stone.
