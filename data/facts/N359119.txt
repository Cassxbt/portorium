The item concerned is foam that is used in Lightspeed Zulu 3 aviation headsets. The foam is further identified  as Banana, Left, SKU number 000000722.F. The foam is a shaped piece of flexible ethylene propylene diene monomer (EPDM) foam. It is designed to be  installed into the ear cup of the Zulu 3 headset. The foam is used for acoustic isolation by blocking external  sounds and creating a seal around the ears, thus reducing ambient noises and sound absorption, which  compliments the active noise cancelling system.
