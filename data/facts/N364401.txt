The product, Frozen Roasted Garlic Cloves, is described as fresh garlic cloves that contain no additives, sugar, water, or preservatives. The cloves undergo a multi-step manufacturing process in which the fresh garlic experiences a Maillard browning reaction. The product will be imported frozen, in bulk quantities of 10 kilograms per carton, from garlic sauce manufacturing plants, and is intended for sale to the garlic sauce seasoning sector. A photograph of the product depicts the garlic cloves with a warm color gradient ranging from light honey gold to rich deep amber brown. The frozen roasted garlic cloves are said to be manufactured by first peeling and then removing the stems of raw garlic cloves. Next the cloves are cleaned with water, drained, roasted, and baked at proprietary temperature levels and durations, subsequent to being frozen at temperatures of approximately negative eighteen degrees Celsius, for importation. The oven used to manufacture the cloves is of food grade 304 stainless steel and described as having three layers, each nine meters long. Hot air enters from the bottom and exits through the top. Fresh garlic cloves are fed into the first top layer and discharged from the third layer. Roasting takes place in the first layer, while baking occurs in the second and third. The Maillard browning reaction is said to occur during the high-temperature baking process when the garlic cloves develop a crispy surface texture and golden yellow color.
