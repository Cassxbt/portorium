The merchandise under consideration is identified as the RoboMIDI and RoboMAX, which you describe as  two tracked, remote-controlled tool carriers that are designed and marketed primarily for forestry and wild  vegetation control. The RoboMIDI and RoboMAX are diesel-powered carriers operated by a multifunction  remote with a working range of approximately 492 feet, allowing precise control of movement, steering, and  attachment positioning. Both models are offered with multiple track configurations to adapt to varied terrain. The machines are not intended for the transportation of goods or passengers over public roads and are not  designed for hauling or pushing. Their functionality is limited to tasks performed via front-mounted work  tools. Although each model can accept a range of attachments, their design, construction, and marketing  demonstrate a principal purpose in forestry and wild vegetation management. The RoboMIDI commonly  carries mulcher and forestry heads, stump grinders, winches, trenchers, and bio-shredders (chippers). The  RoboMAX is typically configured with forestry heads (swinging hammers or fixed teeth), a grass/brush head  for shrubs, pruner discs, bio-shredders, stump grinders, and stone crushers, with an optional sprayer. You state that U.S. customers overwhelmingly pair these machines with forestry heads, and the machines are  imported together with those tools. Without forestry attachments, the machines cannot perform their  intended function. While the attachments may be shipped detached for logistics, they are imported together  with the machines and are integral to their intended forestry use.
