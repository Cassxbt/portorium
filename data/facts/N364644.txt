The items in question are Ceme-Tube® single-use cylindrical tube molds for pouring concrete piers, footings, columns, and bollards. These cast-in-place forms are 4 feet in length but can be stacked to form concrete piers from 12 feet to 20 feet. The forms are made from partially recycled HDPE plastic and available in 8", 10", 12", 18", 24", and 30" diameters.
