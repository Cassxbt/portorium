The merchandise under consideration is the 80 Series Excalibur remote control system, model number  AL-1680, which is imported as a set consisting of two remote controls (key fobs), harnesses, a control  module, an antenna, a sensor, and a siren. The radio remote control key fobs are one-way, four-button remote  controls designed for keyless entry and remote starting. Moreover, the remote transmitters are wireless  remote control devices that lock and unlock a vehicle's doors and trunk and can start a car from a distance  through radio frequency (RF) protocols up to 1,500 feet (approximately 1/4 mile).
