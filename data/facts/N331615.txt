An ingredients breakdown, manufacturing flowchart, and an image of the retail packaging was submitted with your letter. The subject merchandise, “Korean Beefless Bulgogi,” is described as plant based bulgogi style strips marinated in a sweet savory soy sauce. The product is said to contain approximately 40 percent water, 25 percent sauce, 20 percent textured soy protein and 15 percent soybean oil. The product is said to be prepared by first adding water to the textured soy protein to hydrolyze it for 20 to 60 minutes at an ambient temperature. Next, the sauce and soy oil are added to the hydrolyzed mixed ingredients. The complete mixture is grilled at 380-450 degrees Celsius, then cooked for an additional 11 minutes. The cooked marinated textured soy protein: strips are cooled and packaged into plastic bags for retail sale. The packages are frozen and stored for distribution. The product will be imported frozen, in 10-ounce retail packages, net packed. Instructions on the product label include directions for the consumer to microwave, bake, pan fry or air fry the frozen strips between 3 and 7 minutes accordingly.
