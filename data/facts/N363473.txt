The merchandise under consideration is identified as an Alpha Eagle, which you describe as a self-propelled,  boom-mounted machine designed primarily for forestry and vegetation control work. The machine consists  of an articulated chassis with a permanently integrated 33-foot telescopic hydraulic boom mounted to the side  of the machine and a 90° rotating cab with wide visibility and in-cab controls. The Alpha Eagle has a 4-wheel  drive traction system and is powered by a 4-cylinder, liquid cooled diesel engine with 173 HP (129 kW). You state that Alpha Eagle machines are most commonly ordered by customers and imported into the United  States alongside one or more of the following tools: forestry heads with fixed teeth; the New Speed flail head  with a hammer-type forestry rotor; the cutter discs bar, especially designed for pruning tree branches; pincers  for collecting twigs; and stump grinders for removing the stumps of felled trees.
