The item under consideration is a sign frame identified as the “Galvanized Steel H-Frame Sign Stake.”  The  frame consists of four galvanized steel wires welded together in the shape of an “H” with two vertical wires  welded to two horizontal wires. The frame is 24 inches in height and 10 inches in width. It is designed to  support corrugated polypropylene (PP) yard signs by inserting the upper vertical support wires into the flutes  of a corrugated sign panel. The lower vertical support wires are then inserted into the ground to provide  temporary support for outdoor signage such as signs for political campaigns, real estate, contractor  advertising, or construction project signs.
