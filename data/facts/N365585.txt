Alert Guard consists of two devices that connect directly to each other via Wi-Fi, but do not require a Wi-Fi network. Each device features an ABS plastic body with a clip that allows the device to clip onto a loop or hook to an object, and both have internal geometry for the mounting of internal components (consisting of a printed circuit board with resistors, capacitors, diodes, and a small motor for vibrations). There are also two small magnets and mounting parts inside. The Alert Guard devices are intended to operate in pairs; once the signal between the two is out of range, both devices will beep and blink until they are brought back in range. When they are in range, they stop blinking and beeping.
