We’ve reviewed your request for a ruling regarding SKU 160912 Duck Tape which is said to be a utility grade duct tape with a wide variety of uses. Our office has forwarded the duct tape to the Customs and Border Protection (“CBP”) Laboratory. According to the CBP Laboratory, the duct tape is a plain-woven fabric composed wholly of polyester that is coated, covered, impregnated, or laminated on both surfaces. The face layer consists of polyethylene and the backing layer is a rubber adhesive. The total weight of the duct tape is 164.4 g/m2.
