The subject merchandise under review is herbal tincture extracts prepared from various botanicals; namely,  Cryptolepsis sanguinolenta (Ghana), Desmodium molliculum (Peru), Eleutherococcus senticosus (Eleuthero -  China), Uncaria rhynchophylla (Gou Teng- China), and Hawthorn leaves (Bulgaria, Albania, and China). Each is produced by macerating powdered plant parts in 55 percent ethanol at a ratio of one part plant to four  parts solvent. After the maceration process, the liquid is strained and bottled in retail sizes ranging from two  to sixteen ounces. The result is a hydroalcoholic solution containing the soluble constituents of the plant  material.
