The product concerned is a fixed thick film chip resistor used in surface-mount technology (SMT)  applications, part # TFR16GJ273V. This item is an individual passive electronic component consisting of an  alumina ceramic substrate, a printed thick-film resistive element, conductive terminal electrodes at both ends,  and a protective glass overcoat. The resistive element is laser-trimmed to achieve the specified resistance value. The resistor is designed to  provide a predetermined electrical resistance for electronic circuits by limiting current, dividing voltage, and  stabilizing circuit operation. It contains no active semiconductor devices or integrated circuits. The product is imported individually for mounting onto printed circuit boards (PCBs) and is used in a wide  variety of electronic equipment, including automotive electronics, industrial equipment, communication  devices, consumer electronics, and power supplies.
