The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Oskol 2, which is further described as an  Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) system. The subject device is comprised of an iPad  tablet PC within a case that incorporates the proprietary IRISBOND eye-tracking device. On the rear of the  device is a specialized mounting plate and external battery. The iPad is loaded with specific software that  makes it compatible with the eye-tracking device, however, this software does not preclude the addition of  various applications and software available from the Apple store. The subject device is meant to be used by individuals with significant physical disabilities making normal  interaction with a device impossible. The eye-tracking device allows the user to use their eyes to navigate the  screen of the tablet. Furthermore, the device incorporates speech-generation capabilities.
