Technical data sheets and a sample of the powder were also submitted. The powder, which is graded Rotoflex Pale XA8-203 59935/G, is designed for use in the graphic arts and printing ink industries as a metallic pigment in the manufacture of inks. It is not used primarily as a metallic pigment in the manufacture of paint but it can also be used for that purpose. As imported it is stated to be not dispersed in either aqueous or non-aqueous media or in paste form or any other semi-liquid dispersion. The manufacturing process is described as follows:  atomization of refined copper and refined zinc which has been molten into an alloy; atomized powder is then ground four separate times through a ball mill to process the powder from spherical shape to lamellar structure;  the lamellar shaped flakes are then polished and stearic acid is added to prevent agglomeration; and, finally the flakes are blended to produce a uniform batch of powder which is packaged in 55 gallon drums or 12 gallon pails. The subject powder sample was submitted to the Customs Laboratory which determined that it is a copper zinc alloy powder of lamellar structure.
