The merchandise under consideration is the Roku 3 gaming radio remote control. This remote control, which operates on radio frequency (RF), is used exclusively for the Roku 3 video transmitting apparatus. In addition to remotely controlling movies, music, and shows, this radio remote control contains motion-control and gaming buttons for users to play games on the Roku 3, and has a built-in headphone jack for private listening. However, this remote control is not used with a game console.
