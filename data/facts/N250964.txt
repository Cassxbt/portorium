The product is described as glass phenolic laminate. Although you did not provide a sample, you included a material data sheet and pictures. The product is in the form of rigid rectangular sheets measuring 36” to 48” in width, 48” to 108” in length and .008” to 4” in thickness. The sheets are made of phenolic resin that is reinforced with woven glass fabric. The laminate is cured to form a rigid, solid sheet that has lost the character of the woven glass fabric.
