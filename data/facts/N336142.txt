Images were provided in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described in your submission as large “Jello shot” syringes. Each clear polypropylene plastic syringe will have a 60-milliliter capacity. They measure seven inches in length with the plunger fully extended, the syringe barrel is 4.5 inches long, and they are two inches wide. Each syringe lacks any markings with volume measurements and will feature a plastic lid. You indicate they will be imported with 50 empty syringes per package. We note this product would be most similar to the syringes classified within N097125, dated April 7, 2010.
