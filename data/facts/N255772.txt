The merchandise subject to this ruling request is a vocal element, part number 844 860 55, which is a part of a signal tower. This vocal element contains a speaker and can play 15 different MP3 files and can record about 70 minutes of sound. Once a sound file is played, the vocal element keeps repeating the entire sound file until the 24 Volts used to energize it are removed. Moreover, this vocal element, which records and reproduces sound from internal semiconductor media, is attached to the top of a signal tower.
