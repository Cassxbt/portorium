The merchandise under consideration in this request is identified as a wired breakout board, part number  260-00019-000.A2. This board is comprised of a multi-layer printed circuit board (PCB), several lengths of  insulated electrical conductor cables terminated onto the PCB, and a single Molex connector attached to the  PCB. The subject board is located in the right-side earcup of a Sierra model aviation headset. It provides a  stabilized connection point between the main headset overhead cable and the multiple internal cables  (speaker, microphone, power, ground shield, etc.).
