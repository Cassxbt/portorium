Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Liposome Injection is a medicinal preparation containing Doxorubicin Hydrochloride, a cytotoxic anthracycline antibiotic, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of ovarian cancer, AIDS-related Kaposi’s sarcoma, and in combination with bortezomib for multiple myeloma. Doxorubicin Hydrochloride Liposome Injection is encapsulated in liposomes for intravenous use in dosages of 20 mg/10 ml (2 mg/ml) and 50 mg/25 ml (2 mg/ml).
