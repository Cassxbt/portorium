Technical and descriptive information was provided. The spool valve assembly, part number H20391000, is specifically designed to be used in a T-5000 series Air Diaphragm pumps. The assembly completes the pump and consists of a plastic valve body, two plastic plugs and two rubber O-rings. Once installed, an internal metal shaft of the diaphragm pump is inserted through the center of the spool valve and together, pressurized air is produced for the purpose of displacing fluid. The check valve assembly, part number 30002-0000, is specifically designed to be used in Xylem’s belt driven diaphragm pumps. The assembly completes the pump and consists of a rubber flapper, a metal valve cage and a rivet and washer to secure the flapper to the cage. Once installed into the suction and discharge sections of the pump, fluid flows into the pump orifices containing the check valves. Fluid movement actuates the flapper from the valve cage and allows for fluid to flow.
