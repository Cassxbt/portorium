Samples and descriptions of four “Diamond Dotz” craft kits were submitted with your inquiry. Each of the kits contain plastic pouches containing tiny crystal tiles of epoxy resin presorted by color, one stylus, one comfort grip (except in style DTZ12.012), one plastic tray, a 1” square piece of silicone rubber wax and an illustrated instruction sheet. Style DTZ11006 also includes 3 EVA bracelets, approximately 8” x 1”, style DTZ12012 includes 3 stickers approximately 4” x 2.7” that contain the word “LOVE” or “FAB” on them, style DD1024F contains an ABS plastic frame and a polyester fabric approximately 6” x 6” printed with a picture of a unicorn and style DD10002 includes a polyester piece of fabric approximately 19.5” x 23.6” printed with flowers and 2 blue parrots. All of the styles are overlaid with a colored grid that enables the user to follow the pattern and apply the epoxy resin tiles using the provided applicator and wax. Playing with the “Diamond Dotz” craft kit is a creative and entertaining process. The kit’s amusement value is greater than the utilitarian value of the finished article and would therefore be considered an educational craft kit principally designed for the amusement of children ages 8 and older.
