Product specification sheets describe the item as a black powder coated, aluminum distribution rack, which is available in four, six and seven foot sizes. The aluminum construction makes the item lightweight, yet sturdy. At a minimum, the rack consists of two top rails, a two piece base structure, and two side rails. Shelves and drawers are available. This distribution rack is used for installing voice and data equipment, such as patch panels, fiber optics and active components, as well as providing a structured and organized cabling system.
