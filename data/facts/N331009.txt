Images were provided in lieu of samples. The product under consideration is described as the “Patriotic LU Hair Extensions - 3PK,” item number 3903391. This item consists of a set of three plastic, alligator-style hair clips. Each clip is enhanced by a printed plastic decoration with clear fiber optic strands flowing from the bottom. Attached to the clips are battery powered bases that illuminate the plastic decorations and fiber optic strands when the power control switch is activated. The first decoration measures two inches in both height and width, and is a star in red, white, and blue colors along with a clear star-shaped plastic imitation gemstone in the center. The second and third decorations measure 1.5 inches in both height and width. One is a firework design in the colors of red, white, blue, and grey with a red star-shaped plastic imitation gemstone in the center. The other is a heart in red and white colors with a blue heart-shaped plastic imitation gemstone in the center.
