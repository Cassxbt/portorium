The merchandise under consideration is referred to as “Black Biotite Norite (Negresco)” stone. A sample  was submitted with your ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory  for analysis. This analysis has been completed. Black Biotite Norite is a surface-worked black stone mottled with white specks. From the information you  provided, its measurements upon importation are approximately 125 inches long by 78 inches wide and 3  centimeters in thickness. It is designed for use in such applications as countertops and architectural  surfacing. Laboratory analysis has determined that the Black Biotite Norite is an other igneous stone.
