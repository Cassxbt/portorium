A sample identified as item #5606, “Krafty Kids--Memories with Morgan,” was submitted for our examination and is being returned to you as requested. It consists of a retail display box containing a 9”-tall fabric stuffed doll (named “Morgan”), two small, spiral-bound “memory books” (blank paper scrapbooks), four sheets of pressure-sensitive paper stickers (lithographically printed, mainly with pictures and designs), two pen-shaped tubes of glitter glue, and six coated paper frames in assorted die-cut shapes. It appears that this kit will engage the user in at least two different activities, i.e., playing with the doll and decorating the memory books. Since all the items in the kit do not relate to the same particular activity, they will not be regarded as “goods put up in sets for retail sale,” and will be classified separately.
