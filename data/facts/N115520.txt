The merchandise under consideration is various steel brackets, part numbers CM6015, CM6016, CM6095 and CM6096. Each bracket fits into the glass run channel of a door in a Ford Crown Victoria and attaches to the rubber glass run weather strip. Part numbers CM6015 and CM6016 are used on the left front door of the vehicle and part numbers CM6095 and CM6096 are used on the right front door. These brackets serve to anchor the two ends of the weather strip to the vehicle door.
