The sticker sets are produced in a variety ofthemes. The Blue Q catalog was submitted along with a representative sample, Item # SS305, Really intoBirds. The sticker sets consist of small flat die-cut vinyl stickers with permanent adhesive and paper backing.They are sold in small boxes with a total of 32 to 48 small stickers in each box. The boxes will be packed inunits of 6 in a point of purchase (POP) inner pack box. The inner pack boxes will be bulk packed and shippedin master cartons containing 480 sticker boxes.
