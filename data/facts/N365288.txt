Item number AU8561 is identified as a siphon tube set, also referred to as a multi-use liquid transfer pump kit. The set consists of a manually actuated pump that is retail packaged with two siphon hoses, an inflation hose, an extension hose, hose sleeves, a hose connector, a plastic inflator head, an air chuck assembly, and a stainless-steel needle. The pump and the accessories packaged with the pump allow air or liquids to be transferred.
