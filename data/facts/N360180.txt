Brimonidine Tartrate and Timolol Maleate Ophthalmic Drops, imported in 5 mL, 10 mL, and 15 mL, is a  combination medication containing, Brimonidine Tartrate (an alpha-2 adrenergic receptor agonist) and  Timolol Maleate (a beta-adrenergic receptor blocker), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the  reduction of elevated intraocular pressure (IOP) in patients with glaucoma or ocular hypertension who  require adjunctive or replacement therapy due to inadequately controlled IOP.
