The Kaspi Rolling Drawer in Natural (RDN1273) and White (RDW1273) are flat-packed, ready-to-assemble (RTA) wooden storage cubes equipped with four swivel casters for easy mobility. The floor-standing furniture pieces are constructed of medium density fiberboard (MDF) panels with polyvinylchloride (PVC) edge banding and melamine-finished surfaces. The wooden panels of each item are assembled using the included knock-down camlock hardware and dowel pins. Each furniture piece has a single pull string handle and a cavity that allows for the placement, storage, and organization of objects. The dimensions of both items are approximately 10.8 inches long, 10.8 inches wide, and 11.8 inches high.
