The item at issue is a water temperature control assembly, part number 05048424AD / 54253212. The  assembly consists of a thermostat valve (an aluminum housing that incorporates a piston, spring, frame, a  rubber component, a seal cap, and a wax element), a bypass nipple, a temperature sensor and a structural  junction that features coolant bypass passages. As coolant temperature changes, the wax element of the valve  expands or contracts, causing the valve to open. Coolant then flows through the passages to the heater and the  radiator, which prevents the engine from overheating.
