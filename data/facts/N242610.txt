A sample of the “Wrist Rox Tote Kit,” item 11712, was received with your inquiry. It consists of an imitation paperboard play purse which also serves as the retail packaging. Also included are 10 sets of assorted plastic beads in different sizes and shapes (over 1,000 beads in total), elastic cord and an easy to follow instruction sheet. Children will use the kit’s beads and elastic cord to design their own uniquely crafted fashionable wrist bracelets and to engage in the imitative role-play activity of dressing up as a fancy lady. The kit’s amusement value is greater than the utilitarian value of the finished article. As assembled, the completed items will be flimsily constructed and, in all likelihood, will not be used over a long period of time. The kit is principally designed for the amusement of girls 8 years of age and older.
