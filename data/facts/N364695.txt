The item under consideration is the QuickShot NAV Catheter Cable, part number S-45800, an electrical cable assembly used to transfer electrical signals from the CardioWave QuickShot Connect to the QuickShot Catheter. The cable is constructed of three twisted-pair conductors wrapped in braided shielding, surrounded by several individually insulated single-wire conductors, which are wrapped in braided shielding, PTFE tape, and an outer silicone jacket. Each end is terminated with a proprietary multi-pin cylindrical female connector.
