The item under consideration, “Loctite Ablestik QMI519,” is a silver-filled conductive adhesive used in the electronics industry to bond integrated circuits and components to metal leadframes. The product is composed of approximately 80% silver powder with remaining components consisting of bismaleimide resin, methacrylates, and other additives. The purpose of the silver is to provide the electrical and thermal conductivity of the adhesive. The product is sold in syringes with a net weight of 19 grams.
