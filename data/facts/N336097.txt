An ingredients breakdown, description of manufacturing process, and photographs of the items were submitted with your letter. The subject merchandise consists of four frozen seafood products, as described below. Frozen Skewered Vegetable Shrimp consists of shrimp (Penaeus Vannamei, 25 percent), potato, carrot, zucchini, sake wine, basil, and spices. All ingredients are formed, steamed, cooled, frozen, then vacuum packed, and enclosed in cartons for shipment to the United States. Frozen Raw Marinated Shrimp Skewers (Sour & Sweet) consists of shrimp (Penaeus Vannamei, 85 percent), red bell pepper, spices, tomato paste, soy oil, and garlic. All ingredients are formed, steamed, cooled, frozen, then vacuum packed, and enclosed in cartons for shipment to the United States. Frozen Raw Marinated Shrimp Skewers (Sour & Spicy) consists of shrimp (Penaeus Vannamei, 85 percent), chili, spices, soy oil, and lemon. All ingredients are formed, steamed, cooled, frozen, then vacuum packed, and enclosed in cartons for shipment to the United States. Frozen Surimi Crab Stick consists of surimi (35 percent), water, salt, egg white powder, wheat starch, sugar, soybean oil, shrimp flavor, paprika color, and orange color. The surimi, in turn, consists of surimi fish content (45 percent), water, egg white powder, wheat starch, tapioca starch, monosodium glutamate, phosphate, calcium carbonate, soybean oil, sugar, salt, crab flavor, carmine color, and paprika color. All ingredients are weighed, ground, mixed, filtered, formed, steamed, cooled, thin cut, rolled, colored, wrapped, cut, cooled, frozen, film unwrapped, labeled, packed, vacuumed sealed, frozen, and enclosed into cartons for shipment to the United States.
