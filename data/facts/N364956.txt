Images were provided in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described as upcycled vinyl record wall decorations. The merchandise consists of decorative wall art handmade in the United Kingdom using genuine second-hand vinyl phonograph records that are damaged, scratched, or otherwise unsuitable for normal resale/playback. The records are sourced second-hand within the United Kingdom and vary in age, artist, label, pressing, and original country of manufacture. Their original countries of manufacture generally cannot be reliably established. In the United Kingdom, decorative artwork is prepared and cut from permanent self-adhesive vinyl using a Cricut® cutting machine. The design is manually weeded, prepared with transfer tape, and permanently applied to the surface of the record. The finished article is marketed and sold exclusively as decorative wall art/home décor and is not marketed nor sold as recorded media nor intended for audio playback.
