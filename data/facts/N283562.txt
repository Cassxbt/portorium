The merchandise under consideration is the Sharp 4K Ultra-HD LED Display system, model number PN-H701, with diagonal screen sizes of approximately 70 inches. This large monitor, which supports 4K Ultra HD resolution and both landscape and portrait viewing modes, includes four HDMI ports and a USB port. Further, this monitor contains a built-in USB media player and can reproduce video from a USB memory device. The PN-H701 has built-in dual 10W speakers for audio playback; however, does not contain a television tuner or any television reception capablility.
