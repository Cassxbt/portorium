Detailed description and a PDF version of the book were submitted for our review. The item under consideration is a hardcover board book entitled "Julia and Emi’s Hawaiian Fishy Friends."  The book contains 24 illustrated pages with text and measures approximately 7.87” x 7.87”. The book  follows two sisters, Julia and Emi, as they embark on an underwater adventure in Hawaii. The text on each  spread describes interesting facts about the appearance, behavior and unique characteristics of the reef fish  they encountered, accompanied by an illustration of the fish with its common name and Hawaiian name. The  book also emphasizes the importance of ocean safety and caring for the marine environment.
