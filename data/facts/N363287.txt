The product under consideration is described as a self-wound pressure-sensitive adhesive aluminum foil tape. It consists of aluminum foil, approximately 40 microns (0.04 mm) in thickness, coated on one side with an  acrylic pressure-sensitive adhesive. The adhesive-coated foil is slit to a width of approximately 2 inches and  wound into rolls approximately 2,000 feet in length on a 3-inch core. The product is imported in self-wound  rolls without a release liner, backing material, or carrier layer, with the adhesive surface contacting the  aluminum foil surface of the next layer in the roll. The merchandise is imported as a finished  pressure-sensitive adhesive tape ready for industrial use.
