The merchandise under consideration is AM/FM reception apparatus for radiobroadcasting, models INFPRV250, INFPRV90, JBLPRV175, MILPRV22, and MILPRV19. All models have a radio panel with buttons for the control of audio source, station/tuning, and volume. They also incorporate a screen for displaying information, such as time, radio station, musical track information, etc. Additionally, these radiobroadcast receivers, which are typically installed in boats, contain AM/FM tuners, USB audio playback and Bluetooth capability. Moreover, these radiobroadcast receivers are used in motor vehicles and can reproduce sound by reading and decoding audio files from a USB memory device. None of the subject models are capable of operating without an external source of power.
