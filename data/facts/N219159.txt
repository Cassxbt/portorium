The merchandise subject to this ruling is a CT/Snubber PCB and an IGBT PCB. A sample of the CT/Snubber PCB was submitted and is being returned to you as per your request. A photograph of the IGBT PCB was furnished because a sample was not available. The photograph will remain within our file. The CT (Current Transformer)/Snubber PCB is a printed circuit board that is able to handle large current peaks, and is used for the protection of other components/circuits in the inverter itself. This printed circuit board is populated with electronic components, specifically resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, and inductors. As such, it is a printed circuit assembly as defined within Additional U.S.
