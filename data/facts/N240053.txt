The merchandise under consideration is powder coated steel mounts and adapters for projectors, TVs or monitors. Each model is imported with installation hardware. Model PP-FCTA is a suspended ceiling mount adapter, which includes a main ceiling plate, a lower adapter plate, an upper adapter plate, an escutcheon ring, screws, nuts, turnbuckles and galvanized wire. It is designed to hook into an existing building frame and sit hidden above the ceiling tile. It mounts a camera from the ceiling without the use of any other components; to mount a projector from the ceiling, it must be used in conjunction with a universal projector mount (not included). Model PP-4 is a ceiling adapter. It installs on a solid ceiling surface and includes a threaded coupler for attaching a pipe/suspension adapter (not included), four integrated cable channels, and a security screw to lock the pipe into position. Model MAG-PRO is a universal mount with an integrated coupler for mounting projectors weighing up to 10 lbs. It includes the mount, four mounting legs, a CLS, four barrel caps, two star washers and various fasteners. It attaches to the projector and requires an adapter (not included) to mount the projector to a ceiling. Model PSD-SHB is a steel shelf designed exclusively to mount onto Premier Mount’s dual pole cart or floor stand. It measures 20 inches in width by 16 inches in depth and serves to hold audio video equipment, etc. Model PSD-SPA is a single-display adapter bracket designed to fit over the two poles of Premier Mount’s dual pole cart or floor stand. It is a structurally significant part that mounts the flat panel monitor to the cart or floor stand.
