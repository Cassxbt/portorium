The machine utilizes a high-speed spindle equipped with a circulardiamond blade to singulate individual integrated circuits (die) from a processed silicon wafer. The singulationprocess involves securing the wafer on a vacuum chuck, automatically aligning the cutting path to a precisionof 0.001 mm, and performing a “full-cut” through the wafer using a diamond blade rotating at speeds up to60,000 rpm. The DAD3231 emphasizes high performance and expandability, with an option to handle up to8-inch wafers.
