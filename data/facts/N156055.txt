The product consists of black pressure sensitive butyl rubber based adhesive mastic sealant in sausage form for use in the manufacture of road marking tape. The sausages are imported in collapsible wooden crates stacked five or six high. The wooden crates are considered of a kind normally used for such goods and therefore will be classified together with the imported product.
