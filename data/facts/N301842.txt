The four items at issue are tire valves, part numbers TRI416B, VHI758/BG, VHIN321 and VHI546/BG. Each hand-operated valve has a metal cap that seals and protects the valve mechanism when the tire valve is not in use and a threaded stem. The tire valves allow air to flow in or out of a tire once the stem of the valve has been depressed. The brass valves have a pressure rating under 850 kPa.
