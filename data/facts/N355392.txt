The items concerned are referred to as Left and Right Badges that are used in the manufacture of  Lightspeed’s Sierra Aviation Headsets. The two applicable SKU numbers are 250-00019-000.B3 - Left and  250-00020-000.B3 - Right. These items are used in the production of noise canceling aviation headsets. The Left Badge is utilized on the  left ear cup assembly and the Right Badge is utilized on the right ear cup assembly of the Sierra headset. Both badges are made from aluminum and serve the functions of:  Structural Support - The badge is made of aluminum and helps reinforce the ear cup’s structure, which  is important for maintaining a tight seal around the ear—critical for both passive and active noise  canceling. Vibration Control – The badge helps reduce unwanted vibrations or resonance in the ear cup. This can  improve sound clarity and reduce distortion, especially in high-end headsets. Shielding - Aluminum creates a shielding effect against electromagnetic interference, which helps  protect the sensitive electronics used in active noise canceling systems. Aesthetic and Branding - While not directly related to noise canceling, aluminum badges give the  headset a premium look and feel, which can be important for professional or consumer-grade models.
