The item concerned is a bare printed circuit board (PCB) referred to as the “Trilogy Interface PCB” (#1080896+R4). This specialized PCB is designed, manufactured and used exclusively with the Respironics’ Trilogy Ventilator devices. The Trilogy ventilator provides continuous or intermittent positive pressure ventilationfor the care of individuals who require mechanical ventilation. This Printed Circuit Board (PCB) is an integral part of, and is designed to be used exclusively with The Trilogy series of ventilators. The item concerned is a custom-made/designed, four layer printed circuit board made specifically for use in controlling the user interface of the Trilogy ventilator. Since the design of this PCB is specific for use solely within the Trilogy ventilator, there is no other foreseeable use of this PCB by any other party or for any other purpose than in assembling a Trilogy ventilator.
