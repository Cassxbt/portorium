Images were provided in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is the “Set/3 Framed Medallions,” item number WQ650. This set consists of three frames made of pine wood and glass, each containing one hand cast plastic resin medallion that is glued onto a piece of canvas and permanently mounted onto a piece of medium-density fiberboard (MDF). All three frames are constructed the same way, but each medallion has a different design. The frames measure 16 inches in length by 1.5 inches in width by 16 inches in height and include a pair of sawtooth hangers on the back. This is a decorative item that is meant to be hung on the wall.
