A representative sample was submitted with your request and will be returned to you. The product to be imported is described as a steel tank tee assembly, Part Number 07ST-25-2. The assembly consists of a cast stainless steel tank tee from Indonesia and a stainless steel pipe nipple from China. Each of these foreign components is sent to Canada for packaging and subsequently sold as a single unit to approved dealers. The pieces will be assembled at the time of installation by threading the nipple into the tank tee making a water tight fit. The assembled unit is used in a water pressure system to connect a water supply line from a water well to a water storage tank. The tank tee has several threaded ports for various types of valves and accessories such as a pressure gauge, sediment faucet, pressure relief valve, or manostat.
