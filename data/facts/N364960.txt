The three items are non-aerosol home mist sprays sold under the Ofa Karri brand and packaged for retail sale in 200 mL glass bottles fitted with pump sprayers. The product labels describe each item as a natural home spray, and the directions for use instruct users to spray the mist into the air. Item 1, Ofa Karri Botanical Cure, contains alcohol, lemon peel oil, ho leaf oil, peppermint oil, tea tree leaf oil, and eucalyptus radiata leaf oil. Item 2, Ofa Karri Deep Harmony, contains alcohol, vetiver root oil, ho wood oil, pink pepper fruit oil, and may chang fruit oil. Item 3, Ofa Karri Sunshine Therapy, contains alcohol, lemon peel oil, grapefruit peel oil, geranium oil, lavender oil, and bergamot peel oil.
