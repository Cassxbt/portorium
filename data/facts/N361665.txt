The kit consists of four water-soluble hydro dip sheets made of polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) each measuring fiveinches by five inches, five alcohol-based markers, two Halloween-themed patterns/designs printed on paper,two mini binder clips, and a set of instructions packaged together in a retail box.The user secures a hydro  dip sheet to the top of one of the patterns using the binder clips, then fills in the design using the blue, purple,red, black and green markers.They  lay the water-soluble hydro dip film on a water surface provided  thenand dip a mini pumpkin down through the film, then scoop it back up, creating a U-shaped path back to thewater’s surface and transferring the pattern onto the pumpkin.The “Hydro Dip Mini Pumpkin Kit” is considered a set for tariff purposes.
