The article under consideration is described as the Blackburn AirFix CO2 Inflation System/Multi-tool that uses disposable carbon dioxide cartridges to inflate bicycle tires. The product also comes with a 16 gram threadless CO2 cartridge. You state that the product is being marketed as a “hand operated bicycle air pump” with multi-tool accessories. Based on the information provided, the tire inflator is not a pump, but rather a hand operated valve designed to control the flow of carbon dioxide from a cartridge into a tire. The inflator appears to be essentially of plastic with some metal components.
