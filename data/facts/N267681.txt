The merchandise at issue consists of seven models of Kappa cutting and/or stripping machines. The seven models are: the Kappa 206, the Kappa 310, the Kappa 320, the Kappa 322, the Kappa 330, the Kappa 331 and the Kappa 350. The Kappa 206 machine measures and cuts wire, cable and tubing. It can cut a variety of round and flat conductors, hoses, PVC stripes, various foils, paper and fabric. The cutter head can cut tubing with an outer diameter of up to 16mm, AWG26 – AWG2 and a passage width of 3.2 inches. The Kappa 310, AWG34 – AWG10 and flat cable up to 8mm with stripping lengths up to .999.9mm/39.37 inches. The Kappa 320 cuts and strips round cables and wires with outer diameters up to 10mm, AWG30 – AWG8 and flat cable up to 12mm with stripping lengths up to .999.9mm/39.37 inches. The Kappa 322 model cuts and strips round cables and wires with an outer diameter of up to 12mm, AWG30 – AWG5. It can cut and strip multiple inner conductors and flat cables up to 12mm with stripping lengths up to .999.9mm/39.37 inches. The Kappa 330 model can cut and strip round cables with an outer diameter up to 16mm, AWG24 – AWG2 and flat cables up to 40mm with stripping lengths up to .999.9mm/39.37 inches. The Kappa 331 model is a rotary system that cuts and strips round cable and multilayer cables with an outer diameter up to 16mm, AWG23 – AWG2. It performs full and partial pull-off operations on individual layers and strips the outer jackets from jacketed cables with outside diameters of up to 16 mm that are with or without shielding. The Kappa 350 can cut and strip round cables with an outer diameter up to 35mm, AWG14 – AWG5/0 and stripping lengths up to .999.9mm/39.37 inches. In addition, the Kappa 310, 320, 322, 330 and 350 models can cut cables and wires to length, strip the cables with partial and/or full pull off and strip short wires. They can also perform feeding, marking, stacking and coiling of wire and cable.
