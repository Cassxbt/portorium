The “Speedball” game is a free-standing, indoor gaming unit measuring approximately 106” L x 24.5” W x  60.5” H, and is primarily composed of MDF (medium-density fiberboard). It is designed to emulate the  classic “Skee-Ball” arcade game. The unit features a 9-foot long inclined playfield, an electronic scorer,   automated ball return, and 7 target holes. Gameplay involves rolling the balls up the ramp and over a  molded hump aiming to land the balls into the bullseye rings located at the end of the alley. The rings  contain the target holes with values ranging from 10 to 100 points. The objective of the game is to score the  highest number of points possible. The “Speedball” game comes with 4 plastic game balls and operates via a  corded AC adapter.
