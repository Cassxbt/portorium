The merchandise under consideration is a flexible straw bracelet described as the “Wristraw Signature.” The item is a combination wrist bracelet and a flexible straw for consuming beverages and is available in three sizes: 18 centimeters (cm), 19 cm, and 22 cm. The primary components are a flexible corrugated steel tube and a silicon containment tube. The flexible steel tube is inserted into the silicon tube and held in place by two steel plugs at each end. When needed, the item can be removed from the wrist, and the flexible steel tube can be removed from the containment tube and be used as a drinking straw.
