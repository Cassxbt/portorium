The items in question are five (5) steel frame weldments engineered specifically for Machine Lab’s RoboCraftsman Cell containerized robotic industrial forming system. The RoboCraftsman Cell employs two programmed, synchronized industrial robotic arms (manipulators) to shape sheet metal incrementally without using hard dies or molds, a process referred to as roboforming. The RoboCraftsman Cell’s robotic arms are also programmed to cut and drill material by changing the manipulator’s end-of-arm tooling. The Cell also laser scans the material for measurements, adjustments, and quality control. Neither the robotic manipulators nor any other components of the RoboCraftman Cell will be imported with the five subject weldments. The first item, #4-01710, is the Weldment Base Frame R1. This component serves as the structural platform for the RobotCraftsman Cell. The industrial robot mounts to a carriage plate that interfaces with a gear rack installed on the rail structural weldment that is then installed to the side frame weldment. This assembly is bolted to both the based frame weldment and the floor. The second item, #4-01885, is the Weldment Base Frame R2. This component is similar the #4-01710 Weldment Base Frame R1. The third item, #4-01643, is the Weldment Forming Frame. This fixture component serves as the material supporting structure for the RoboCraftsman Cell. The fixture is mounted to the base frames and supports the sheet metal to be processed by the industrial robots. The four item, #4-01949, is the Weldment Rail Structure, High Capacity. This component serves as the structural platform for the RoboCraftsman Cell. The industrial robot mounts to a carriage plate that interfaces with a gear rack installed on this structure. The fifth item, #4-02051, is the Weldment Side Frame. This component serves as the structural platform for the RoboCraftsman Cell. The industrial robot mounts to a carriage plate that interfaces with a gear rack installed on the structural rail weldment that is then installed to this side frame weldment. In your submission, you state that all five weldments are specifically designed to enable robotic, dual-sided incremental forming, scanning, and cutting of sheet metal parts. The weldments include features such as reinforcement gussets, passthroughs for electronics, and precision alignment features for maintaining robot accuracy during operation. These features enable a platform for robotic forming, scanning, cutting, drilling, and potentially other manufacturing operations not currently developed.
