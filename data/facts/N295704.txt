The item under review is identified as “The Chiller.” The article is a cylindrical beverage holder, which is approximately 10 inches by 5 inches by 5.5 inches. The exterior is molded ethylene vinyl acetate (EVA) plastic, that contains circular openings for the cans or bottles and provides insulation for the hard plastic interior that is filled with a freezable fluid. The freezing fluid is a polymer suspension of sodium 2-propenoate, also known as sodium polyacrylate. This item can hold three 12 ounce cans or bottles placed through the side or a single 750 milliliter bottle placed through the top. This item is intended to chill a small quantity of personal beverages. It does not include a strap, closure, or other means of being carried.
