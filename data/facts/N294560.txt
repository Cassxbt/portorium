LOWILITE® UV B1260 stabilizer is described as a liquid blend of a phenolic antioxidant and ultraviolet light stabilizers. It consists of magnesium carbonate (CAS# 57834-33-0), bis(1,2,2,6,6-pentamethyl-4-piperidyl) sebacate (CAS# 41556-26-7), benzenepropanoic acid, 3,5-bis(1,1-dimethylethyl)-4-hydroxy-, C13-15- branched and linear alkyl esters (CAS# 171090-93  -0), and methyl 1,2,2,6,6-pentamethyl-4-piperidyl sebacate (CAS# 82919-37-7). The product is said to be developed specifically to protect polyurethanes exposed to exterior weathering conditions. It is also suitable for light stable polyurethane-based pastes.
