Model 1PAFFLEXR is identified as a 6" rechargeable USB fan or a portable stroller fan. The circular fan  features a power button, indicators for charging and fan speed, a hook that allows the fan to be hung and  three flexible legs that allow the fan to be placed on a flat surface or wrapped around the handle of a stroller,  a chair, a car seat, etc. The fan is powered by a rechargeable battery.
