The item is referred to as the Kewl-It Chiller, model number KEWL-IT. It is a beverage-cooling device  designed to rapidly chill standard canned or beverage containers used in a home freezer. The beverage cooler  is comprised of two aluminum components both of which are extruded with an Aluminum Association Series  Designation (AASD) of 6064. It includes four stainless steel threaded knobs with an 5 millimeters (M5) stem  size and plastic handle. The item measures approximately 9¼ inches long by 12 1/8 inches high by 4 9/16  inches wide. The beverage cooling device is designed to accommodate up to two 12-ounce standard-sized  beverage cans or any other beverage container of the same diameter up to 12 inches long.
