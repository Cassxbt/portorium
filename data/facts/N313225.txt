The first item under consideration is identified as the AirSense 10 Power Supply, Part No. R370-7407, which you describe as a 90W AC adaptor designed to work exclusively with ResMed Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP) respiratory devices. The function of the power supply is to provide primary power directly to the CPAP machine by connecting it to a standard wall outlet where the adaptor converts the alternating current input of 100-240 V to an output of 24 V, 3.75 A maximum direct current output. You state that the power supply has a specialized output that is uniquely designed to work solely with the ResMed CPAP machine by detecting the type and model of machine and facilitating a serial communication between the main microprocessor and the humidifier and/or alarm modules. The second item under consideration is identified as the Astral External Battery, Part No. 27919, which you describe as an external battery designed to provide increased battery capacity and autonomy for the Astral series of ventilators, which are used in hospital and home environments. The external battery incorporates lithium-ion battery packs, an embedded direct current (DC) input port, an integrated DC output cable, and a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA). The DC output cable has been specially designed to include an ID pin, which is used exclusively by the Astral system, and allows the external battery to properly send signals and communicate with a second external battery or the Astral ventilator. Up to two external batteries can be connected to an Astral device to provide a total of 24 hours of autonomy during typical use.
