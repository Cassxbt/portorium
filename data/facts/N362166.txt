The “Men’s Perry Ellis Magnifico” (SKU # PEA23F0249A-2E) is a man’s closed-toe, closed-heel, lace-up  sneaker-style shoe featuring a polyurethane upper. The outsole is made from thermoplastic rubber with a  very thin application of leather dust or fiber application on the external surface that contacts the ground. Additionally, a foxing-like band is present where the outer sole unit overlaps the upper material by more than  ¼ inch. Although the shoe has an athletic appearance, including lace closures, it does not have sufficient  underfoot cushioning that is characteristic of athletic shoes. The F.O.B. price per pair of the completed  footwear is $10.18.
