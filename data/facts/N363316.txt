The item at issue with this request is an aircraft cockpit Multifunction Control and Display Unit (MCDU)  control panel subassembly, identified by part number 865-000163-2Z. The control panel subassembly is a  component of the MCDU. A complete MCDU is installed in the cockpit of an aircraft and functions as the  interface between the flight crew and the aircraft’s Flight Management System. The Flight Management  System allows the the flight crew to input, select, modify, and display flight management information  including the flight plan, navigation, and aircraft system data, et al. In it’s imported condition, the control  panel subassembly cannot function as a complete MCDU because it does not contain an LCD display nor is it  calibrated or tested for final use. The control panel subassembly is comprised of a number of components,  including an electronic control assembly (ECA) which is a printed circuit board assembly populated with  several push-button momentary contact switches, LEDs, resistors, photodiodes, a 40-pin electrical connector,  and other minor electrical components. The ECA is further assembled onto a supportive backplate and front  bezel with cutouts for the various push-button switches. After importation, an LCD display panel is added  and the device is calibrated and tested.
