Drawings were provided. The items in question are referred to as Compression Tubes/Ni-Resist Spacers, part numbers 22-11004, 22-13000, 22-15005, 22-16004 and 22-17001. The items function as compressors for a stack of pump diffusers and as a pump spacer. The compression tubes/spacers are manufactured to specific dimensions and tolearances and are used in centrifugal pumps. The centrifugal pumps are used in various applications, such as for fluid transfer, gas processing, mine dewatering, and saltwater disposal.
