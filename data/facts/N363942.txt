Item D280148, the “Fento Display-Stand,” is a floor-standing mesh display stand designed for use in retail spaces. The body and legs are constructed of a matte black, powder-coated steel tube frame. A black stretched-steel mesh display panel extends the full length and width of the frame. Various straight hooks will be mounted onto the mesh panel and are used to hold and display products offered for retail sale. A removable foam-board nameplate bearing the company name “Fento” is positioned at the top of the stand. The base is fitted with four twist-out stabilizing feet made of rubber and plastic. At the time of importation into the United States, the stand will be imported unassembled with all hardware necessary for assembly. Once assembled, it will measure approximately 27.17 inches long by 24.65 inches wide by 71.26 inches high.
