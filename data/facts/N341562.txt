Item OEM25-0562, described as a “Child Handheld Fan,” is a foldable fan intended to be used by waving it back and forth to provide a cool stream of airflow to a child’s face. The handheld fan consists of two components: a 100 percent polyvinyl chloride (PVC) frame of sticks/ribs and a textile fabric composed of 100 percent polyester woven, printed fabric. The folded fabric is glued to the ribs of the PVC frame on one side. The PVC frame is held together by an aluminum loop. The fan measures 17 inches in length by 9 ½ inches in height when fully opened and measures 1 inch in length by 9 ½ inches in height when closed.
