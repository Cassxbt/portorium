Photographs and descriptive literature were provided for our review. The “Erase n’ Play Tic-Tac-Toe,” item number 112-147, is comprised of 2 fruit motif Tic Tac Toe game boards measuring approximately 6.3” by 6.3” and 20 eraser game pieces (4 eraser designs: kiwi, apple, lemon and watermelon). In Tic Tac Toe, the first player to place 3 like game pieces in a row, vertically, horizontally, or diagonally, is the winner of the game. The game is intended for children ages 4 years and older.
