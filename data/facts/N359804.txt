The IndeeLift People Picker Upper (model PPU-S) is described as equipment used to lift individuals from the  floor to a sitting or standing position. Its main components include a vertical post, a mounting ramp seat, an  actuator assembly, a battery unit, a wired remote, rise-assist handles, and dual safety belts. The mounting  ramp seat lowers to the ground and serves either as a seat or a standing platform capable of lifting individuals  weighing up to 300 pounds to a height of 30 inches.
