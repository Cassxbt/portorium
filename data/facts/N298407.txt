This office reviewed the documentation and photos you submitted. The article under consideration is described as “hand-painted relief gallery art – bicycle”. It consists of a decorative image of a bicycle crafted out of galvanized metal and mounted to a rectangular pinewood base. Both the decorative metal bicycle and the pinewood base are hand-painted. The article is 23 inches wide by 35 inches high and is to be used for decoration on the wall or above the fireplace as wall art. The wall art is composed of different components (base metal and wood) and is considered a composite good.
