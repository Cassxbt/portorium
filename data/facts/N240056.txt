From the flyer which you supplied, the Audit-Buddy can measure temperature and humidity at three different heights due to its integral carbon rod and a fan to draw in the air. At intervals selected by the user, it will measure, time stamp, and record the temperature and humidity at up to the three different heights. A typical use would be to track temperature and humidity variations near racks of thermally sensitive equipment. The temperature measurement accuracy is listed as plus or minus .9 Farenheit and the humidity accuracy is listed at 3 percent relative humidity.
