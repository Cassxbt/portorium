The four items at issue are tire valves, item numbers VH1543C, VH1543D/BG, VH1545E/BG, VH1546D/BG and VH1546E/BG. Each hand-operated valve has a metal cap that seals and protects the valve mechanism when the tire valve is not in use and a threaded stem. The tire valves allow air to flow in or out of a tire once the stem of the valve has been depressed. The brass valves have a pressure rating of 448.15 kPa.
