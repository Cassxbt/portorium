Futaba, Inc. The product under consideration is referred to as “Glass Fiber Reinforced Plastic (GFRP) Closet Rod”. The  product is composed of a polyester resin that has been reinforced with glass fibers. The rod is hollow with a  consistent cross-section and is available in both round and oval profiles, each cut to a standard length for ease  of installation. The rods are painted in various decorative finishes such as dull brass, dull chrome, dull nickel,  matte black, and oil-rubbed bronze. The product is imported in a ready-to-use condition as a horizontal rod  installed in a closet to hold clothing suspended from hangers.
