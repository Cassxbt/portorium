The ruling was requested on various decorative signs and wall hangings. Photographs were provided for review. Item “Sample 6” is a glass plaque in a wood frame measuring 20”W x 11” H. Silk screened in the center of the glass is the word DREAM in bold capital letters. It is designed for hanging on a wall. Item “Sample 7” is a printed iron sign measuring approximately 18”W x 24.5”H. The metal is intentionally weathered. Its design includes a curved arrow around three sides of the middle oval. Eleven battery-operated LED lights dot the arrow portion and are controlled by an on/off switch on the side. Onto the sign are affixed paper decals which are printed, via 4 color printing, with the words, “EAT HERE It’s Cheap and Homemade” in stylized fonts. Item “Sample 8” is a printed iron sign measuring approximately 7”W x 16.5”H. The metal is intentionally weathered. It is shaped roughly like a hand with the index finger pointing downward. Five battery-operated LED lights dot the center and finger of the hand. Onto the sign are affixed paper decals which are printed, via 4 color printing, with the words, “Open ALL NITE LAUNDRY ROOM Drop your pants HERE” in stylized fonts. Item “Sample 9” is a canvas wall hanging measuring 17.5”W x 17.5”H. Machine-printed onto the canvas is the image of a snowman. He is wearing a top hat and garland of evergreens with a bow and holly leaves with berries around his neck. Surrounding his base are more evergreens, pine cones, holly and berries. He is holding a small evergreen tree on which is perched a cardinal. On his base of snow is etched a poinsettia design, and snow flurries fill the air.
