Sitagliptin Phosphate Monohydrate is an antidiabetic drug of the dipeptidyl peptidase-4 (DPP-4) inhibitor class. It is indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus. Spironolactone is an aldosterone-receptor antagonist with diuretic, antihypertensive and antiandrogen activities. It is indicated for the management of primary hyperaldosteronism, congestive heart failure, essential hypertension, hypokalemia, heart failure and edema. Ticagrelor is a P2Y12 platelet inhibitor indicated to reduce the risk of myocardial infarction and stroke in patients with acute coronary syndrome (ACS) or a history of myocardial infarction (MI).
