FACTS:The subject merchandise, style number 131411, described as a “No Fly Zone® Rugged Quilted Dog Blanket,” measures 34 x 44 inches and consists of three layers:  a 100 percent cotton woven denim bottom; 100 percent polyester fill; and a 60 percent polyester, 40 percent acrylic knit fleece top. The edges of the blanket are finished with self-fabric denim binding. The item is designed and marketed for the animal to lie on in the car, home or crate.
