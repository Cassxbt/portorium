Product information and photos were submitted for our review.The products under consideration are many styles of wooden bookends crafted from solid eastern white pine(Pinus strobus, a coniferous wood). They vary in size from 7" high by 5" wide by 3 1/2" deep to 8 1/2" highby 7" wide by 3 1/2" deep. The bookends have a generally “L” shape and are adorned with wooden shapes torepresent a cactus, a fox, a space ship, flowers, or other motifs.
