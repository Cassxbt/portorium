Item 1, Ofa Karri Body Tenderness Soothing Body Oil, is a body massage oil packaged for retail sale in a 100 mL glass bottle. The directions instruct the consumer to massage the oil generously into the desired areas until fully absorbed. The ingredients listed on the packaging label include olive and sunflower oils; maritime pine, ginger, black spruce, caraway, tarragon, clove bud, black pepper, lemongrass, and marjoram essential oils; arnica and St. John’s wort extracts; and tocopherol, limonene, eugenol, citral, linalool, and geraniol. Items 2 and 3, Ofa Karri Ofadisia Women Sensual Body Oil and Ofa Karri Ofadisia Men Sensual Body Oil, respectively, are body massage oils packaged for retail sale in 100 mL glass bottles. The directions for both products instruct consumers to apply the oil generously to the body and massage it into the skin as desired. The precautions state that the products are for external use only and should not come into contact with the eyes, mucous membranes, or intimate areas. Item 2, Ofa Karri Ofadisia Women Sensual Body Oil, contains olive, castor, evening primrose, jojoba, and sunflower oils; ylang-ylang, geranium, patchouli, palmarosa, and frankincense essential oils; and tocopherol, geraniol, citronellol, linalool, benzyl benzoate, limonene, citral, farnesol, benzyl salicylate, eugenol, and isoeugenol. Item 3, Ofa Karri Ofadisia Men Sensual Body Oil, contains olive, castor, evening primrose, jojoba, and sunflower oils; Scotch pine, clove bud, vetiver, Atlas cedar, and may chang essential oils; and tocopherol, eugenol, limonene, citral, linalool, geraniol, and citronellol.
