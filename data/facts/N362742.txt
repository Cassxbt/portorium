The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Sixunited STHT1 – AMD AI Max+ 395 Thin Mini  ITX Motherboard. The subject device is comprised of a printed circuit board (PCB) upon which is mounted  an AMD accelerated processing unit (APU) as the critical component. This APU integrates a central  processing unit (CPU) and a graphics processing unit (GPU) into a single semiconductor device. Additionally, the PCB incorporates 32GB of memory, network interface controllers, storage interface  connectors, power management circuity, and various other connection points for expansion. The subject  motherboard will be used as the central computing component within computers and data servers in various  applications such as gaming, graphics processing, and artificial intelligence.
