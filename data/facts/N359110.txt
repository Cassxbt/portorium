The Sea Buckthorn Oil Suppositories are packaged for retail sale in a paperboard box containing 10  suppositories. According to the product label, the suppositories are intended for vaginal or rectal use to  provide moisture and comfort. The ingredients include hydrogenated palm kernel oil, Hippophae rhamnoides  (sea buckthorn) fruit oil, polyglyceryl-3 polyricinoleate, and butylated hydroxytoluene.
