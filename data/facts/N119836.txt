The item in question is a non-dairy frozen dessert product (edible ice) made from coconut milk and other ingredients. Photos accompanying your letter indicate that it will be available in two different flavors (strawberry and chocolate), and will be supplied in 1-pint (16 fl. oz.) retail tubs. The item will be imported, stored, sold and consumed (like ice cream) in a frozen condition. The strawberry-flavor version contains water, coconut milk, strawberry, sugar, maltodextrin, dextrose monohydrate, mono and diglycerides of fatty acids, locust bean gum, guar gum, carrageenan, salt and ascorbic acid. The chocolate-flavor version contains coconut milk, water, sugar, cocoa powder, maltodextrin, dextrose monohydrate, mono and diglycerides of fatty acids, locust bean gum, guar gum, carrageenan and salt.
