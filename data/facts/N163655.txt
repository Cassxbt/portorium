Samples were provided. There are two articles at issue in your submission for a ruling request. The first article is a round tape measure with a textile cover designed to resemble a panda bear. The measuring tape that pulls out is 60 inches in length, and is marked in inches on one side and centimeters on the other. The measuring tape retracts by pushing a button under the panda’s nose. The bottom edge of the tape measure remains visible when the textile cover is on. The second article is described as a row counter for use by knitters and crochetiers. The article is a small two-digit counter that increases by one each time a button at the top is depressed. The counter can be zeroed manually by turning the digit wheels at the side of the article. The counter can be held in the hand or worn around a user’s neck by means of an attached cord.
