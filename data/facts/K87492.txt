The merchandise under consideration consists of four insulated cables and one connector. Samples of all five items were submitted with your ruling request. They are identified in your ruling request as RJ 45 8P8C Plug (P/N: 9213E024-009), MD 8 P/M to BNC Plug (P/N: 9302E024-001), Phone Plug 6P4C to 6P4C (P/N: 9207E024-005), USB Cable (P/N: 9903E024-006) and A1012 Series Din Sockets. The RJ 45 8P8C Plug is a 24AWG, 8 conductor standard LAN cable with standard telephone type male connectors at both ends measuring approximately 70 inches. This cable is designed for use in a Local Area Network (LAN). The MD 8 P/M to BNC Plug is a 3C-2V 75 OHM Coaxial Cable measuring approximately 40 inches long with a male BNC plug on one end and a male 8 pin round connectors at the other ends. From the information provided by you, this cable is designed for connecting a medical personal computer (PC) to a medical device monitor. The Phone Plug 6P4C to 6P4C is a 26AWG, 4 conductor standard telephone cable rated at 150 V. This cable measures approximately 72 inches long with male connectors at both ends. The USB (Universal Serial Bus) Cable is a 4 conductor cable rated at 30 V with two 28AWG and two 24AWG wires measuring approximately 40 inches. This cable has a 4-pin Type A USB port plug at one end and a Type B at the other end. As per your literature, this cable serves as a connection between a PC and other USB devices. The Series Din Socket is a miniature circular female 6-pin socket/connector with a full metal shield. This connector is designed to be permanently attached to a printed circuit board (PCB) protruding on a 90º right angle and accepts a round male 6-pin plug from other devices such as a keyboard or mouse. While your request describes the cables as telecommunication cables, part number 9903E024-006 can not be identified as such.
