The item under consideration is identified as the “LED Work Light”, Model Number 902. The work light is described as a portable hand held light that is powered by three AAA batteries. The work light is comprised of an aluminum housing with 16 Light Emitting Diodes (LEDs). The LEDs are housed under a clear lens at the top portion of the shaft. The housing features a hand grip that is located below the LED light. The base of the work light has a built-in magnet, which allows for the light to be stuck on a vehicle or any other magnetic surface. A metal hook is attached to the top of the work light allowing it to be hung from the wall, ceiling, etc. Below the hook is a gripped hex nut, which allows the light to be placed on any surface.
