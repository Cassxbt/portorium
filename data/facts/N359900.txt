Samples were submitted with your ruling request and were forwarded to the U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) Laboratory for analysis. This analysis has been completed. The products under consideration are three styles of gift bows. The first, “Wrapping Paper Bow: Scattered Confetti,” (MIN-005-WBW), features a white background with multicolored dots on them. The second, “Wrapping Paper Bow: Colourful Kilim,” (MIN-00M-WBW), have either multicolored stripes or different colored sections on each bow. The third, “Foil-Pressed Wrapping Paper Bow: Gold Ferns,” (MIN-00G-WBW), has an off-white background with gold leaf patterns on them. Your submission indicates they are made of polyethylene and calcium carbonate. The laboratory analysis confirmed the presence of the calcium carbonate. We note that the calcium carbonate acts as a filler for the polyethylene and the bows have the character of plastic.
