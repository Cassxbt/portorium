DESCRIPTION OF MERCHANDISE       :    The submitted sample is a textile representation of a bear measuring approximately 12 inches in height. The figure is composed of plush fabric with plastic eyes and a plastic nose and is fully stuffed with textile material. The item is clothed in a removable felt like textile hat and a felt like textile vest secured by means of hook and loop                              type closure material. The hat and vest are suitable for use principally with the figure and are considered accessories of the figure. Accessories which are suitable for use solely or principally with toy animal figures are classified with those figures. HTS PROVISION     :    Toys representing animals or non human creatures, stuffed.
