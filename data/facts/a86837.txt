The submitted samples are referred to as a Nap N' Play Activity Mat set and a Nap N' Play Diaper Bag. The activity mat set consists of a cushioned mat, comforter, stuffed animal, rattles and a tote bag. The components of this set will be imported separately. These items will have an outer shell made from a polyester fabric. The activity mat which measures approximately 31 x 35 inches is stuffed with a polyester fiberfill. It has an animal print on one side and a polka dot pattern on the other. There is an additional 5 x 25 inch stuffed area sewn seven inches from one of the narrower edges. Four self fabric strips are sewn to the mat between this cushioned area and the edge. These strips have "hook and loop" closures and are designed to affix the toys to the mat. The comforter measures approximately 20 x 30 inches, is stuffed with a polyester fiberfill and features the same fabric prints as the mat. The submitted stuffed animal is a 9 inch bunny and it contains polyester fiberfill. The infant rattles and infant key ring are made of plastic. The polka dot print tote bag is approximately 16 x 20 inches and has a drawstring closure. The Nap N' Play Diaper Bag has a 100 percent cotton outer shell and is stuffed with a polyester fiberfill. The bag is approximately 10 x 10 x 3 inches and has a overlapping flap top opening with "hook and loop" closures. There are pockets on each side and the bag has a shoulder strap.
