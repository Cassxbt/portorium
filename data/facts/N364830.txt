This is in response to your submission of September 3, 2026, requesting a ruling on the classification of Dog Food. The subject merchandise consists of two varieties of prepared dog food sold under the “A Pup Above”® brand. Both products are fully cooked, formulated exclusively for consumption by dogs, and are not intended for human consumption. The products are packaged in individual one-pound vacuum-sealed pouches. The one-pound pouches are subsequently packaged for retail sale in configurations containing 3, 7, 10, or 12 one-pound pouches. The retail packages are labeled for sale directly to the consumer. The first product, Pork Recipe Dog Food, is composed of pork, pineapple, sweet potatoes, green beans, herbs, vitamin premix, water, and bone broth. The second product, Beef Recipe Dog Food, is composed of beef, potatoes, tomatoes, carrots, peas, safflower, herbs, vitamin premix, water, and beef bone material.
