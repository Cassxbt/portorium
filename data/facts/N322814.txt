This is in response to your letter dated November 16, 2021, requesting a ruling on classification, country of origin marking and the eligibility of the USMCA on three Substrate with Seeds. The subject merchandise, Substrate with Seeds, is described as a pre-fertilized and pre-seeded substrate that is for use in passive hydroponics. It is said to contain peat moss, coconut fiber, enriched black soil, kaolin, expanded perlite, multicote, latex, dolomitic lime, conductive wetting agent and seeds. The peat moss, coconut fiber, enriched black soil, kaolin, expanded perlite, latex, and seeds are products of Canada. The dolomitic lime, multicote and conductive wetting agent are products of the USA. The Substrate with Seeds will be imported in three varieties: Substrate with Tower Basil Seeds, Substrate with Purple Basil Seeds and Substrate with Capucine Seeds. The Substrate with Seeds products will be imported in plastic bags within boxes and is ready for planting in planters.
