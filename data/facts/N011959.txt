The merchandise under consideration consists of a hanging shower caddy, a toilet tissue holder and a tension pole caddy. Each comes in two styles, a hexagonal design and a mesh design. All are made of chrome plated steel or nickel plated steel. The “Shower Caddy” fits over the shower head and has three shelves to hold soap, shampoo, etc. The “Toilet Tissue Holder” is designed to rest on a horizontal surface and hold up to three rolls of toilet paper. The “Shelf Corner Tension Pole Caddy” has a tension pole that fits ceiling to floor in the corner of a shower stall with four shelves that can hold shampoo, soap, razors, sponges, etc.
