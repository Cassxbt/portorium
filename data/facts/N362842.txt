The subject Automated Lumber Bundle Formation System is engineered to create lumber bundles. The  lumber bundles are formed by an automated stacking machine that is positioned between upstream and  downstream conveyors and other machines. Due to its overall size, the system will be imported as modules  ready for assembly, with all modules imported in a single shipment. The first section consists of a series of conveyors referred to as two Haul Out Chain Modules, an Automated  Lumber Distribution Module, and a Lumber Flow Accumulation Module. The Haul Out Chain Modules  transport lumber from an upstream Sorter Module (not imported with the system) to the Automated Lumber  Distribution Module, which is a chain-type conveyor mounted on a platform that moves horizontally on rails. This platform transfers lumber to the Lumber Flow Accumulation Module, a stationary chain-type conveyor  that conveys the lumber to the Unscrambler. The Unscrambler arranges randomly accumulated lumber pieces into a parallel and evenly-spaced  orientation, preparing them for downstream automated bundle-forming operations. This machine may be  configured as a “single” or “double,” with the double configuration consisting of two single units stacked  vertically, but otherwise identical in function. The next group of machines includes Speed Up Chains and Package Maker Accumulation Transfer Units,  both specialized conveyors. The Speed Up Chains regulate the spacing, timing, and sequencing of lumber  planks as they exit the Unscrambler. The Package Maker Accumulation Transfer Unit, a chain-type 

conveyor, arranges the planks according to specified bundle dimensions. This is accomplished by adjusting  conveyor speed and buffering planks into organized rows, which are then transferred to the Package Maker  for bundle assembly. The Package Maker is the core of the automated bundle-forming system, responsible for aligning, stacking,  and bundling the incoming rows of planks. Incorporated into this unit are two submodules: the Pressing Foot  module, which secures planks in place during bundling, and the Secondary Lift module, located in the lower  section of the Package Maker. The Secondary Lift module lowers the assembled bundles, enabling the  Package Maker to continue stacking rows and maintain uninterrupted automated stacking cycles. The final group of machines includes an Outfeed Bundle Synchronization Module, Package Outfeed Rollers,  a Strapping Machine, a Strapping Machine Infeed Roller, a Strapping Machine Outfeed Transfer, a Package  Doubler, and an Outfeed Package Transfer. The Outfeed Bundle Synchronization Module, Package Outfeed  Rollers, Strapping Machine Infeed Roller, Strapping Machine Outfeed Transfer, and Outfeed Package  Transfer are all conveyors. The Strapping Machine straps the bundles before they are conveyed to the  Package Doubler, which stacks the bundles for retrieval by a forklift operator. The system also incorporates a Makeup Deck and Re-entry Bundle Sequencing Module to handle rejected or  reprocessed lumber. Both units are chain-type conveyors designed to transport and reintegrate rejected  lumber back into the system.
