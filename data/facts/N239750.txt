The first subject product Ethylenediamintetraacetic Acid (EDTA) is a polyamino carboxylic acid widely used in chelation therapy in the medical community to treat acute and chronic lead poisoning by pulling toxins including heavy metals such as lead, cadmium, and mercury from the bloodstream. It is also widely used to dissolve lime scale. The second subject product Calcium Disodium EDTA is a chelating agent used as a food additive to prevent chemical reactions that can lead to color and flavor deterioration in food products. The third subject product Tetrasodium EDTA is a chelating agent used to sequester and decrease the reactivity of metal ions in cosmetics and personal care products.
