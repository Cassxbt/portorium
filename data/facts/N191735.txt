Four representative samples were submitted along with their respective technical specification sheets. The four materials are identified as styles Pharoah, Kendo, Plaintex and Organza ESP. Two samples, Pharoah and Kendo, have been sent to the Customs and Border Protection laboratory for testing and their classification will be addressed in ruling number N193997. The samples are being retained by this office. The specification sheet for the style Organza ESP indicates it will be imported in a width of 183 centimeters. No width was provided for the style Plaintex, and no lengths were given for either style. The style Plaintex is stated to consist of a woven base cloth composed of 100% polyester fibers (filament) that has been coated on both sides with a vinyl based plastics material. The specification sheet indicates the face side has been coated with one layer of plastics material and the back has been coated with 3 layers of plastics material. Your request letter indicates the vinyl layers are non cellular and are coatings and not laminations. The coating is only visible to the naked eye on one side, the back side.
