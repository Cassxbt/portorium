Representative samples were included with your submission and will be retained by this office. The products you plan to import are Type 301, 304 and 316 welded, circular stainless steel tubes. These cold-drawn tubes have a wall thickness of 0.010 inch (0.254 mm) or 0.015 inch (0.38 mm) and an outside diameter ranging from 0.180 inch (4.57 mm) to 0.591 inch (15.01 mm). They will be imported in lengths ranging from approximately 2 to14 inches and will ultimately be made into parts for medical use. The tubes have the following nickel content: Type 301 (6 - 8%), Type 304 (8 - 10.5%) and Type 316 (10 -14%). In addition, the Type 316 tubes contain 2 - 3% molybdenum.
