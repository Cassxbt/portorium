A sample of the “Artsy Tote” craft kit, item 9018262, was received with your inquiry. It consists of an imitation paperboard play purse which also serves as the retail packaging. Also included are six sets of assorted plastic beads in different sizes and colors (over 400 beads in total), elastic cord, a plastic bead tray and an easy to follow instruction sheet. Children will use the kit’s beads and elastic cord to design their own uniquely crafted fashionable wrist bracelets and to engage in the imitative role-play activity of dressing up as a fancy lady. The kit’s amusement value is greater than the utilitarian value of the finished article. As assembled, the completed bracelets will be flimsily constructed and, in all likelihood, will not be used over a long period of time. The kit is principally designed for the amusement of girls 6 years of age and older.
