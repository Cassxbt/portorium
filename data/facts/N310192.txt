The ruling was requested for Item P/N 646, folding corrugated cardboard box. A sample was submitted for our review, and will be retained for reference. Item P/N 646 is a corrugated paperboard box measuring approximately 7.5” H by 7.5” W by 3.5” deep. The box is cut to shape in a flat state and is glued together along one vertical side. The box is cut and scored so as to be folded to shape and closed by means of tabs and slots. It is printed in one color with brand, product, and contact information. On one side, the box has a perforation that, when removed, allows the box to function as a dispenser for the packaged product.
