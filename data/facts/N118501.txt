You have requested a classification ruling on unassembled, hollow wooden posts. You state that the unassembled posts are comprised of four wooden boards measuring approximately 10” (w) x 2” (d) or 12” (w) x 2” (d) and 8’ to 12’ in length. The wooden boards are composed of mixtures of spruce, pine, and fir. Each board is tongued on one edge and plowed on the other, allowing for the boards to be joined at right angles, ultimately forming a square which will enclose an existing wood or steel structural post. You suggested a tariff classification of 4418.60.0000, which provides for Builders’ joinery and carpentry of wood: Posts and beams.
