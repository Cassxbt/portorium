The products under consideration are fine metallic powders, identified as 90iSC DAP (Type 4) Solder Powder, 90iSC AGP (Type 3) Solder Powder, and 97SC AGP (SAC 305 Type 3) Solder Powder. The stated components include tin (CAS no. 7440-31-5), silver Ag powder (CAS no. 7740-22-4), bismuth (CAS no. 4470-69-9), antimony (CAS no. 7440-36-0), copper metal (CAS no. 7440-50-8), and nickel (CAS no. 7440-02-0). A representative sample of each item was submitted with your request and was sent to the U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) laboratory for analysis. CBP laboratory analysis has determined that the 90iSC DAP (Type 4) and 90iSC AGP (Type 3) samples contain tin, silver, bismuth, antimony, and copper. The 97SC AGP (SAC 305 Type 3) sample was determined to contain tin, silver, and copper. The silver content for each sample is estimated to be more than 2 percent of the sample by weight, with the 90iSC DAP (Type 4) and 90iSC AGP (Type 3) samples estimated to contain more than 3 percent silver by weight. Furthermore, the laboratory determined that more than 90 percent of each sample has a particle size smaller than 0.5mm.
