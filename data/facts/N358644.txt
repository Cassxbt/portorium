The item at issue is a fuel injector, part number 0432191581. The fuel injector consists of a housing that  encases a spring, a plunger-like mechanism, and other components. Once activated by a high-pressure pulse  of fuel supplied by a fuel injection pump, the tension of the internal spring is overcome to spray a controlled   mist into the cylinder head of a vehicle. The fuel injector is constructed primarily of steel and is used in  marine diesel engines.
