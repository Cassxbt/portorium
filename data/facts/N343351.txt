Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The items under consideration are replacement water filters, listed under model numbers WDP-F07, WDP-F08, WDP-F27, WDS-F27 and WDP-F38. The lead-free replacement refrigerator filters are designed to reduce chlorine, contaminates, and odor for better tasting water. The filters are pressed into a designated slot of the water filtration system of a refrigerator, and purified water is then dispensed through the door of the refrigerator. The filters are to be replaced by a consumer approximately every six months and discarded. The filters are substantially similar and are only differentiated by a slight change in shape that allows them to fit into a specific model refrigerator.
