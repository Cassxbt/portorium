A sample was submitted withyour ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. Thisanalysis has been completed. Branco Dunas is an off-white stone with streaks and flecks of dark gray. From the information you provided,it will measure between 115 to 122 inches in length, between 65 to 75 inches in width, and either 0.75 inchesor 1.5 inches in thickness. After importation, it will be cut to size and shape for such applications as kitchencountertops, bathroom surfaces, and surfaces for office furniture. Laboratory analysis has determined that Branco Dunas is an igneous stone comprised predominantly ofquartz and alkali feldspars, and meets the geological definition of granite. The sides of the stone have beensimply cut or sawn, and the top has been polished.
