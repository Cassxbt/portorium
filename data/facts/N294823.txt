The first product under consideration is P UM ACTUATOR-AHLS LH (Automotive Hood Lifting Device), model name 2590805(79150-C5000). The actuator consists of a steel piston housed inside a steel tube and is located inside the engine compartment mounted to the vehicle’s hood hinge. It is linked to a vehicle’s sensors and an electronic control unit, as described in your ruling request. The function of the Automotive Hood Lifting Device is to lift the hood in the event of a pedestrian collision, thereby absorbing the energy of the impact and lessening the severity of an injury. When a vehicle’s sensors detect an impact, a micro gas generator (MGG) provides pneumatic pressure that activates the pistons and raises the rear portion of the hood.
