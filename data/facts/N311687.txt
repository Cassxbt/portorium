The merchandise under consideration is identified as Wiper Modules with DC Motor (Wiper Modules). You submitted five Wiper Modules, PN W000077735, PN W000060390, and PN W000050762, which are motor assemblies with a maximum output of 60 W, PN W000076689, which is a motor assembly with a maximum output of 67.3 W, and PN W000079033, which is a motor assembly with a maximum output of 65 W. Each of the Wiper Modules consist of an electric DC motor, the pivot assembly, the shaft assembly, and the grommet assembly. The Wiper Modules are intended to be used in a windshield wiper system for a motor vehicle by transforming the rotational motion of the motor to oscillatory motion for the wipers. We would note that the wiper arms and/or wiper blades are not attached to the subject Wiper Modules at the time of importation.
