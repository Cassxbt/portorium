The merchandise under consideration is described as an electric motor for submersible wastewater pumps  which is identified by model number DSC4. The electric motor consists of a stator, rotor, and output shaft  with bearings but does not include any type of gearing or any attachments. The subject motor is a multi-phase  AC motor with maximum power output of 42.5kW and it is intended to be mated to a pump assembly which  will be deployed in municipal wastewater systems, industrial effluent handling, drainage, and similar  applications where the complete pump assembly operates submerged in the pump medium. We note that the  pump is not imported with the electric motor.
