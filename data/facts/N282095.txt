You have submitted two samples, which will be returned to you under separate cover. You refer to the first sample as the “ice pack bag.” It is an insulated cooler bag constructed with an outer surface of man-made textile material. The cooler bag is designed to provide storage, protection, organization, and portability to food and beverages during travel. It is also designed to maintain the temperature of food and beverages. The cooler bag has one interior storage compartment with a plastic lining and a layer of foam plastic between the lining and the body of the bag. The cooler bag has a top zipper closure and one strap. There is also a gel pack permanently incorporated into the body of the cooler bag. The bag and gel pack are considered a composite good for tariff purposes.
