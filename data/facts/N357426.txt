The merchandise at issue with this request is identified as the CO Jumper, 6 Pin, part number  300-00066-000.A3, which is further described as a cable assembly specifically used within the Delta Zulu  aviation headset. The subject assembly is constructed of six individually insulated conductors that are wired  to a 5-pin “PICO” connector. The other end of the conductors are unterminated. The cable assembly is meant  to connect the sensor PCBA within the headset to the earcup in order to deliver electrical signals monitoring  the various audio levels. We note that the signals are electrical and not for audio or communication.
