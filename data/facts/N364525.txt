The “Front Face Panel” is an assembled wooden furniture cabinet panel that measures between 10-42" long, 4-24" wide, and 3/4" thick. The panel is comprised of beech, maple, or red oak, a particleboard base, lumber-banded edges with solid hardwood staves, 1 mm veneer, and is not stained, painted, or surface coated. Dependent on the length and the width of the assembled panels, they will be installed onto wooden cabinet furniture as doors and drawers.
