The submitted samples are a pair of windshield wiper covers you call “Winter Shield,” packaged together for retail sale. The wiper covers are two sleeves, made up from nylon fabric. Each sleeve measures 32” x 5-1/4”. They are used to cover windshield wipers during very cold weather to prevent the wipers from sticking to the windshield of a vehicle. They may be used to clear snow from the windshield of a parked car but are to be removed before driving.
