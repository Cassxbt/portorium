The imported merchandise consists of two types of wall support arms, the FX Series and the RX Series. The FX and RX Series provides for wall mounting of computer peripherals within the medical work environment. The FX and the RX Series can be configured by the end user to secure laptops, flat panel monitors, keyboards (with or without mouse), a CPU and printers. All computer peripherals are supplied by the purchaser or end user. The FX and the RX Series allows the user to sit or stand depending upon the configuration. The FX and RX Series can be shipped with various track lengths to meet user requirements or the unit can be retrofitted in the field with extension arms if the requirements change and extra reach is needed. The FX Series is manufactured with a height adjustable arm which contains a gas cylinder to adjust the arms up or down depending on the desired height. A gas cylinder assembly inside the arm adjusts through a bolt mechanism. The user inserts an allen wrench to articulate the arm to the desired height depending upon the weight load installed in the counter-balance movement mechanism. The RX Series of arms allows for length adjustment but not height. Both series models do not contain wires and are manually operated. They are made of 90% aluminum, 5% steel and 5% polyurethane.
